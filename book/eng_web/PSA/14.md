# Psalms 14

*For the Chief Musician. By David.*

**1** > The fool has said in his heart, “There is no God.”> > They are corrupt.
> > They have done abominable deeds.
> > There is no one who does good.

**2** > Yahweh looked down from heaven on the children of men,> > to see if there were any who understood,
> > who sought after God.

**3** > They have all gone aside.> > They have together become corrupt.
> > There is no one who does good, no, not one.

**4** > Have all the workers of iniquity no knowledge,> > who eat up my people as they eat bread,
> > and don’t call on Yahweh?

**5** > There they were in great fear,> > for God is in the generation of the righteous.

**6** > You frustrate the plan of the poor,> > because Yahweh is his refuge.

**7** > Oh that the salvation of Israel would come out of Zion!> > When Yahweh restores the fortunes of his people,
> > then Jacob shall rejoice, and Israel shall be glad.
