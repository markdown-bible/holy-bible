# Psalms 29

*A Psalm of David.*

**1** > Give unto the LORD, O ye mighty, give unto the LORD glory and strength.[^164]

**2** > Give unto the LORD the glory due unto his name; worship the LORD in the beauty of holiness.[^165][^166]

**3** > The voice of the LORD is upon the waters: the God of glory thundereth: the LORD is upon many waters.[^167]

**4** > The voice of the LORD is powerful; the voice of the LORD is full of majesty.[^168][^169]

**5** > The voice of the LORD breaketh the cedars; yea, the LORD breaketh the cedars of Lebanon.

**6** > He maketh them also to skip like a calf; Lebanon and Sirion like a young unicorn.

**7** > The voice of the LORD divideth the flames of fire.[^170]

**8** > The voice of the LORD shaketh the wilderness; the LORD shaketh the wilderness of Kadesh.

**9** > The voice of the LORD maketh the hinds to calve, and discovereth the forests: and in his temple doth every one speak of his glory.[^171][^172]

**10** > The LORD sitteth upon the flood; yea, the LORD sitteth King for ever.

**11** > The LORD will give strength unto his people; the LORD will bless his people with peace.
