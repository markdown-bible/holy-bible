# Psalms 131

*A Song of degrees of David.*

**1** > LORD, my heart is not haughty, nor mine eyes lofty: neither do I exercise myself in great matters, or in things too high for me.[^697][^698]

**2** > Surely I have behaved and quieted myself, as a child that is weaned of his mother: my soul is even as a weaned child.[^699]

**3** > Let Israel hope in the LORD from henceforth and for ever.[^700]
