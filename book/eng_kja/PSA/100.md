# Psalms 100

*A Psalm of praise.*

**1** > Make a joyful noise unto the LORD, all ye lands.[^572][^573]

**2** > Serve the LORD with gladness: come before his presence with singing.

**3** > Know ye that the LORD he is God: it is he that hath made us, and not we ourselves; we are his people, and the sheep of his pasture.[^574]

**4** > Enter into his gates with thanksgiving, and into his courts with praise: be thankful unto him, and bless his name.

**5** > For the LORD is good; his mercy is everlasting; and his truth endureth to all generations.[^575]
