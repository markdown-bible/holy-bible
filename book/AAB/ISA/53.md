# Isaiah 53

## The Suffering Servant

**1** > Who has believed our message?> > And to whom has the arm of the LORD been revealed?[^191]

**2** > He grew up before Him like a tender shoot,> > and like a root out of dry ground.> He had no stately form or majesty to attract us,> > no beauty that we should desire Him.

**3** > He was despised and rejected by men,> > a man of sorrows, acquainted with grief.> Like one from whom men hide their faces,> > He was despised, and we esteemed Him not.


**4** > Surely He took up our infirmities> > and carried our sorrows;[^192]> yet we considered Him stricken,> > struck down by God, and afflicted.

**5** > But He was pierced for our transgressions,> > He was crushed for our iniquities;> the chastening[^193]> that brought us peace was upon Him,> > and by His stripes we are healed.[^194]

**6** > We all like sheep have gone astray,[^195]> > each one has turned to his own way;> and the LORD has laid upon Him> > the iniquity of us all.


**7** > He was oppressed and afflicted,> > yet He did not open His mouth.> He was led like a lamb to the slaughter,> > and as a sheep before her shearers is silent,
> > so He did not open His mouth.

**8** > By oppression and judgment He was taken away,> > and who can recount His descendants?> For He was cut off from the land of the living;[^196]> > He was stricken for the transgression of My people.

## A Grave Assigned


**9** > He was assigned a grave with the wicked,> > and with a rich man in His death,> although He had done no violence,> > nor was any deceit in His mouth.[^197]


**10** > Yet it was the LORD’s will to crush Him[^198]> > and to cause Him to suffer;> and when His life[^199]> is made a reparation offering,[^200]> > He will see His offspring, He will prolong His days,
> > and the good pleasure of the LORD will prosper in His hand.

**11** > After the anguish of His life,[^201]> > He will see the light of life[^202]> > and be satisfied.> By His knowledge My righteous Servant will justify many,> > and He will bear their iniquities.

**12** > Therefore I will allot Him a portion with the great,[^203]> > and He will divide the spoils with the strong,[^204]> because He has poured out His life unto death,> > and He was numbered with the transgressors.[^205]> Yet He bore the sin of many> > and made intercession for the transgressors.
