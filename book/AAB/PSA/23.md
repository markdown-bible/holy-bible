# Psalms 23

## The LORD Is My Shepherd

*A Psalm of David.*

**1** > The LORD is my shepherd;[^50]> > I shall not want.

**2** > He makes me lie down in green pastures;> > He leads me beside quiet waters.

**3** > He restores my soul;> > He guides me in the paths of righteousness
> > for the sake of His name.

**4** > Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death,[^51]> > I will fear no evil,> for You are with me;> > Your rod and Your staff, they comfort me.


**5** > You prepare a table before me> > in the presence of my enemies.> You anoint my head with oil;> > my cup overflows.

**6** > Surely goodness and mercy will follow me> > all the days of my life,> and I will dwell in the house of the LORD> > forever.[^52]

## Footnotes

[^50]: See Revelation 7:17.
[^51]: Or the valley of deep darkness
[^52]: Literally for a length of days
