# LETTER TO KEHILLAH IN PHILIPPI 3

**1** As to the rest, Achim b'Moshiach of mine, have simcha in Adoneinu.[^57]To keep writing the zelba thing[^58]to you is not an irksome bother to me, but for you it is a te'udat bitachon (safeguard).

**2** Be shomer and on your guard and watch out for those[^59]kelevim (dogs),[^60]watch out for the evil po'alim (workers), watch out for the “circummutilators”.[^61]

**3** For we are the Bnei HaMilah,[^62]the ones whose avodas kodesh is by the Ruach Hakodesh[^63]and whose kavod is in Moshiach Yehoshua and who take no bitachon in the basar,

**4** Even though I could be having bitachon also in the basar. If any other person thinks he has grounds to have bitachon in the basar, I have more:[^64]

**5** Bris milah on the eighth day;[^65]from Bnei Yisroel by birth; of the tribe of Binyamin; a speaker of Lashon HaKodesh, Ivrit of Ivrit-speaking horim, a Hebrew of Hebrews;[^66]with regard to the Torah, from the kat haPerushim;[^67]

**6** With regard to kin'a (zealousness), persecuting the Adat HaMoshiach;[^68]with regard to tzidkat HaTorah,[^69]I was medakdekim bmitzvot unreproachable.[^70]

**7** But what things were revach (gain, profit) to me, these things I considered loss, on account of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach.[^71]

**8** But even more so, I consider all to be loss on account of the excellency of the da'as of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua Adoneinu,[^72]on account of whom I suffered the loss of all things and I consider them as nothing, in comparison, that I may gain Moshiach,[^73]

**9** And be found in him, not having my own Tzidkot (self-achieved righteousness, by definition a self-righteousness based on chumra stringency)[^74]from gezetz, but the Tzedek[^75]through emunah[^76]in Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, the Tzidkat Hashem based upon emunah.[^77]

**10** I want to have da'as of Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach, and of the gevurah (power) of the Techiyas HaMoshiach and the deveykus (attachment to G-d) of Moshiachʼs yissurim (sufferings),[^78]being formed into the mode of being of Moshiachʼs death,[^79]

**11** If somehow I may attain to the Techiyas HaMesim.

**12** Not that already I obtained or already have been made shleimut, but I pursue this tachlis (final end, aim) that I may lay hold of[^80]that for which I was laid hold of by Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.[^81]

**13** Achim b'Moshiach, I do not consider myself to have laid hold;[^82]but one zach (thing) I do, forgetting the things behind, and stretching forward to the things ahead,

**14** According to the tachlis I pursue the prize of the Shomayim Aliyah ascent of Hashem, the upward k'riah (call) of HaShem b'Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua.[^83]

**15** Therefore, as many as would be shleimut, let us think like this:[^84]and if in anything your machshavot (thoughts) are different,[^85]even this Hashem will reveal to you.

**16** Fort (nevertheless), let us march in line with what we have attained, let us hold to the same.[^86]

**17** Achim b'Moshiach, together be imitators of me.[^87]Take note of the ones walking as you have a mofet in us.[^88]

**18** For many, of whom I was often telling you, and now also I say with weeping, walk as oyvim (enemies) of Moshiachʼs Etz.[^89]

**19** Their destined end[^90]is churban (destruction, Gehinnom). Their g-d is their appetite,[^91]and their kavod, what they glory in, is their bushah (shame). Their machshavot are set on the Olam Hazeh.[^92]

**20** But the torat haEzrakhut[^93](citizenship) we conduct exists in Shomayim,[^94]from where also we eagerly await a Moshi'a (Savior), a Go'el, Adoneinu Rebbe, Melech HaMoshiach Yehoshua,

**21** Who will transfigure the basar of our humiliation into the demut of the mode of being of the guf kavod of Moshiach, according to the pe'ulah[^95](action, work) of his ko'ach (power),[^96]even to the subjecting of all things to himself.[^97]
