# TEHILLIM 59

**1** (For the one directing. Set to Do Not Destroy. Mikhtam Dovid. When Sha'ul sent and they watched [Dovid's] bais to kill him)

**2** > Save me from mine enemies, O Elohai;
> protect me from them that rise up against me.

**3** > Deliver me from the po'alei aven (workers of iniquity),
> and save me from anshei damim (bloody men).


**4** > For, hinei, they lie in wait for my nefesh;
> the fierce stir up strife against me;
> not for my peysha, nor for my chattat, Hashem.

**5** > They run and prepare themselves without my avon (wrong, fault);
> awake to help me, and see.

**6** > Thou therefore, Hashem Elohim Tzva'os,
> Elohei Yisroel,
> rouse Thyself to visit[^75]> kol HaGoyim;
> be not merciful to any bogedei aven (wicked boged).[^76]
> Selah


**7** > They return at erev;
> they growl like a kelev (dog),
> and prowl around the Ir.

**8** > Hinei, they belch out with their mouth;
> charavot (swords) are in their sfatot (lips);
> for who, say they, doth hear?

**9** > But Thou, Hashem, shalt laugh at them;
> Thou shalt have all the Goyim in derision.

**10** > O my Strength, I will be shomer to watch for Thee;
> for Elohim is my stronghold.

**11** > The G-d of my chesed shall come to meet me;
> Elohim shall let me see [the end] of my shorer (watchers).[^77]


**12** > Slay them not, lest my people forget;
> scatter them by Thy power;
> and bring them down, Adonoi mogineinu (our Shield).

**13** > For the chattat of their mouth and the davar of their sfatayim,
> let them even be caught in their ga'on (pride);
> and for the cursing and lying which they speak.

**14** > Consume them in chemah (wrath),
> consume them, till they are no more;
> and let them know that Elohim ruleth in Ya'akov
> unto the ends of ha'aretz.
> Selah


**15** > And at erev let them return;
> and let them growl like a kelev,
> and prowl around the Ir.

**16** > Let them wander up and down for okhel (food),
> and howl if they be not satisfied.


**17** > But I will sing of Thy oz;
> yes, I will sing aloud of Thy chesed in the boker;
> for Thou hast been my stronghold
> and refuge in the day of my tzoros.

**18** > Unto Thee, O my Strength, will I sing;
> for Elohim is my strongold, and the Elohim of my chesed.
