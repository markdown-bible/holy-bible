# TEHILLIM 92

**1** (Mizmor. Shir. For Shabbos)

**2** > It is tov to give thanks unto Hashem,
> and to make music unto Thy Shem, O Elyon;

**3** > To proclaim Thy chesed in the boker,
> and Thy emunah baleilot,

**4** > Upon a ten-stringed instrument, and upon the nevel (lyre);
> to the melody of the kinnor (harp).

**5** > For Thou, Hashem, hast made me glad through Thy work;
> I will joy in the works of Thy hands.


**6** > Hashem, how great are Thy works!
> And Thy machshe'vot (thoughts) are very deep.

**7** > An ish ba'ar (brutish, senseless man, dolt) knoweth not;
> neither doth a kesil (fool) understand this.

**8** > When the resha'im spring up like the esev (grass),
> and when all the workers of iniquity do flourish,
> it is that they shall be destroyed adei-ad (forever).

**9** > But Thou, Hashem, art exalted l'olam.

**10** > For, hinei, Thine enemies, Hashem,
> for, hinei, Thine enemies shall perish;
> all the workers of iniquity shall be scattered.


**11** > But my keren shalt Thou exalt like the keren of a re'eim (wild ox);
> I shall be anointed with fresh shemen.

**12** > Mine eye also shall look on mine foes,
> and mine ears shall hear of the resha'im
> that rise up against me.


**13** > The tzaddik shall flourish like the palm tree;
> he shall grow like a cedar in Levanon.

**14** > Those that are planted in the Beis Hashem
> shall flourish in the khatzerot Eloheinu (courts of our G-d).

**15** > They shall still bear forth fruit in old age;
> they shall be deshenim (stalwart, vigorous) and ra'ananim (luxuriant, fresh);

**16** > To proclaim that Hashem is yashar;
> He is my Tzur, and there is no unrighteousness in Him.
