# TEHILLIM 110

**1** (Of Dovid. Mizmor)> 1Hashem said unto Adoni,[^143]
> Sit thou at My right hand,
> until I make thine enemies
> a footstool for thy feet.

**2** > Hashem shall stretch forth
> the rod of thy might out of Tziyon;
> rule thou in the midst of thine[^144]> enemies.[^145]

**3** > Thy[^146]> people, willing in the Day of thy[^147]> Army,
> will be arrayed in the splendor of kodesh (holiness);
> from the womb of the dawn,
> cometh to thee[^148]> thy young men[^149]> as the tal (dew).[^150]

**4** > Hashem hath sworn, and will not relent,
> Thou[^151]> art a kohen l'olam al divrati Malki-Tzedek
> (kohen forever in respect to the order of Malki-Tzedek).[^152]


**5** > Adonoi at thy right hand shall dash melachim (kings)
> in pieces in the Yom Afo (Day of His Wrath).

**6** > He[^153]> shall judge among the Goyim,
> He shall heap up geviyyot (corpses);
> He shall dash in pieces Rosh (Head);[^154]> al Eretz Rabbah (over Great, Whole Earth).[^155]

**7** > Of the brook in the Derech shall he[^156]> drink;
> therefore shall he lift up [as victor] the Rosh.[^157]
