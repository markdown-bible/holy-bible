# TEHILLIM 128

**1** (Shir HaMa'alot)> 1 Ashrei kol yerei (fearers of)Hashem;
> that walketh in His drakhim.

**2** > For thou shalt eat the labor of thine hands;
> happy shalt thou be, and it shall be tov with thee.


**3** > Thy isha shall be as a fruitful gefen
> inside thine bais;
> thy banim like olive shoots
> around thy shulchan.

**4** > Hinei, thus shall the gever be blessed
> that feareth Hashem.


**5** > Hashem shall bless thee out of Tziyon;
> and thou shalt see the tov of Yerushalayim
> all the days of thy life.

**6** > Yea, thou shalt see thy bnei banim,
> and shalom upon Yisroel.
