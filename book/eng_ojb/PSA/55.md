# TEHILLIM 55

**1** (For the one directing. With Neginos. Maskil of Dovid)

**2** > Give ear to my tefillah, O Elohim;
> and veil not Thyself from my techinnah (supplication).

**3** > Attend unto me, and hear me;
> I wander restlessly in my si'ach (anxiety), and aloud I groan;

**4** > Because of the voice of the oyev (enemy),
> because of the oppression of the rasha (evildoer);
> for they cast aven (iniquity) upon me,
> and in af (wrath, anger) cherish their malice against me.


**5** > My lev writhes within me;
> and the terrors of mavet are fallen upon me.

**6** > Yirah va'ra'ad (fear and trembling) are come upon me,
> and pallatzut (horror, shuddering) overwhelmed me.

**7** > And I said, Oh that I had wings like a yonah!
> For then would I fly away and be at rest.

**8** > Hinei, then would I flee far off
> and lodge in the midbar.
> Selah

**9** > I would hasten to my place of shelter
> from the ruach so'ah (wind of the tempest) and storm.


**10** > Destroy, Adonoi, and confuse their leshon;
> for I see chamas (violence) and riv (strife) in the Ir (city).[^65]

**11** > Yomam and lailah they go about her[^66]> upon the chomot thereof;
> aven also and amal are in the midst of her.[^67]

**12** > Ruin is in the midst thereof;
> oppression and mirmah (guile, fraud, deceit) depart not from her rechov.

**13** > For it was not an oyev (enemy) that reproached me;
> then I could bear it;
> neither was it he that had sinah (hatred) for me that did exalt himself against me;
> then I could have hid myself from him;

**14** > But it was thou, an enosh (a man) equally,[^68]
> my aluf (intimate, friend), and me'yuda'i (deep acquaintance, familiar friend).[^69]

**15** > Together we took sweet sod (intimate conversation, fellowship, counsel),
> and walked unto the Beis Elohim with the throng.

**16** > Let mavet seize upon them,
> and let them go down chayyim into Sheol;
> for ra'ot (evils) are in their dwellings, and among them.


**17** > As for me, I will call upon Elohim;
> and Hashem shall save me.

**18** > Erev, and boker, and at tzohorayim (noon),
> will I pray, and cry aloud;
> and He shall hear my kol (voice).

**19** > He hath redeemed my nefesh in shalom
> from the battle that was against me;
> for there were rabbim against me.

**20** > El (G-d) shall hear, and afflict them,
> even He that abideth kedem (of old, forever).
> Selah
> Because they do not change,
> therefore they fear not Elohim.


**21** > He[^70]> hath laid his hands on such
> as are at shalom with him;
> he hath broken his Brit.

**22** > The words of his mouth were smoother than butter,
> but war was in his lev;
> his devarim were softer than shemen,
> yet they were drawn swords.


**23** > Cast thy burden upon Hashem,
> and He shall sustain thee;
> He shall never suffer the tzaddik to be moved.


**24** > But Thou, O Elohim, shalt bring them down into the pit of shachat;
> anshei damim umirmah (bloody and deceitful men)
> shall not live out half their yamim;
> but I will trust in Thee.
