# TEHILLIM 121

**1** (Shir HaMa'alot)> 1 I will lift up mine eyes unto the harim (hills),
> from whence cometh ezri (my help).

**2** > Ezri (my help) cometh from Hashem,
> Oseh Shomayim va'Aretz.


**3** > He will not allow thy regel to slip;
> He that is shomer over thee will not slumber.

**4** > Hinei, He that is shomer over Yisroel
> shall neither slumber nor sleep.


**5** > Hashem is the One shomer over thee;
> Hashem is thy shade upon thy right hand.

**6** > The shemesh shall not strike thee by day,
> nor the yarei'ach by night.


**7** > Hashem shall preserve thee from kol rah;
> He shall preserve thy nefesh.

**8** > Hashem shall be shomer over thy going out and thy coming in
> from this time forth, and even v'ad olam.
