# TEHILLIM 20

**1** (For the one directing. Mizmor Dovid)

**2** > Hashem hear thee in the Yom Tzarah (day of trouble);
> the Shem Elohei Ya'akov set thee up on high;[^16]

**3** > Send thee help from the Kodesh (Holy Sanctuary),
> and strengthen thee out of Tziyon;

**4** > Remember all thy minchot,
> and accept thy olat;
> Selah.


**5** > Grant thee according to thine own levav,
> and fulfil kol etzah (plan, counsel) of thine.

**6** > We will shout for joy at thy Yeshu'ah (salvation),
> and in the Shem of Eloheinu we will lift up our banners;
> Hashem grant all thy requests.


**7** > Now I have da'as that Hashem hoshi'a (He saves) His Moshiach;
> He will answer him from His Sh'mei Kodesh (Holy Heaven)
> with the saving strength of His right hand.

**8** > Some trust in chariots, and some susim:
> but we will trust in the Shem Hashem Eloheinu.

**9** > They are brought down and fallen,
> but we are risen up, and stand firm.


**10** > Hoshi'ah, Hashem, HaMelech!
> May He hear us on the day when we call.

## Footnotes

[^16]: i.e., out of reach of your enemies
