# TEHILLIM 36

**1** (For the one directing. Of Dovid, the eved Hashem)

**2** > Peysha speaketh to the rashah within my lev,
> that there is no pachad Elohim (fear of G-d) before his eyes.

**3** > For he flattereth himself in his own eyes,
> until his avon (iniquity) be found to be hateful.

**4** > The words of his mouth are iniquity and mirmah (deceit);
> he hath ceased to be wise, and to do tov (good).

**5** > He deviseth evil upon his mishkav (bed);
> he setteth himself in a derech that is not tov;
> he abhorreth not rah.


**6** > Thy chesed, Hashem, is in HaShomayim;
> and Thy emunah (faithfulness) reacheth unto the clouds.

**7** > Thy tzedakah is like the great mountains;
> Thy mishpatim are tehom rabbah (a great deep);
> Hashem, Thou preservest adam (man) and behemah (beast).


**8** > How precious is Thy chesed, O Elohim!
> Therefore the Bnei Adam find refuge
> under the shadow of Thy wings.

**9** > They shall be abundantly satisfied with the abundance of Thy Beis;[^31]
> and Thou shalt give them to drink of Thy river of delight.

**10** > For with Thee is the Makor Chayyim (fountain of life);
> in Thy Ohr shall we see ohr.


**11** > O continue Thy chesed unto them that know Thee;
> and Thy tzedakah to the yishrei lev (the ones upright of heart).

**12** > Let not the regel ga'avah (foot of pride) come against me,
> and let not the yad of the resha'im drive me away.

**13** > There are the po'alei aven (workers of iniquity) fallen;
> they are thrown down, and shall not be able to rise.
