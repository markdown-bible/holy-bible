# TEHILLIM 22

**1** (For the one directing. To the Deer of the Dawn. Mizmor Dovid)

**2** > Eli, Eli, lamah azavtani?[^18]
> Why art Thou so far from my yeshu'ah, and from the words of my groaning?

**3** > Elohai, I cry yomam, but Thou hearest not;
> and v'lailah, and am not silent.


**4** > But Atah Kadosh,
> O Thou enthroned on the tehillot Yisroel.

**5** > Avoteinu trusted in Thee;
> they had bitachon, and Thou didst deliver them.

**6** > They cried unto Thee, and were delivered;
> they had bitachon in Thee, and were not disappointed.


**7** > But I am a tola'at, and no ish;
> a reproach of men, and despised of the people.[^19]

**8** > All they that see me mock me;
> they shoot out the lip, they shake the rosh, saying,

**9** > He trusted in Hashem;
> let Hashem rescue him; let Him deliver him, since He delights in him.


**10** > But Thou art He that took me out of the womb;
> Thou didst make me trust when I was upon the breasts of immi.

**11** > I was cast upon Thee from the womb;
> Thou art Eli from the womb of immi.

**12** > Be not far from me;
> for tzoros is near; and there is none to help.

**13** > Parim rabbim (many bulls) have encompassed me;
> strong bulls of Bashan have beset me round about.

**14** > They opened wide their mouths upon me,
> like an aryeh roaring and tearing prey.


**15** > I am poured out like mayim,
> and all my atzmot are out of joint;
> my lev is like wax;
> it is melted away within me.

**16** > My ko'ach is dried up like baked clay;
> and my leshon cleaveth to my jaws;
> and Thou layest me in the aphar mavet (dust of death).


**17** > For kelavim have surrounded me;
> the Adat Mere'im (congregation of evil men) have enclosed me;
> KA'ARU YADAI V'RAGELAI (they pierced my hands and my feet).[^20]

**18** > I can count all my atzmot;
> the people stare and gloat at me.

**19** > They divide up my garments among them,
> and for my clothing they cast goral (lots).[^21]


**20** > But be not Thou far from me, Hashem;
> O my strength, hasten Thee to help me.

**21** > Deliver my nefesh from the cherev,
> my yechid (only one, only) [nefesh] from the power of the kelev (dog).

**22** > Hoshieini from the mouth of the aryeh;
> for Thou hast heard me from the karnayim of the wild bulls.


**23** > I will declare Thy Shem unto my Achim;
> in the midst of the Kahal will I praise Thee.

**24** > Ye that fear Hashem, praise Him;
> all ye Zera Ya'akov, give Him kavod;
> and fear Him, all ye Zera Yisroel.

**25** > For He hath not despised
> nor disdained the enut ani (affliction of the afflicted);
> neither hath He hid His face from him;
> but when he cried unto Him, He heard.

**26** > My tehillah shall be from Thee in the Kahal Rav;
> I will fulfill my nedar before them that fear Him.

**27** > The anavim (meek) shall eat and be satisfied;
> they that seek Him shall praise Hashem.
> Let your levav live forever!


**28** > All the ends of HaAretz shall remember
> and turn unto Hashem;
> and all the mishpechot of the Goyim
> shall bow down in worship before Thee.

**29** > For the Meluchah is Hashemʼs:
> and He is the Moshel over the Goyim.


**30** > All they that thrive upon HaAretz
> shall eat and worship;
> all they that go down to the aphar (dust) shall bow before Him;
> even he who cannot keep alive his own nefesh.

**31** > Zera shall serve Him;
> it shall be told to the generation [to come] concerning Adonoi.

**32** > They shall come, and shall declare His tzedek
> unto an Am Nolad (a [future] people that shall be born),
> that He hath done this.
