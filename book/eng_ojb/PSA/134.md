# TEHILLIM 134

**1** (Shir HaMa'alot)> 1 Hinei, barakhu Hashem, kol avedei Hashem,
> which balailah stand[^168]> in the Bais Hashem.

**2** > Lift up your hands in the Kodesh,[^169]
> and barakhu Hashem.

**3** > Yevarekhecha Hashem from Tziyon,
> Oseh Shomayim va'Aretz (the Maker of Heaven and Earth).
