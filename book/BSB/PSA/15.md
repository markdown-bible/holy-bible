# Psalms 15

## Who May Dwell on Your Holy Mountain?

*A Psalm of David.*


**1** > O LORD, who may abide in Your tent?> > Who may dwell on Your holy mountain?


**2** > He who walks with integrity> > and practices righteousness,
> > who speaks the truth from his heart,

**3** > who has no slander on his tongue,> > who does no harm to his neighbor,
> > who casts no scorn on his friend,

**4** > who despises the vile> > but honors those who fear the LORD,
> > who does not revise a costly oath,

**5** > who lends his money without interest> > and refuses a bribe against the innocent.
> He who does these things> > will never be shaken.
