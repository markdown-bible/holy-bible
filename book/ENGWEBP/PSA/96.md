# Psalms 96

**1** > Sing to the LORD a new song!> > Sing to the LORD, all the earth.

**2** > Sing to the LORD!> > Bless his name!
> > Proclaim his salvation from day to day!

**3** > Declare his glory among the nations,> > his marvelous works among all the peoples.

**4** > For the LORD is great, and greatly to be praised!> > He is to be feared above all gods.

**5** > For all the gods of the peoples are idols,> > but the LORD made the heavens.

**6** > Honor and majesty are before him.> > Strength and beauty are in his sanctuary.

**7** > Ascribe to the LORD, you families of nations,> > ascribe to the LORD glory and strength.

**8** > Ascribe to the LORD the glory due to his name.> > Bring an offering, and come into his courts.

**9** > Worship the LORD in holy array.> > Tremble before him, all the earth.

**10** > Say among the nations, “The LORD reigns.”> > The world is also established.
> > It can’t be moved.
> > He will judge the peoples with equity.

**11** > Let the heavens be glad, and let the earth rejoice.> > Let the sea roar, and its fullness!

**12** > > Let the field and all that is in it exult!
> > Then all the trees of the woods shall sing for joy

**13** > > before the LORD; for he comes,
> > for he comes to judge the earth.> He will judge the world with righteousness,> > the peoples with his truth.
