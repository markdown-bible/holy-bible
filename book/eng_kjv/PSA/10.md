# Psalms 10

**1** > Why standest thou afar off, O LORD? why hidest thou thyself in times of trouble?

**2** > The wicked in his pride doth persecute the poor: let them be taken in the devices that they have imagined.[^38]

**3** > For the wicked boasteth of his heart’s desire, and blesseth the covetous, whom the LORD abhorreth.[^39][^40]

**4** > The wicked, through the pride of his countenance, will not seek after God: God is not in all his thoughts.[^41]

**5** > His ways are always grievous; thy judgments are far above out of his sight: as for all his enemies, he puffeth at them.

**6** > He hath said in his heart, I shall not be moved: for I shall never be in adversity.[^42]

**7** > His mouth is full of cursing and deceit and fraud: under his tongue is mischief and vanity.[^43][^44]

**8** > He sitteth in the lurking places of the villages: in the secret places doth he murder the innocent: his eyes are privily set against the poor.[^45]

**9** > He lieth in wait secretly as a lion in his den: he lieth in wait to catch the poor: he doth catch the poor, when he draweth him into his net.[^46]

**10** > He croucheth, and humbleth himself, that the poor may fall by his strong ones.[^47][^48]

**11** > He hath said in his heart, God hath forgotten: he hideth his face; he will never see it.


**12** > Arise, O LORD; O God, lift up thine hand: forget not the humble.[^49]

**13** > Wherefore doth the wicked contemn God? he hath said in his heart, Thou wilt not require it.

**14** > Thou hast seen it; for thou beholdest mischief and spite, to requite it with thy hand: the poor committeth himself unto thee; thou art the helper of the fatherless.[^50]

**15** > Break thou the arm of the wicked and the evil man: seek out his wickedness till thou find none.

**16** > The LORD is King for ever and ever: the heathen are perished out of his land.

**17** > LORD, thou hast heard the desire of the humble: thou wilt prepare their heart, thou wilt cause thine ear to hear:[^51]

**18** > To judge the fatherless and the oppressed, that the man of the earth may no more oppress.[^52]
