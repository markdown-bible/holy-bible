# THE REVELATION TO JOHN 5

## The Scroll and the Lamb

**1** Then I saw in the right hand of him who sits on the throne a scroll written on the inside and[^81]outside, sealed with seven seals.

**2** And I saw a mighty angel proclaiming with a loud voice, “Who is worthy to open the scroll and break its seals?”

**3** But no one in heaven[^82]above or on earth or under the earth could open the scroll or look inside it.

**4** So I began to weep loudly because no one was found who was worthy to[^83]open the scroll or look inside it.

**5** Then one of the elders said to me, “Do not weep; behold, the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has[^84]conquered; he is the one who can open the scroll[^85]and its seven seals.”

**6** Then I[^86]saw a Lamb that looked like it had been slain; he was standing among the elders in the midst of the throne and the four living creatures. He had seven horns and seven eyes, which are the[^87]seven spirits of God sent out into all the earth.

**7** He went and took the scroll out of the right hand of him who was sitting on the throne.

**8** When he took the scroll, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders fell down before the Lamb. Each of them had a harp and golden bowls full of incense, which[^88]are the prayers of the saints.

**9** And they sang a new song:
> “Worthy are yoʋ to take the scroll
> and open its seals,
> for yoʋ were slain, and by yoʋr blood yoʋ redeemed[^89]> us for God
> out of every tribe, tongue, people, and nation.

**10** > Yoʋ have made[^90]> them[^91]> kings and priests to our God,
> and[^92]> they will reign on the earth.”


**11** Then I looked, and I heard[^93]something like the voice of many angels surrounding the throne, the living creatures, and the[^94]elders. Their number was myriads of myriads and thousands of thousands,

**12** and they were saying with a loud voice,
> “Worthy is the Lamb who was slain
> to receive power and riches, wisdom and strength,
> honor, glory, and blessing!”


**13** Then I heard every creature in heaven, on earth, under the earth, in the sea, and all that is in them, saying,
> “To him who sits on the throne and to the Lamb,
> be blessing and honor, glory and power,
> forever and ever![^95]> Amen.”


**14** And[^96]I heard the four living creatures saying, “Amen.” And the[^97]elders fell down and[^98]worshiped.
