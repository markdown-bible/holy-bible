# THE SECOND LETTER OF JOHN 1

## Greeting

**1** The elder to the chosen lady and her children, whom I love in truth—and not only I, but also all who know the truth—

**2** because of the truth that abides in us and will be with us forever:

**3** Grace, mercy, and peace will be withus from God the Father and from[^1]the Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, in truth and love.

## Walking in Truth and Love

**4** I was overjoyed to find some of yoʋr children walking in the truth, just as we have been commanded by the Father.

**5** And now I ask yoʋ, dear lady—not as[^2]writing yoʋ a new commandment, but one we have had from the beginning—that we love one another.

**6** Now this is love, that we walk according to his commandments. This is the commandment, just as you have heard it from the beginning, that you should walk in it.

**7** For many deceivers have[^3]come into the world, who do not confess that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh. This is the deceiver and the antichrist.

**8** Watch yourselves, so that[^4]we do not lose what[^5]we have worked for, but may receive a full reward.

**9** Everyone who[^6]transgresses and does not abide in the teaching of Christ does not have God. Whoever abides in the teaching[^7]of Christ has both the[^8]Father and the Son.

**10** If anyone comes to you and does not bring this teaching, do not receive him into your house, and do not greet him;

**11** for whoever greets him takes part in his evil works.

## Final Greetings

**12** Although I have much to write to you, I would rather not use paper and ink. Instead, I hope to come to you and speak face to face, so that[^9]our joy may be full.

**13** The children of yoʋr chosen sister greet yoʋ.[^10]Amen.
