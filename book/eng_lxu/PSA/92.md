# Psalms 92

*(93) For[^180]the day before the Sabbath, when the land was first inhabited, the praise of a Song by David.*

**1** > The Lord reigns; he has clothed himself with honour: the Lord has clothed and girded himself with strength; for he has established the world, which shall not be moved.

**2** > Your throne is prepared of old: you are from everlasting.

**3** > The rivers have lifted up, O Lord, the rivers have lifted up their voices,[^181]

**4** > at[^182]> the voices of many waters: the billows of the sea are wonderful: the Lord is wonderful in high places.

**5** > Your testimonies are made very sure: holiness becomes your house, O Lord,[^183]> for ever.
