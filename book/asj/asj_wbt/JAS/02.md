# Jeeŋ 2

## Kiiŋ nɛ ni nɛ doonchi chi chi kɛ

**1** Bɛŋwaanɛŋ, si nɛ gɛɛ shéŋ li Taa wu tɛɛbeŋ wu Jiso Krai wu nuuŋ Taa wu bunɔŋa lɛ, kiiŋ nɛ ni nɛ doonchi chi chi kɛ.

**2** Ntiitɔɔ lɛ kifɛ bɛniiŋ bɛfɛ nuuŋ be dza be lɛ li yih yinɛ yi kinshewu, wumu lii ndu yi dzeeŋ, tuu lii munchi mu tɛɛmi kwaa, wumu nuuŋ fiee wi wu kifoo, wu lii ndu yi shaanshɛ.

**3** Nɛ se dza nɛ tee li wi wu kifoo wɛɛ le, “Lemɛ fe yɛ,” mɔɔ nuuŋ le, “Bɛ ɔ shee fɛkwiiŋ fɛni li nshɛ-ɛ,” Nɛ se nyɛ bufii nuuŋ li wi wu chuumɛ yi wɛ-ɛ, nɛ tee li wuu le, “Bɛ ɔ shee fɛni wi wu nyɔŋa.”

**4** Ɛ nɛ dza nɛ fɛ lɛ, kɛ nɛ mɛɛŋ baa ki to kɛ bɛniiŋ bɛ sondini bɛnsa bɛ shéŋ yi tii ni? Mɔɔ fini doonchi fiɛɛ le nɛ kɛmi baa chi chi lɛ be-eŋ lɛnti nɛɛ ni?

**5** Bɛŋwaanɛŋ bɛ nuuŋ shéŋ yɛŋ, yikɛ yɛŋ nɛ woo, ɛ nɛɛ bɛniiŋ bɛ kifoo lɛ bɛ laaŋkwiiŋ bɛ Nyɔ tsaa le be gbɛɛŋ be kɛmɛ kimbeenchɛ ki yisɛ ni? Ɛ nɛɛ be bɛ Nyɔ gii yi nyɛ be kintsii lɛ bumfɔŋ bwee li lɛnti bu tɛ̀ kaachɛ wi kwikwi wu kɔŋgisi wu ni?

**6** Se nuuŋ le, nɛ dzɛti finɛ nɛ tuu nɛ nyɛɛ wi wu kifoo buya lɛ yini dze, nɛ ŋiŋgi tɛ nuuŋ wi wu kwa. Bɛniiŋ bɛ michi be shiiti li yi yinɛ-ɛ be chiiti beŋ be giiŋgi bɛ beŋ li yi-ih yi bɛnsa nuuŋ baŋ? Ɛ nɛɛ bɛniiŋ bɛ kwa-a ni?

**7** Ɛ nɛɛ be bɛ yeti jɛ́ yi bifi kii chuŋɛ bukooŋ bu kɛmi ŋguŋu bu bɛchoŋ beŋ lu bwɛɛ ni?

**8** Gbɛɛnɛ yɛɛŋ nɛ ni nɛ giki nchi wu Mfɔŋ wu Nyɔ wu nuuŋ lɛ kiŋwaati kee li. Nchi wuni wɛ le,> “Ɔ kɔŋgisi wi wu nuuŋ li wɛɛ lichiŋ sɔɔ kɔŋgisi yi ya.”Ɛ nɛ feti lɛ, kɛ nɛ feti baa bujɔŋ.

**9** Se nuuŋ le, ɛ nɛ doonchi chi chi, kɛ ɛ bibifi bi nɛ feti, kɛ nɛ bondɛ baa nchi wu kɔɔ beŋ.

**10** Nɛ kɛɛ le ɛ wi kichi bɛnchi bɛ Nyɔ-ɔ bɛchu, ɛ wu bondɛ mumkpaŋ, kɛ wu bondɔɔ bɛchu.

**11** Fi se fiɛɛ lɛ, kifɛ Nyɔ tɛ̀ tee le,> “Kiiŋ kwɛɛ wi kɛ nyumɛ wi giiŋ bɛ kwɛɛ wi kɛ nyumɛ wi kɛ,”ɛ nɛɛ yih yi tɛ̀ tee le,> “Kiiŋ ɔ ni wo wi kɛ.”Ɔɔ mɛɛŋ ki giiŋ bɛ kwɛɛ wi kɛ nyumɛ wi kɛ, ɔɔ tuu ɔ wo wi, kɛ ɔ bondɔɔ nɛɛ nchi wu Nyɔ.

**12** Nɛɛlɛ, nɛ tiiti nɛ feti biee si bɛniiŋ bɛ Nyɔ gii naa yi saa be biki nchi wu busi bɛniiŋ li bumfa-a.

**13** Nɛ ni nɛ kii le Nyɔ gii ke yi sɛki nsa, yi tɛ̀ kɔɔ nshiiŋ li wi wu tɛ̀ mɛɛŋ ki kɔɔ kɛ nshiiŋ li wi wumu kɛ. Se nuuŋ le, wi wu keeti nshiiŋ li wi wumu gii naa bo li nsa-a.

## Fiee fi doonchi le wi gɛɛɔ shéŋli Nyɔ-ɔ nuuŋ kiŋge kee ki shaaŋ

**14** Bɛŋwaanɛŋ, ɛ wi dza tiiti le wu gɛɛɔ shéŋ li Nyɔ-ɔ, kiŋge kee doonchi yɛ lɛ kɛ, fiɛɛ gii fi fi wu la? Shéŋ yi wu gɛɛ nuuŋ yi soo wu ni?

**15** Bɛ dzɔɔ le, ɛ ŋwaana wu nyuŋ kɛ wu kwɛɛŋ dza fuki ndú mɔɔ biee bijɛ jo chichi,

**16** ɛ wu bɛ lɛ wɛ-ɛ, ɔ tee li wuu le, “Nyɔ tiiŋ mwɛ li wɛ-ɛ, ɔ kɛmɛ ndú bɛ biee bijɛ.” Ɔɔ tee lɛ kwaa, wɛ nyɛ wu biee bi wɛki nsiŋ, fiɛɛ se fi wu la?

**17** Nɛ mɛɛŋ baa ki ŋɛŋ kɛ le ɛ wi tiiti le wu gɛɛɔ shéŋ li Nyɔ-ɔ, kiŋge kee ndoonchɛ lɛ nsiŋ, kɛ shéŋ yi wu gɛɛ kwaa yɛɛ li yee li ni?

**18** Wi wumu nuuŋ dza tee le, fi wi wumu nuuŋ le wu gɛɛɔ shéŋ li Nyɔ-ɔ, fi wi wumu nuuŋ le ni feti nuuŋ kiŋge ki shaaŋ. Nse ntiitɔɔ le doonchɛ si wi nuuŋ gɛɛ shéŋ li Nyɔ-ɔ kiŋge kee nuuŋ yɛ ki shaaŋ kɛ, ŋgii ndoonchɛ wu si mi ŋgɛɛɔ shéŋ li Nyɔ-ɔ nuuŋ li kiŋge keŋ ki shaaŋ li.

**19** Ɛ wi beŋ le Nyɔ nuuŋ mumkpaŋ, kɛ fi fiɛɛ bujɔŋ. Mɔɔ, bɛnchindaa bɛ Kiŋkundi-i beenchi baa nɛɛlɛ be se chɛndi, be kɛɛti.

**20** Wɛɛ wi ɛ kibere. Wɛki le ndoonchɛ le ɛ wi gɛɛ shéŋ li Nyɔ-ɔ mfɛru nsiŋ, kɛ fi fiɛɛ yee nɛɛ ni?

**21** Mɔɔ fiee fi tɛ̀ fɛ Nyɔ se ŋɛŋ le Abrahaŋ wu baa wusɛŋ kɔchɔɔ wi we tɛ̀ nuuŋ kiŋge kee ki shaaŋ ni? Tɛ̀ dzɔ ŋwani wu Adzi, giiki li taantaa chi muntofi le fɛ muntofi lu li Nyɔ-ɔ.

**22** Nɛ mɛɛŋ ki ŋɛŋ kɛ le shéŋ yi tɛ̀ gɛɛ li Nyɔ-ɔ bɛ kiŋge kee ki shaaŋ tɛ̀ deŋgi kikaa kimumkpaŋ ni? Kiŋge kee ki shaaŋ tɛ̀ doonchɛ le shéŋ yi tɛ̀ gɛɛ li Nyɔ-ɔ tɛ̀ nuuŋ ɛ yi yisɛ.

**23** Fi se kɔchɛ si bɛ tɛ̀ tsɛɛ lɛ kiŋwaati ki Nyɔ-ɔ ki tiiti le,> “Abrahaŋ tɛ̀ gɛɛ shéŋ li Nyɔ-ɔ,
> kii lɛ, Nyɔ se ŋɛŋ le wɛ nuuŋ wi wu tsaaŋ.”Bɛ se tuu bɛ teenyi wu le sinɛŋ Nyɔ.

**24** Nɛ tuu nɛ ŋɛŋ le fiee fi ti feti Nyɔ se ŋɛŋ le wi kɔchɔɔ wi we nuuŋ kiŋge kee ki shaaŋ, nuuŋ yɛ shéŋ yi wi gɛɛ li Nyɔ-ɔ kwaa kɛ.

**25** Fi fiɛɛ yɛɛŋ si Lahab wu tɛ̀ nuuŋ kinjikwiiŋ tɛ̀ nuuŋ. Fiee fi tɛ fɛ Nyɔ se ŋɛŋ le wu kɔchɔɔ wi we tɛ nuuŋ kiŋge kee ki shaaŋ. Tɛ̀ fi bɛntoŋkii bɛ Isɛlɛɛ li wuu yi, se doonchɛ be dze yimi yi jetɛ le be wɛɛŋ be to lijiŋ lɛ yi-i.

**26** Fi se fiɛɛ le, nɛɛ si fiana ti chiiŋgi li nyaŋ yi bwi-iŋ yi to fiee fi kwi lɛ, ɛ lɛ si fi fiɛɛ nɛɛ bɛ wi wu giki shéŋ li Nyɔ-ɔ mfɛru nimɛ chi shaaŋ nsiŋ kɛ wɛ fiee fi kwi.
