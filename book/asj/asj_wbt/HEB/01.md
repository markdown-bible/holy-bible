# Hiburu 1

## Nyɔ yɔ yɛ li bee li nuuŋ lɛ kimfimɛ ki Ŋwani-i

**1** Tɛ mɛɛŋ lindɛɛri, Nyɔ yeti li bɛte taa bɛsɛŋ li bimfimu bi bɛntomfɔŋ bee li, li kiŋga ŋge bɛ lɛ dzé chichi chichi.

**2** Se nuuŋ le, li jo yi lɛ kimɛrisɛ yini-i, yi yɔ yɛ li bee li nuuŋ lɛ kimfimɛ ki Ŋwani-i. Ɛ Ŋwani wuni wu yi tsaa yɛ le gii nuuŋ kinjila li biee bichu-u. Ɛ nɛɛ wu wu Nyɔ tɛ̀ tɔŋ nshɛ bɛ biee bichu kɛti li tsáŋ yee li.

**3** Kiŋ'wofu ki Nyɔ-ɔ bɛndi kɛɛ li yi yee li, se gbɛŋgi nuuŋ mbosɛ yi Nyɔ kibɛɛ. Ɛ wu wu feti kiboo bɛ nshɛ se nuuŋ lu. Se feti biee bini nuuŋ nɛɛ bɛ jɛ ye yi kɛmi buŋga. Tɛ̀ maaŋ kimaaŋ ki bibifi bisɛ-ɛŋ. Le maaŋ lɛ, se yɛɛ liboo shee lɛ kibɛnɛ ki kigɔŋɛ ki Nyɔ yi Bunɔŋa-a.

## Ŋwanɛ Nyɔ ko wu yaa bɛnchindaa bɛ Nyɔ-ɔ

**4** Fi se tuu fi nuuŋ le, Nyɔ tɛ̀ fɛ Ŋwani ko ŋge yaa bɛnchindaa bɛ Nyɔ-ɔ, nɛɛ si bukooŋ bu Nyɔ tɛ̀ nyɛ wu ko tɛ ŋge bu yaa bu bee.

**5** Tɛ kii le wu koɔ ŋge kifɛ nchindaa wu Nyɔ wumu nuuŋ yɛ lu wu Nyɔ tɛ̀ saa yi teeŋ wu le,> “Ŋwani kɛ,
> Kɛ le bɛŋ yani mi nteeɔ le ɛ mi baa wa, kɛ.”Wumu nuuŋ yɛ lu wu tɛ̀ saa wu tee kii wuu le,> “Ŋgii ni nuuŋ tee.
> Ni nuuŋ Ŋwanɛŋ ni.”

**6** Tɛ̀ gii nuuŋ mfi wu Nyɔ tɛ̀ nuuŋ le yi toŋ Ŋwani wu ŋkosi wuni li nshɛ yini, yi tɛ̀ tee le,> “Bɛnchindaa bɛ Nyɔ-ɔ bɛchu kɛmi le be ni be guundi wu.”

**7** Fiee fi Nyɔ tɛ̀ tee kii bɛnchindaa bee nuuŋ fini le,> “Yi ti feti be be nuuŋ si mfiee,
> yi feti tɛ be be nuuŋ bɛniiŋ bee bɛ nimɛ-ɛ
> be nuuŋ si nimi chi wi-i.”

**8** Le tuu to li Ŋwani-i, tee le,> “Hooo Nyɔ, ɔ gii naa ɔ shee li kabara wa-a ɔ ni ɔ sɛki mfi kwi ma nsiŋ.
> Ntsɛ wu tsaaŋ gii naa ni nuuŋ kimbaaŋ ki bumfɔŋ bwo-o.

**9** > Ɔ kɔŋgisɔɔ nuuŋ fiee fi tsaaŋ, ɔ baanini fiee fi bifi.
> Nɛɛ lɛ, mi wu Nyɔ yi nuuŋ Nyɔ ya
> mi n'yɛɛkɔɔ wɛ, ɔ yaa kintutu ko,
> mi se mfwaɔ wɛ bɛ mbee yi bumfɔŋ bu kinɛɛtinɛ-ɛ.”

**10** Yi se tuu yi ka yi tee li Ŋwani-i le,> “Taa, ɛ wɛ wu tɛ̀ tɔŋ nshɛ fɛmbɛɛchɛ-ɛ,
> se biee bichu bi nuuŋ liboo nuuŋ mfɛru wu tsaŋ ya.

**11** > Biee bini gii bi ma, ɛ wɛ kwaa ɔ gii ɔ ba lu.
> Bi gii bi doŋ si ndu.

**12** > Ɔ gii ɔ baaŋ bi si kiŋkoŋ.
> Ɔ gii ɔ fiiki bi si ndu yi doŋ.
> Se nuuŋ le, ɔ gii ɔ ba ɔ ni ɔ nuuŋ nɛɛ si ɔ nuuŋ lɛ.
> Biya bio nuuŋ tɛ naa ma kɛ.”

**13** Ɛ nchindaa wu Nyɔ wu la wu Nyɔ tɛ̀ saa yi tee li wuu le,> “Shee lɛ kibɛnɛ keŋ ki kigɔŋɛ-ɛ,
> nsiiŋ gɛɛŋ bo mfi wu mi ŋgiiki
> bɛniiŋ bo bɛ mbani-i wɛ limfwe
> si kilaanda ki bikaa bio-o,
> Ɔ tɔmi bikaa bio li bee ni?”

**14** Mɔɔ bɛchindaa bɛ Nyɔ-ɔ bani nuuŋ nɛɛ si fiana yi nindi li Nyɔ-ɔ, bɛ yi tundi be be deŋgi be fii bɛniiŋ bɛ gii be kɛmɛ kinsofu nɛɛ ni?
