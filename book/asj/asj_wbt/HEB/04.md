# Hiburu 4

## Kii nlɛ li mfufinɛ wu Nyɔ-ɔ

**1** Fi se fiɛɛ le, ŋkaachɛ wu Nyɔ tɛ̀ gɛɛ le bɛniiŋ gii be naa be lɛ li mfufinɛ we mɛɛŋɔ lemi fe. Bɛdɛɛni tɛ ni tɛ chɛndi le kiiŋ bɛ naa bɛ bichɛ bɛ ŋɛŋ wi wumu li be-eŋ mɛɛŋ ki kɔchɛ kɛ le lɛ kɛ.

**2** Ntiitɔɔ ni kifɛ ntoŋ wu dzeeŋ wuni bɛɛɔ kituŋ li bee li nɛɛ yɛɛŋ si tɛ̀ bɛ li bee. Se nuuŋ le, ntoŋ wulu wu be tɛ̀ woo tɛ̀ mɛɛŋ ki fi kɛ be kɛ, kifɛ wu tɛ̀ bɛ lɛ bee, be ba mbeenchɛ nsiŋ.

**3** Li bee tɛ be-eŋ nuuŋ tɛ lɛ mfufinɛ wulu. Bɛniiŋ bamu baa bɛ Nyɔ tɛ̀ tee le,> “Shéŋ tɛ̀ tɔnyɛ mi, nchini le,
> ‘Nuuŋ be tɛ naa le, be lɛ kintsii keŋ ki mfufinɛ-ɛ kɛ.’”Nyɔ tɛ̀ yeti lɛ, fi nuuŋ yɛ le kintsii ki Mfufinɛ-ɛ fuki kɛɛ kɛ, kifɛ nimɛ chee tɛ̀ ma kituŋ fɛmbɛɛchɛ-ɛ mfi wu tɛ̀ tɔŋ nshɛ.

**4** Tɛ kii baa le bɛ tɛ tsɛɛ lɛ Kiŋwaati ki Nyɔ-ɔ kintsii kimu kii jobɛ chi mfomɛnyaaŋ chini, lɛ dze yini le,> “Nyɔ tɛ̀ niŋ nimɛ chee chichu,
> le bo jo mfomɛnyaaŋ yi fufɛ.”

**5** Fɛ kintsii kɛɛ yi tuu yi tee le, “Be nuuŋ tɛ naa lɛ lɛ mfufinɛ wɛ-ɛŋ kɛ dududu.”

**6** Fi mɛɛŋ fiɛɛ fi lemi le bɛniiŋ bamu gii be lɛ li mfufinɛ wuni. Bɛniiŋ bɛ tɛ̀ saa be woo ntoŋ wu dzeeŋ wuni baa se tɛ̀ mɛɛŋ ki lɛ kɛ kifɛ be tɛ̀ woo nɛɛ, se nuuŋ le, be tɛ̀ faaŋ mfɛru si Nyɔ tiiti.

**7** Nɛɛlɛ, Nyɔ se tuu yi kaari yi gɛɛ jobɛ chimu chi yi teenyi le, “Bɛŋ.” Si yi tɛ̀ gɛɛ lɛ yi tuu yi yɔ fiee fimu lɛ kimfimɛ ki Mfɔŋ Daafi ɛ biya tɛ̀ ka ŋge tɛ tɛ̀ wuki kituŋ le,> “Ɛ nɛ woo Nyɔ yeti li be-eŋ bɛŋ,
> kiiŋ nɛ ni nɛ tɛɛmi bikoo kɛ.”

**8** Ɛ nuuŋ le kintsii ki Joshwa tɛ̀ dzɔ bɛniiŋ gɛɛŋ lɛ bɛ be fe kɛɛ tɛ̀ nuuŋ kintsii ki mfufinɛ kilu-u, kɛ bee Nyɔ yi tiiti yɛ kii jobɛ chimu le ɛ chi mfufinɛ kɛ.

**9** Fi se fiɛɛ le jobɛ chi mfufinɛ mɛɛŋ chɛɛ limfwe li bɛniiŋ bɛ Nyɔ-ɔ si chi Nyɔ tɛ̀ fufɛ chɛɛ.

**10** Fi nuuŋ le, ɛ wi lɛ mfufinɛ wuni, kɛ mwɛ fufɔɔ kituŋ li nimɛ chee chichu-u nɛɛ yɛɛŋ si Nyɔ tɛ̀ fufɛ li chee chi nimɛ-ɛ.

**11** Bɛdɛɛni tɛ ni tɛ gwenini ŋge ke tɛ lɛ mfufinɛ wuni, le kiiŋ wi wumu ni dza we, le ɛ kii kikoo ki tɛɛmɛ si bɛniiŋ bɛ ŋkosi baa kɛ.

**12** Nɛ ni nɛ kii le jɛ yi Nyɔ yɛ we, yi nindi. Yi keeti yi yɛki nyɔ yi bɛ fɔŋ bimbe bichu. Yi ti lii yi gɛɛŋ yi bo fɛ ntsɛ bɛ fiana yi wi taashi, yi lii tɛ yi gɛɛŋ yi bo fɛ biyooŋ bɛ mfɔŋ yi wi taashi. Yi ti fushi bɛmbeechɛ mɔɔ biee bi nuuŋ li shéŋ yi wi-i.

**13** Fiee fimu nuuŋ yɛ lu dududu fi Nyɔ tɔŋ fi nuuŋ fi ni fi nyiki yi li yi-i kɛ. Biee bichu yeŋgi lɛ yi-i lii gbanaa. Ɛ yi yi tɛ̀ kɛmi le tɛ bɛ tɛ ku bɛ bimfimu bisɛŋ li yi-i limfwe.

## Jiso nuuŋ kikoo ki bɛte muntofi ki nɔŋa

**14** Bɛdɛɛni si tɛ̀ bee tɛ̀ tee le tɛ kɛmi baa kikoo ki bɛte muntofi ki nɔŋa, ki nuuŋ Jiso wu Ŋwanɛ Nyɔ, tɛ̀ miri tɔ kiboo yɛɛ liboo, tɛ̀ kɛmɛ kimbeenchɛ mkpaaŋ ki tɛ̀ ti tiiti li bɛniiŋ li.

**15** Kikoo ki bɛte muntofi kisɛŋ kini nuuŋ wi wu nuuŋ tɛ ba ŋkɔwu nshiiŋ bɛ bee nsiŋ kii mbiaŋu bɔ wusɛŋ kɛ. Nuuŋ wi wu mɔnchɛ tɛ̀ bɛ tɛ lɛ wuu lɛ dzé chi-i si bɛɛ lɛ bee li, se nuuŋ le ti mɛɛŋ ki fɛ kɛ bubiɛɛ kɛ.

**16** Bɛdɛɛni, tɛ ni tɛ kiŋgisi tɛ bɛɛ mɛŋinɛ nsiŋ li kabara wu Nyɔ fɛ kintsii ki yi doonchi shéŋ yee yi dzeeŋ, le ke se ni keeti nshiiŋ li bee li tuu doonchɛ shéŋ yee yi dzeeŋ fi bee mfi wu fiee yɛki bee.
