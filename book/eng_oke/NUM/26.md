# Numbers 26

**1** that the Lord spake to Mosheh and to Elazar bar Aharon the priest, saying:

**2** Take the account of all the congregation of the sons of Israel, from a son of twenty years and upwards by the house of their fathers, everyone that goeth out with the host in Israel.

**3** And Mosheh and Elazar the priest spake with them, and directed to number them in the plains of Moab, by the Jordan (over against) Jericho, saying:

**4** From a son of twenty years and upward, as the Lord commanded Mosheh, and the sons of Israel who went forth from the land of Mizraim.

**5** Reuben, the first-born of Israel: the sons of Reuben, Hanok, the family of Hanok; Phallu, and the family of Phallu;

**6** of Hezron, the family of Hezron; of Karmi, the family of Karmi.

**7** These are the families of Reuben, and they who were numbered of them were forty-three thousand seven hundred and thirty.

**8** And of the sons of Phallu, Eliab;

**9** And the sons of Eliab, Nemuel, and Dathan, and Abiram: these are Dathan and Abiram who called the congregation that gathered against Mosheh and Aharon in the congregation of Korach, in their gathering against the Lord,

**10** when the earth opened her mouth and swallowed them with Korach, and the congregation died, being devoured by the fire, two hundred and fifty men; and they became a sign;

**11** but the sons of Korach died not.

**12** The sons of Shimeon by their families: of Nemuel, the family of Nemuel; of Jamin, the family of Jamin;

**13** of Zerach, the family of Zerach; of Shaul, the family of Shaul.

**14** These are the families of Shemeon, twenty-two thousand two hundred.

**15** The sons of Gad after their families; of Zephon, the family of Zephon; of Haggi, the family of Haggi; of Shumi, the family of Shumi; of Aggi, the family of Aggi;

**16** Of Ozni, the family of Ozni; of Heri, the family of Heri;

**17** of Arod, the family of the Arodi of Areli, the family of Areli.

**18** These are the families of the Beni Gad, the number of whom was forty thousand five hundred.

**19** Of the sons of Jehudah, Her and Onan: but Her and Onan died in the land of Kenaan;

**20** Of the sons of Jehudah after their families: of Shelah, the family of Shelah; of Pherez, the family of Pherez; of Zerach, the family of Zerach.

**21** Of the sons of Pherez: of Hezron, the family of Hezron; of Hamul, the family of Hamul.

**22** These are the families of Jehudah; the number of them seventy-six thousand five hundred.

**23** The sons of Issakar by their families: Tola, the family of Tola; of Phua, the family of the Phuni;

**24** Of Jasub, the family of Jasub; of Shimron, the family of Shimron.

**25** These are the families of Issakar; their number sixty-four thousand three hundred.

**26** The sons of Zebulon by their families: of Sared, the family of Sared; of Elon, the family of Elon; of Jahleel, the family of Jahleel.

**27** These are the families of Zebulon, by their number sixty thousand five hundred.

**28** The sons of Joseph by their families, Menasheh and Ephraim,

**29** The sons of Menasheh: of Makir, the family of Makir; and Makir begat Gilead; of Gilead, the family of Gilead.

**30** These are the children of Gilead: of Jezar, the family of Jezar; of Helek, the family of Helek;

**31** And of Asriel, the family of Asrieli; and of Shekem, the family of Shekem;

**32** And of Shemida, the family of Shemida; and of Hepher, the family of Hepher.

**33** And Zelophechad bar Hepher had no sons, but daughters; and the names of the daughters of Zelophechad were Mahali, and Nohah, Hegelah, Milchah, and Thirzah.

**34** These are the families of Menasheh, and their number fifty-two thousand seven hundred.

**35** These are the sons of Ephraim by their families: of Shuthelah, the family of Shuthelah; of Bekir, the family of Bekir; of Tachan, the family of Tachan.

**36** And these are the sons of Shuthelah: of Heran, the family of Heran.

**37** These are the families of the Beni-Ephraim, the number of whom was thirty-two thousand five hundred. These are the sons of Joseph by their families.

**38** The sons of Benjamin by their families: of Bela, the family of Bela; of Ashbal, the family of Ashbal; of Ahiram, the family of Ahiram;

**39** Of Shefuphim, the family of Shefuphim; of Hupham, the family of Hupham.

**40** And the sons of Bela, Arede and Naaman; the family of the Aredi; and of Naaman, the family of Naaman.

**41** These are the sons of Benjamin after their families, and their number forty-five thousand six hundred.

**42** These are the children of Dan, after their families: of Shuham, the families of Shuham. These are the families of Dan after their families,

**43** And the number of the families of Shuham sixty-four thousand four hundred.

**44** The sons of Asher, after their families: of Jimna, the families of Jimna; of Jeshvi, the families of Jeshvi; of Beriah, the family of Beriah.

**45** Of the sons of Beriah: of Heber, the family of Heber; of Malkiel, the family of Malkiel.

**46** But the name of the daughter of Asher was Sarach.

**47** These are the families of the Beni Asher, by their number fifty-three thousand four hundred.

**48** The sons of Naphtali, after their families: of Jahziel, the families of Jahziel; of Gunni, the families of Gunni;

**49** of Jezer, the family of Jezer; of Shelem, the family of Shelem.

**50** These are the families of Naphtali by their families, and their number forty-five thousand four hundred.

**51** These were the numbered of the sons of Israel, six hundred and one thousand seven hundred and thirty.

**52** And the Lord spake with Mosheh, saying:

**53** Unto these thou shalt divide the land for a possession, according to the number of their names.

**54** To the many thou shalt make large their possession, and to the few thou shalt make their possession small; to each according to his number shalt thou give his inheritance.

**55** Nevertheless the land shall be divided by lot, according to the names of the tribes of their fathers they shall inherit.

**56** By the lot shall their inheritance be divided among the many and the few.

**57** And these are the sons of Levi, after their families: of Gershon, the families of Gershon; of Kehath, the family of Kehath; of Merari, the families of Merari.

**58** These are the families of Levi: the family of Libni, the family of Hebron, the family of Machli, the family off Mushi, the family of Korach And Kehath begat Amram.

**59** And the name of Amram’s wife was Jokebed, a daughter of Levi, who was born to Levi, in Mizraim; and she bare unto Amram Aharon, and Mosheh, and Miriam their sister.

**60** And unto Aharon were born Nadab and Abihu, Elazar and Ithamar.

**61** But Nadab and Abihu died in their offering strange fire before the Lord.

**62** And the numbered of them were twenty-three thousand of all the males from a month old and upwards: for they were not numbered among the children of Israel, because no possession was given to them among the children of Israel.

**63** These (are they whom) Mosheh and Elazar the priest numbered of the sons of Israel, in the plains of Moab, by Jordan, (over against) Jericho.

**64** But among these there was not a man of them whom Mosheh and Aharon the priest numbered when they counted the children of Israel in the wilderness of Sinai.

**65** For the Lord had said to them that dying they should die in the wilderness; and not a man of them remained, except Kaleb bar Jephuneh and Jehoshua bar Nun.
