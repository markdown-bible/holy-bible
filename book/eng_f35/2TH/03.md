# 2 Thessalonians 3

## A request for prayer

**1** Finally, brothers, pray for us, that the word of the Lord may spread rapidly and be glorified, as also with you,

**2** and that we may be delivered from evil and malignant men; for not all have faith.[^48]

**3** But the Lord is faithful, who will strengthen you and guard you from the malignant one.[^49]

**4** We have confidence in the Lord concerning you, that you both do and will do the things we command.[^50]

**5** Now may the Lord direct your hearts into the love of God and into the perseverance of Christ.[^51]

## Don't be irresponsible or lazy

**6** Now we command you, brothers, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that you withdraw from every brother who behaves irresponsibly and not according to the tradition that they[^52]received from us.

**7** For you yourselves know how you ought to imitate us, because we did not behave irresponsibly among you;

**8** neither[^53]did we eat anyone's bread without paying; rather we worked night and day, with difficulty and hardship, so as not to be a burden to any of you;

**9** not because we do not have the right, but in order to offer ourselves to you as a model for you to imitate.

**10** Yes, because when we were with you we gave you this command: “If anyone does not want to work,[^54]neither let him eat!”

**11** Yet we hear that some among you are behaving irresponsibly; not working, just meddling.

**12** Now to such individuals we command and exhort by our Lord Jesus Christ that they work in quietness and eat their own bread.

## Isolate the disobedient

**13** But as for you, brothers, do not lose heart in well doing.

**14** If anyone does not obey our word in this letter, take note of him and do not associate with him, that he may be shamed;

**15** yet do not regard him as an enemy, but admonish him as a brother.[^55]

## Benediction

**16** Now may the Lord of peace Himself give you the peace[^56]during every situation, in every way.[^57]The Lord be with you all.

## Sign-off

**17** The greeting of Paul, with my own hand, which is the ‘trademark’ in every letter that I write.[^58]

**18** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen.
