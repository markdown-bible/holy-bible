**12** Nidjiki8eg! Nagadjiidiok, ega iaga pejig a8iag kina8akag kidji odji apitci madjideedj acitc kidji apitci ega 8i teb8etag, pinici kidji k8ekigaba8ita8adjin Kije Manidon ka pimadizindjin.
