# KALECIA 4

**15** Kigi kitci modjigendana8aban ii apitc! Kicpin ki iji minosegiban, kidagi manadjabibindizom kidji gi minijieg kickijigo8an. Kiketcinameniminom ii! Adi dac ka ijiseieg?
