## Kitci mane a8iagog 8abamagani8i8ag 8ak8ig

**9** Ick8a dac ii, nigi 8abamag kitci mane a8iagog, ozam ki mane8ag kidji gi agimagani8i8adj. Kakina pepakan ooma akig 8aodjibanig. **10** Kakina ikidobanig e kitci kiji8e8adj:> “Kikije Manidominan> > ka abidj kitci ogima8i tesabi8aganikag> acitc aa Mantcenices,
> mi igi8e kagi ag8acimigo8ak.”

**12** e ikido8adj:> “Amen! Teb8e iidi!
