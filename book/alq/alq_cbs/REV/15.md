Tcigagam kitcigamig mi maia 8aseantcigan ka ijinag8anig iji tajigaba8ibanig igi. Ajeni nodag8atcigani otakonana8aban, Kije Manidon kagi minigo8agobanen.

**3** Mi maia pejig8an enaamazo8apan Mois, 8in kagi anokita8adjin Kije Manidon, kagi nigamogobanen, e manadjia8adjin Mantcenicesan. Oo8eni dac nigamobanig:> “Kije Manido ka Tibendjigean, ma8adji ka kitci macka8izian!

**4** > Tebendjigean! Kakina a8iag kikitcit8a8inig.
