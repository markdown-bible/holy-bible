# Psalms 47

*To the chief Musician. Of the sons of Korah. A Psalm.*

**1** > All ye peoples, clap your hands; shout unto[^105]> God with the voice of triumph!

**2** > For Jehovah, the Most High, is terrible, a great king over all the earth.

**3** > He subdueth the peoples under us, and the nations under our feet.

**4** > He hath chosen our inheritance for us, the excellency of Jacob whom he loved. Selah.

**5** [^106]> God is gone up amid shouting, Jehovah amid the sound of the trumpet.

**6** > Sing psalms of[^107]> God, sing psalms; sing psalms unto our King, sing psalms!

**7** > For[^108]> God is the King of all the earth; sing psalms with understanding.

**8** [^109]> God reigneth over the nations;[^110]> God sitteth upon the throne of his holiness.

**9** > The willing-hearted of the peoples have gathered together, [with] the people of the[^111]> God of Abraham. For unto[^112]> God [belong] the shields of the earth: he is greatly exalted.
