# Psalms 43

**1** > Judge me, O[^80]> God, and plead my cause against an ungodly nation; deliver me from the deceitful and unrighteous man.

**2** > For thou art the[^81]> God of my strength: why hast thou cast me off? why go I about mourning because of the oppression of the enemy?

**3** > Send out thy light and thy truth: they shall lead me, they shall bring me to thy holy mount, and unto thy habitations.

**4** > Then will I go unto the altar of[^82]> God, unto the[^83]> God of the gladness of my joy: yea, upon the harp will I praise thee, O[^84]> God, my[^85]> God.

**5** > Why art thou cast down, my soul? and why art thou disquieted within me? hope in[^86]> God; for I shall yet praise him, [who is] the health of my countenance, and my[^87]> God.
