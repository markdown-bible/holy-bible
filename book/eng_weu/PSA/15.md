# Psalms 15

*A Psalm by David.*

**1** > LORD, who shall dwell in your sanctuary?> > Who shall live on your holy hill?

**2** > He who walks blamelessly and does what is right,> > and speaks truth in his heart;

**3** > he who doesn’t slander with his tongue,> > nor does evil to his friend,
> > nor casts slurs against his fellow man;

**4** > in whose eyes a vile man is despised,> > but who honours those who fear the LORD;
> > he who keeps an oath even when it hurts, and doesn’t change;

**5** > he who doesn’t lend out his money for usury,> > nor take a bribe against the innocent.
> He who does these things shall never be shaken.
