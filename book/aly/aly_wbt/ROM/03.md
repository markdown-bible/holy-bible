# Romans 3

**1** Akngey-kenh-angkwarr apenteyelel, mwerr apek aynteyel artwa mpwaretyek Jew-kenh-angkwarr? Akngey-kenh-angkwarr Jew-rnem mwerr-ayteng aneyel?

**2** Ikwer-rnem-tangkwel Akngeyel angka ikwerenh arrernek, rernem apenterl-ayntetyek.

**3** Jew ingwer-rnemel arraty-angkwarr apentey-angenh. Akngey-kenh-angkwarr anwantherr menty-anem ipmelhey-angenh, rernem ipmelhek-apeny. Akngeyel angka arrwekel arrernek, nthakenh iparey-angenh.

**4** Arrangkw! Ingwerenty irrtywerrenty, Akngey-kenh angkekety intwemelirrey-angenh. Akngey-kenh angka arrwekeleny alakenh aynteyel: “Akngey-kenh angka arraty innga, nthakenh ingwerentyel court-warl arrerney-angenh.”

**5** Jew-rnemel apmwangkwarr apententy, arraty Akngeyel renh-rnem atweyenh. Ingwerel-rnem renh-rnem areyenh, rernemarl Akngey-kenh-angkwarr apmwelhilek. Rernem iterl-arem-anem, Akngey arraty inngarl. Arraty ahern-areny-rnem atweyenh? Mwerr Akngeyel apek renh-rnem tyarlewetyek apmwangkwarr? Arrangkw innga. Arraty-angkwarr ra Jew-rnem anwenhantherr akngakeyenh, anwantherr ingkerrenh apmwelhilek-kety.

**7** Jew ingwer-rnem angkek, ayeng Akngey-kenh-angkwarr apmwelhilenty, rernem Akngey renh iterl-arem-anem arraty inngarl. Akngeyel apek ayenh menty atwey-angenh. Mwerr alakenh?

**8** Ingwer-rnemel ayenh ayntarrp-ilek, ayeng alakenh rwewilerrenty-arrpantey. Ayeng nthakenh Akngey-angkwarr arratyirrey-angenh-arrpantey. Akngeyel renh-rnem atweyenh.

## Ahern-areny-rnemel arraty-angkwarr apentey-angenh

**9** Jew-rnem anwantherr apek mwerr-ayteng ingwer-rnem-angkwarr, anwantherr angka Akngey-kenh-akerting? Arrangkw. Anwantherr apmwakert ingkerrenh aneyel, Jew-rnem-then Jew-weny-rnem-then.

**10** Akngey-kenh angka arrwekeleny alakenh aynteyel: “Nthakenh anyent apek Akngey-angkwarr amentel arratyirrey-angenh. Rernem arratyirretyek-arrpantey arrkernelhenh ywarn.

**11** Rernem Akngey renh iterl-arey-angenh. Arraty-angkwarr apentetyek arrkernelhey-angenh.

**12** Rernem intwemelirrek Akngey-kety, ingkerrenh-antey. Rernem apmwangkwarr apenteyel, ingkerrenhel.

**13** Rernem arrakertel apmwel-areth ileyel. Rernem irrtywerremel ileyel.

**14** Rernem apmwilem, ilterl-anemel.

**15** Rernem ingwer-rnem atwetyek arrkernelheyel.

**16** Rernem irrwernelheyel, rwewilirreyel.

**17** Rernem tnhwerant-arey irrentirreyel ingwer-rnem-itwek.

**18** Rernem Akngeyek iterrey-angenh arrangkw, amentewarl.” Akngey-kenh angka arrwekeleny aynteyel.

**19** Alakenh Akngeyel angka arrwekeleny arrernek, Jew-rnemek, rernem iterretyek ikwer. Arraty-arrpantey tnakelhemarl-kety, anwantherr ahern-areny-rnem iterl-aretyek, rernemarl apmwangkwarr ingkerrenh alhenh.

**20** Anyent apek nthakenh Akngey-angkwarr arratyirrey-angenh, angka arrwekeleny-angkwarrant apententy. Akngey-kenh angka arrwekeleny-angkwarr, rernem iterl-arelhem rernem apmwakert-antey aneyel.

## Akngeyel ileyel ingkerrenh ikwerenh-angkwarr arratyirretyek, rernem Jesus renh tnakenty

**21** Aley alakenh anwantherr arratyirretyek Akngey-kenh-angkwarr. Alakenh angka arrwekeleny aynteyel. Alakenh Akngeyel arrernek ikwerenh-angkwarrant, Moses-kenh-weny-angkwarrant.

**22** Rernem Jesus Christ tnakenty, rernem Akngey-angkwarr arraty anem, Jew-rnem-then Jew-weny-rnem-then ingkerrenh-antey.

**23** Ingkerrenh nthakenh Akngey-kenh-angkwarr arraty aney-angenh ament, Akngey irrwerl-areny iterl-arey-angenh, rarl rlterrk innga aneyel. Akngeyel iwerr anwekantherr mpwarek Jesus Christ-angkwarr. Ra ingkerrenhek katy ilwek, ra renh-rnem anyentelhilek Akngey-warl, rernem Akngey-kenh-angkwarr arraty anayntetyek, alyelk.

**25** Awank-akerr arrwekelenyel-rnem atnwenth-tangkwel atwenh, aker tyenkerr arrernetyek, rernem Akngey-angkwarr arratyirretyek. Alakenh Akngeyel Christ renh anthek, rernem ipmek atwetyek, ingkerrenhek katy ilwetyek, tyenkerr-apeny, ingkerrenhel. Akngey ra ingkerrenhek lwewarrernelhek, ra arraty innga aneyel. Rernem renh tnakenty, Akngeyel renh-rnem ayntarrp-iley-angenh, alyelk-apeny rernem anayntetyek. Apmwangkwarr alhenh-rnem ra ahern-areny menty tyarlewek, ywerrelhilemel atwey-angenh. Christ ra ingkerrenhek katy ilwek, rernem arraty ingkerrenh anayntetyek. Ranem ikwerenh-angkwarr arratyilek, rernem mwerr-angkwarr apenterl-ayntetyek. Rernem arraty-angkwarr anem, rernem Jesus renh apententy.

**27** Alakenh-they ingkerrenhel nthakenh arraty-arrpantey tnakelhey-angenh, Jesus-ant tnakem. Angka arrwekeleny-angkwarr apententy nthakenh arratyirrey-angenh Akngey-kenh-angkwarr. Anwantherr akalty, anwantherr Jesus renh tnakenty anwantherr Akngey-kenh-angkwarr arraty aneyel. Angka arrwekeleny arrangkw.

**29** Akngey ranem ingkerrenhek aneyel, Jew-rnemek-then, artwa Jew-weny-rnemek-then.

**30** Rernem Jesus renh tnakenty, Akngeyel renh-rnem arraty antwem, Jew-rnem-then, Jew-weny-rnem-then.

**31** Anwantherr Jesus renh tnakeyel, anwantherrap angka arrwekeleny-kety ipmelhey-angenh. Arrangkw, aleth-antey iterl-areyel Akngey-kenh-angkwarr apenterl-ayntetyek.
