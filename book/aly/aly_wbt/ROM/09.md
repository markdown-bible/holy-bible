# Romans 9

## Akngeyel Israel-areny-rnem-tangkwel rlwanek, ikwerenh-rnem anetyek

**1** Ayeng Jesus Christ-angkwarr arraty angkeyel, irrtywerremel angkey-angenh, arraty ayeng angkeyel. Itnweng Rlwart ra akalty ayengarl angkek-penh.

**2** Ayeng aleth amperrngirreyel.

**3** Ayeng artewentyel iterreyel atyenh-rnem Christ-warl anyentirretyek,

**4** Akngeyel Israel-areny-rnem rlwanek, rernem alerikw ikwerenh-rnem anetyek, Ra renh-rnem ilkwilek. Ran ikwer-rnem lwewarrernelhek ra atyetyart rlterrk ilkwakert aneyel. Akngeyel ikwer-rnem angka arrwekeleny arrernek. Ra renh-rnem ilek ikwer-rnem-itwel anayntetyek. Akngeyel renh-rnem akalty-anthek, rernem arraty-angkwarr apenterl-ayntetyek tyaty ilkwitwel. Ra iwerr mpwarek ikwer-rnem akngerr-inger.

**5** Arrwekeleny rernem atyetyart ilkwernem anenh, Abraham, Isaac, Jacob. Ikwer-rnem-they-anem Jesus Christ aremirrek ingkern-penh. Anwantherr Akngey tnaketyek atyetyart ilkwa ingkerrenh-kenh.

**6** Akngey-kenh angka aynteyel-antey, ipmelhey-angenh. Ingkern-penh ingwer-rnem Israel-kenh-rnem-arrpantey ilelheyel. Arrangkw, rernem Israel-kenh-angkwarr apentey-angenh arrangkw, ikwerenh aney-angenh arrangkwarl. Iwerr rarl mpwarek Jew ikwer-rnem, Jew ingwer-rnemel renh arraty apentey-angenh.

**7** Ingwer-rnem ayntarrp-ilelheyel Abraham-kenh-rnem-arrpantey. Rernem Abraham-kenh-rnem aney-angenh. Akngeyel renh ilek, “Isaac-angkwarr atha ngkwenh-rnem akngerram-akngerrileyenh.” Arrangkw ahern-areny-kenh-angkwarr. Ikwerenh-rnemant, Akngeyel arrwekel alhelenhey-alpenhan. Rernem Akngey-kenh-rnem innga.

**9** Akngeyel Abraham renh ilek, “Aherrety anyent-penh ayeng apey-alpeyenh. Sarah-el ampa akely atnyeneyenh.” Isaac renhey-antey Akngeyelarl alhelek-antey.

**10** Angka nhenh iterl-aray! Rebekah-kenh atyewenheng-atherr, aynikw anyent-kenh-antey, artwamp Isaac-kenh, awey atherr, awayikw ikwerenh ayteyikwek awelhetyek. Alakenh-anyem Akngeyel arrernek ikwer.

**11** Jacob Esau awey mwerrant-atherr. Akngeyelant ayteyikw rlwanek arrwekel-antey, ratherr born-irrey-alenty. Ra Rebekah renh ilek, “Awey ilkwa arrwekeleny ayteyikw ikwerenhek awelhetyek.” Ikwerenh-angkwarrant ra renh rlwanek. Ahern-areny-kenh-angkwarr renh arrerney-angenh arrangkwarl. Ra rlwanek, angkan ikwerenh arlpimpil-ayntetyek ikwer-angkwarrant. Alakenh ra arrernek ikwer arrwekel.

**13** Akngey-kenh angka arrwekeleny aynteyel: “Ayeng Jacob-ek inkwerelhek, Esau-ek arrangkwarl.”

**14** Nthakenh? Akngeyel arraty ikwer arrernek, ngay?

**15** Ra Moses renh ilek alakenhey arrpemarl, “Ayengan ilkelhenty, atha ingwer-rnem rlwanem, kelarl arntarnt-arem renh-rnem. Ingwer-rneman arrangkwarl.”

**16** Ran ilkelheyel-angkwarr, Akngeyel ingkerrenh arntarnt-arey-angker. Alakenh-anyemarl aynteyel, ahern-areny-kenh-angkwarr-weny, ikwerenh-angkwarrant. Angka ikwerenh alakenh aynteyel, anyent.

**17** Akngey-kenh angka arrwekeleny alakenh aynteyel. Akngeyel Pharaoh renh ilek, “Atha ngenh atyetyart arrernek, ingkerrenhel ayenh iterl-aretyek, ayengarl rlterrk ilkwakert. Angka atyenh arlpimpirreyenh apmer arrpanenh-warl.”

**18** Alakenh-anyem Akngeyel ingwer-rnem ingkerrenh arntarnt-arem arwa alhewer, ra ilkelhentyan. Ra ilkelhenty, ra ingwer-rneman awerrarl mpwarem, ikwer awelheyangarl.

**19** Ingwerentyel ayenh ayernem, “Alakenh anwekantherr arrernek kel arrwekel-antey. Ilekek-anem Akngeyel anwenhantherr akngakem?”

**20** Artwa ingwerentyel ahern atnthepenh mpwarem tyampit apek, kap apek. Anwantherr ahern-penh arnwarnekarl arrpemarl, ahern-arenyarl. Alakenh-anyem ahern-arenyel Akngey renh nthakenh tyerney-angenh. Ikwerenh-angkwarr arraty innga aynteyel, ament arrpemarl. Apmwamwam mpwarem, mwerr-angkeran mpwarem. Alakenh atnthepenh arrpemarl, ingwer apmwamwam ingweran mwerr-angkerarl arnwarnetyek, rarl ilkelheyel-angkwarr.

**22** Alakenh-apeny Akngeyan. Ra ingwer-rnem mpwarek, ikwer-rnem ahelirrek, renh-rnem ywerrelhiletyek-anek. Ranem renh-rnem menty amek-arek. Ranem ilkelheyel rernem ikwerenh-angkwarrant apenterl-ayntetyek. Ingwer-rnem ra arrwekel-antey rlwanek, ra renh-rnem arntarnt-aretyek. Alakenh ingkerrenhel renh iterl-aretyek rarl rlterrk innga aneyel.

**24** Akngeyel akngerr anwenhantherr arrwekel-antey rlwanek, Jew-rnem-then, Jew-weny-rnem-then.

**25** Hosea-kenh pip alakenh aynteyel: Akngeyel renh-rnem ilek, “Akngerr ingwer-rnem atyenh-rnem-weny, rernem atyenh-rnem alerl aneyenh. Awank-akerr ayeng inkwerelhey-angenh ikwer-rnem anenh. Ayeng ikwer-rnem inkwerelheyenh-anem.

**26** Arrwekel atha renh-rnem ilek, ‘Arrantherr atyenh-rnem aney-angenh.’ Apmer ingwer-areny-rnem atha antweyel, ‘Alerey atyenh-rnem.’ Ayeng innga itetharl aneyel.

**27** Isaiah-kenh pip aynteyel arrpemarl, irrkaty-angkwarr, pip ingwer-itwek aynteyel arrpemarl, ‘Israel-kenh-rnem akngerr innga aneyel, atha irrpetyap itethileyenh.

**28** Atha Ingkartel anamerl arratyiletyek ayerneyenh arlta ingkernel, arraty ilelhetyek.’

**29** Isaiah-el arrwekel ingkwernek pip-warl, ‘Ingkartel rlterrk Sodom-areny-rnem-then Gomorrah-areny-rnem-then althenek. Ra menty atwey-angenh Jew arrwekeleny-rnem altheney-angenh, ywerrelhiletyek-anek. Ra Jew ingkerrenh altheney-angenh, ingkern-penh anwantherr aneyel-antey.’”

## Israel-kenh-rnemel Akngey renh arraty tnakey-angenh

**30** Jew-weny-rnem arrkernelhey-angenh-antey angka arrwekeleny-angkwarr arraty anetyek. Akngeyel renh-rnem arraty arek, rernemarl renh tnakek.

**31** Israel-kenh ingwer-rnem arrkernelhenh arraty anetyek-arrpantey, angka arrwekeleny-angkwarr apentemel. Ywarn-antey ipantey rernem arrkernelhenh.

**32** Alakenh-anyem Akngey-kenh angka pipel aynteyel. Akngey angkek, “Atha apmer Zion-el apwert arrkngert arrernem. Ingwer-rnem aytnem-apeny atyenh-angkwarr-kety, rernem aytnem, angath-antey. Renh-rnem ankertewek angath-antey. Angath-antey aytnek. Rernem atyenh-angkwarr apentenh angath-antey. Alakenh-anyem rernem apmwewarl angkepirrek. Alakenh-anyem ra apwert aynteyel. Ingwerentyel Akngey renh tnakenty, ra nthakenh apatey-angenh apmwewarl irrey-angenh.”
