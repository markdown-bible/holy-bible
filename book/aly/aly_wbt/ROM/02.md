# Romans 2

## Akngeyel ingkerrenh akngakeyenh arraty

**1** Ntwa ingwer-rnem ayntarrp-ilenty, nga apmwelhilemarl. Akngeyel areyel, ntwarl ilek-ilek mpwareyel apmwangkwarr apenteyel. Nga ayntarrp ilelhem ament. Akngey-kenh-angkwarr arratyirretyek-arrpantey.

**2** Akngeyel apmelerr-angkwarr atwem, ingwerentyel apmwangkwarr mpwarenty. Arraty-angkwarr alakenh.

**3** Ntwa iterl-areyel Akngeyel apmwernemant atwenty-arrpantey. Akngeyel ngenh atwem arrpemarl, ntwa apmwa mpwarenty.

**4** Nga apek iterreyel, Akngeyel ngenh arntarnt-arenty-arrpantey. Akngey ngkweng mwerr lwewarrernelhem, nga ikwer-warl angkepirretyek.

**5** Nga apek apmwekety angkepirrey-angenh, Akngeyek mwantyel awelhey-angenh, Akngeyel ngenh atwemarl arlta ikwerenhel. Ingkerrenhel renh iterl-areyenh, angka ikwerenh arraty inngarl aynteyel.

**6** Arlta ingkern ikwerenhel, ra ingkerrenh ilek-ilek mwerr-angker antheyenh, rernemarl ikwerenh-angkwarr apentenh. Mwerr-angkwarr apententy, ran iterreyel intem-antey anayntetyek. Akngeyel renh itethileyenh arrpemarl arlta ingkernel.

**8** Ingkart-kety intwemelirreyel, ament-angkwarr alheyel-rnem, ranem renh-rnem ingkerrenh atwem, Jew-rnem-tangkwel, Jew-weny-rnem-then. Akngeyel renh-rnem arntety antheyenh.

**10** Mwerr-angkwarr apenteyel-rnem, Akngeyel mwerr anayntem ikwer-rnem-itwel, ikwer-rnem inkwerelheyenh, Jew mwerr-rnem arrwekel-antey, Jew-weny-rnem-then.

**11** Akngeyel-anem ingkerrenh-antey akngakeyenh arraty-angkwarr, Jew-rnem-then Jew-weny-rnem-then.

**12** Jew-weny-rnem Akngey-kenh angka arrwekelenyek akwerrp-rnem, ament-warl alheyel-rnem, Akngeyel renh-rnem ywerrelhileyenh. Jew ingwer-rnem arwarl awelhenh-rnem, rernem Akngey-kenh-angkwarr apentey-angenh, Akngeyel renh-rnem atwem angka arrwekelenyarl aynteyel-angkwarr ikwer-rnem. Ingwer-rnem Akngey-kenh-angkwarr arratyarl anenh-rnemant, Akngeyel renh-rnem arraty antwem.

**14** Jew-weny-rnemel Akngey-kenh angka apentey-angenh-antey, angka Moses-elarl arrernek-angkwarr. Rernem angka arrwekeleny ikwer-rnem-kenhant apenteyel. Akngey-kenh angka arrwekeleny iterl-arenh, akwerrngelant atnyenenh. Akngeyel renh-rnem iylpangkelhilenh, rernem iterretyek, nthenh-angkwarr apenterl-ayntetyek, apmwangkwarr apek, mwerr-angkwarr apek.

**16** Arlta ingkernel, Akngeyel ingwelem Jesus Christ arrerneyenh, rarl ingkerrenh akngaketyek, nthakenharl rernem iterreyel-angkwarr. Rernem irrtyertewelhey-angenh, Akngeyel areyelarl-kety. Alakenh-anyem angka mwerr-angker athan ileyel.

## Jew-rnemel angka Akngey-kenh arrwekeleny-angkwarr ywarn apenteyel

**17** Alakenh aynteyel Jew-rnemek, arrantherr Akngey-kenh-rnem akwel, Akngey-kenh angkek akalty-arrpantey, arraty-angkwarr apenteyel-arrpantey.

**19** Arrantherr Jew-rnem tnakelheyel arrantherr ingkerrenh mwerr-angkwarr akalty-antheyel, atnwenheyel renh-rnem, ingwemerntel aneyel-apeny-rnem, rernem angkepirretyek.

**20** Arrantherr Jew-rnem tnakelheyel arrantherr akwerrp-rnem akalty-anthenty-arrpantey, angka arrwekeleny-angkwarr atnyenetyek. Arrantherr tnakelheyel, arrantherr Angka Akngey-kenh-angkwarr apenterl-anenty-arrpantey.

**21** Arrantherr ingwer-rnem akaltyilem, arrantherr ament akaltyirrey-angenh arrpemarl. Arrantherr ingwer-rnem ilem, anyelkel iney-angenh anetyek. Arrantherr anyelkelarl ineyel.

**22** Arrantherr ingwer-rnem akaltyilem, ingwer-kenh mpweny ikngwey-angenh anetyek. Arrantherr ingwer-kenh mpweny ikngwem arrpemarl. Mpwarekarl-penh ingkart-arrpantey-rnem-kety apmwernem arrantherr ipmelhetyek-arrpantey arrkernelheyel ywarn. Arrantherr anyelkel ineyel-antey.

**23** Arrantherr tnakelheyel arrantherr Akngey-kenh arrwekeleny-angkwarr apenterl-anenty-arrpantey. Arrantherr Akngey-kenh arrwekeleny-angkwarr apmwelhileyel. Angka Akngey-kenh arrwekeleny aynteyel: “Jew-rnem arrantherr apmwangkwarr apenteyelarl-kety, alakenh-anyem apmer ingwer-areny-rnem Akngeyek irrpelthirreyel.”

**25** Artwa mpwarenty, mwerrarl artwekenh-angkwarr. Angka impen nhanyem angka arrwekeleny-angkwarr apenterl-ayntetyek. Angka arrwekeleny renh apentey-areny, ikwer inkwerelhemel. Arrantherr Akngey-kenh-angkwarr apmwelhilenty, arrantherr arrangkwarl, ikwerety intwemelirrem.

**26** Artwa Jew-weny ikwer-rnem-kenh-angkwarr artwirrey-angenh arrangkw, rap Akngey-kenh-angkwarr apententy, rap arraty-angkwarr aneman, Jew arraty-angkwarr aneyel-rnem-apeny. Akngeyel renh-rnem mwerr antweyel, Jew artwernemel Akngey-kenh angka arraty apenteyel-apeny. Jew-weny-rnemel arrenhantherr ayntarrp-ilem, arrantherr Akngey-kenh angka innga apentey-angenh-they.

**28** Rernem Akngey-kenh-angkwarr arraty apententy, rernem Jew-kenh-angkwarr arraty apentem-apeny. Jew ingwer-rnem angath-antey apenteyel, arrangkw, arraty innga apentey-angenh.

**29** Ingwerel-rnem apenteyel Jew-rnem innga apenteyel-apeny. Itnweng Rlwartel renh-rnem akalty-anthek, Moses-kenh-angkwarr-weny. Akngey ranem ikwer-rnem inkwerelhem arrpemarl, ahern-areny-kenh-angkwarr-weny.
