# Romans 11

## Akngeyel Israel-areny irrpetyapant atnyeneyel

**1** Atha ayernem arrpemarl, Akngeyel ikwerenh-rnem menty ipmek? Arrangkw. Ayeng Israel-kenh aneyel arrpemarl, Abraham-kenh arrpemarl, Benjamin-kenh.

**2** Aray, Akngeyel ikwerenh-rnem menty ipmey-angenh, arrwekel ikwer-rnem arrernek. Angka arrwekeleny alakenh aynteyel, Elijah-iperrek. Elijah-el Israel-kenh-rnem ayntarrp-ilek.

**3** “Ingkartay, angkakert ngkwenh-rnem ipmek-antey atwek. Rernem apwert ilperr-ilperr ngkwenh-rnem akek. Ayeng anyentant ngkweng inkwerelheyel. Rernem atyeng nthweyel, ayenh atwetyek arrpemarl.”

**4** Akngeyel Elijah renh alakenh ilek, “Atha artwa 7,000 atyenh atnyeneyel-antey, rernemarl atyengety iparelhey-angenh-antey, ingkart-arrpantey Baal-ek inkwerelhey-angenh.”

**5** Aley alakenh-apenyarl aynteyel, irrpetyapant aneyel-antey, Akngey-kenh, iwerr ilenh arrernek arrwekel. Akngey rantey renh-rnem rlwanek, renh-rnem arntarnt-aretyek.

**6** Aray, Akngeyel arrwekel-antey renh-rnem rlwanek, rernem ikwer awelhey-angenh anenhel. Rernem amentewarl alhenh. Akngey-kenh alakenh aynteyel. Ra ikwer-rnem ilkelheyel-antey, rernem angkepirretyek.

**7** Israel-kenh-rnem Akngey-kenh angka arrwekeleny-angkwarr arraty anetyek-arrpantey arrkernelhenh. Irrpetyapant Akngey-kenh-angkwarr arraty anenh, renh-rnem rlwanek-rnemant, ingwer-rnem arrangkw-antey. Ingwer-rnem awerrarl anenh amentewarl anenh. Akngeyel renh-rnem awerr mpwarek.

**8** Akngey-kenh angka arrwekeleny alakenh aynteyel: “Akngeyel renh-rnem ilpertilek, anngernem apmwilek, rernem arraty arekerr. Ra iylpa renh-rnem apmwilek, rernem arraty awekerr.” Akngey renh iterl-arey-angenh, aparrng-apeny anenh, iylpa arwengk-apeny, arraty awey-angenh. Aley aynteyel-antey.

**9** David-el Akngey renh ilek, “Akngeyay, rernem tyenkerr aternpel arlkweyel amernekant artewentyel iterremel, renh-rnem ilpertilay, renh-rnem atwa.

**10** Akngeyay, annga renh-rnem aparrngilay, warrk ilkwa anthay.”

## Ingwer-inger Akngeyel ingwer-rnem itethileyenh

**11** Atha ayernem arrpemarl. Israel-kenh-rnem amentirrek Akngey-kety, Akngeyel renh-rnem aleth-antey ipmey-angenh Arrangkw. Rernem amentirrek Akngey-kety, Akngeyel-anem apmer ingwer-areny-rnemant-anem itethilem. Israel-areny-rnem-anem iterretyek, angkepirretyek apek.

**12** Israel-kenh-rnem Jesus-kenh angka ikwerety kel ipmelhek. Akngeyel-anem ingwer-rnemant itethilek. Israel-kenh-rnem angkepirrenty, ingkerrenh-anem inkwerelhem.

**13** Ayeng arrekantherr-warl angkem, arrantherr Jew-weny arleng-areny-rnem. Akngeyel ayenh arrekantherr-warl thwenek, athan arrenhantherr akalty-anthetyek. Warrk impen innga atha arrenhantherr akalty-anthetyek. Jew-rnem rernem arenty, rernem alharlkwem. Rernem iterrem-anem angkepirretyek Akngey-warl. Ikwer-penh Akngeyel-anem renh-rnem itethileyenh.

**15** Jew rernem Jesus-kenh angkekety ipmelhek. Akngey ingwer-rnemek inkwerelhek. Israel-kenh-rnem angkepirrenty, Akngeyel renh-rnem itethileyenh.

**16** Artekerran mwerr-akert, arwerl mwerr arrpemarl, alperr-rnem arlenyirrey-angenh. Israel-kenh-rnem rernem arwerl mwerr-apeny anenh. Arlperr arlenyirrekarl, Akngeyel iwek, ingwer-anem rnkernemel. Arlperr apely-rneman Jew-weny arrantherr-apenyarl. Akngeyel arrenhantherr pweytilek Jew arrwekel anenh-rnem-warl, ingkerrenh-anem arrenhantherr arntarnt-aretyek, arwerl mwerr-warl pweytilem-apeny.

**18** Arrantherr tnakelhey-angenh anetyek. Arrantherr iylpangketyek, Akngeyel arrenhantherr pweytilek ingkernan, Jew-rnem-tangkwel arrernek arwerl impen-apeny. Arrwekel ikwer-rnem-angkwarr-tangkwel. Jew-rnem-angkwarr ayntenhey-alpek Jesus-warl-athek.

**19** Alakenh arrantherr angkeyel, “Arwerl arleny-rnem iwek-apeny, ra Jew-rnem iwek, anwenhantherr pweytilem ikwer-warl, arwerl mwerr-warl pweytilem-apeny.”

**20** Arrantherr kel arraty angkeyel. Akngeyel renh-rnem iwek arlperr arlenyarl iwem-apeny, rernemarl Jesus ikwer irrpelthirrenh. Rernem renh tnakey-angenh-they ra renh-rnem iwek. Jesus renh tnakenty mwerrarl, ikwer iterretyek, renh tnaketyek arrekantherrenh-angkwarr rlterrk-weny arrangkwarl. Rant iwerr arrekantherr mpwarek ingkerrenhek. Alakenh tnakelhey-angenh arrangkw, mwanty iterretyek.

**21** Akngeyel Jew-rnem arrwekeleny-rnem amek-arey-angenh-apeny, ra nthakenh arrenhantherr amek-arey-angenh arrpemarl.

**22** Iterl-aray! Akngey ra ingkerrenhek inkwerelhem, ra nthakenh amek-arey-angenh. Israel-kenh-rnem Jesus ikwerety amentirrek, ra renh-rnem kel amek-arey-angenh iwek. Arrekantherr ra inkwerelhek. Amentirrenty, ranem iweyenh arwerl arleny iweyenh-apeny. Arrantherr ikwerenh-angkwarr apententy, ra mwerr arntarnt-arem.

**23** Israel-arenyel-rnem Jesus renh tnakenty, ra renh-rnem itethilem, rnkernem angkep, anyentelhilem ikwerenh-rnem-warl.

**24** Iterl-aray, arwerl anyent-apeny, Jew-rnem-tangkwel anenh, angka anyent-angkwarr apentenh. Angkep anyentelhilem ikwerenh-rnem-warl. Angkep rnkerneynem ikwerenh-rnem-warl, angkep aylpeyneyel ikwerenh-rnem-warl. Amentew-ament alherl-alhenty-apeny.

**25** Atyenh-rnem, atha arrenhantherr angka anyelkel-antey ilem, arrantherr iterretyek, tyak iterl-arelhetyek. Akngeyel Israel-kenh-rnem ilpertilek, Jew-weny-rnem apmer arrpanenh-they Akngey-warl anyentirretyek.

**26** Ikwer-penh Israel-kenh-rnem itethileyenh, Akngey-kenh angka arrwekeleny aynteyel arrpemarl, “Apmer Zion-they itethiley-angker ra apetyeyenh, Israel-kenh-rnem apmwekety mwerrileyenh.

**27** Atha apmwa ikwer-rnem-kety iweyenh. Alakenh atha iwerr mpwarem ikwer-rnem.”

**28** Jew-rnem menty angka mwerr-angkerek ipmelhekarl-kety rernemarl arlkarirrenharl-kety, ranem angka arrernek Jew-weny-rnemek, rernem-anem apentetyek ikwerenh-angkwarr, Jesus renh tnaketyek. Jew arrwekeleny-tangkwel ra arntarnt-arek, Jew-rnemek iylpelirrey-angenh-antey. Angka arrernek ikwer-rnem arrwekel inngantey, ipmelhey-angenh arrangkw.

**29** Arrwekel ra renh-rnem alhelek anthetyek. Akngeyel renh-rnem rlwanek, ilek-ilek renh-rnem anthek. Ra angka ikwer-rnem arrernek. Ra nthakenh ikwer-rnem-kety iylpelirrey-angenh, aleth-antey arntarnt-areyel.

**30** Alethang-rnem, arrwekel arrantherr Akngeyek awelhey-angenh. Aley-anyem ra mwerr-angkwarr arrekantherr lwewarrernelhek, Israel-kenh-rnem ikwer awelhey-angenh-kety, Jesus renh tnakey-angenh-kety.

**31** Lyert Israel-areny-rnem Akngeyek awelhey-angenh. Akngeyel renh-rnem mwantyel arntarnt-arenh, rarl arrenhantherr mwantyel arntarnt-areyenh-apeny.

**32** Aray, Akngeyel ingkerrenh ilem, rernem arraty awelhey-angenh anem. Ranem ahern-areny ingkerrenh mwantyel arntarnt-aretyek.

## Paul Akngey ikwer inkwerelheyel

**33** Akngey ranem tyak innga, akalty innga aneyel ingkerrenh-angkwarran, ahern-areny-rnem-angkwarr.

**34** “Ahern-arenyel nthakenh Akngey akalty-anthey-angenh.” Angka ingwer aynteyel,

**35** “Ahern-arenyel nthakenh Akngey renh antheyney-angenh.”

**36** Rantey ahern-then alker-then arrpmernek. Lyert ra anterrkweyel. Ra arrpemarl ingkerrenh lyertenyilem. Ran rlterrk innga aneyel. Ahern-arenyel renh tnaketyek intem-antey. Amen.
