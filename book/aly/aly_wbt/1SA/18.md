# 1 Samuel 18

## Saul-el David alharlkweyel

**6** David-el artwa arlpenty Goliath ipmew atwew-penh, Israel-wenh-rnem ingkerr alperlewenh apmer-warl. Apmer arrpanenh-they arelh-rnem apetyew, renh-rnem rlwateyew, rntweynenh aylelheynenh inkwerelhemel. Ingwer-rnem teram akely pelayerrenh, ingwer-rneman ampwal ketay-apenyarl pelayerrenh.

**7** Rernem alakenh aylelherl-apenh:
> “Saul-el ahelengkw akngerr atwew,
> David-elan ahelengkw akngerr inngarl atwew!”


**8** Saul-el awew, rap ahelerrew artewenty innga. Ra iterrew alakenh, “Rernem angkeyel, David-elan ahelengkw akngerr inngarl atwew atyeng-angkwarr. Atyetyart apek renh arrerneyew arrkernelheyel.”

**9** Ikwer-theyan rap alharlkwemel renh arntarnt-arenh intem-antey.

**10** Arlta ingwerelan INGKARTel inweng apmwa arrpemarl thwenew Saul-warl. Ra artewentyel arwengkerrenh, warl kwenel anenh, arlkenty iwenh. David-an ampwal ketay-apeny ikwer pelayerrenh apmelerr-angkwarr. Saul-el ran irrtyart atnyenenh itepel,

**11** rap iwew, alakenh iterremel, “Arnelty-angkwarr atha renh akwerterrp atantheyew!” David-an arnangerrew anamerl-antey irrtyart-wety. Atherr-inger ra renh atantheyew-anew. Atherr-inger David anamerl-antey ikwer-wety arnangerrew. ...
