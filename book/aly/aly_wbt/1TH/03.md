# 1 Thessalonians 3

**1** Kel arleng-alenty anantherr anenh tawen Athens nhenh-itwew, alharntemel anenh arrewantherr. Silas aylanth angkerrew, “Mwerrangay aylanth arteny anem? Timothy-ant apek alhemer Thessalonica-warl.”

**2** Renhantap aylanth thwenew arrewantherr-warl. Anantherr ingkerr warrkerreyel, angka mwerr-angker Akngey-wenh ilerl-apaynteyew apmer arrpanenh-itwew. Renhap aylanth thwenew, rlterrkelemel akalty-anthaynteyew arrenhantherrenh.

**3** Akngeyel ilew arrwekel-antey, ingwer-rnemelarl irrwernerl-aneyenh. Rernemap apmelerr-angkwarr irrwernem Christian-rnem anenhantherrenh. Anantherran iterrew arrewantherr, angkeperrewerr apek Akngey-wety, ingwerentyel arrenhantherrenh irrwernentyel.

**4** Anantherrarl anenh arrewantherr-itwel, anantherr ilenh alakenh, “Ingwer-rnemelan Christian-rnem anenhantherrenh irrwerneyenh, apmwangkwarr mpwaremel.” Inngarlap anewantherr alakenh-anyem aynteyel.

**5** Alakenh-wety anantherr artewentyel iterrenh arrewantherr, angkeperrewerr arrantherr. Anantherr iterrenh alakenh, “Alep Akngeyant rernem apenteyel-antey? Arrangkw apek. Ywarn apek akalty-anthenh renh-rnem. Arrenty ilkwel apek renh-rnem arrkernew, rernem Akngey-wety ipmelhenheyew.” Alakenh-wety aylanth Timothy thwenew. Anantherr iterl-areyew, arrantherr arraty anentyew Akngeyew.

## Timothy inkwerelhemel apey-alpew angkakert

**6** Aylanthap mpwelhenh arrpemarl Timothy-ew, rarl apey-alpenhew. Rarl apey-alpewel, iley-alpew-anem aylewanth, “Weth-rnem arraty-antey aneyel Akngey-angkwarr, Jesus apentemel. Mwerrant-arey aneyel arntarnt-arerremel. Rernem inkwerelhemel arrpemarl iterreyel anewantherr. Rerneman ilkelheyel, mpwelenhanthenh areyew arrpemarl.”

**7** Ya, aylantharl angka awew, aylanthap inkwerelhew artewenty. Angkap arrewantherr-akert awew, aylernanthenhap irrngerney-anthew. Ingwer-rnemel irrwerneyel-antey anenhantherrenh, arlkanap alpwelyeleyel. Anantherranap inkwerelhemel-antey iterreyel arrewantherr.

**8** Aleyan arlka mwerr-anem anantherr awelhem iterl-aremel, arrantherrarl Akngeyew aneyel-antey intem rlterrk.

**9** Ya, anantherr iterl-areyel alakenh, Akngeyelarl arrenhantherrenh arntarnt-areyel. Alakenh-anyem ra anenhantherrenh irrngerney-anthem. Anantherr intem-antey inkwerelhemel renh nakem, arrantherrarl ikwer awelheyneyel.

**10** Anantherr rlterrkel mwanty Akngey ayernem, thweneyew angkep arrewantherr-warl. Arrantherran akaltyerreyel-antey Akngey-wenh-angkwarr. Anantherran intem-antey akalty-anthaynteyew arrenhantherrenh.

**11** Alakenhewarl Akngeyan anantherr ayerneyel Jesus-then, “Thwenenh-atherr, anantherr alheyew Thessalonica-warl, renh-rnem areyew.”

**12** Anantherrap Jesus ayernem, arrantherraw mwerrant-arey anaynteyew, arntarnt-arerr-aynteyew athamerremel ingwer-rnemew-then. Anantherrarl arrewantherr athamerrem-apeny, alakenh.

**13** Jesus anantherr ayernem, arrenhantherrenh rlterrkeleyew, arrantherr arraty anaynteyew intem-antey. Akngeyelap arem, arrantherrarl arraty aneyel apmwewety. Rap inkwerelheyenh arrewantherr, Jesus-arl apey-alpeyenhel irrwerl-they antyerl ikwerenh-rnem-akert.
