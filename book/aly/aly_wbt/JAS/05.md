# James 5

## Pwertay mwantyelhelenh-arey, ilek-ilek akngerrarl atnyeneyel-rneman

**1** Ingwerentyel arrantherr akngerrarl atnyenem. Irrpetyapewant ayeng angkeyel aleyan. Ampwal akngerrel arrenhantherrenh ilperteley-angker. Arrantherrap artnemer artewentyel, ingwer-ingweran apmwanemarl aynteyenh-wetyarl.

**2** Rap alakenh aynteyel, ilek-ilek mwerr-angker arrewantherrenh kwaty-apenyarlap ran. Ilkwetangkwel, rap artwerretyerrem-anem. Amern arrewantherrenh intenty-apeny. Arnkwa mwerr-angker arrewantherrenh interrkel arlkwenty-apeny, altywer-altywerelem. Apwert arrewantherrenh apmwerrenty-apeny. Ya, ilek-ilek mwerr-angker arrewantherrenh apmwerreyenh. Ilek-ilek akngerr mwerr-angker atnyenem arrantherr ankety irremel, ament atnyenem.

**4** Arrantherrap akngerr arrernem, warrkerreyew katen-itwew, ilemel payemeleyew renh-rnem arraty-angkwarr. Innganap arrangkwarl! Arrantherrap akelyant payemelem, warrk ipmelhentyelan. Rernemap artnem arrewantherr. Akngeyelanap renh-rnem awemarl artnentyan.

**5** Arrantherrap ilek-ilek mwerr-angker akarrelem, ahern nhenh-itwew inkwerelhemel anemel. Yew-yew, artewenty innga Akngeyan arrewantherr ahelerrem.

**6** Mwerr-rnem-alew, arrantherrarl ayntarrp-ilemel atwem arwa? Rernem arraty anem, arrantherrap ikwer-rnem-wety arlkarerremarl. Rernem ahelerrey-angenh arrewantherr, arrantherrap renh-rnem ipmew atwem.

## Akeleyt mpwelhaynteyew, Jesus-arl apey-alpeyenhew

**7** Atyenh-rnem, arternp akeleyt mpwelhaynteyew, Jesus-arl apey-alpeyenhew. Artewenty iterrey-angenh. Artwa amern atherrk lyapelheley-angkerew iterrenh-arey. Ra ahern-tangkwel angernem, ntang-anem rlker-anthem. Rap arternp mpwelhem kwatyew rntwentyew. Rap arternp mpwelhem amern atherrk lyapentyew ahern-itwew.

**8** Kel alakenhap arrantherran Jesus-ewan arternp mpwelhemer, rlterrk anemel, inngarl ra apey-alpeyenh-wety.

**9** Nthakenh arrantherr ilterrey-angenh, tyel-angkerrey-angenh, Akngeyel arrenhantherrenh court-emelewerr. Yew-yew, ingwer-ingweran Jesus-arl apetyeyenh-wety, ayern-aynteyew ingkerrenh.

**10** Atyenh-rnem, angkakert-rnemew iterrenh-arey. Awank-akerr rernem angka Akngey-wenh ilerl-apenh. Ingwer-rnemel renh-rnem irrwernenh, pwarrk anthenh. Rernemap ipmelhey-angenh, arrangkw. Arternp rlterrk mpwelhenh Akngeyew intem. Rap inkwerelhenh ikwer-rnem, mwerr antwemel. Arrantherr renh-rnem iwarrernemer. Artwamp Job wethew iterrenh-arey. Rlterrkarl ikwer ayntenh, Akngeyewant ra intem iterrenh. Rlterrkarl ayntew-penh, Akngeyan ikwer athamerrenh. Aleyan rlengk Akngey athamerrem ikwerenh-rnemew irrkaty-angkwarr.

**12** Atyenh-rnem, angka rlterrk ingwer nhenhew iterrenh-arey. Arraty angkayntenh-arey intem. Nthakenh angkey-angenh irrtywerremel. Nthakenh ingwer-rnem terikemeley-angenh. Arwa arraty-angkwarrant angkayntenh-arey! Alakenh arrantherr tyernemer arraty-antey. Arrantherr apek arraty angkey-angenh, Akngeyelanap apmwa antweyenh arlta anyent ikwerel, rarl court-emeleyenheng-arey akngerr.

## Intem-antey Akngey-warl-athek angkaynteyew, irrpeltherrey-angenh

**13** Rlterrk anyentew apekan aynteyel, ra apek arlka apmwarl aneyel. Ra Akngey ayernemer alpemeleyew. Ingweran irrngerney apek aneyel. Ra Akngeyew inkwerelhemel aylelhemer.

**14** Ingweran arntety apek aynteyel. Ra atyetyart tyaty-areny-rnem ilemer, apetyeyew ikwer-warl. Rerneman Akngey ikwer ayernemer, itepel renh arnep-arnemel, anter alipel renh apernemel. Alakenh-anyem Akngey ikwer ayernemer mwerreleyew, itna Jesus itnewemel.

**15** Arrantherrarl alakenh ayernem, iterremel Akngeyew, arntety rap mwerrerremarl. Ra apek arntetyerrew, apmwel-ayeth-tangkwel ra anenharl. Akngeyelap apmwa ikwer-wety irrarlkem, awakelhey-angenh ikwer.

**16** Alakenh-wety Christian anyentan ilelhemer Christian ingwer-rnemewan, rarl apmwelhelew, sorry-anem angkemel. Rap ilemer, Akngey ikwer ayerneyew. Ra apek arntetyerrew, apmwel-ayetharl anenh, Akngeyelan renh mwerrelemarl. Alakenharl aynteyel: ingwerenty Akngeyew arraty aneyel, rap Akngeyan alhewerarl ayernem. Rap renh awemarl, mwerr mpwaremel ikwer.

**17** Artwamp Elijah wethew iterrenh-arey. Artwamp ra angkakert Akngey-wenh anenh awank-akerr. Artwa anantherr-apenyarlap ran. Rap Akngey angka rlterrk-angkwarr ayernew alakenh, “Apmer nhenh arlenyela!” Inngarlap aherrety irrpetyew-atety ayntew artwerrety. Kwaty rntwey-angenh, arrangkw.

**18** Ikwer-penhan ra ayernew, kwaty thweneyew-anem. Inngarlap kwatyan rntwew apmer ikwer-itwew. Amern atherrkeny-akerr-anem angkep lyapew ikwer-itwew.

**19** Atyenh-rnem, anyent apek angkeperrem Akngey-wety, apmwel-ayeth-anem anemel. Christian ingwerelan renh alpemelemer. Arternp-antey ikwer angkemer, raw Akngey-warl angkeperrenhey-alpeyew.

**20** Alakenhan mwerrarl. Akngeyel apmwa ikwer-wety irrarlkem-anem awakelhey-angenhel, iylpelerremel-anem apmwa ingkerrew. Kel-antey.
