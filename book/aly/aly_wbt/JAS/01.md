# James 1

**1** Atha James-el pip nhenh ingkwerneyel. Ayengan Akngeyew warrkerreyel Jesus Christ-ew-then. Atha pip nhenhan thweneyel arrewantherr ingkerr-warl Akngey-wenh-rnem-warl, apmer arleng-areny-rnem-warl-then.

## Rlterrk ayntentyeng-arey, inkwerelhemelant intem anenheyew

**2** Atyenh-rnem Christian-rnem, angka nhew iterr-ayntenh-arey. Arrantherran inkwerelhemelant anemer, apmwarl ayntemeng-arey. Ingwer-rnemel arrkernemeng-arey arrenhantherrenh, Akngeyewantap anyentew iterr-ayntenh-areyan. Alakenh-angkwarr arrantherr rlterrkerrem, ipmelhey-angenh-anemarl.

**4** Ya, intem renh apententy arrantherr, arrantherrap inngarl arratyerrem, rlterrkerrem Jesus-apeny.

**5** Christian anyent apatemel iterrem apek, “Akngey-wenh-angkwarran atha iterl-arey-angenharl. Nthakenh irrem ayeng?” Ra Akngey ayernemer, renh akalty-antheyew. Akngeyel mwerr-angker anthey-angker, nthakenh menty awey-angenh.

**6** Anyentel renh ayerneyew arraty-angkwarr, irrpeltherrey-angenhel. Raw alakenh iterreyew, “Ya, Akngeyelan inngarl ayenh alpemeley-angker.” Nthakenh ra atherr-angkwarr iterremel ayerney-angenh, “Akngey atyeng awelhey-angenh apek.” Ra iterrenty atherr-angkwarr, ranap kwatyel alperr irrwerlap-anenty-apenyarl, arwarl irrwerlap-aneyelarl.

**7** Alakenhanap ra rlterrk-weny-anteyarl. Akngeyelanap nthakenh anthey-angenharl, alakenh-anyem ayernentyan. Nthakenh renh akalty-anthey-angenh, arrangkw.

## Apwert akngerr angwarey-angenh

**9** Christian anyent arrangkw apek apwert-weny anem. Ra iterl-aremer, Akngeyarl ikwer artewentyel ilkelheyel. Alakenh iterl-aremel ra inkwerelhemel anem.

**10** Christian ingwerelan apwert akngerr apek atnyenem. Nthakenh ra tangkemel apwert-akert aney-angenh. Ra iterl-aremer arrpemarl, Akngeyarl ikwer-then artewentyel ilkelheyel. Alakenh iterl-aremel ra inkwerelhemel anemer arrpemarl. Artwa apwert akngerr-akert ra pretty-apeny, ahernelarl rtneyel-apeny.

**11** Aherretyelan ingkeny tyerrelhenhey-alhem, rwenp-anem irrem. Ingkenyel ampernem pretty renh, rap arleny-anem irrem, aytnem-anem. Alakenh-anyem artwa apwert akngerr-akert rap atnengkanerrem-anem, apal-antey apwertewant inkwerelherl-anenty. Ilek-ilek mwerr-angker ikwerenh arrangkwerreyelpem pretty yanh-apeny.

## Rlterrkarl ayntemeng-arey

**12** Ingwer-rnemel arrenhantherrenh irrwernem, awelhey-angenh ikwer-rnem. Rlterrkel-antey Akngeyan apent-aynteyew, rap inkwerelhemarl arrewantherr. Rap akngeynem, ikwer-itwel aney-alpeyew intem-antey. Rap alakenh-anyemarl aynteyel ikwerarl ilkelheyel-rnemewan.

**13** Anyent apek iterrem apmwel-ayeth-angkwarr. Nthakenh ra alakenh angkey-angenh, “Akngeyan ilkelheyel, ayeng apmwel-ayeth iterreyew.” Arrangkw alakenhan! Akngeyan alakenh ilkelhey-angenharl. Anyentel Akngey renh nthakenh arrkerney-angenh, apmwel-ayeth mpwareyew.

**14** Anyent iterrenty, ikwerenharrp-angkwarr alheyew, rap apmwelhelemarl. Ament-antey ra ilkelhem, apmwangkwarr alheyew. Alakenh iterremel-antey ra angkeperrem Akngey-wety, rap irrwernelhem ament-antey.

**15** Ra iterrem, ikwerenharrp-angkwarr alheyew, rap anamerl-antey apmwa mpwarem. Rap apmwelhelem innganem, nthakenh ipmelhey-angenh-anem. Akngeyelap renh ilteyenh arlta anyent ikwerel, ingkerrarl ra ayerneyenheng-arey. Rap renh irrenteyenh intem-antey.

## Akngeyel anthem mwerr-angker

**16** Atyenh-rnem, mwanty awelhenh-arey angka nhenhew, arrantherr apmwangkwarr-anem alhewerr.

**17** Ilek-ilek mwerr-angker anantherr arem ahern nhenh-itwew. Akngeyel rarl anyentel mpwarew, rarl anthew anewantherr. Ra mpwarew ingkeny, alkent, antyarr-akerr. Rernemap ingwer-anem irrem itwernew-then kwenyel-then. Akngey ran nthakenh ingwererrey-angenh, arrangkw. Irrkaty intem ra aneyel.

**18** Angka arraty ikwerenh ilew anewantherr, anenhantherrenh-tangkwel ayerrpelemel, ikwerenh-anem aneyew. Angka nhenhel iteth atnyenem intem-antey. Anantherran impen ikwerenharl. Ilek-ilek ingwer-rneman ra mpwarew anantherr-apeny-wenyarl.

## Angka Akngey-wenhew awelh-aynteyew

**19** Atyenh-rnem, mwanty awelhenh-arey, atharl akalty-antheyel-angkwarr. Ingwerarl angkeyelew, arternp-tangkwel awelheyew ikwer. Anamerl-antey rwenperremel angkey-angenh. Alakenh-weny.

**20** Artwa ahelengkwan nthakenh arraty aney-angenh Akngey-warl-ilwengk.

**21** Rap ipmelhemer apmwewety-tangkwel, raw Akngeyew-anem warrkerreyew. Arrantherr apmwel-ayeth-tangkwel anenh. Aleyan arrantherr ingwer-rnem-apeny-weny anemarl, Akngey apentey-angenh-rnem-apeny-weny. Angkarl ra arrewantherr anthew, intem-antey itetheleyew. Nthakenh arrantherr apmwel-ayeth iterrey-angenh, arrangkw-anem.

**22** Arrantherran angka Akngey-wenhew awelh-aynteyew. Arrkentant iylpelant awey-angenh. Intem-antey mpwaraynteyew, rarl akalty-antheyel-angkwarr.

**23** Ingwerel apek awem angka ikwerenh, arwarlap ra aweman. Ranap glass-itwewarl inngerr arelheyel-apeny, rap iylpelerremel-anem anperl-alhem inngerr ikwerenhew. Alakenh arrantherr nthakenh arrkentant angka awey-angenh, iylpelerrewerr angka ikwer.

**25** Angka yanh mwerr-angker inngarl! Ra akalty-antheyel, Akngeyel rarl apmwewety irrarlkew. Anantherr inkwerelhemel ikwer awelhem inteman iylpelerrey-angenh, Akngeyelanap inngarl anenhantherrenh irrngerney-anthem.

**26** Anyentan alakenh iterrem, “Ayengan Christian inngarl, Akngeyantarl apenteyelarl.” Angka apmwel apek ra iltem. Apmwel-ayeth apek ra angkem ingwer-rnemew. Ranap mwerr-arrpantey nakelhem ament-antey. Akngeyarl ra apenteyel-arrpantey.

**27** Christian inngan ingwer-rnemew athamerrem. Ampa alhamp-rnem ra arntarnt-arem arelh inpert-rnem-then, rlterrkarl ayntemeng-arey. Ingwerentyel renh arrkernem, apmwangkwarr alheyew, rap mentyarl rlterrkel awem, angkeperrey-angenhel.
