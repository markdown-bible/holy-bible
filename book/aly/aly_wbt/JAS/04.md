# James 4

## Akngey-angkwarr intem anaynteyew

**1** Arrantherran intem ilterreyel. Alakenh irrem, arrantherrarl ament-antey iterreyel. Arrantherrap ament iterr-ayntem, inkwerelhemel anaynteyew, ingwer-rnemew iterrey-angenh. Alakenh-anyemap arrantherr ilterreyel.

**2** Angwarem arrantherr ilek-ilek mwerr-angker rlengkeny, nthakenh iney-angenh. Arrantherrap ilterrem atwerremel ikwer-rnem-they. Ipmew apek-anem ingwerenty atwem, ampwal renh-rnemap iney-alkenh. Arraty-angkwarran Akngeyant-tangkwel ayerneyew.

**3** Nthakenh ra arrenhantherrenh anthey-angenh, arrantherrarl ament iterremel ayerneyelan. Apmwel-ayetharlap alakenhan ayerneyel, ingwer-rnemew iterrey-angenhel.

**4** Arrantherr Christian ilelhem, Akngey arraty apentey-angenharlap arrantherran, arrangkw. Nthakenh Akngeyew innga ilkelhey-angenh, ilek-ilek ahern nhenh-arenyewarl arralty-aneyelan. Alakenh-wenyarl ra aynteyel. Arrantherr apentem ahern nhenh-areny-rnem, nthakenh arrantherr Akngey apentey-angenharl.

**5** Ran anenhantherrenh kanper iterl-areyel. Ra iterl-arem, anantherr ilek-ilek angwarey-angker. Alakenhewarlap ra anenhantherrenh mpwarewan. Angka ikwerenhelanap alakenh-anyemarl aynteyel.

**6** Ra anthem ilek-ilek mwerr-angker irrwerl-areny anewantherr athamerremel. Angka ikwerenhel alakenh aynteyel: “Anyent ilkwa ilelhem, Akngeyan ikwer athamerrey-angenharl. Ingwer akely ilelhem, Akngeyan ikwer athamerremarl.”

**7** Alakenh-wety arrantherr Akngey-warl anthelhemer, warrkerremel ikwer. Arrenty ilkwewety rlterrk anemel, Akngeyewant awelhemel. Arrenty rap anterremarl arrewantherr-wety.

**8** Akngey-warl angkeperrenhey-alpenh-arey, rap arrer innga anem arrewantherr-itwel. Ipmelhenh-arey apmwewety. Arratyerrenh-arey-anem. Intem iterrey-angenh arrewantherrenharrp-angkwarr. Akngey-wenh-angkwarrant iterr-aynteyew.

**9** Inweng anthelheyew ikwer-warl, rap inkwerelhem arrewantherr nakemel. Nthakenh arrantherr nakelhey-angenh. Nyerrerremel ilelhenh-arey, angkeperrenhey-alpemel apmwewety.

**11** Atyenh-rnem, tyel-angkey-angenh ingwer-rnemew. Ingwerenty apek tyel-angkem ingwerew ayntarrp-ilemel, rap angka Akngey-wenh arraty apentey-angenharl. Alakenh angkemel rap angka renh apmwelemarl.

**12** Akngey rant anyent atyetyart anewantherr aneyel. Rarlap angkan anewantherr anthew. Rant anyentelarl court-emelem anenhantherrenh. Ingwerenty ra ayerrpelemarl, ingwerentyan ra irrenteyenh rwa ilkwewarl intem ampeyel-warlarl. Mwantyelhelenh-arey! Nthakenh tyel-angkey-angenh ingwer-rnemew.

## Anantherr ingkerrel iterl-arey-angenh, nthakenh arlta ingwerelan irreyenh

**13** Ingwer-rneman arrantherr angkem alakenh: “Ingwelarreng anantherr alhey, ilek-ilek akngemel apmer ingwer-warl arleng. Anantherrap aney-alhey apmer ikwer-itwew arleng-alenty, tharlemel-aynteyew ilek-ilek inaynteyew-then. Alakenhanap apwert akngerrarl mpwarem.”

**14** Nthakenh alakenh angkey-angenh! Apal arrantherr angkeyel. Nthakenh arrantherr iterl-arey-angenh, ingwepenharl irreyenhelan. Arrantherr kwert-apeny, wetharl rlwar anem-tangkwel, rap ywerrenhem-anem.

**15** Alakenh angkenty mwerrarl: “Akngey ilkelhentyan, ingwepenh anantherr iteth-anteyarl anem. Ra ilkelhentyan, kel anantherr alhem apmer ingwer-warl, tharlemel-aynteyew.”

**16** Nthakenh arrantherr nakelhemel ilkwarrpantey angkey-angenh. Alakenh angkentyan apmwel-ayetharl.

**17** Ingwerentyel apek iterl-areyel, alakenh aneyew arraty-angkwarr, rap alakenh anemer. Alakenh apek ra aney-angenh, Akngey-angkwarranap apmwel-ayetharl mpwarem.
