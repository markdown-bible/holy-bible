# Revelation 10

## Antyerl ingwerelan pip ataperr antwerrkeyel

**1** Atha arew antyerl alkenh ingwer-anem altyerr-angkwarr, irrwerl-theyarl atwarrenhey-alhew. Kwenpayel renh artew, arnkwel-apeny. Mpwelarrel akapwert-angkwarr amperelew. Inngerrarl itwern-apeny amparrkenh. Arlaw atherran rwa lherrm-apeny.

**2** Rap pip ataperr antwerrkenh. Ingwerentyel arrwekel-antey pip renh arlpimpelew. Rarl rtney-alhew ahern-itwew, ingkety arraty-ampeny alay-itwewarl, ayleng-ampeny ahern artwerrety-itwew.

**3** Rap atayel arlkenty iwew lion-apeny. Rarl arlkenty iwewel atha awew awely-awely angkenty-apeny. 7-inger ra angkew alakenh-anyem.

**4** Awely-awely angkenty-penh, ayeng arrkernelhew, pip ineyew, angka ikwerenh ingkwerneyew ikwer-warl. Ingwerentyarlap angkewan irrwerl-they, “Menty ayntey! Angka ingkwerney-angenh, awely-awelyarl angkew 7-inger. Ingwerenty iley-angenh!”

**5** Antyerl atyetyart rarl rtnenh alay-itwew ahern-itwew-then, rap irrwerlerrew itep arraty-angkwarr alker-warl-athek.

**6** Rap angkew, “Awelhenh-arey ingkerrenh-antey! Akngey ran intem-antey aleth aneyel! Rap mpwarew alker, ahern, alay-then ilek-ilek. Innga atha ileyel, rarl aweyel-wety. Nthakenh ra arlengew-alenty mpwelhey-angenh, arrangkw!

**7** Awank-akerr ra iterrew, “Nthakenh atha ilem ahern nhenhan, atharl mpwarewan?” Ranap akngerr iley-angenharl, rarl iterrenhan. Artwa angkakert-rnemant ilenhan ra. Aleyanap arrantherr ingkerrenhel iterl-areyenh, antyerl namp 7-elarl terampet ikwerenh angkelh-angkelheleyenhel.”

**8** Irrkaty wethan angkew atyeng arrpemarl irrwerl-they, “Alha antyerl atyetyart ikwer-warl, alayelarl rtneyel-warl ahernel-then. Pip weth ineyew, rarl antwerrkeyel, pip arlpimp renh.”

**9** Ayengap alhew antyerl ikwer-warl, pip ikwer ayerneyew. Rap angkew atyeng, “Nhanteyaw! Pip nhenh ina! Arlkwanem renh! Arrakert-itwew antemarn-apeny arwerl ankerr-penh-antey. Atnert akey-angkerarlap ran.”

**10** Athap pip renh inemel arlkwew-anem, rarl ilanthew-angkwarr. Ya, inngarl antemarn-apeny arrkernew. Atharl arlkwew-penh, atnert ayenh akew.

**11** Ingwerenty atyeng angkew, “Alha arrpemarl, angka Akngey-akert ileyew ingkerrenhew, rernemaw iterl-areyew, nthakenharl irreyenh ikwer-rnemew. Atyetyart ilkwernemew-then ila. Akngerr apmer arrpanenh-itwew ila; angka ingwerek-ingwer, irrpwerl-then, arlter-then, arrkay-then.”
