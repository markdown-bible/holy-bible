# Revelation 12

## Rtnwem ywarn arrkernelheyel,arelh anyent antwerrkeyew

**1** Ikwer-penhan ilengem ingwer-lkwer aremerrew tyak innga alker-itwew. Arelhanap ra, arelh anyent-anem alkerel anerl-anenty. Arnkwan ikwerenh itwern-apeny. Ra rtnew alkent-itwew. Akapwert-itwew ampwal mwekart-apeny mwerr-angker atyetyart-wenh antyarr 12-akert.

**2** Arelh irrepern ra arntetyarl awelhewarl artnenh.

**3** Athap arew, ilengem ingwer-lkwer aremerrew tyak innganem alker-itwew. Rtnwem ahelengkw-apeny rnter-rnter, ilkwa inngarlap ran, akapwert-rnem ampwal mwerr-angker 7 mwekart akely-apeny atyetyart-wenh-rnem-akert, rewely-rnem 10 arrpemarl.

**4** Rap kanty-anem akngelhenh ingweram-angkwarr, antyarr-rnem iwemel kantyel alker-they ahern-warl. Rap rtney-alhew arrwekel-ampeny arelh-itwew, ampa akely rakemel arlkweyew.

**5** Arelhel awey akely-anem atnyenew. Rtwemelanap nthakenh awey renh rakey-angenh. Ingwerentyelan renh inew anamerl-antey, akngeynemel Akngey-warl-athek chair ilkwa ikwerenh-warl-then. Renhanap Akngeyelarl rlwanew, aneyew atyetyart ilkwa ahern ingkerrew. Rarl ilkwerreyenhel, rap ingkerr arntarnt-areyenh arawerrng rlterrk-akertel.

**6** Arelh ran irrtyert-anterrew ahern arlpaw-ampeny-warl. Apmer ikwer-itwew Akngeyel alalewew ikwer arrwekel-antey, ra aynteyew. Akngey ikwer mpwelhenh, renh arntarnt-areyew. Ra arntarnt-arenh alkent 42-ew-atety.

**7** Ikwer-penhan irrwerl-areny-rnem atwerrenh-anem. Antyerl alkenh itnan Michael ra yerl-alhew antyerl ikwerenh-rnem-akert, rtnwem ilkwa renh atweyew antyerl apmwernem-then.

**8** Michael ikwerenh-rnemelan rtnwem ilkwa antengelemel atwew ikwerenh-rnem-then artewenty, renh-rnem irrwerl-they irrentemel arleng intem-antey.

**9** Ya, rernem rtnwem renh iwew kwenel-athek ahern-warl. Ran arrwekeleny weth, arrenty alkenh. Itna ingweran Satan. Ran irrtywalth, irrtywerl-amperney-angker ingkerr ahern-itwew, rernem apmwel-ayeth anaynteyew. Michael antyerl ikwerenh-rnem-akertel renh iwew kwenel-athek, antyerl apmwernem-then.

**10** Atha awew ingwerenty arlkew atayel irrwerl-they alakenh:
> “Akngeyan Atyetyart Ilkwa Ingkerrew!> > Arawerrng rlterrk-akertel ra anenhantherrenh itethelem ipmewety.> Rap arrernew Alerikw Jesus Christ atyetyart arrpemarl.> > Arrenty alkenh Satan rtnenh Akngey-itwel arrwekel-ampeny intem, ingwel itwernew,> ayntarrp-ilemel Akngey-wenh-rnem irrtywerremel.> > Michael antyerl ikwerenh-rnem-akertelan
> > renh irrentew intem-antey arawerrng rlterrk-akertel.


**11** > Akngey-wenh-rnem rernem arrenty Satan antengelew,> > Jesus Christ Akngey-wenh yep-yep akely ra katy ilwew ikwer-rnem,
> > arrnga thelemel arwerl iylpeyl-atherr-itwew.> Alakenh-anyem rernem arrenty Satan antengelew.> > Ingwer-rneman ilkelhenh, renh-rnem atweyew ipmew-antey.> Akngey-wenh-rnemelan angka innga ikwerenh ilayntenh,> > ingwer-rnem-wety atererrey-angenh, arrangkw.> Alakenh arrpemarl rernem arrenty Satan renh antengelew.


**12** > Alakenh-wety ayengan angkeyel arrewantherr irrwerl-areny, ‘Inkwerelhemel anaynteyew!’> > Arrantherran ahern-areny-rnem alay-areny-rnem, arrewantherr rlterrkarl aynteyenh.> Arrentyan atwarrew, aney-alpeyew arrewantherr-itwel!> > Aleyan ra ahelerreyel artewenty innga, rarl iterl-areyel,> arlta ra arrer-anem irreyel, Akngeyelarl ikwer arrngerteweyenh intem-antey.”


**13** Rtnwemelarl iterl-arew, Michael-elarl renh iwew ahern-warl, rap iterrew, “Ya, atha arelh weth alwerney-anem awey akely-akert.”

**14** Ingwerentyelanap arelh renh anthew aympelh atherr irrety ilkwepenh, raw arlkerelerreyew-awaty apmer ikwerenh-warl arlpaw-warl, Akngeyelarl ikwer alalewew arrwekel-antey. Apmer ikwer-itwew rtnwemel nthakenh renh antwerrkey-angenh, arrangkw. Akngeyelap arelh renh arntarnt-arenh alkent 42-ew-atety.

**15** Rtnweman ikwer arrkernelhenh arrngerteweyew, mwerr-antey alkerelerrewerr. Ra rlkwetherrew kwaty akngerr arrakert-they atnyerr-apeny, arelh renh akngeyew kwatyel.

**16** Ahernanap altywererrew, renh alpemeleyew. Kwaty rtnwemelarl rlkwetherrew, ra altywer ikwer-angkwarr thelhelew, arelh ra mwerr-antey alkerelerrew-anem.

**17** Rtnweman ahelerrew arelh ikwer artewenty innga, rap alhew, ampa ikwerenh-rnem atweyew, ampa aperlikw-rnem-then. Rerneman Akngey-wenh angkewarl awelheyel-rnem, intem-antey angka Jesus-akert ileyel-rnem ingwer-rnemew.

**18** Rtnwem ran rtnenh arlwerengel kwaty alay impalth-itwew.
