# Revelation 15

## Antyerl-rnem 7 apmwa anthey-angker-rnem

**1** Ikwer-theyan tyak ilkwa ingwer-lkwer-anem aremerrew alker-itwew. Athap apatemel itnyweneyelpew, antyerl-rnem 7 arew-anem apmwa anthey-angker-rnem. Akngeyan ahelerreyel innga apmwernemew, rap anthew arawerrng 7 antyerl ikwer-rnem, antheyew apmwernem akngerrew. Apmwarl ipmelheyelpeyenh-anem. Akngey ran ahelerrey-angenh-anem, arrangkw-anem.

**2** Athap arew glass-apeny-anem, ayntenharl arlperrty irlwart innga kwaty-apeny, alay ilkwapeny. Kwaty alay ra amparrkenh rwa amperl-anenty-apeny. Akngerr innga rtnenherrenh alay impalth-itwew, weth-rnem rlterrkarl anew-rnem arraylenty weth-angkwarr, kwaty alay-they tyerrelhewarl. Rernem renh antengelew, kwenerrey-angenh ikwer. Ampwal weth rapeny apwert-penharl mpwarew, ikwer rernem kwenerrey-angenh arrpemarl. Namp ikwerenh atnyeney-angenh rlwitwew itep-itwew-then, arrangkw, 666 wethey. Akngeyelan renh-rnem anthew ampwal ketay-apeny-rnem-anem.

**3** Rernemap aylelherrenh Moses-wenh arreyn weth, angkakert Akngey-wenh awank-akerrarl anenh. Arreyn ingweran yep-yep akely-wenh rernem aylelherrenh arrpemarl alakenh:
> “Akngey ngant anyent rlterrk arawerrng ilkwakert.
> Ngan Atyetyart Ilkwa Ingkerrenhew.
> Ilek-ilek mwerr-angker mpwaremel,
> intem-antey arraty-angkwarr mpwarem.
> Ngan atyetyart ilkwa aneyel apmer arrpanenhew!

**4** > Ingkerrenhel nakemer mwerr-angkwarr,
> itna ngkwenh mwerr antwemel.
> Ngant anyent mwerr-angker inngan.
> Ngkweng mwanty awelhem,
> ngkweng-warl-athek mwanty ilelhemel.
> Akngerr apmer arrpanenh-arenyel areyenh,
> ntwarl arraty-angkwarr mpwarem intem,
> rernemap arleng-they apeyarr-alheyenh,
> ngkweng-warl-athek kwenerreyew.”


**5** Ikwer-they atha irrwerl itnywenew arrpemarl. Akngey-wenh tyaty ilkwa rtnenh, arriwan altywerarl. Kwenan rtnenh kalikw impen Ingkart-wenh, Akngey rarl ikwer-itwew aneyel.

**6** Tyaty ilkwa ikwer-they antyerl weth-rnem 7 tyerrelhew, apmwa anthey-angker-rnem. Rernem arnkwa arlpenty-akert arlter innga. Arnelty-angkwarr ikwer-rnem-itwew pilt gold-penh amparrkew.

**7** Atnwenth 4 iteth weth-rnem rtnenh-antey. Anyentelan irrkwa akely-rnem 7 gold-penh anthew antyerl 7 weth-rnemew apmwa anthey-angker-rnemew. Akngeyelan ngkwarl wayn-apeny-anem thelew irrkwa 7 ikwer-rnem-warl. Alakenh ra ahel inngarl irrewan. Rarlap ran, intemarl aneyel, rap ahelerrem innga akngerr apmwernemew.

**8** Kwert ilkwa tyerrelhew tyaty ilkwa ikwer-they, Akngey rarl anenh arawerrng ilkwakert. Nthakenh ingwerentyan aylpey-angenh tyaty-warl kwert-wety, arrangkw-antey. Alerl-antey ingwer-ingwerant rernem aylpayntem. Antyerl 7 weth-rnem-tangkwel ipmelhem, apmwarl anthem-penh.
