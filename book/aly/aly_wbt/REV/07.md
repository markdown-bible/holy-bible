# Revelation 7

## Rernem akngerr Akngey-wenh-rnem-warl rlwangkwarr ingkwerneyel, 144,000

**1** Ikwer-penhan atha arew antyerl-rnem 4 altyerr-itwel, rernemap rtnenherrenh ahern nhenh-itwew. Ingweran rtnenh akngerrakw, ingweran altwerlarl, ingweran ayerrerarl, ingweran antekerrarl. Rernemap arrngert rtnenh awer-wety, awer pwewerr ahern-warl, kwaty alay-warl-then arwerl-rnem-warl-then.

**2** Akngeyan angkew, “Awelhenh-arey! Atha arrenhingkerrenh ileyel, alheyew ahern-then kwaty-then apmweleyew.” Antyerl ingwer-anem tyerrelhey-alhew akngerrakw-they, rap rwalep antwerrkenh. Rwalep ra Akngey iteth-wenharl. Antyerlan arlkew-anem antyerl 4 ingwer weth-rnemew,

**3** “Alerl-tangkwel arringkerr mpwelhenh-arey! Rlwetangkwel aningkerr warrkenh Akngey-wenh-rnem-warl ingkwerney. Ikwer-penhant arringkerr apmwelem ahern-then kwaty alay-then arwerl-rnem-thenan.”

**4** Ingwerentyel atyeng ilew, renh-rnem-warlarl ingkwerneyenh akngerr inngey, 144,000. Rernemanap artwamp Israel-wenh alerikw-rnem 12-penh, awank-akerrarl anenh.


**5** > Judah-wenh-rneman 12,000 anenh, renh-rnem-warlarl ingkwernew rlwangkwarr,
> Reuben-wenh-rneman 12,000 anenh,
> Gad-wenh-rneman 12,000 anenh,

**6** > Asher-wenh-rneman 12,000 anenh,
> Naphtali-wenh-rneman 12,000 anenh,
> Manasseh-wenh-rneman 12,000 anenh,

**7** > Simeon-wenh-rneman 12,000 anenh,
> Levi-wenh-rneman 12,000 anenh,
> Issachar-wenh-rneman 12,000 anenh,

**8** > Zebulun-wenh-rneman 12,000 anenh,
> Joseph-wenh-rneman 12,000 anenh,
> Benjamin-wenh-rneman 12,000 anenh.
Ya, renh-rnem-warl ingkerrenh alakenh ingkwerneyenh.

## Akngerr apmer arrpanenh-areny

**9** Ikwer-penhan athap arew, akngerr rtnenherrerl-alhenty. Akngerr innga. Nthekwern count-emeley-angenh, arrangkw. Apmer arrpanenh-areny; angka ingwerek-ingwer, irrpwerl-then arlter-then arrkay-then. Rernemap rtnenherrenh arrwekel-ampeny chair ilkwa atyetyart-wenh-itwew, arrwekel-ampeny chair yep-yep akely-itwew-then. Rernem arnkwa arlter-akert, waly-rnem antwerrkemel.

**10** Rernemap aylelherrenh artewentyel alakenh-anyem:
> “Akngeyay! Ngant anyent aneyel chair ilkwa atyetyart-wenh-itwewan!
> Ntwethenant yep-yep akelyel-thenant mpwelak anenhantherrenh itethelem ipmewetyan!”

**11** Ampwa alkenh 24 weth-rnem-then atnwenth iteth 4 weth-rnem rernem rtnenherrenh chair arrerenty. Antyerl ingkerrenhan rtnenherrenh ikwer-itwew arrpemarl, ampwernem-then atnwenth-rnem-then amperelelhemel. Rernemap kwenerrew-anem chair ilkwa atyetyart-wenh arrwekel-ampenyel, atnertel-ayeth ayntemel, nakemel Akngey alakenh:

**12** > “Ya, Akngey anewantherrenh mwerr-angker! Ingkerrel anantherr renh nakemer!
> Ra ingkerrenh iterl-areyel! Ra arawerrng rlterrk-akert!
> Ra ilek-ilek mwerr-angker mpwarew anewantherr ingkerrew!
> Ingkerrenhel renh mwerr antwerl-anemer intem-antey aleth! Ya, arraty alakenh-anyem!”

**13** Ampwa alkenh anyentel ayenh ayernew, “Arlter-akert yanh-rneman, ntwan apek renh-rnem iterl-areyel? Nthenh-areny-rnem rerneman?”

**14** Atha tyernew, “Atha iterl-arey-angenh. Ntwant iterl-areyel.” Ra angkew, “Ingwer-rnemelan rlterrk mpwarenh ikwer-rnem, atwemel renh-rnem, artewentyel pwarrk anthemel. Alakenhaymarl ayntenh-angkwarr, rerneman Akngey-wety angkeperrey-angenh-antey. Rernem iterl-arenh-antey, yep-yep akely Akngey-wenh katy ilwewarl ikwer-rnem, arrnga thelemel. Rernem apmwangkwarr-tangkwel anenh, rernemap awelhew-anem yep-yep akely ikwer. Arrnga ikwerenhel ra apmwa ingkerrenh irrarlkew ikwer-rnem-wety. Arnkwerneman mwerr-angker arlter-rnem ikwer-rnem-wenh, renh-rnemarl irrarlkew arrnga ikwerenhel.

**15** Alakenhaym-wety:> Rernem rtnenherreyel arrwekel-ampeny chair ilkwa Akngey-wenh-itwel,> > warrkerremel ikwer arltel-then kwenyel-then tyaty ilkwa ikwerenh-itwew.> Ya, Akngeyan aneyel chair ilkwa ikwer-itwel, arntarnt-aremel ikwerenh-rnem, iwerl-alhey-angenh.

**16** > Akngey-wenh yep-yep akely ra rtneyel chair ilkwa arrerenty.> > Artwa yep-yepew-artey-apenyarlap ran.> Ra renh-rnem arntarnt-areyel,> > ayteng-akngemel kwaty mwerr-angkerarl anterreyelarl-angketyarr-angkwarr.> Kwaty renharl antywem-penh, itetharlap aleth-antey aneman.> > Angayakw irrey-angenh, angkethakw irrey-angenh-anem, arrangkw-anem.> Itwernel ampey-angenh-anem, ampepenyel rwenperrey-angenh.
> Akngeyel rantey-anem irrarlkeyenh anngerneman.> > Rernem arrangkey-angenhan anaynteyew intem-antey.”
