# Revelation 20

## Arrenty ilkwa renhap arertneyelaherrety 1,000-ew-atety

**1** Ikwer-penhan atha altyerr-angkwarr arew antyerl anyent, irrwerl-theyarl kwenel-athek atwarrenty. Rap key antwerrkew irrka intwer ikwer. Itep ingwerel ra tyeyn awerr arlpenty antwerrkew.

**2** Athap arew-anem rtnwem arrwekeleny weth. Ran arrenty atyetyart itnan Satan. Antyerlel renh antwerrkew, renhap arrertnew tyeyn wethel. Antyerlel Satan renh arerteyenh arlengew-alenty, aherrety 1,000-ew-atety.

**3** Antyerl rap rtnwem-anem iwew irrka intwer ikwer-warl, akentemel key-akertel artewenty inngey. Akententy nthakenh ingwerentyel altywereley-angenh aherrety 1,000-ew-atety, rtnwem ra tyerrelhewerr, ra apmer arrpanenh-areny-rnem irrtywerremel ilayntewerr arrpemarl. Irrkitwew renh antwerrkenty-penh aherrety 1,000-ew-atety, antyerlel ra renh tyarleweyenh-anem alerl-antey.

**4** Ikwer-penhan atha chair ilkwa mwerr-angker-rnem akngerr arew. Chair ikwer-rnem-itwew akngerr anew-anenh. Akngeyel arrwekel-antey ikwer-rnem anthew arawerrng ilkwa, ingwer-rnem court-emelemel ayerneyew. Rernemarl iteth anenhel ahern-itwew, rernemap alhewer alhenh, angka Jesus-akert ilerl-apemel, angka Akngey-wenh-then awelhemel. Rernemap nthakenh kwenerrey-angenh arraylenty ikwer. Rernem kwenerrey-angenh ampwal ikwer-apeny mpwarewewarl. Nthakenh renh-rnem amperney-angenh rlwangkwarr itep-angkwarr-then. Ingwer-rneman arlkarerrenh ikwer-rnem-wety, angka ikwer-rnem-wenh awelhey-angenh, rernemap renh-rnem atwew ipmew-antey, akapwert akerl-alhemel. Akngeyelanap renh-rnem itetheleynew, rernemap ilkwa aney-alpew Jesus Christ-itwel aherrety 1,000-ew-atety.

**5** Nhenh-rnem-mernt itetherrew. Ingwer-rneman awenng-itwewarl aynterl-aneyenh-antey aherrety 1,000-ew-atety. Akngeyelap renh-rnem itetheleyenh-anem.

**6** Arrwekeleny weth-rneman ilwewarl-penh-rneman rernem arraty inngarl aneyel, iteth-anem, rernemap irrngerney innga. Nthakenh rernem ilwey-angenh arrpemarl. Nthakenh ingwer-rnemel renh-rnem iwey-angenh rwa ilkwewarl, arrangkw. Rernemap warrkerrerl-aneyenh Akngeyew Jesus Christ-ew-then aherrety 1,000-ew-atety, atyetyart-rnem anemel Jesus-itwel.

## Arrenty Satan renhap iweyel rwa ilkwewarl

**7** Jesus Christ ra atyetyart anerl-anenty-penh aherrety 1,000-ew-atety, antyerl rap arrenty Satan renh irrka intwer ikwer-they tyarleweyenh.

**8** Rap irrkethey tyerrelheyenh, apmer arrpanenh-warl ingwer-rnem-anem irrtywerremel ilerlap-aynteyew ahern nhenh-itwew. Apmer yanh-rnemel itna atherr-akert, Gog-then Magog-akert-then. Arrenty Satan-el renh-rnem akarreleyenh ingkerrenh-antey artewenty atwerreyew, artwa army-areny akngerr inngey, alperr-apeny.

**9** Rernemap alhew iwerr arrpanenh-angkwarr ahern nhenh-itwew, apmer Akngey-wenh-rnem-itwew aremerrey-alhemel, tawen ikwer-rnem-wenh-itwew-then. Apmer yanhewan Akngey artewenty ilkelheyel. Artwa army-areny-rnemap amperelew Akngey-wenh-rnem, rernemap tawen ikwer-rnem-wenh-then amperelew, atwerreyew ikwer-rnem-itwew. Akngeyelanap renh-rnem artwerelew, rap rwa thwenew irrwerl-they, renh-rnem ywerrelheleyew.

**10** Antyerlel arrenty Satan iwew rwa ilkwewarl, kwert ikwangarl intenh-warl. Rwa irrkaty-warl arraylenty-thenarl artwa angkakert-then renh-atherrenhan iwew arrwekel-antey. Artwa angkakert wetharl irrtywerremel ilenh. Rernemap artewenty inngey kwarnelh-aynteyenh, arrangk-aynteyenh arlta arrpanenhel itwernew ankwarengel-then aleth-antey.

## Akngeyelarl ingkerrenh-anteyayernerl-aneyenh-akert

**11** Ikwer-penhan atha altyerr-angkwarr arew chair mwerr-angker ilkwa atyetyart-wenh, ntwarlkerarlap ran. Akngey rarl anenh chair ikwer-itwew. Ikwer-wety ahern-then alker-then anterrew, ywerrenhemel aleth-antey.

**12** Athap ingkerrenh-antey arew-anem, arrwekel-antey ilwewarl ahern-itwew; ilkwernem-then, akely-rnem-then, atyetyart-rnem-then warrkenh-rnem-then-akert. Rernemap itetherrenhey-alpew-anem. Ingwer-rneman ilwew kwaty alay-itwew arrwekel-antey, ingwer-rneman awenng-itwewarl ayntenharl, ingwer-rneman ayntenh apmer itnan Ipmernem-angketyarr-itwewarl. Rernemap rtnenherrenh arrwekel-ampeny-itwew chair ilkwitwew. Pwek akngerr ayntenh ikwer-itwew. Pwek ingwer-rneman akentewarlarl, rernemanap angka akngerr-akertarl ilew, rernemarl anenh-akertarl, ahern nhenh-itwew. Akngeyelap pwek ingwer-anemarl akentewew. Pwek ranap impen ikwerenh, itna ikwerenh-rnem-wenh-akertantarl. Rerneman iteth intemarl anerl-aneyenh. Pwek-rnem arenty-penh Akngeyel ingkerrenh ayernew court-emelemel. Rernemap ingkerrenh-antey arrwekel-itwew rtney-alhew chair ilkwitwew. Akngeyel renh-rnem-anem ayernew anyentarl-anyentarl, “Nthakenh ngan anenh ahern nhenh-itwew?”

**14** Akngeyelap itna akngerr ingwer-rneman arey-angenh pwek impen ikwerenh-itwew. Renh-rnem ra iwew rwa ilkwewarl-anem. Alakenh-anyem rernem atherr-inger ilwew. Arrkwekel-antey rernem kel ilwew ahern-itwew. Ra apmer itna Ipmernem-angketyarr iwew rwa ilkwewarl. Rap apmer ikwerew-artey-then iwew rwewarl, ranap itna Ipma.
