# Revelation 5

## Pip ataperrelewarl yep-yep akely-then

**1** Ikwer-penhan atha altyerr-itwew-anem arew, irrkaty wetharl anenh chair ilkwa mwerr-angker ikwer-itwew. Itep arraty-ampeny-itwelan ra atnyenenh pip arlpenty ataperrelewarl. Pip ikwer-itwelan angkarl ayntenh arrwekel-ampeny-itwel ingkern-ampeny-itwel-then. Nthekwern arey-angenh, ankerel-apeny 7-arl akentemel rlterrkelew, nthekwern renh arlpimpeley-angenh.

**2** Athap antyerl alkenh arew, rarl angkenh atayel alakenh: “Angwenharl ran, Akngeyelarl renh rlwanew, anker-apeny-rnem akeyew, pip nhenh arlpimpelemel akenteweyewan? Artwa mwerrelant akentewey-angker!”

**3** Ingkerrenh ahern-areny-rnem-then, ahernel kweneng-areny-rnem-then, irrwerl-areny-rnem-then rernem ywarn iterremel ingwerentyew nthwenh, pip renh akenteweyew, rernem areyew.

**4** Atharl arew-penhan, ayengap artnew artewentyel-anem. Rerneman nthwew ywarn-antey artwa mwerrew, ra akenteweyew pip renh, rernem areyew.

**5** Ampwa alkenh anyent atyeng angkew, “Artney-angenh! Nhak araw! Yanharl arawerrng ilkwakertan, ran Layen Judah-wenharl. Arrengikwan ikwerenh David-arl. Ran ingkerrenh antengelew. Rant anyentel akentewey-angker.”

**6** Ikwer-theyan atha arew yep-yep akely, rarl rtnenh chair ilkwa mwerr-angker arrerenty. Atnwenth iteth 4 weth-rnem, 24 alkenh weth-rnem-then rernem rtnenherrenh, amperelemel yep-yep akely renh. Athap arew yep-yep renh, ayengap iterrew, “Kwerrm yanh-rnem-anem ara! Renhan ipmew-antey atwewarl apekarl, rap itetherrenhey-alpew-anem.” Ra atnyenew rewely 7 annga 7-then. Anngernemanap inweng 7 Akngey-wenh-rnemarl, renh-rnemarl thwenew apmer arrpanenh-warl ahern-warl.

**7** Chair ilkwarenyel pip renh-antey antwerrkenh itep arraty-angkwarrel. Yep-yep akely ran arrer-apeny-anem alhew, pip-anem inemel ikwer-they.

**8** Atnwenth iteth-rnem 4 ampwa alkenh 24-then rernem kwenerrew yep-yep akely ikwerel, atnertel-ayeth ayntemel. Rernemap antwerrkenh ampwal ketay-apeny, irrkwethen gold-penh. Irrkwernem-they kwert mwerr-angker itntenh Akngey-warl-ilwengk, renh irrngerney-anthaynteyew. Ikwerenh-rnemarl angkem ikwer, ran irrngerney irremarl, kwert mwerr-angker ikwerarl ra irrngerney irrem-apeny.

**9** Rernemap song ngwampeny aylelherrenh yep-yep akely ikwer-warl-ilwengk, renh nakemel:
> “Ngant anyent mwerrarl. Ntwant anyentel iney-angker pip renh akentewemel!
> Nga katy ilwew, arrnga thelemel ahern-areny-rnemew.
> Alakenh-anyem ntwa akngerr payemelemel angkepelew apmer arrpanenh-they, angka ingkerrenhew, irrpwerl-thenew, arlter-thenew, arrkay-rnemew-then.
> Ntwap renh-rnem angkepelew Akngey-warl-athek, rernem anaynteyew ikwerenh-angkwarr.

**10** > Aleyan Akngey alkenh ikwer-rnemew. Rerneman irrngerneyarl aneyel, ikwer warrkerremel.
> Rernemap alkenh-anem aneyenh intem-antey, arntarnt-aremel apmer arrpanenhew.”


**11** Athap arew chair ilkwa renh arrpemarl. Atnwenth iteth 4 weth-rnem-then alkenh 24 weth-rnem-then rernem rtnenherrenh-antey. Antyerl-rneman rtnenherrenh, renh-rnem amperelelhemel, akngerr inngel. Nthekwern count-emeley-angenh, arrangkw.

**12** Rerneman atayel aylelherrenh alakenh-anyem:
> “Yep-yep akely renh nakaynteyew! Renharl ipmew-antey atwew, ranap mwerr-angker inngarl!
> Alkenharlap ran, arawerrng ilkwakert, alkenh inngarl aneyel apmer arrpanenhew!
> Ran rlterrk, akalty, arraty inngarl iterreyel, ingkerrenh iterl-areyel!”


**13** Ikwer-penhan atha awew, ingkerrenharl aylelherrenh. Ingkerrenh ahern-areny-rnem-then, ahernel kweneng-areny-rnem-then, kwaty-areny-rnem-then, alker-areny-rnem-then rernem aylelherrenh atayel alakenh-anyem:
> “Ingkerrenhelan renh nakeyew, chair mwerr-angker-itwelarl aneyel, yep-yep akely renh-then nakemel!
> Ratherranap mwerr, arawerrng ilkwakert. Alkenh inngarl ratherr aneyel apmer arrpanenhew!
> Anantherr kwenerr-aynteyew ikwer-atherr-warl-ilwengk, aleth aylelherremel, renh-atherr nakemel, ipmelhey-angenh!”


**14** Atnwenth iteth 4 weth-rnem, chair arrerentyarl rtnenherrenh-rnem, rernem angkew, “Ya! Amen!” Alkenh 24 weth-rnem kwenerrew arrpemarl, atnertel-ayeth ayntemel.
