# Revelation 1

## Angka nhenhelan ileyel, ingwer-ingwer ahern nhenh-itwew aynteyenh

**1** Akngeyel angka anthew Jesus Christ-ew, ra ileyew ikwerenh-rnemew, alerl-antey ingwer-ingwer alakenharl aynteyenh-akert. Rap antyerl ikwerenh thwenew warrkenh ikwerenh-warl atyeng-warl John-warl. Antyerl ra ilek-ilek lwewarrernew atyeng.

**2** Atha nhenh ingkwerneyel, ilek-ilek atharl arew-penh. Angka nhenhan Akngey-wenh, Jesus-elarl lwewarrernew antyerl ikwer.

**3** Ingwerentyel angka nhenhan ilerl-anenty, Akngeyel renh arntarnt-arayntem. Ingwerentyan awelhenty mwanty iterremel, Akngeyel renh arntarnt-arayntem arrpemarl. Arlta arrer-anem irreyel.

## John-el angka nhenhan ingkwerneyel Akngey-wenh-rnemew

**4** Ya, alakenh atha pip nhenh ingkwerneyel Christian-rnem-warl, apmer Asia-ampeny-warl. Arrantherrarl aneyel apmer 7-itwel, arrantherrap Akngey-warl-athek akarrerrem apmelerr, renh nakeyew. Akngey ran arrwekeleny anenh, ahern nhenh arnwarney-angenhel-antey. Iteth ra aneyel, iteth ra anayntem intem-antey. Atha nhanyem katy ayerneyel arrewantherr, ra athamerremel arntarnt-araynteyew. Alakenh-angkwarran arrantherr mwerrant-arey anaynteyew. Inweng-rnem 7 Akngey arrerentyarl aneyel, rarl chair ikwa atyetyart-wenh-itwew. Renh-rnem atha ileyel, arrewantherr katy ayerneyew, Akngey ra athamerremel arrenhantherr arntarnt-araynteyew.

**5** Ya John-el atha Jesus Christ-then ayerneyel. Rant iley-angker Akngey-wenh-angkwarran. Atha renh ayerneyel arrewantherr, ra athamerremel arntarnt-araynteyew, arrantherraw mwerr-angkwarr anaynteyew. Jesus-tangkwel Akngeyel itethelew ipmepenh. Ahern nhenh-itwewan artwa alkenh-rnem apmer arrpanenh-areny aneyel. Jesus Christ alkenharl ingkerr-angkwarr ahern nhenh-itwew aneyel. Anantherr ingkerrenh intwemel-alhenh Akngey-wety. Jesus-anap aletharl ilkelhenh anewantherr. Ra katy ilwew, apmwa akngeyew anantherr-wety. Arrnga ikwerenh thelew, anenhantherrenh angkepeleyew Akngey-warl-ilwengk.

**6** Aleyan Jesus-el anenhantherrenh arrernew, warrkenh Akngey-wenh-rnem-anem anaynteyew. Nakeyn-aynteyew Jesus renh, rarl alkenh aneyel intem-antey! Ya, arratyarl yanh!

**7** Awelhenh-arey atyeng arrantherran! Ingwer-ingweran Jesus apey-alpeyenh ahern nhenh-warl. Arrantherrap renh areyenh, rarl apey-alpeyenh kwenpay-angkwarr. Ingkerrelan areyenh anngel. Weth-rnemel renharl atwew-rnemel-then areyenh arrpemarl. Ingkerrenh apmer arrpanenhel artnalhelerreyenh ikwer. Ya, arraty wenh! Alakenh-anyemarl aynteyenh!

**8** Akngey alakenh angkeyel, “Ayengan arrwekel anenh. Awank-akerr ayeng anenh, ahern nhenhan arnwarney-angenhel-antey. Aleyan ayeng iteth-anteyarl aneyel. Ahern nhenh-then alker nhenh-then aleth-antey ywerrenheyenharl, ayengan iteth intemarl anerl-aneyenh. Ayengan Atyetyart Ilkwa Ingkerrenhew aneyel.”

## John-el Jesus areyel altyerr-angkwarr

**9** Ayeng John, Jesus-wenh warrkenh arrantherr-apeny. Ra alkenharl aneyel warrkenh ikwerenh-rnemew. Ingwer-rnemel ayenh irrwerneyel arrenhantherrenh-apeny. Kel mwerrarl alakenh. Anantherr renh apent-ayntem angkeperrey-angenh, rarl anenhantherrenh rlterrkel-ayntem. Ayeng alhenh, angka mwerr-angker Jesus-akert atha ilerl-apenh. Rernem ayenh antwerrkew, thwenew-anem apmer nhenh-warl Patmos-warl. Apmer ahern akelyarlap ran, kwaty alay ampep-itwew, tyayl-apeny.

**10** Weth-antey Akngey-wenh arltel ayeng angkenh ikwer-warl-ilwengk. Ra Inweng ikwerenh thwenew arawerrng rlterrk-akert atyeng-warl. Athap awew ingwerenty-anem, artep-angkwarr angkey-alhenty. Rap atayel inngey angkey-alhew terampet-apeny.

**11** Rap atyeng angkew, “John-ay! Atha ngkweng ilek-ilek lwewarrerneyew. Pip ina. Ingkwerna ilek-ilek nhaym, atharl ngkweng lwewarrernem. Ntwap thwena Akngey-wenh-rnem-warl, apmer 7-areny-rnem-warl. Rernem Akngey-warl-athek akarrerrem apmelerr. Thwena Ephesus-areny-rnem-warl, Smyrna-areny-rnem-warl, Pergamum-areny-rnem-warl, Thyatira-areny-rnem-warl, Sardis-areny-rnem-warl, Philadelphia-areny-rnem-warl, Laodicea-areny-rnem-warl-akerr. Thwena ingkerrew-antey.”

**12** Athap artepang arew-anem. Atha areyew, angwenh atyeng angkeyelan. Athap arew-anem, ampwal-rnem 7 inngarl rtnenh, gold-penharl arnwarnew. Irrwerl-itwewan ikwer-rnem-itwew ayntenh irrkwernem anter olive-angketyarr, lherrmarl ampenh-akert.

**13** Ampep-itwewan rtnenh Artwa Inngarl Akngey-wenh-apeny, arnkwa arlpenty-akert ingketyew-atety. Arnelty-angkwarran pilt gold-el-apenyarl arrtyernenh.

**14** Altan arlterel kwenpay-apeny. Annga atherran lherrmel-apenyarl arrtyernenh.

**15** Ingkety atherran ampenh, ayen rwenp rwethey tyerrenty-apeny. Atayel ra angkenhan kwaty atnyerr anterrenty-apeny.

**16** Rap antyarr 7 antwerrkenh itep arraty-ampenyel. Arrakert-theyan nayp arlpenty-apeny tyerrelhenharl, arreylp atherr-angkwarrarl. Inngerrelan arrtyernenh ingkeny arlta ampepel-apeny.

**17** Atharl artepang aremel renh arew, ayengap kwenerremel aytnew ipmapeny. Itep arraty-ampenyel-anem ayenh arnew, rap angkew, “Atererrey-angenh. Ayengan arrwekel anenh. Awank-akerran ayeng anenh, ahern nhenhan arnwarney-angenhel-antey. Ahern nhenh-then alker nhenh-then ywerrenheyenharl, ayengan iteth intemarl anerl-aneyenh.

**18** Ayengan iteth aneyel! Ayeng ilwew-tangkwel, aleyan ayeng intem-antey iteth-anemarl. Ingwer-rnem atha itetheleman, ingwer-rneman atha ipmemarl, rernem aynteyew ipmernem-itwelarl.

**19** Ya, John, ingkwerna ingkerrenh ilek-ilek, ntwarl arew altyerr-angkwarr. Ingkwerna, ilek-ilek arlta nhenhelarl ntwa arew. Ingkwerna, ingkerrenh nhanyem atha ngkwengarl lwewarrernew, ingwer-ingwerarl aynteyenh.

**20** Ntwa areyel ampwal-rnem 7, gold-penharl arnwarnew-rnem? Atha anayntelem ngkweng, ntwa iterl-araynteyew. Rernem Akngey-wenh-rnem ileyel, rernemarl akarrerreyel apmer 7-itwew. Ilekarlap antyarr 7 nhenh-rnem, atharl antwerrkeyel itep arraty-ampenyel? Athap ngkweng anayntelem arrpemarl, ntwa iterl-araynteyew. Rernem ileyel antyerl-rnem 7-arl, rernemarl Akngey-wenh-rnem arntarnt-areyel apmer 7 weth-rnem-itwew.”
