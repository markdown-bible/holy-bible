# Revelation 6

## Anker-apeny-rneman iylweyel-anem anyentarl-anyentarl

**1** Yep-yep akely wethel atnyenenh-antey pip ataperr renhan, anker-apeny 7-elarl antwerrkenh. Atharl altyerr arenh-angkwarr, rap anyent-tangkwel iylwew. Atnwenth iteth weth-rnem 4 anenh-antey. Anamerl-antey anyentan angkew, kwaty awely-awelyarl angkenhey-alpeyel-apeny, “Apetya!”

**2** Athap arew nantew arlter-anem. Ingwerentyarl anerl-alhenh artep-itwel pewenarr-akert. Ingwerentyel renh anthew ampwal mwekart-apeny atyetyart-wenh gold-penh, akapwert-warl arrerneyew. Artwa army-areny rlterrkarlap ran, rap ahern-warl-ilwengk anperl-alhew arawerrng rlterrk-akert, ingkerrenh atwemel antengeleyew, Akngey-wetyarl arlkarerreyel-rnem.

**3** Yep-yep akelyel iylwew anker-apeny ingwer-anem pip ikwer-they, namp 2-anem. Atha awew, atnwenth iteth namp 2-anem angkenty, “Apetya!”

**4** Athap arew nantew ingwer-anem, rnter-rnter rwapeny. Ingwerentyan angkew ikwer, rarl artep-itwel anerl-alhenhew, “Ahern yanh-warl alha! Ahern-areny-rnem ahel-antha, rernem atwerr-aynteyew ipmew-antey. Nthekwern rernem aney-angenh-anem mwerrant-arey, arrangkw.” Renhap ingwerentyel anthew nayp arlpenty-anem.

**5** Yep-yep akelyel iylwew anker-apeny ingwer-anem pip ikwer-they, namp 3-anem. Atha awew, atnwenth iteth namp 3-anem angkenty, “Apetya!” Athap arew nantew irrpwerl-anem. Ingwerentyarl anerl-alhenh artep-itwel, ampwal ntang weigh-emeleyew-arelh-akert.

**6** Athap awew, ingwerentyarl angkenty. Nthakenh renh arey-angenh. Ra apek atnwenth iteth 4 weth-rnem-itwew ampepewelew. Rap angkew, “Aleyan aherrety-anem anem, rlterrk inngarl ayntemel. Katen-rneman artwerrety anem. Ntang alyepant-anem aynteyel akngerrew, rernem arlkweyew. Kel ntang-then amern-then dear-anem irrem. Arwerl olive-angketyarr-then arwerl grapes-angketyarr-then mwerr-antey menty anayntem.”

**7** Yep-yep akelyel iylwew anker-apeny ingwer-anem pip ikwer-they, namp 4-anem. Atnwenth iteth namp 4-anem angkew, “Apetya!”

**8** Athap arew nantew arrkay-anem. Ingwerentyarl anerl-alhenh artep-itwel, itnan Ipmarl. Ingwerelan renh apentenh, itnan Ipmangketyarr-itwel. Ingwerentyan angkew ikwer-atherrew, “Atha arawerrng-anem mpwelewanth antheyel. Ahern-warl alhenh-atherr apmer arrpanenh-warl, atwerl-alpaynteyew ipmew-antey. Ingwer-rnemant atantherl-alpayntenh-atherr nayp arlpenty-akertel. Ingwer-rneman amern arlkwey-angenhel-ayntenh-atherr, rernemaw ilweyew-ayteng. Ingwer-rneman arntetyel-ayntenh-atherr, rernemaw ilweyew arrpemarl. Thwenenh-atherr atnwenth ilek-ilek ahelengkw-rnem ingwer-rnemewan, renh-rnem thnweyew ipmew-antey. Ingkerr-weny, ingwer-rneman mentyarl aneyew.”

**9** Yep-yep akelyel iylwew anker-apeny ingwer-anem pip ikwer-they, namp 5-anem. Altyerr-itwel atha arew inweng-rnem, itetharl anenh ahern-itwel arrwekel. Rernem angka Akngey-akert ilenh, ilek-ilek rarl mpwarew-akert. Ingwer-rnemelanap angka ikwer-wety arlkararrew, renh-rnem-anem atwew ipmew-antey. Rernem kweneng-apert anenh apwert ilperr-ilperr yanhel. Apwert ikwer-itwew rernem arrtyenh atnwenth ilek-ilek Akngey-warl-ilwengk.

**10** Rernemap arlkenh atayel, “Akngey, Ingkart nga alkenh anewantherr ingkerr-angkwarr! Ngant anyent mwerr, ngant arraty angkey-angker! Ilenger ntwa ingkerrenhan rnkerneyenh ayernemel? Ilenger ntwa atweyenh weth-rnem, anenhantherrenharl atwew-penhan ipmew-antey?”

**11** Ingwerentyelan renh-rnem anthew arnkwa arlter-rnem, rernem aylpeyew. Ra angkew, “Alerl mpwelhenh-arey. Atha ingwer-rneman rlwanew, ilweyew irrkaty-angkwarr. Rernemanap arrewantherr apelty. Rernem atyengew warrkerreyel arrpemarl. Ikwer-penhan atha renh-rnem court-emeleyenh, arrenhantherrenharl atwew-penh ipmew-antey.”

**12** Atha arew, yep-yep akelyel iylwew anker-apeny ingwer-anem pip ikwer-they, namp 6-anem. Ahernan artewentyel-anem akngelhew. Ingkenyan arrtyerney-angenh-anem irrew, atemwengk-anem irrew kwenyel-apeny-anem. Alkentan rnter-rnter-anem irrew arrngapeny.

**13** Antyarr-rneman aytnew-anem ahern-warl, amern aytnem-apeny arwerl-they rwarr ilkweng-arey.

**14** Alkeran ywerrerl-alhew-anem, pip ataperrelem-apeny. Apwert ilkwa ingkerrenhan akngelhenh, ingweram-angkwarr-anem aremerrenh apmer arrpanenh-itwew. Apmer ahern kwaty alay ampep-itwew ingkerrenhan rernem akngelhenh arrpemarl.

**15** Akngerran atererrew-anem, rernemap anterrerrenh. Ingkerrenh anterrenh: artwa alkenh-rnem apmer arrpanenh-areny-rnem, artwa ahelengkw alkenh-rnem, man akngerr-akert-rnem, kapment-areny-rnem, ingwer-rnemewarl warrkerreyel-rnem, amentarl warrkerreyel-rnem-akerr. Rernemap anterrerrenh, irrtyert-aylpeyew apwert-itwew intey-angketyarr-warl.

**16** Rernemap arlkenh apwert-rnemew alakenh: “Aytneyew anantherr-warl, irrtyert-akwerneyew weth-atherr-wety! Yanharl chair mwerr-angker ilkwitwew aneyel-wety irrtyert-akwerneyew, ra arewerr! Yep-yep akely-wety-then irrtyert-akwerneyew, rarl anewantherr ahelerreyel, atwewerr ipmew-antey!

**17** Arltan rlengk ayntem, Akngeyarl angkew, 'Aylak ingkerrenh court-emeleyenh, rwewarl iwemel.' Anantherr nthakenh arrngert rtney-angenh ikwer-atherr-wety.”
