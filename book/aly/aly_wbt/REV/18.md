# Revelation 18

## Tawen ilkwa Babylon arrangkw-anemarl

**1** Ikwer-theyan atha antyerl ingwer-anem arew altyerr-angkwarr, irrwerl-theyarl atwarrenhey-alpenh arawerrng rlterrk-akert. Ran artewenty amparrkenh, lyenenty arrtyernew apmer arrpanenh-itwew ahern-itwew.

**2** Ra atayel arlkenh alakenh:
> “Arrangkw-anem! Tawen ilkwa Babylon atakelhew!
> Apmwamwan-rnemant aneyel-anem yanh-itwew:
> inweng apmwerneman, arrenty-rnem,
> antyiper-rnem, thip irrpwerl-rnem-then.
> Moses-an angkew, ‘Yanh-lkwer arlkwey-angenh!’

**3** > Apmer yanh-areny-rnemel intem apmwel-ayeth mpwarenh.
> Rernemap ayntenh ingwer-rnem-itwew apmwangkwarr.
> Akngerr apmer arrpanenh-areny alhenh apmer yanh-warl,
> renh-rnem iwarrerneyew, apentemel apmwangkwarr.
> Atyetyart apmer arrpanenh-areny alhenh apmer yanh-warl,
> ilek-ilek apmwel-ayeth mpwareyew irrkaty-angkwarr.
> Ingwer-rneman apmer arrpanenh-areny tharlemeley-angker rernem alhenh apmer yanh-warl,
> ilek-ilek mwerr-angker tharlemeleyew ankety-rnemew,
> man akngerr inaynteyew ikwer-rnem-they.
> Ingkerrenh arwengk-arwengk irrenh ngkwarl antywenty-penh-apeny.”


**4** Atha awew ingwerentyak-anem, irrwerl-theyarl arlkenty alakenh:
> “Atyenh-rnemay! Awelhenh-arey atyeng!
> Iwerl-alhenh-arey apmer apmwa yanh!
> Anerl-aney-angenh apmwernem-itwew yanh.
> Apmwangkwarr iwarrerney-angenh,
> athan arrenhantherrenh atwewerr arrpemarl,
> irrkaty-angkwarr atha renh-rnem atweyenh-apeny artewenty.

**5** > Rernem intem-antey apmwel-ayeth mpwarem,
> apmwa weth mpwer tywenem-apeny alkerew-atety.
> Akngeyelan renh-rnem iterl-areyel kanper,
> rernemarl apmwel-ayeth mpwarem.

**6** > Rerneman arrenhantherrenh intem-antey irrwernenh.
> Atwenh rerneman arrenhantherrenh artewenty,
> artewenty inngarl renh-rnem-anemarl angkep-antey atweyew!
> Angkep atweyneyew artewenty innga apmwa yanh-rnem!

**7** > Rerneman ankety anenh, nakelhemel intem-antey.
> Apwert akngerr-akert, arnkwa akngerr-akert,
> ilek-ilek mwerr-angker-akert.
> Rernem angkenh, ‘Anantherr atyetyart aneyel akngerrew apmer arrpanenhel.
> Anantherr artnemel aney-angenh akertemwengk, arrangkw.’
> Kel arrantherran renh-rnem-anem awakelhemel akertemwengk amperrgeleyew!

**8** > Tangkemel rernem angkenty-penh Akngeyel renh-rnem anamerl-antey court-em-elemel artewenty atweyenh arawerrng ilkwa ikwerenh-akertel.
> Rernemap angayakwarl anerl-aneyenh akertemwengk,
> pwarrkerreyenh arntety ilkwakert,
> Rernemap ilwernerreyenh-anem.
> Akngeyelan rwa thweneyenh apmer Babylon-warl,
> rap amperl-aneyenh ywerreyew-atety.”Alakenhan antyerl ingwerentyak atyeng angkew.


**9** Tawen yanhan amperl-aneyenhel, kwert akngerr arlwa anerl-aneyenh ikwer-they. Ingkerrenhelan areyenh. Atyetyart apmer arrpanenh-areny-rnem rerneman apmer yanh-warl alhenh, ilek-ilek apmwa mpwareyew. Rernemap apmwangkwarr ayntenh ingwer-rnem-then apmer ikwer-areny-rnem-itwew – apwert akngerr-akert, arnkwa akngerr-akert, ilek-ilek mwerr-angker-akert. Rernemap kwert areyenh, artnemel artewenty arrangkeyenh apmer ikwerew, atyetyart yanh-rnem.

**10** Arleng-they rernem apmwa yanh arerl-aneyenh. Rernemap atererreyenh, nthakenh arrer-apeny alhey-angenh, Akngeyelarl renh-rnem arrtyewerr arrpemarl. Artewenty inngey rernem artneyenh alakenh angkemel:
> “Atyewey pwertay! Irrepern-rnem Babylon-areny-rnemay! Ywerrelheleyenh ingkerrenh-antey!
> Rernem anenh atyetyart-tangkwel, aleyan arrangkw-anemarl!
> Tawen ikwer-rnem-wenh ilkwa rlterrk anenh!
> Artwa army-areny akngerrelan tawen renh arntarnt-arenh!
> Aleyan Akngeyel anamerl-antey tawen renh ywerrelheleyenh-anem!”


**11** “Tharlemeley-angker-rnem rernem-then arrangkeyenh tawen ikwerew arrpemarl, iney-angker-rneman ingkerr-anemarl ipma aneyel. Nthakenh man iney-angenh-anem, arrangkw-anem.

**12** Rernemap apateyelpeyenh, ayernerr-aynteyenh-anem, ‘Angwenhel-aneman anewantherr-itwew gold, thilp, man ilek-ilek mwerr-angker-then ineyenh? Angwenhel-anem anewantherr-itwew ineyenhan arnkwa mwerr-angker, arwerl mwerr-angker intenty-then? Angwenhel-anem anewantherr-itwew ineyenhan ilek-ilek arwerl-penh, apwert-penh, ayen-penh-akerr?

**13** Angwenhel-anem anewantherr-itwew ineyenhan ilek-ilek mwerr intenty alyerremwey-then, ngkwarl wayn, anter olive, pelaw-akerr? Angwenhel-anem anewantherr-itwew ineyenhan pwelek-rnem, yep-yep-rnem, nantew-rnem, pakey-akerr? Angwenhel-anem anewantherr-itwew ineyenhan irrepern-rnem, anantherrarl pwerep-anthenh, arwa arrangkwew warrkerreyew? Nthakenh ingwerentyel ilek-ilek iney-angenh-anem anewantherr-itwew, arrangkw-anem!’ Alakenh iterr-aynteyenh tharlemeley-angker yanh-rnem.

**14** Rernemap angkeyenh alakenh, ‘Tawen Babylon-ay! Arrangkw-anem! Ngan apmer ilkwetangkwel anenh. Aleyanap ilek-ilek mwerr-angker ngkwenh-rnem arrangkw-anem. Nga ilek-ilekek ahenty anenh, rernemap arrangkw-arem. Man ngkwenh arrangkw-anem irrew. Nthakenh inenhey-alpey-angenh, arrangkw.’ Alakenh-anyem rernem angkerl-aneyenh.

**15** Rernemap man akngerr innga inenh, apmer ikwer-warlarl alhenh, ilek-ilek tharlemel-aynteyew. Arleng-they rernem arerl-aneyenh. Rernemap atererreyenh, nthakenh arrer-apeny alhey-angenh, Akngeyel renh-rnem arrtyewerr arrpemarl. Rernemap artneyenh artewenty inngey,

**16** alakenh angkemel:> “‘Pwertay! Irrepern Babylon areny-rnem! Arrangkw-anem ingkerrenh-antey!
> Tawen ikwer-rnem-wenh ilkwa inngarl anenh!
> Arnkwa arlkeny-arlkeny-warl rernem aylpenh!
> Arlkitwew ilek-ilek gold-penh arrernew apwert mwerr-angker-rnem-then!

**17** > Akngeyelanap ilek-ilek mwerr-angker anamerl-antey ywerrelhelew-anemarl!’“Akngerr ingwer-rneman kwaty alayel artarnpenhew rtnwangkwarr, man ineyew apmer arrpanenhel. Rernemap arleng-they arerl-aneyenh warrkenh ikwer-rnem-wenh-thenel.

**18** Kwert rernem arerl-aneyenh, arlwar anerl-aneyenh rwa ilkwa inngethey apmer ikwer-itwew. Rernemap angkerrerl-aneyenh alakenh, ‘Tawen ingwer-rneman tawen nhanyem-weny. Tawen nhenhan ilkwa ingwer-rnem-angkwarrarl.’

**19** Rernemap akapwert-warl ahern arrerneyenh, rernemarl amperrngerreyenh apmer yanh-areny-rnemew, artewenty inngey artneyenh alakenh angkemel:
> “‘Pwertay! Irrepern Babylon areny-rnem! Arrangkw-anem ingkerrenh-antey!
> Akngerr ingwer-rneman apmer ikwer-warl alhenh, kwaty alayel artarnpenhemel rtnwangkwarr,
> ilek-ilek tharlemeleyew, man inaynteyew tawen ikwer-areny-rnem-they.
> Aleyan Akngeyel tawen renh ywerrelheleyelpew-anemarl!’

**20** > Irrwerl-areny-rnem arrantherran inkwerelhenh-arey-anem! Apmer apmwa yanh arrangkw-anem!
> Inkwerelhenh-arey-anem Akngey-wenh-rnemay! Inkwerelhenh-arey-anem kwertengerl Jesus-wenh-rnemay! Inkwerelhenh-arey-anem artwa angkakert Akngey-wenh-rnemay!
> Babylon areny-rnemelan arrenhantherrenh atwenh, pwarrk ilkwa anthemel.
> Aleyanap Akngeyel renh-rnem angkep atwew irrkaty-angkwarr.”


**21** Ikwer-penhan antyerl ilkwel apwert ilkwa irrwerlelew, kwaty alay-warl-anem iwew. Rap angkew:
> “Athap apwert ilkwa iwew kwaty alay-warl, raw ywerrey-alhewarl.
> Kel alakenh Akngeyel tawen renh Babylon ahern-warl iweyenh,
> ran arrangkwerreyelpeyenh. Nthakenh arey-angenh-anem.

**22** > Angwenhel arreyn mwerr-angker aylelhey-angenh-anem apmer ikwer-itwew, arrangkw-anem.
> Angwenhel ketay-apeny inkwerelhemel pelayerrey-angenh-anemarl.
> Angwenhel terampet inkwerelhemel pelayerrey-angenh-anemarl.
> Arrangkw-anem, akeleyt-anem.
> Angwenhel ampwal mwerr-angker-rnem nthakenh mpwarey-angenh-anem ikwer-itwew.
> Angwenhel-anem ntang athey-angenh amernew, arrangkw-anem.

**23** > Tawen ikwer-itwew angwenhel-anem lyenenty arrtyey-angenh-anem.
> Artwernem-then arelh-rnem-then nthakenh itengerrey-angenh-anem.
> Apmer ikwer-areny-rnem tharlemeley-angker-rnem rernem tyak innga anenh.
> Ingwer-rnem apmer arrpanenh-areny-rnem renh-rnem nakenh.
> Ngangker-akert-rnemel ingwer-rnem irrtywerl-ampernenh apmer yanh-itwew.
> Akngerr ingwer-rneman apmer arrpanenh-areny-rnem awelhenh ikwer-rnemew.

**24** > Tawen Babylon-areny-rnemel ipmew renh-rnem atwenh artwa angkakert Akngey-wenh-rnem.
> Akngey-wenh-rnem ingkerrenh, akngerr ingwer-rnem-then rernem ipmew atwenh.
> Alakenh-anyem Akngeyel tawen Babylon ywerrelhelew-anem ikwer-areny-rnem-then.”
