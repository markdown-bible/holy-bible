# Luke 2

## Mary-el awey akely Jesus atnyeneyel

**1** John Baptise-emeley-angker atnyeneweng-arey, atyetyart anenh itnan Quirinius, ranap atyetyart apmer Syria-itwew. Artwa ilkwa ingweran apmer Rome-itwew anenh, itnan Atyetyart Augustus, ranap atyetyart ilkwarl apmer arrpanenhew. Ra ikwerenh-rnem ilanthew, apmer renh-rnem atnyenew-warl alherleweyew, itna pip-warl ingkwernelheyew. Aleyant alakenh ikwerenh-rnem itna pip-warl ingkwernelh-ayntew.

**3** Akngerr ingwerentyan apmer ikwer-rnem-wenh-warl alpew, renh-rnemarl atnyenew-ampeny-warl, itna pip-warl ingkwernelheyew.

**4** Joseph tawen Nazareth-itwew anenheng-arey, Galilee-ampeny. Ran tawen Bethlehem-areny, Judea-ampeny. Apmer irrkaty-itwew atyetyart David-arl awank-akerr anenh-itwew, Joseph-anap David-wenh-anteyarl anew. Joseph-an Nazareth-they Bethlehem-warl alpew, itna pip-warl ingkwernelheyew.

**5** Mary-an ikwerel-arleng alpew arrpemarl. Akely-penh Mary Joseph-ew anthew, ratherrap arrangkw-antey aynteyang-penh-weny-antey. Mary-anap Inweng Mwerr-angker-akert mpwelhenh-anem.

**6** Ratherrarl Bethlehem-warl arrernelhew, ratherrap apmerew-arrpantey nthwenh, wak pwelek-wenhel-anem aylpemel aynteyew. Wak ikwer-itwew Mary-el awey akely atnyenew, rap renh ataperrelew pantyel. Rap athenew ampwal atherrkew-arelh-warl, pwelek-rnemarl wanteyelew-arelh-warl.

## Antyerlel yep-yepew-artey-rnem ileyel, Jesus-arl atnyenew

**8** Ankwarengel artwa yep-yepew-artey-rnem Bethlehem arrerenty anenh. Rerneman apmelerrel-ayeth yep-yep-rnem arntarnt-arenh.

**9** Antyerl Ingkart-wenhan aremerrey-alhew, rtney-alhew ikwer-rnem-itwew. Ranap rlterrk Ingkart-wenh-akert, rap ingkenyel-apeny arrtyernew. Yep-yepew-artey-rneman atererrew ikwer-wety.

**10** Antyerl ikwer-rnem angkew, “Atererrey-angenh! Ayengan apetyew, angka mwerr-angker ileyew. Angka nhenhel arrenhantherrenh ingkerrenh irrngerney-antheyenh.

**11** Tawen Bethlehem-itwew, atyetyart David-arl anenh-itwew, ampa akely ngwamp atnyenew. Ran apmwa irrarlkey-angker, itnan Christ. Renharl Akngeyel ilew, rarl ahern nhenh-warl thweneyew, ranap atyetyart ingkerrenhew aneyenh. Nhanyem ran, Akngeyarl angkew, ra ahern nhenh-warl thweneyew.

**12** Atharl arrewantherr lwewarrernem, ampa akely renh areyew. Arrantherr alhenty Bethlehem-warl, ampa akely renh wak pwelek-wenh-itwew arey-alhenh-arey. Ra ataperrel aynteyel pantyel ampwal atherrkew-arelh-itwew, pwelek-rnemarl wanteyelew-arelh-itwew.” Alakenh-anyem antyerlan yep-yepew-artey-rnemew angkew.

**13** Antyerlarl angkew-penh, antyerl akngerr-anem aremerrew irrwerl-they apmer ikwer-itwew, rernemap Akngey alakenh nakew:

**14** > “Arrantherr ingkerrenhel Akngey irrwerl-areny mwerr-angker ilenh-arey! Akngeyan inkwerelhem arrewantherr. Mwerr-angkwarr aneyel-rnem rernem arlka mwerr-anem aneyenh!”

**15** Ikwer-penhan antyerl-rnem ywerrerl-alhew-anem, rernemap irrwerl-warl alpew-anem. Yep-yepew-artey-rnem angkerrew, “Mpa, alhey Bethlehem-warl, ampa nheng areyew, antyerlelarl anewantherr ilew.”

**16** Rernem anperlarr-alhew anamerl-antey, wak pwelek-wenh ikwer nthweyew. Wak ikwer-itwew rernem aylpey-alhew, Mary-then Joseph-then arey-alhew ampa irrngarl akely-akert. Ran ayntenh ampwal atherrkew-arelh-itwew.

**17** Rernem renh arew, rernemap aynikw-then amikw-then angka ilew, antyerlelarl ikwer-rnem iley-alhew.

**18** Yep-yepew-artey-rnemel angka ikwer-atherr ilew-penh, Mary intem-antey angka ikwer iterreynenh. Rernemap alpew-anem wak pwelek-wenh ikwer-they yep-yep-rnem-warl. Rernemarl alperlewenhel, rernem inkwerelhemel aylelheynenh Akngeyew alakenh, “Inngarl nhenhan! Anantherr ampa akely arey-alhew, antyerlelarl anewantherr ilew-angkwarr!” Ingwer-rnemel renh-rnem awew, inkwerelhemelarl aylelheynenh Akngeyew, rernemap apatew.

## Joseph-el-then Mary-el-then ampa akely tyaty ilkwewarl akngeyneyel, renh itneweyew Jesus

**21** Ampa akelyarl atnyenew-penh, arlta 7-penh, Joseph-then Mary-then Jerusalem-warl ikwer-akert alhew. Ratherr renh tyaty ilkwa Jew-rnem-wenh-warl akngeynew. Ratherrap renh Jew-rnem-wenh-angkwarr artweleyew, renh itnewemel Jesus, antyerlelarl Mary arrwekel-antey ilew.

**22** Alkent anyent-penh Joseph-then Mary-then Jesus-akert angkep alpew Jerusalem-warl, tyaty ilkwa ikwer-warl arrpemarl, Moses-elarl akalty-anthenh-angkwarr. Ratherr Jesus tyaty ilkwewarl akngeynew, Akngey-warl renh antheyew Moses-wenh-angkwarr.

**23** Moses-wenh-angkwarr alakenh aynteyel: “Awey arrwekeleny atnyenem, aynelikw-then amelikw-then renh Akngey-warl-tangkwel anthem.”

**24** Angka rlterrk ikwer-angkwarr arrpemarl, “Ampa atnyenem, amelikwan pwelker atherr Akngeyew anthem.” Alakenh-warl Joseph-then Mary-then tyaty ilkwewarl alhew, pwelker atherr antheyew, artwa rltwernemel katy arrtyeyew Akngey-warl-athek.

**25** Ikwereng-arey artwamp anyent Jerusalem-itwew anenh itnan Simeon. Ran mwerr-angker, ran angka rlterrk Akngey-wenh mwanty apentenh. Ran Akngeyew arternp mpwelhenh. Ra areyew, ikwerenh-rnem Israel-wenh-rnem renh-rnem tyarlewenty, ikwer-rnem-wenh-angkwarrarl anenh-wety.

**26** Inweng Mwerr-angker ikwer awank angkew, “Ngarl itetharl aneyel-awaty, ntwap inngarl artwa renhan areyenh, Akngeyelarl thwenew.”

**27** Arlta ikwerel-antey Joseph-then Mary-then Jesus-akert tyaty ilkwewarl alhew, Inweng Mwerr-angkerel artwamp Simeon ilew, tyaty ilkwewarl arrpemarl alheyew.

**28** Simeon-el ampa akely Jesus arey-alhew, rap renh iney-alhew. Rap renh antwerrkew-anem, angkemel irrwerl-warl Akngey-warl-athek alakenh:

**29** > “Ingkart ngan ilkwarl! Angka awankarl ntwa atyeng ilew, rap inngarl aleyan ayntem!
> Aleman ayeng mwerr-anem, ayengan mpwelheyel-anem ilweyew-athath.

**30** > Atha awey akely arew-anem, ntwarl anewantherr-warl thwenew.
> Rarl ilkwerreyenhelan, ra apmwa akngerr-wety irrarlkeyenh. Alakenh ngarl arrwekel-antey iterrew, renh thweneyew ingkerrew.

**32** > Ra lyenenty-apeny, rarl alethangew-rnem iwerr lwewarrerneyenh ngkweng-warl-athek.
> Ingkerrelan ngkwenh-rnem Israel-wenh-rnem mwerr antweyenh.”Alakenh-anyem Simeon angkenh Akngey-warl-athek.

**33** Aynikw-then amikw-then Jesus-wenh apateyelpew, ratherrarl awew, Simeon alakenh angkenh.

**34** Artwamp ra angka mwerr-angkwarr angkew ikwer-atherr, rap Mary-ew-anem angkew, “Akngeyelan ampengkw ngkwenh thwenew, raw Israel-wenh-rnem ingkerrew angkeyew. Ingwer-rnem awelheyelarl-rnem Akngey-itwel aney-alpeyenh. Awelhey-angenh ingwer-rneman, ingwer-angkwarr rwelarl aney-alpeyenh. Ingwer-rnemel renh-rnem angka Akngey-wenh ilerl-aneyenh. Awelhey-angenh-rneman arlkarerreyenh, rernemap apmwel-ayeth angkerl-aneyenh intem.

**35** Rernem irrtyertel apmwel-ayeth angkeyenh-rnem, iterrerl-anemel apmwel-ayeth rernem, kel ingwer-rnemelan iterl-areyenharl. Mary, ngethen arlkan apmwerrerl-alheyenh, ntwarl areyenh, ampengkw ngkwenh ilwenty.” Artwamp Simeon-elan alakenh-anyem Mary ilew.

**36** Jerusalem-itwew arelhampw innga anenh, itnan Anna. Ranap angkakert, angka Akngey-wenh iley-angker. Ranap alerikw Phanuel-wenh, Asher-wenh-antey anew. Aleyakank ra itengerrew, rap anenh mpwernikw-akert aherrety 7-ew-atety, rap inpert-anem irrew ingwer-ingwer.

**37** Rap inpert anenh, amperrel anenh aherrety 84-ew-atety. Arlta arrpanenh ra tyaty ilkwewarl alhenh, Akngey-warl-athek angkaynteyew. Itwernew-then ingwel-then ra angkaynteyew ikwer, rap amern arlkwey-angenh anenh, ra Akngeyew angkaynteyew-athath.

**38** Simeon Mary-ew angkenheng-arey, Anna-an aylpenhew arrpemarl tyaty ilkwewarl. Ingwer-rneman anenh arrpemarl. Rernem artwa ikwer mpwelhenh, Akngeyelarl thweneyenh. Raw Jerusalem-areny-rnem tyarleweyenh, ikwer-rnem-wenh-angkwarrarl anenh-wety. Anna-el ampa akely arew, ra inkwerelhemel Akngey nakew, rap ingkerrew-anem ilew angka Jesus-akert.

**39** Joseph-el-then Mary-el-then pwelker atherr Akngey-warl anthew-penh, ratherrap angkep alpew ampa akely-akert Nazareth-warl, Galilee-ampeny-warl.

**40** Awey Jesus rap ilkwerreynenh-anem, Akngeyew akaltyerremel. Akngeyan ikwer inkwerelhew.

## Jesus apmerrk-anem tyaty ilkwitwew aneyel

**41** Aherrety arrpanenhel Jew-rnem akarrerrem, kwenty arlkweyew. Rernemarl akarrerrem, amern ilperr-ilperr arlwarelheley-angenh arlkwew-arlkwem, aker yep-yep-then. Rernemap iylpangkem, antyerl Akngey-wenhelarl awey alkenh-rnem Egypt-areny-rnem-wenh atweynenh awank-akerr. Rap mentyarl arew awey alkenh-rnem Israel-wenh-rnem-wenh, renh-rnem ipmew atwey-angenh. Aynikw-then amikw-then Jesus-wenh apmelerr Jerusalem-warl alhenh. Jesus-an apmerrk-anem, 12-anem, ranap aleyant ikwer-atherrel-arleng alhew.

**43** Arlta irrpetyap-penh rerneman ingkerrenh ipmelhew, Nazareth-areny-rnem alperlewew-anem. Jesus-an Jerusalem-itwew artenyarl anenh, aynelikw-then amelikw-then iterl-arey-angenh, apalel ratherr alpenh.

**44** Ratherran iterrew, ra ikwerenh-rnemel-arleng alperl-anenty-arrpantey. Alpenh ratherr ingwel-anem irrew, ratherrap nthwenh-anem, ingwer-rnem ikwer ayernemel.

**45** Arrangkw. Ratherrap ikwer ywarew, ingwepenh-antey ingwer-ingweran angkep arrpemarl Jerusalem-warl alpew, ikwer nthweyew.

**46** Arlta atherr-penhelan ratherr renh arey-alhew-anem tyaty ilkwa Jew-rnem-wenh-itwew. Rarl artwa angka Moses-wenhew akalty-anthey-angker-rnemel-arleng ampepel anenh. Jesus rap ikwer-rnem awelhenh, renh-rnem ayernemel-anem.

**47** Rernemap apatew, rarl renh-rnem ametyew-arrernenh.

**48** Aynelikw-then amelikw-then Jesus-wenhel arey-alhew, ratherrap apatew arrpemarl ikweran. Amikw ikwerenh ikwer angkew, “Ampay! Ilekewarl nga alakenh-anyeman iterrew? Aylernanthap ngkweng ywarn iterrew, apmer arrpanenhel ngkweng nthwenhan!”

**49** Jesus-elan ayernew, “Ilekewarl mpwelanth atyeng nthwenh? Mpwelanthap iterl-aremer, Akngey atyenh-wenh apmerel ayeng anerl-aneyew!”

**50** Ratherran iterl-arey-angenh-antey, rarl ikwer-atherr angkewan.

**51** Jesus-an ilkwatherrel-arleng Nazareth-warl alpew-anem, rap intem-anem ikwer-atherr awelhenh. Amikwan angka ikwerenh-rnemew intem-antey iterreynenh.

**52** Rap ilkwerrew, arraty-angkwarr-anem akalty-anem ra anenh. Akngeyan ikwer inkwerelhew, ingwer-rnem-then ikwer ilkelhew.
