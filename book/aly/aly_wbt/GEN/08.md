# Genesis 8

**1** Akngey ra iterl-antey-areyel, iylpelerleweyang New-rnem-then atnwenth-rnem-then. Akngeyel awer tyarlewek. Kwatyarl akelyirreynek ahern ingkerrenh artwerretyiletyek. Kel alakenh-anyem Akngeyel awer thwenek.

**2** Akngeyel arrkngert arrernek, kwaty ahern-they thelhelenhek. Akngeyel kwaty apeynt ywerrelhilek. Akngeyel kwaty alker-areny ywerrelhilek arrpemarl.

**3** Arlta 150-penh kwaty akelyant-anem ahernel rtnenh. Alkent 5-ek-atwety kwaty ra akely-anem irreynenh.

**4** Kwatyarl atnyerr ywerrekel, rtnwa ilkwa ra Ararat apwertel-anem arrernelhey-alpek.

**5** Alkent 10-penh apwert ingkerrenhan akethel-anem arem-anemirrek. Alkent 10-ek-atwetyarl rtnek-penh kwaty akelyirrek.

**6** Alkent anyent-penh Noah-el iwerr tyet altywerilek, ra aretyek. Noah-el thip anyent irrpwerl angerl thwenek. Noah arntarnt-arelhenh thip ikwer ra apey-alpentyek. Ahern artwerretyirreyek-atwety thip alkerel-antey alhenh. Alkerel-antey ra amperelenh, kwaty-antey ra arenh.

**8** Kwaty ywerreynentyek Noah ra thwenek thip pwelker-anem, kwaty ywerreynentyek ra aretyek. Thip anyent ra arrernelhetyekan ikwer arrangkwarl.

**9** Kwatyantarl ayntek. Ra apey-alpek arrpemarl, rtnwa ilkwa ikwer-warl. Noah-el thip anyent renh anterrkwey-ayntek, kwen arrpemarl akwernemel rtnwa ilkwa ikwer-warl.

**10** Arlta 7-penh, Noah-el pwelker anyent renh arrpemarl thwenek.

**11** Thip ra apey-alpek atwerrp, alperr anyent-akert-anem. Noah ra arek kwatyarl ywerr-anemirrek.

**12** Arlta 7-penh pwelker renh thwenek arrpemarl, ra arlta ikwerel apey-alpeyang ywerrey-alhek.

**13** Noah-el iwerr altywerilek-anem, rtnwa ilkwa renh ahern artwerretyirrenty aretyek. Ra arek ahernan atwerrety-anem. Noah-el arek kwatyarl kel-anem ywerreyneyel.

**14** Alkent atherr-penh ahernan ingkerrenh-angkwarr artwerrety-anem.

**15** Akngeyel Noah renh ilek, “Ngan ngkwenh-rnem-akert tyerr-anemelha, rtnwa ilkwa ilenh-theyan.

**17** Atnwenth ilenh-rnem-then thip-rnem-then ahern alheyel-rnem-then ingkerrenh tyarlewa rernem akngerr arrpemarl irretyek ahern nhel.”

**18** Noah rernem-tangkwel atwarrek rtnwa ilkwa ikwer-they.

**19** Atnwenth ingwerek-ingwer ingkerrenh atwarrek rtnwa ilkwa ikwer-they, amentew-ament.

**20** Noah-el apwert ilperr-ilperr arrernek, aker ampernetyek. Noah-el tyenkerr INGKART renh anthek, kwenirremel angkek.

**21** Akngeyel aker intenek mwerr-angker ikwakert. Rernemarl apmwel-areth aneyelek, ampa akely-penh, atha ahern apmweley-angenh-anem. Akngeyel renh ilek, “Atha ahern-areny-rnem nthakenh atwey-angenh weth atha atwek-apeny, menty-anem. Anyent-inger-antey athan irrarlkek. Akely-penh apmwangkwarr alkenhirreyelan, awaretyel.

**22** Atha ahern-areny ingkerrenh atwey-angenh-anem. Ahern nhenh intem-antey aynteyenh. Rernem amern lyapelheletyek, amern akwetyetyek. Ahern nhenh ywerrey-angenh arltel, ingwel, aherretyel, alyangkwel ywerrey-angenh. Ahern nhenh aherretyel ywerrey-angenh, ahern nhenh ingwel ywerrey-angenh. Ahern nhenh intem aneyenh ahern nhenh ywerreyek-atwety.”
