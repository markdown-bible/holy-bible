# Genesis 39

## Joseph ra aneyel Potiphar-kenh warlel

**2** Joseph ra anenh Potiphar-kenh warlel.

**3** Potiphar ra iterl-arek INGKARTelarl Joseph renh arntarnt-arenh, INGKARTel renh ilek-ilek anthek ampwal-rnem.

**4** Potiphar-el renh arrernek ampwal ingkerrenh arntarnt-arayntetyek warl ingkerrenh, ranem Atyetyart-apeny anenh, Potiphar renh arrernek-angkwarr. Joseph rarl Potiphar-itwek warrkirrenh mwerr-angkwarr Potiphar renh INGKARTel arntarnt-arek arrpemarl. Ra ilek-ilek warrkirrenh Potiphar ikwer.

**6** Joseph-el ilek-ilek ingkerrenh arntarnt-arenh. Potiphar atnaratyirrek, amernekant iterrenh.

**7** Joseph inngerr mwerr anenh. Potiphar-kenh anew Joseph ikwer iterrenh, Joseph-ek iytenyirrek, ywarn-antey. Arelh ra renh ayernek, ikwer-itwek ayntetyek.

**8** Joseph menty angkek, “Kel, menty! Ayeng ilkelhey-angenh. Anew ngkwenh mwerr atyeng aneyel. Rarl ayenh arrernek, warl atha arntarnt-arayntetyek, warrkenh-rnem-then, ampwal-rnem-then. Nthakenh atha INGKART-angkwarr apmwel-areth mpwarey-angenh.”

**10** Arelh ra aleth-antey ayernenh renh, ikwer-itwek ayntetyek. Joseph ra arelh ikwer awelhey-angenh, arlkarirrenh.

**11** Arlta anyentel, Joseph ra alhek, warl Potiphar-kenh-warl warrkirretyek. Potiphar-kenh anewikwel arnkwethey-anem renh anterrkwemel ilek, “Nga atyeng-itwek ayntetyek.” Joseph ra warl-they anterrek. Arelh ra renh arnkwa aleth-antey anterrkwenh. Warrkenh ingwer-rneman warl kwen aney-angenh arrangkw, arelh rantarl anenh.

**13** Arelhel renh arek, rarl anterrenh, arnkwa ikwerenh iwerl-alhemel.

**14** Arelh ra warrkenh-rnemek arlkelhilek, “Artwa Hebrew yanh arenherr-arey, rarl anenhantherr kwantheyel. Ra apmwel-areth mpwaretyek atyeng arrkernelhek, ayeng ikwerety arlkekarl.

**15** Rarl ayenh awek, ayengarl arlkek, ra aketh-warl anterrek, arnkwa ikwerenh iwerl-alhemel kwen-itwek-antey.”

**16** Potiphar-kenh anewel arnkwa renh atnyenenh, Potiphar ra apey-alpetyek-atwety.

**17** Arelhel irrtywerremel ilek, “Artwa Hebrew warrkenh yanhan arrkernelhek, ayenh mpweny inetyek.

**18** Ayeng ingwer-rnemek arlkek. Artwa ra aketh-warl-anem anterrek, arnkwa ikwerenh iwerl-alhemel.”

**19** Potiphar ahelirrek-anem,

**20** Joseph renh tyayl-warl akwernek, Pharaoh-kenh artwa ingwer-rneman tyayl-itwek anenh-warl. Joseph ra tyayl kwenel anenh, INGKARTel renh aleth-antey arntarnt-arenh.

**21** INGKARTel Joseph renh arntarnt-arenh tyayl-itwek aleth-antey. Atyetyart tyayl-arenyel Joseph renh arntarnt-arenh arrpemarl.

**22** Atyetyart tyayl-areny Joseph ikwer Atyetyart-anem ra arrernek tyayl-areny ingwer-rnemek anayntetyek.

**23** INGKARTel Joseph renh arntarnt-arenharl, atyetyart tyayl-arenyel Joseph ikwer atnaratyirrek.
