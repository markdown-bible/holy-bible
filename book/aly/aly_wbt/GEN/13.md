# Genesis 13

## Abraham Lot ratherr amentant-atherr alheyel

**1** Abram Sarai atherr alhek apmer arlpaw Canaan-ampeny-warl, Lot ra ikwer-atherr-angkwarr alhek arrpemarl.

**2** Abram ampwal akngerr-akert alhek, pwelek-rnem-then, yep-yep-rnem-then, nanikwet-rnem-then, thilp-then, gold-then.

**3** Abram ra apmer arrpanenhel aneynenh, apmer arlpaw antekerr-ampeny-itwek. Ayerrer-anem ra alpek, Bethel tawen-warl. Kalikw-anem rap ampepek artey-alhek, apmer ikwerenh. Ayntey-alhek tawen Ai-they ampepel, Bethel tawen-they angath.

**4** Apey-alpek antekerr-they, ra aynteynek apwert ilperr-ilperr-akert ikwer-itwel, irrkatyel arrpemarl. Ra kalikw akely-anem artek. Ra kwenirremel Akngeyek-anem angkek inkwerelhemel.

**5** Lot-el akngerr arrpemarl atnyenek, yep-yep-rnem-then, nanikwet-rnem-then, pwelek-rnem-then, warrkenh ikwerenh-rnem-then.

**6** Apmeran akely inngarl ayntenh atnwenth-rnem-then ingwerek-rnem-then. Atherrkarl atnwenth ingkerrenhel arlkwenh. Abram-kenh warrkenh-rnem-then Lot-kenh warrkenh-rnem-then atnwenth arntarnt-arey-angker-rnem rernem apmer-they ahel-anem ilterrenh.

**8** Abram angkek Lot-ek, “Aylakan mwerrant-atherrarl anetyek. Aylekakenh-rneman mwerrant-arey arrpemarl anetyek, ilterrey-angenh. Aylak arrer nthakenh ilterrey-angenh. Artwa aylekakenh ilterrey-angenh anetyek.

**9** Ahern arlpawel nhan! Aylak amentant atherr alhey. Ngarl altwerl alhem-kety, ayengan akngerrakwarl alhey. Ngarl akngerrakw alhem, ayeng altwerlarl alhey.”

**10** Ikwer-they, Lot alhek arrwekel-antey. Kwaty akngerr ra arey-alhek Jordan Ilpay-itwek. Aleth-antey ra Ilpay atherrkeny aynteynenh ikwer-angkwarr apmer Zoar-ek-atwety.

**11** Jordan Ilpay ingkerrenh ikwer-tangkwel rlwanek akngerrakw-ampeny. Rarl akngerrakw alhek, Abram amentant atherr ratherr irrek, arlperrtyirremel.

**12** Abram anek apmer Canaan-ampenyel. Lot alhek tawen Sodom arrerenty, wak-rnem artey-alhek, artwa apmwernem arrerenty-itwek, rernem INGKARTek irrpelthirrenh.

**14** INGKARTel Abram renh ilek, “Apmer ingkerrenh-angkwarr ara!

**15** Atha ngenh anthey, nga intem-antey anayntetyek, apmer arrpanenh ilenhek, ngkwenh-rnem-akert.

**16** Atha ngkwenh-rnem akngerram-akngerrileyenh, arrantherr akngerr-anem ahern nhel anetyek. Ngkwenh-rnem akngerrirreyenh ahern akely akngerr aynteyel-apeny.

**17** Nga angkepelarreng apmer nhel alhaynta, atharl ngenh anthetyek.”

**18** Wak kalikw akely-rnem lhelherl-alhek akngeynemel apmer arwerl arnenty-akert ikwer-itwek aney-alhek apmer Mamre-arrangarr, Hebron-itwek. Apmer ikwer-itwek apwert ilperr-ilperr tyenkerrek-arelh ra arrernek, Akngey ikwer inkwerelhemel kwenirrek.
