# Genesis 35

## Benjamin awey akely atnyeneyel, Rachel ilweyel

**16** ... Jacob ikwerenh-rnem-akertel Bethel ikwer-they alpek. Rernem apmer Eprath-they arleng-antey alpenh. Rachel ra arntety awelhenh.

**17** Arelh warrkenh rarl Rachel renh arntarnt-arenh. Arelh ra Rachel renh ilek, “Ntwa awey atnyeneyel.”

**18** Rachel ra ilwetyek-anem irrek, ra awey renh itnewek Ben-Oni. Rachel kel ilwek. Aynelikw ikwerenh renh itnewek Benjamin. ...

## Isaac ilwek-anem

**27** Jacob ra alpek aynikw ikwerenh apmer-warl, apmer itnan Hebron, itna ingweran Mamre, Kerath Arba-they arrer.

**28** Aherrety 180 ra anenh. Isaac ra ilwek.

**29** Alerikw ikwerenhel atherr Esau-el, Jacob-el atherr renh apmer-itwek akwernek, rarl ilwek. ...
