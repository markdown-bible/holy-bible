# Genesis 41

## Pharaoh-el altyerr areyel

**1** Aherrety atherrel Joseph ra tyaylel anenh. Pharaoh ra altyerr arek. Akngey ra arekan, Nile ilpay impalthel ra rtnenh.

**2** Pwelek-rnem anter-akngerr 7-pel tyerrelhey-ayntek kwaty-they.

**3** Kel aremel pwelek 7-pel ngkwernant-anem kwaty ikwer-they-antey arrpemarl tyerrelhey-ayntek.

**4** Pwelek ngkwernant-rnemel pwelek anter-akngerr-rnem akwernek-anem. Pharaoh ra akemirrey-ayntek-anem, rarl altyerr arek-penhant.

**5** Ankwa arrpemarl aynteynek, altyerr ingwer-anemarl arek. Ra ntang ilkwernem arek.

**6** Ingwer-rneman arleny aynterrk-rnem.

**7** Arleny-anterrk-rnemel ntang ingwer-rnem kwernek. Pharaoh akemirrey-ayntek arrpemarl altyerr renh arek-penhan.

**8** Ingwepenh Pharaoh ra artewentyel iterrek. Artwa ingwerek-ingwer-anem yangkelhek, ra renh-rnem ayernetyek, rarl altyerr arek-penh. Ngangker-akert-rnemek ra yangkelhek. Ywarn-antey ra renh-rnem ayernek. Rernem ikwer arraty anaytiletyek-arrpantey, ywarn-antey rarl altyerr arek.

**9** Artwa warrkenh wayn anthey-angker ra. Ra angkek, “Ayeng iterl-arelhek-anem, atharl apmwel-areth mpwarek.

**10** Pharaoh aylernek ahelirrek, tyayl-warl akwernek. Aylernanth arey-alhek Hebrew anyent, tyayl kwen-itwek.

**11** Aylernanth atherrel-antey altyerr arekan. Hebrew rant arraty innga ilek, aylernantharl altyerr arekan.

**13** Ayeng warrk-warl alpek, amern amperney-angkeran atwekarl, Hebrew rarl ilek-angkwarr-antey. Arraty inngarl ra ilenhan, Hebrew ran.”

**14** Pharaoh ra Joseph ikwer yangkelhek, renh ra ayernetyek, rarl altyerr arek-penh. Artwernemel Joseph renh tyayl-they tyerrek. Ra althelhek, arnkwa mwerrel-anem aylpemel, Pharaoh ikwer-warl-anem alhek.

**15** Pharaoh ra ilelhek, “Atharl altyerr arekan, ingwer-rnemel atyeng anayntiley-angenh. Ngenhant atha awek, mwerr inngan, anayntiley-angkeran.”

**16** Joseph ikwer-warl ilelhek, “Athan iterl-arey-angenharl, Akngeyelant anayntilem.”

**17** Atyetyart ra ilelhek, “Ayeng ilpay-itwel rtnenh, atharl altyerr arek,

**18** pwelek anter-akngerr 7-pel kwaty-they tyerrelhey-ayntenty.

**19** Ikwer-penhan atha arek, pwelek ngkwernant-rnem kwaty ikwer-they-antey tyerrelhey-ayntenty arrpemarl.

**20** Pwelek ampwel-rnem pwelek anter-akngerr-rnem kwernek.

**21** Rernem arlkwek-penhan, rernem ngkwernant irrkaty-antey anenh. Ayeng akemirrenhey-alpek, anamerl-antey.”

**22** “Atharl altyerr arek ntang 7-pel mwerr-angker ilkwernem.

**23** Ikwer-penhan atha arek ntang arleny aynterrk-rnem-anem.

**24** Ntang arlenyel-rnem ntang mwerr-angker ilkwernem kwernek. Atha altyerr renh ilek ngangker-akert-rnem-warl, rernem atyeng arraty iletyek-arrpantey, rernem iterl-arey-angenh arrangkwarl.” Joseph-el ikwer anayntilek, “Altyerr yanh arek, ntwa arekan, irrkant-atherr aynteyel. Akngeyel ngkweng lwewarrerneyel, rarl alakenh-anyem mpwareyenh.

**26** Aherrety 7-pelant ntwa arlkweman, pwelek anter-akngerr arlkwem-apeny, alakenhaym ra aynteyelan. Alakenh-anyem ntwa ntang arleny 7-pel arlkwey-angenh, ntwa aherrety 7-pel arlengelan arlkwey-angenh arrpemarl. Aherrety 7-pelant ntwa arlkweman, ntang mwerr-angker ntwa arlkwem-apeny. Alakenh-anyem ra aynteyelan.

**27** Pwelek 7-pel ampwernem, aherrety 7-pel apmwernem-apeny arrpemarl, pwelek 7-pel anter-akngerr-rneman aherrety 7-pel mwerr-rnem-apeny, aherrety atherrk-angketyarr, atherrkeny. Nthakenh arlkwey-angenh arlenyelan. Alakenh-anyem ntwa pwelek ampwerneman arlkwey-angenh, ntwa aherrety 7-pel arlenyelan arlkwey-angenh arrpemarl. 7-pel aherretyel aherrety ilkwa aynteyenh amern lyapey-angenh.

**28** Akngeyel lwewarrernek altyerr-angkwarr rarl alakenh-anyem mpwareyenh.

**29** Aherrety mwerr-angker 7-pel aynteyenh apmer Egypt-el, amern-angketyarr.

**30** Aherrety apmwernem 7-pel aynteyenh Egypt ikwer-itwek-antey. Amernek ywarn iterreyel, amern arrangkw.

**31** Aherrety apmwel rernem angayakw irreyenh, amern-wenyel. Rernem aherrety mwerr-rnem ingkerrenh iylpelirreyenh.

**32** Akngeyel lwewarrernek, ntwa alakenh-anyem iterl-aretyek, rarl alakenh mpwareyenh.

**33** Artwa mwerr innga arrerney-areny Egypt-itwek Atyetyart ingkerrenhek anayntetyek.

**34** Artwa ingwer arrernem, rernem amern-rnem arntarnt-arayntetyek. Rernem ntang akngerr innga inetyek aherrety 7-pel apmwernem-kety, amern-angketyarr rernem arntarnt-aretyek, amern akngerr arlkwemel.

**35** Arlwarey-arlwarey akwernem warl ilkwewarl, rernem ikwer-penh arlkwayntetyek aherrety apmwernem 7-pelek-atwety, amern ingkerrenh ywerremel. Ingkerrenh-antey iteth anetyek-awaty amern ntang akngerr arlkwemel.

**36** Rernem amern renh arrernek aherrety 7-pel ikwerel, amern-weny irrentyek, amernek apatentyek.”

**37** Pharaoh ingwer-rnemek inkwerelhekan, ntang-rnem arrernetyek amern-weny irrentyek.

**38** Pharaoh angkek, “Warrkenh mwerr-angker anwantherr arrernem Joseph-arl, ran Akngey-kenh itnweng-akert.”

**39** Pharaoh-el Joseph renh ilek, “Akngeyel ngenh akaltyilek, ntwa alakenh-anyem mpwaretyek, ntwa ingkerrenh-angkwarr iterl-areyel ngan mwerr innga ingwer-rnem-theyan.

**40** Atha ngenh Atyetyart arrernem apmer ntwa arntarnt-arayntetyek. Ingkerrenh ngkweng awelhetyek. Ayengant Atyetyart ngkweng anayntem.”

## Pharaoh-el Joseph renh arrernek, atyetyart Egypt-areny-rnemek

**41** “Atha ngenh Atyetyart arrernem Egypt-itwek, Egypt-areny-rnem ingkerrenhek.”

**42** Egypt-areny Pharaoh ra ampwal iltyek-arelh gold-penh mpwarekarl iltyethey lhelhek, Joseph iltyewarl arrernek. Arnkwa mwerr-rnem-then anthek ra aylpetyek. Tyeyn itek-arelh gold-penh mpwarekarl Joseph-warl tyeyn arrernek.

**43** Joseph renh pakey mwerr-angker anthek, ra alhayntetyek ikwerel. Ingkerrenh ikwer arlkek, “Iwerr arnangelhenh-arey Joseph ikwer.” Alakenh-anyem Joseph ra Atyetyart anenh Egypt-areny ingkerrenhek.

**44** Pharaoh-el Joseph renh ilek, “Ayeng anyent Atyetyartan ingkerrenh ngkweng awelh-ayntetyek, ayengan atyetyart arrwekel, ngarl Atyetyart atyeng-theyan.”

**45** Ra Joseph renh itnewek Zapenath Paneah. Joseph ra anew inek, Potiphera-kenh alerikw, itnan ikwerenh Asenath.

**46** Joseph ra aherrety 30 anek, Pharaoh-el Atyetyart arrernek Egypt-areny ingkerrenh ra arntarnt-aretyek. Ra ingkerrenh-angkwarr arntarnt-arerl-apenh, warrk-itwek.

**47** Aherrety 7 ingweran anek, mwerr-angkerarl. Amern akngerr rernem arlkwenharl.

**48** Ntang ingkerrenhel ra inek-penhan ra renh-rnem warl ilkwewarl arlwarey-arlwarey arrernek, tawen ilkwernem-itwek, ntang renh-rnem ra arrernek ingkerrenh arlkwayntetyek.

**49** Joseph-el ntang arlwarey-arlwarey akngerr arrernek, nthakenh ingwer-rnemel count-emiley-angenh.

## Joseph-kenh alerikw atherr

**50** Arrwekel-antey amern ingkerrenh ywerrey-alenty, Joseph-then anew ikwerenh-then ratherr awey atherr ikwer-atherrenh anek.

**51** Arrwekeleny itnewek Manasseh, alakenh angkemel, “Akngeyelarl ayenh arntarnt-arek, ayeng iterrey-angenh arrangkw-anem, ayeng apmerek alharntey-angenh anem.”

**52** Awey ingweran itnewek Ephraim-arl alakenh angkemel, “Akngeyel ayenh ampa akngerr anthek, apmer ikwer-itwek rlterrk innga atyeng ayntenh-itwek.”

## Amern-weny rernem anenh

**53** Aherrety 7-pel mwerr-rneman ywerrenhek-anem.

**54** Aherrety 7-pel ingwer apmwernem ayntek, amern-weny-rnemarl, Joseph-el ilek-angkwarr-antey. Apmer ingkerrenh-itwek, amern lyapey-angenh ayntek. Apmer aparnpel. Amern akngerr-antey Egypt-itwek ayntenh. Egypt-itwek rernem angayakw-anem irrek, amern-wenyel.

**55** Rernem Atyetyart Pharaoh renh ayernenh amernek. Ra angkek, “Joseph ayerneyek alhenherr-arey, ra arrenhantherr amern anthetyek. Arrantherr ikwer awelheyek alhenherr-arey, rarl arrenhantherr ileman.”

**56** Ra amern-weny-anem irreynenh, ra apmwa innganem ayntek, amern-wenyel. Joseph-el warl ilkwa altywerilek, amern-anem Egypt-areny-rnem tharlemilek, rernemarl angayakw irrek.

**57** Apmer arrpanenh-they Egypt anyent ikwer-warl akarrirrek, amern-angketyarr-warl. Aherrety apmwa apmer arrpanenh-itwek-anem ayntek, amern-weny arrangkw, amern lyapey-angenh.
