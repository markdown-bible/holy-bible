# Genesis 23

## Sarah ilweyel

**1** Sarah aherrety 127 anenh, apmer Kiriath Arba ilwemel-antey ikwer-itwek-antey. Apmer itna ingweran Hebron, Canaan-itwek.

**3** Abraham rarl amperrngirrek ikwer. Ikwer-penh Abraham Hitt-kenh-rnem-warl alhek, ra renh-rnem ayerney-alhek,

**4** “Ayengan alethang. Nthenhek atha anew atyenh arrewaly akwernem? Apmer mwerr ngay ntwa akely atyenh anthem?”

**5** Rernem angkek, “Ngarl Atyetyart ilkwan intey nthenhek ngarl arlkwententyelan? Kel, ntwa intey yanhek anew ngkwenh arrewaly akwerna.”

**7** Abraham angkek, “Ephron ayernerr-arey intey renh parrik impalth-itwek tharlemiletyek. Atha man ilkwan anthem, arrantherr ayenh ingkerrel aytaretyek, atharl anthem.”

**10** Ephron apmerek-artwey, artwa Hitt-kenh apmer tern ilkwa arriw-itwek-antey anenh. Abraham-el apmer ikwer ayernek.

**11** Ephron angkek, “Kel, intey atha anthey, ingwerel-rneman ngenh aytaremel ntwarl atyeng-they intey ineman.”

**12** Abraham angkek, “Man ilkwa innga atha ngenh anthem, arraty-angkwarr.”

**14** Ephron ra ilek, “Menty! Thilp 400 ntwa payemeley-areny inteyekey! Arwa ngkweng aknga, man-weny. Atha ngenh menty anthek, man-weny-anem.”

**16** Abraham-el artwa Hitt-kenh 400 thilp anthek, artwa akngerrel aytarek-angkwarr. Alakenh Abraham-el apmer Ephron-kenh payemelek ikwerant-anem, ikwerenh-rnem-akert, intey-akert, arrewaly renh akwernetyek. Apmer ranap arwerl-angketyarr.

**19** Abraham-el anew ikwerenh intey ikwer-itwek akwernek, rarl payemelek-itwek, Hitt-kenh-rnem-itwek.
