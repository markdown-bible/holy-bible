# Colossians 1

**1** Atha Paul thwenek, Ingkartel ayenh arrernek angkakert Christ Jesus-kenh. Timothy-el-then, Ingkart-kenh-angkwarr apenteyel arrpemarl.

**2** Timothy aylanth pip nhenh thwenem arrekantherr-warl, Colossae tawenel aneyel-rnem-warl, iylpareth awelheyel-rnem-warl. Akngey anwekantherrenh Ingkartel arrenhantherrenh mwantyel arntarnt-arem, arrantherr mwerrant-arey anayntetyek.

**3** Anantherr arrekantherr aleth-antey ayerneyel Ingkart Jesus Christ. Ingkartek anwantherr inkwerelheyel, aynikw ikwerenhek.

**4** Anantherr kel awek, arrantherrarl Jesus Christ tnakeyel, arrantherr Ingkart-areny ingkerrenh arntarnt-areyel. Alakenh-anyem anwantherr Ingkartek angkem inkwerelhemel.

**5** Arrantherr Ingkart tnakeyel, rarl arrwekel ilek, arrantherr iteth intem-antey anayntetyek. Arrantherr angka mwerr-angker Ingkart-kenh awenh.

**6** Angka mwerr-angker Ingkart-kenh arlpimpirreyel apmer arrpanenh-warl. Rernem apmer arrpanenhek akalty-antheyel. Rernem tnakek inkwerelhemel. Alakenh-anyem arrantherr angka akwerrpel awenh arrpemarl, “Ingkart ingkerrenhek ilkelheyel” wenh.

**7** Artwa Epaprahas-el arrenhantherrenh akalty-anthenh alakenh, “Ingkart ingkerrenhek ilkelheyel” wenh. Ra Ingkart Jesus-angkwarr warrkerrenh, anwantherr warrkerreyel-apeny.

**8** Ranem arrenhantherrenh akalty-anthenh, Ingkart-kenh Itnwengel arrenhantherrenh akalty-anthenh arrpemarl, ingwer-rnem-itwek mwerrant-arey anayntetyek. Alakenh-they anwantherr angka awek-anem, arrekantherr-penh.

**9** Alakenh-anyem anwantherr Ingkart renh-anem ayernek. Anantherr aleth-antey ikwer angkeyel, arrantherr Ingkart-kenh-angkwarr arraty apentetyek, rarl iterreyel-angkwarr, Itnweng Rlwartel arrenhantherrenh akalty-anthetyek, arrantherr arraty iterretyek. Inweng-angkwarr akaltyirreyn-ayntetyek aleth-antey, ingwer-rnem arntarnt-arayntetyek mwerr-angkwarr. Arrantherr artwentyel innga akaltyirretyek angka ikwerenhek.

**11** Ingkartel arrenhantherrenh rlterrkam-rlterrkileyenh. Ingwerentyel arrenhantherrenh apmwewarlilekerr, arrantherr inkwerelhemel aleth apent-ayntetyek ikwerenh-angkwarrant mwerr-angkwarr-ayteng.

**12** Anantherr-anem Akngetyek inkwerelhetyek, rarl angka arrwekel arrernek, arrantherr apmer alker-itwek aney-alpetyek.

**13** Ran anwenhantherrenh apmwekety rakek, rantey arrpemarl alerikw ikwerenh arrernek, Atyetyart anwekantherrenh anetyek, anwantherr ikwerenh-rnem anayntetyek.

**14** Ingkart-kenh alerikw ranem anwekantherr katy ilwek, apmwa anwekantherr-wety iwetyek, anwantherr iteth intem-antey anayntetyek.

## Alerikw ahern-areny ingkerrenhek anetyek

**15** Anantherr nthakenh Akngey irrwerl-areny arey-angenh. Alerikw ikwerenh atyetyart apmer arrpanenhek. Alerikw retangkwel arrwekel anenh ahern-wenyankel. Alerikw ikwerenh ingkerrenhek anayntirrek-anem, Ingkart anyent rey-antey.

**16** Rlterrk ikwerenhel ra irrwerl-areny-rnem-then ahern-areny-rnem-then arrpmernek. Aleyarl anwantherr areyel-anem. Ikwer-angkwarr inweng-rnem-then ingkerrenh arrpmernek, nthakenh arey-angenh-rnem. Alerikw rlterrk ikwerenh-angkwarr ra ingkerrenh arrpmernek, atyetyart ingkerrenhek anayntetyek.

**17** Alerikw ra kel anenh, ahern nhenh-wenyankel. Ingkartel ahern-areny-rnem-then arrpmerney-angenh-antey, rant anenhan. Alerikwel ingkerrenh-antey atnyeneyel, ahern-areny-rnem ingkerrenh atnyenetyek.

**18** Christ-then Tyaty-areny-rnem anyent-antey, Jesus Christ akapwert-apeny atyetyart aneyel ingkerrenhek. Anyent arlka ikwerenh-apeny anem, inweng anyent-akert rey-antey ikwer-rnem warrkerreyel. Ingkartel renh-tangkwel ipmepenh akemelhilek, alerikw ra ingkerrenhek atyetyart anetyek.

**19** Aray, Ingkartelant alerikw ikwerenh arrernek, ikwer-apeny anayntetyek Ingkart irrkaty-antey ikwer-apeny, anyent rey-antey.

**20** Alerikw ikwer-angkwarr alker-areny ahern-areny ingkerrenh ikwerenh-rnem ayerrpilek. Alerikw ikwerenh arwerl iylpeyl-atherrel ilwemel, ra mwerr-angkwarr mpwarek ingkerrenhek anwantherr apenterl-ayntetyek, mwerrant-arey anayntetyek, arternpel.

**21** Arrantherr Ingkart-wety ament-warl alhenh. Arrantherr apmwangkwarr alhenh ament iterremel, Ingkart-wety arlkarrirrenh.

**22** Alerikw-anem arwerl iylpeyl-atherrel ilwek, arrantherr Ingkart-kenh-angkwarr arraty anetyek. Ra ikwer-warl anyentelhilek, alyelk mwerr anayntetyek, Ingkartel arrenhantherrenh tyatyemeley-angenh.

**23** Arrantherr Ingkart-kenh-angkwarr anetyek, angka mwerr renh rlterrk tnakemel. Arrantherr-anem angkek iylpelirrey-angenh, Ingkartel arrekantherr arrernek, arrantherr iteth intem-antey anayntetyek. Ingkartel ayenh rlwanek, angka mwerr-angker Ingkart-kenh ilerl-apayntetyek ahern-areny ingkerrenhek.

**24** Ayenh apek menty atwem mwerrarl. Christ Jesus arlka menty anthelhek. Ayeng arlka menty anthelhek arrpemarl tyaty-areny ingkerrenhek.

**25** Akngeyel ayenh arrernek, tyaty-areny-rnemek, warrkirretyek, angka ikwerenh ingkerrenhek akalty-anthetyek, ingkerrenhek warrkirretyek.

**26** Arrwekel angka anyelk-tangkwel ayntenh, Aley angka anaynt-anem Ingkart-areny ingkerrenhek, rernem aketh-antey aweyneyel.

**27** Ingkart ilkelheyel, arrantherr Jew-weny-rnem akalty anetyek, angka ikwerenhek, arrantherr apmer mwerr-angker-itwek aney-alpetyek, Christ-kenh-angkwarr.

**28** Rernem-anem Jesus Christ-warl anyentirremel, Jesus Christ tnakeyenh. Anantherr renh-rnem akalty-anthem, rernem arraty-angkwarr apenterl-ayntetyek, ingwer-rnemel renh-rnem ilpertilekerr.

**29** Christ-el ayenh rlterrkileyel. Alakenh-anyem ayeng warrkirreyel, alhengkerr irrey-angenh. Atha ingwer-rnem akalty-anthayntetyek ipmelhey-angenh.
