# 2 Thessalonians 1

**1** Atha Paul-el pip nhenh angkakert thweneyel. Atha Silas-el Timothy-el-anem anantherrarl angka nhenh ingkwerneyel arrewantherr Akngey-wenh-rnemew Thessalonica-areny-rnemew. Arrantherran Akngey-wenh-rnemarl, Jesus Christ-arl apenteyel-rnemarl.

**2** Anantherr ayernem Akngey Alerikw Jesus-then, athamerr-aynteyew arrewantherr, inweng-then arternpel-aynteyew.

## Akngeyelan court-emeleyenh ingkerrenh arraty irrkaty-angkwarr

**3** Anewantherrenh-rnemay Christian-rnem! Anantherran Akngey nakem intem arrewantherr. Mwerr anantherr inkwerelhemel angkem ikwer-warl, arrantherrarl intem apenteyel-antey arraty. Iterreyel rlterrk ikwer-warl irrpeltherrey-angenh. Arrantherrap intemarl ingwer-rnem arntarnt-areyel arraty-angkwarr. Arraty alyep-tangkwel arrantherr apentenh, aleyanap arraty inngangkwarr-anem apenteyel.

**4** Alakenhap anantherr arrenhantherrenh nakayntem, anantherrarl alheyelel, Akngey-wenh-rnem arerl-apaynteyew apmer arrpanenhew. Anantherr renh-rnem ilem, arrantherrarl rlterrk iterremel aneyel Akngeyew, ipmelhey-angenh. Ingwer-rnemel arrenhantherrenh irrwerneyel. Arrantherranap renh apenteyel intem-anteyarl.

**5** Anantherr iterrem intem arrewantherr. Rlterrkarl ayntenheng-arey, arrantherr rlterrk-anteyarl anenh. Akngeyelan ikwerenh-rnem arntarnt-arem intem-antey, rlterrkarl anemewan ikwer. Ingwer-ingweran ra ingkerrenh rnkern-ayneyenh, ayern-aynteyew renh-rnem. Court-emeleyenh ra ingwer-rnem, ikwerenh-rnemarl irrwernenhew. Akngey ran ingkerrenhew arraty aneyel intem.

**7** Rap mwerr mpwareyenh anewantherr, rernemarl anenhantherrenh ingkerrenh irrwernenh-wety. Rap alakenh mpwareyenh, Jesus-arl aremerreyenh irrwerl-they antyerl ikwerenh-rnem-akert, rwethen-akert. Rernem ingkerr apetyeyenh arawerrng ilkwakert, rwakert-then.

**8** Akngeyelan pwarrk ilkwa antheyenh renh iterl-arey-angenh-rnemewan. Rernem anenh, angka ikwerenhew Jesus-akertew awelhey-angenh.

**9** Nthakenh rernem aney-alpey-angenh Jesus-itwel irrwerl, rarl atyetyart ilkwa aneyel-itwelan. Arlta ikwerel ra renh-rnem irrenteyenh, rarl apetyeyenh arawerrng ilkwakert. Rernemap aney-alpeyenh ament arrpemarl arleng ingwer-rnem-wety intem-antey. Arlta ikwerel ikwerenh-rneman aney-alpeyenh anyentel ikwer-itwel. Ya, Christian-rnem ingkerr aney-alpeyenh, atayel-anem renh nakey-alpeyenh irrwerl-itwew alakenh, “Jesus ran inngarl mwerr-angker!” Arrantherran angka ikwerenhew awelhenh, anantherrarl ilerl-apenhew, arrantherrap ikwer-itwel aney-alpeyenh nakemel arrpemarl.

**11** Alakenh-wety Akngeyan ayernem intem, arrenhantherrenh arntarnt-araynteyew, arrantherraw ikwer-angkwarr anaynteyew. Anantherrap ayernem, arrenhantherrenh rlterrkel-aynteyew, arrantherraw ikwer warrkerr-aynteyew. Arrantherr ikwer warrkerreyew, rlterrk ikwerant iterremel.

**12** Arrantherr ikwer alakenh warrkerrem, ingwer-rnemelap arayntemarl. Rernem inkwerelhem arrewantherr, Jesus nakemel. Arrenhantherrenhap rernem nakem arrpemarl. Akngeyel rethen arrenhantherrenh nakem. Alakenh ayntem, Akngey-then Jesus-then ratherr mwerrarl mpwarem arrewantherr.
