# Psalms 100

**1** “A psalm of praise.” Raise a voice of joy unto the LORD, all ye lands!

**2** Serve the LORD with gladness; Come before his presence with rejoicing!

**3** Know ye that Jehovah is God! It is he that made us, and we are his, His people, and the flock of his pasture.

**4** Enter into his gates with thanksgiving, And his courts with praise; Be thankful to him, and bless his name!

**5** For the LORD is good; his mercy is everlasting; And his truth endureth to all generations.
