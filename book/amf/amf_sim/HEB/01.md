# Ibra 1

## Barjo Yisa Naasaka Kinin Giyoono

**1** Barjo ena wodanka waana waana goytika Barjokala qansayse eenna giyaynaka wosa eykenana thoothi wodana giydine.

**2** Taaki macchin wodan koka yer wulsa toydi maataa yisa Naasaka wona giydine. Barjoar yer wulam kinin pijoono Naasa yintea goynkane.

**3** Kidi yinti Barjo gobshinsa shaakane; kidi yisa dantanka Barjo Imbaabesana kalaane. Yisa kantan upsinka pijadhin wulam keedhayse ki yedade. Eenaamaar kesa seesora yinin gazinsonsa budonte cecinte wuldara gobshinka sagi maataa Barjosa mizaqimbar ki dooqade.

## Yesus Barjosa Gaananadara Kinin Sagayno

**4** Barjo Naasana imbhoono naabeno Barjo gaananasa naabendara kattsi sagine; ogodettsi kidi kedara sagine.

**5** Harrna hambhidianna, Barjo yisa gaananasa kalaana> “Ya isa Naasine;
> inta haam kina adhidine”pir,> “Inta kisa Imba ida maate;
> kidiar isa Naasi kida maate”hamayse kinin giyaa hayne?

**6** Pir Barjo biraa Naasa yinteam peendar yinin nittsenka,> “Barjosa gaanana wul kumpurdhayse kina ke goodime”ki hamade.

**7** Barjo yisa gaananaka> “Isa gaananaam wuradettsi ida haye;
> ina waadimaynaamaar nuusa laabhinadettsi ida haye”ki hamade.

**8** Yisa Naasaka ko hanna,> “Barjo, hasa bitamon borkotono rooro wul lay koda dee;> > hasa eenaam tipamoka hada wottse.

**9** > Tipamon waadimanam nashayse siyon waadimanam ha jibbade.
> Ogo goynka hasa Barjoa haam kamayse hasa aanammadara kattsi sagi hayayse haam wozisen zaytinka ki qadhade”ki hamade.

**10** Yin pir,> “Imbo, ya ena birayse peenam ha pijade;> > cecinoar hasa aanin waadimanone.

**11** > Igina kaya ke kaye;> > ya rooro wul daada dee.> Kedi wul apaladettsi keda gecche;

**12** > apaladettsiar keam hada umbhuse;> > apaladettsiar ookaada ooke.> Yaar rooro wul lay ookimattane;> > hasa levensa macchi qolehe”ki hamade.

**13** Barjo gaananasa kalaana> “Haam jibbaynaam hasa hayamon demente inin hayenka yekka isa mizaqinte dooqaa”hamayse kinin giyaa hayne?

**14** Ta keeda gaanana wul Barjoyn dhacchaynaam kenin kelshoadettsi nittsadhayna Barjona waadadhayna manpasinatayu?
