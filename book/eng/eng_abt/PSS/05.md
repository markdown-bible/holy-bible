# Psalms of Solomon 5

*A Psalm. Of Solomon.*

**1** > O Lord God, I will praise thy name with joy,
> In the midst of them that know thy righteous judgments.

**2** > For thou art good and merciful, the refuge of the poor;
> When I cry to thee, do not silently disregard me.

**3** > For no man taketh spoil from a mighty man;
> Who, then, can take aught of all that thou hast made, except thou thyself givest?

**4** > For man and his portion lie before thee in the balance;
> He cannot add to, so as to enlarge, what hath been prescribed by thee, O God.


**5** > When we are in distress we call upon thee for help,
> And thou dost not turn back our petition,
> For thou art our God.

**6** > Cause not thy hand to be heavy upon us,
> Lest through necessity we sin.

**7** > Even though thou restore us not, we will not keep away;
> But unto thee will we come.


**8** > For if I hunger, unto thee will I cry, O God;
> And thou wilt give to me.

**9** > Birds and fish dost thou nourish,
> In that thou givest rain to the steppes that green grass may spring up,

**10** > So to prepare fodder in the steppe for every living thing;
> And if they hunger, unto thee do they lift up their face.

**11** > Kings and rulers and peoples thou dost nourish, O God;
> And who is the help of the poor and needy, if not thou, O Lord?

**12** > And thou wilt hearken—for who is good and gentle but thou—
> Making glad the soul of the humble by opening thy hand in mercy.

**13** > Man’s goodness is bestowed grudgingly and on the morrow;
> And if he repeat it without murmuring, even that is marvelous.

**14** > But thy gift is great in goodness and wealth,
> And he whose hope is set on thee shall have no lack of gifts.

**15** > Upon the whole earth is thy mercy, O Lord, in goodness.

**16** > Happy is he whom God remembereth in granting to him a due sufficiency;
> If a man abound over much, he sinneth.

**17** > Sufficient are moderate means with righteousness,
> And hereby the blessing of the Lord becometh abundance with righteousness.

**18** > They that fear the Lord rejoice in good gifts,
> And thy goodness is upon Israel in thy kingdom.

**19** > Blessed is the glory of the Lord for he is our king.
