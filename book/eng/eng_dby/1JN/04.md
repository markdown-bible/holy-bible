# 1 John 4

**1** Beloved, believe not every spirit, but prove the spirits, if they are of[^14]God; because many false prophets are gone out into the world.

**2** Hereby ye know the Spirit of[^15]God: every spirit which confesses Jesus Christ come in flesh is of[^16]God;

**3** and every spirit which does not confess Jesus Christ come in flesh is not of[^17]God: and this is that [power] of the antichrist, [of] which ye have heard that it comes, and now it is already in the world.

**4** Ye are of[^18]God, children, and have overcome them, because greater is he that [is] in you than he that [is] in the world.

**5** They are of the world; for this reason they speak [as] of the world, and the world hears them.

**6** We are of[^19]God; he that knows[^20]God hears us; he who is not of[^21]God does not hear us. From this we know the spirit of truth and the spirit of error.

**7** Beloved, let us love one another; because love is of[^22]God, and every one that loves has been begotten of[^23]God, and knows[^24]God.

**8** He that loves not has not known[^25]God; for[^26]God is love.

**9** Herein as to us has been manifested the love of[^27]God, that[^28]God has sent his only begotten Son into the world, that we might live through him.

**10** Herein is love, not that we loved[^29]God, but that he loved us, and sent his Son a propitiation for our sins.

**11** Beloved, if[^30]God has so loved us, we also ought to love one another.

**12** No one has seen[^31]God at any time: if we love one another,[^32]God abides in us, and his love is perfected in us.

**13** Hereby we know that we abide in him and he in us, that he has given to us of his Spirit.

**14** And we have seen, and testify, that the Father has sent the Son [as] Saviour of the world.

**15** Whosoever shall confess that Jesus is the Son of[^33]God,[^34]God abides in him, and he in[^35]God.

**16** And we have known and have believed the love which[^36]God has to us.[^37]God is love, and he that abides in love abides in[^38]God, and[^39]God in him.

**17** Herein has love been perfected with us that we may have boldness in the day of judgment, that even as he is, we also are in this world.

**18** There is no fear in love, but perfect love casts out fear; for fear has torment, and he that fears has not been made perfect in love.

**19** We love because he has first loved us.

**20** If any one say, I love[^40]God, and hate his brother, he is a liar: for he that loves not his brother whom he has seen, how can he love[^41]God whom he has not seen?

**21** And this commandment have we from him, That he that loves[^42]God love also his brother.
