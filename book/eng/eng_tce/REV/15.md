# THE REVELATION TO JOHN 15

## The Seven Angels with Seven Plagues

**1** Then I saw another great and marvelous sign in heaven: seven angels with the seven final plagues, because with them the wrath of God is completed.

**2** I also saw something like a sea of glass mixed with fire, and those who had overcome[^243]the beast, his image,[^244]and the number of his name were standing by the sea of glass, holding[^245]the harps of God.

**3** They sang the song of Moses, the servant of God, and the song of the Lamb:
> “Great and marvelous are yoʋr works,
> O Lord God, the Almighty!
> Righteous and true are yoʋr ways,
> O King of the[^246]> nations!

**4** > Who could not fear[^247]> yoʋ, O Lord,
> and glorify yoʋr name?
> For yoʋ alone are holy.
[^248]> All will come
> and worship before yoʋ,
> for yoʋr righteous acts have been revealed.”


**5** After this I looked,[^249]and the sanctuary of the tent of witness in heaven was opened,

**6** and[^250]out came the seven angels with the seven[^251]plagues. They were clothed in pure bright[^252]linen, with golden sashes wrapped around their chests.

**7** Then one of the four living creatures gave the seven angels seven golden bowls full of the wrath of God, who lives forever and ever.

**8** And the temple was filled with[^253]the smoke from the glory of God and from his power, and no one could enter the temple until the seven plagues of the[^254]seven angels were completed.
