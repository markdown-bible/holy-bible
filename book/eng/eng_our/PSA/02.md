# Psalms 2


**1** > *Why do the nations rage,*[^1]> > *and the peoples plot a vain thing?*

**2** [^2][^3]> *The kings of the earth*[^4]> *take a stand,*> > *and the rulers take counsel together,*
> > *against Adonai , and against*[^5]> > *his Anointed,*[^6]> > saying,

**3** > “Let’s break their bonds apart,> > and cast their cords from us.”

**4** > *He who sits in the heavens will laugh.*> > *'Adonay [Lord] will have them in derision.*

**5** > Then he will speak to them in his anger,> > and terrify them in his wrath:

**6** [^7][^8]> *“Yet I have set*[^9]> *my King on my holy hill of Zion [Mountain ridge, Marking].”*[^10]

**7** > I will tell of the statute.> > Adonai said to me,[^11]> > *“You are my son.*
> > *Today I have become your father.*[^12]

**8** [^13]> *Ask of me, and I will give the nations for your inheritance,*[^14]> > *the uttermost parts of the earth for your possession.*

**9** > You shall break them with a rod of iron.> > You shall dash them in pieces like pottery.”[^15]

**10** > Now therefore be wise, you kings.> > Be instructed, you judges of the earth.

**11** > Abad ·Serve· Adonai with fear,> > and rejoice with trembling.[^16]

**12** [^17]> *Kiss the Son,*[^18][^19]> *lest he be angry, and you perish on the way,*> > *for his wrath will soon be kindled.*
> > *Blessed are all those who take refuge in him.*
