# Psalms 16


**1** > Preserve me, God, for in you do I take refuge.

**2** > My soul, you have said to Adonai, “You are my Lord.> > Apart from you I have no good thing.”

**3** > As for the saints who are in the earth,> > they are the excellent ones in whom is all my delight.


**4** > Their sorrows shall be multiplied who give gifts to another deity.> > Their drink offerings of blood I will not offer,
> > nor take their names on my lips.

**5** > Adonai assigned my portion and my cup.> > You made my lot secure.


**6** > The lines have fallen to me in pleasant places.> > Yes, I have a good inheritance.

**7** > I will bless Adonai, who has given me counsel.> > Yes, my heart instructs me in the night seasons.

**8** > I always set Adonai before me.> > Because he is at my right hand, I shall not be moved.

**9** [^31]> *Therefore my heart is glad, and my kavod ·weighty glory· rejoices.*> > *My body shall also dwell in safety.*

**10** [^32]> *For you will not leave my soul in Sheol ·Place of the dead·,*[^33]> > *neither will you allow your holy one to see corruption.*[^34]

**11** > You will show me the path of life.> > In your presence is fullness of joy.[^35]> In your right hand there are pleasures forever more.
