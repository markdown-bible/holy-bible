# Isaiah 11

**1** [^52]> *A shoot will come out of the stock of Jesse [My husband],*> > *and a*[^53]> > *netzer ·branch· out of*[^54]> > *his roots*[^55]> > *will bear fruit.*

**2** [^56]> *Ruach Yahweh [Spirit, Breath of He sustains breathing] will rest on him:*[^57]> > *the ruach ·spirit, breath· of wisdom and*[^58]> > *understanding,*
[^59]> > *the ruach ·spirit, breath· of counsel and*[^60]> > *might,*
[^61]> > *the ruach ·spirit, breath· of knowledge and of*[^62]> > *the fear of Adonai .*

**3** [^63]> *His delight will be in the fear of Adonai .*
> *He will not judge by the sight of his eyes,*> > *neither decide by the hearing of his ears;*

**4** > *but with righteousness he will judge the poor,*> > *and decide with equity for the humble of the earth.*> *He will strike the earth with the rod of his mouth;*[^64]> > *and with the breath of his lips he will kill*[^65]> > *the wicked.*

**5** > Justice will be the belt of his waist,> > and faithfulness the belt of his waist.[^66]

**6** > The wolf will live with the lamb,> > and the leopard will lie down with the young goat;
> > The calf, the young lion, and the fattened calf together;
> > and a little child will lead them.

**7** > The cow and the bear will graze.> > Their young ones will lie down together.
> > The lion will eat straw like the ox.

**8** > The nursing child will play near a cobra’s hole,> > and the weaned child will put his hand on the viper’s den.

**9** > They will not hurt nor destroy in all my holy mountain;> > for the earth will be full of the knowledge of Adonai,
> > as the waters cover the sea.

**10** It will happen in that day that the nations will seek[^67][^68]*the root of Jesse [My husband],*[^69]*who stands as a banner of the peoples;*[^70]*and his resting place will be kavod ·weighty glorious·.*

**11** It will happen in that day that 'Adonay [Lord] will set his hand again the second time to recover the remnant that is left of his people from Assyria [Level plain], from Egypt [Abode of slavery], from Pathros, from Cush, from Elam, from Shinar, from Hamath, and from the islands of the sea.

**12** He will set up a banner for the nations, and will assemble the outcasts of Israel [God prevails], and gather together the dispersed of Judah [Praised] from the four corners of the earth.

**13** The envy also of Ephraim [Fruit] will depart, and those who persecute Judah [Praised] will be cut off. Ephraim [Fruit] won’t envy Judah [Praised], and Judah [Praised] won’t persecute Ephraim [Fruit].

**14** They will fly down on the shoulders of the Philistines [To roll in dust (As an insult)] on the west. Together they will plunder the children of the east. They will extend their power over Edom [Red] and Moab [From father], and the children of Ammon [Tribal people] will obey them.

**15** Adonai will utterly destroy the tongue of the Sea of Egypt [Abode of slavery]; and with his scorching wind he will wave his hand over the River, and will split it into seven streams, and cause men to march over in sandals.

**16** There will be a highway for the remnant that is left of his people from Assyria [Level plain], like there was for Israel [God prevails] in the day that he came up out of the land of Egypt [Abode of slavery].

In the parallel fulfillment verse, the Greek word for Nazarene is Nazaphnoe, coming from the Aramaic word for twig or branch. Note on the Greek: The Greek word for a citizen of Nazareth [Branch, Separated one] is Nazarethaios. But Matthew uses Nazaphnoe ·branch·, not Nazarethaios [a person from Nazareth]. The King James Version of the Bible, published in 1611, translates the original Greek word as “a person from Nazareth [Branch, Separated one]” regional but modern scholars now believe this translation to be incorrect. The Bible and Matthew both make it clear Yeshua was a “citizen of Nazareth,” of Galilee, in Judea. (Matt 2:22b-23)
