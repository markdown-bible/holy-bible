# TEHILLIM 54

**1** (For the director. With stringed instruments. A maskil. For Dovid,

**2** when the Ziphim came and said to Sha'ul, Dovid is in hiding among us)

**3** > Hoshieini (save me), O Elohim, by Shimecha,
> and vindicate me by Thy gevurah (might, power).

**4** > Hear my tefillah, O Elohim;
> give ear to the words of my mouth.

**5** > For zarim (strangers) are risen up against me,
> and oppressors seek after my nefesh;
> they have not set Elohim before them.
> Selah


**6** > Hinei, Elohim is mine Ozer (helper);
> Adonoi is with them that uphold my nefesh.

**7** > He shall recompense the rah (evil) unto mine enemies;
> in Thy emes cut them off.


**8** > I will sacrifice a nedavah (freewill offering) unto Thee;
> I will praise Shimecha, Hashem; for it is tov (good).

**9** > For He hath delivered me out of all my tzoros;
> and mine eye hath looked in triumph upon mine oyevim (enemies.)
