# TEHILLIM 131

**1** (Shir HaMa'alot, of Dovid)> 1Hashem, my lev is not haughty,
> nor mine eyes lofty;
> neither do I exercise myself in gedolot,
> or in things too high for me.

**2** > Surely I have stilled and quieted my nefesh,
> as a child that is weaned of immo;
> my nefesh is even as a weaned child.


**3** > Let Yisroel hope in Hashem
> from henceforth and ad olam.
