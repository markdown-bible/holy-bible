# TEHILLIM 129

**1** (Shir HaMa'alot)> 1 Many a time have they afflicted me from my ne'ur,
> may Yisroel now say;

**2** > Many a time have they afflicted me from my ne'ur (youth);
> yet they have not prevailed against me.

**3** > The choreshim (plowers) plowed upon my back;
> they made long their furrows.

**4** > Hashem is tzaddik;
> He hath cut asunder the cords of the resha'im.

**5** > Let them all be confounded
> and turned back that hate Tziyon.[^164]

**6** > Let them be as the khatzir (grass) upon the housetops,
> which withereth before it groweth up;

**7** > With it the kotzer (reaper) filleth not his yad;
> nor he that gathereth sheaves, his bosom.

**8** > Neither do they which go by say,
> Birkat Hashem be upon you;
> we bless you b'Shem Hashem.
