# YESHAYAH 53

**1** > Who hath believed our report?
> And to whom is the Zero'a Hashem[^158]> revealed?

**2** > For he shall grow up before him as a tender plant,
> and as a Shoresh (Root, Shoresh Yishai, Moshiach Ben Dovid)[^159]> out of a dry ground;
> he hath no form nor comeliness;
> and when we shall see him,
> there is no beauty that we should desire[^160]> him.

**3** > He is despised and chadal ishim (rejected by men);
> a man of sorrows, and acquainted with suffering;
> and we hid as it were our faces from him;
> he was despised, and we esteemed him not.


**4** > Surely he hath borne our sufferings,
> and nasah (carried)[^161]> our sorrows;
> yet we did esteem him stricken,[^162]
> smitten of G-d,[^163]> and afflicted.

**5** > But he was pierced[^164]> for our transgressions,
> he was bruised mei'avonoteinu (for our iniquities);
> the musar (chastisement) that brought us shalom[^165]> was upon him [Moshiach];
> and at the cost of his [Moshiachʼs] chaburah (stripes, lacerations) we are healed.

**6** > All we like sheep have gone astray;
> we have turned every one to his own derech[^166]> (way);
> and Hashem hath laid on him[^167]
> the avon (iniquity, the guilt that separates from G-d) of us all.


**7** > He was oppressed, and he was afflicted,
> yet he opened not his mouth;
> he is brought as a seh[^168]> (lamb) to the slaughter,
> and as a sheep before her shearers is dumb,
> so he openeth not his mouth.

**8** > He was taken from prison and from judgment;
> and who of his generation declared?
> For he was cut off[^169]> out of Eretz Chayyim[^170]
> mipesha ami (for the transgression of my people [Yisroel]),
> nega[^171]> (plague) lamo ([fell] on him).[^172]

**9** > And he made his kever (grave) with the resha'im,
> and with the oisher[^173]> (rich man) bemotayv (in his deaths, intensive plural should be translated singular, death);
> because he had done no chamas (violence),
> neither was any mirmah (deceit) in his mouth.[^174]


**10** > Yet it pleased Hashem to bruise him;
> He hath put him to suffering;
> when Thou shalt make his nefesh an asham offering for sin,
> he (Moshiach) shall see zera,[^175]
> He shall prolong his yamim (days)
> and the chefetz Hashem(pleasure, will of Hashem) shall prosper in his[^176]> hand.

**11** > He[^177]> shall see of the travail of his[^178]> nefesh, and shall be satisfied;
> by knowledge of him[^179]
> shall Tzadik Avdi (My Righteous Servant)[^180]> justify many;[^181]
> for he[^182]> shall bear their avon (iniquities).

**12** > Therefore will I divide him a portion with the great,
> and he shall divide the spoil with the strong;
> because he hath poured out his nefesh unto mavet (death);
> and he was numbered with the transgressors;
> and he nasah[^183]> (bore) [like the Yom Kippur scapegoat] the sin of many,
> and made intercession[^184]> for the transgressors.[^185]
