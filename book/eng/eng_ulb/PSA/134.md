# Psalms 134

*A song of ascents.*

**1** > Come, bless Yahweh, all you servants of Yahweh,
> you who serve during the night in Yahweh's house.

**2** > Lift up your hands to the holy place
> and bless Yahweh.

**3** > May Yahweh bless you from Zion,
> he who made heaven and earth.
