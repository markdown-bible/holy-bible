# PSALMS 114

**1** Alleluia. In the going out of Israel from Egypt; of the house of Jacob from the heathen people.

**2** Judah was made the hallowing of him; Israel the power of him.

**3** The sea saw, and fled; Jordan was turned aback.

**4** Mountains full out joyed as rams; and little hills as the lambs of sheep.

**5** Thou sea, what was to thee, for thou fleddest; and thou, Jordan, for thou were turned aback?

**6** Mountains, ye made full out joy as rams; and little hills, as the lambs of sheep.

**7** The earth was moved from the face of the Lord; from the face of God of Jacob.

**8** Which turned a stone into a pond of waters; and an hard rock into wells of waters.
