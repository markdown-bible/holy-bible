# PSALMS 113

**1** Alleluia. Servants, praise ye the Lord; praise ye the name of the Lord.

**2** The name of the Lord be blessed; from this time now, and till into the world.

**3** From the rising of the sun till to the going down; the name of the Lord is worthy to be praised.

**4** The Lord is high above all folks; and his glory is above heavens.

**5** Who is as our Lord God, that dwelleth in high things;

**6** and beholdeth meek things in heaven and in earth?

**7** Raising a needy man from the earth; and enhancing a poor man from drit.

**8** That he set him with princes; with the princes of his people.

**9** Which maketh a barren woman dwell in the house; a glad mother of sons.
