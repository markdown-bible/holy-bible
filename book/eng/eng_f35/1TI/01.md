# 1 Timothy 1

## Greeting

**1** Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ by the command of God our Savior and Sovereign Jesus Christour hope,[^1]

**2** to Timothy, true son by faith: Grace, mercy, peace from God our[^2]Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

## Paul refers to their last conversation

**3** You recall that I urged you to remain in Ephesus, when I went into Macedonia, in order that you should command[^3]certain persons to stop teaching a different doctrine

**4** and occupying themselves with myths and endless genealogies,[^4]which cause disputes rather than the godly training which is by faith.[^5]

**5** Now the goal of that command is love, out of a pure heart and a good conscience and a sincere faith;[^6]

**6** some having strayed from these have been turned aside[^7]to empty talk,

**7** desiring to be teachers of the law, not understanding either what they say or the things that they affirm.[^8]

**8** Now we know that the law is good if one uses it correctly,

**9** knowing that law is not made for a righteous person but for lawless and rebellious ones, for ungodly and sinners, for unholy and profane, for father-smiters and mother-smiters, for murderers,[^9]

**10** for fornicators, for sodomites,[^10]for kidnappers,[^11]for liars, for perjurers, and for whatever else is contrary to the sound doctrine

**11** as defined by the Gospel[^12]of the glory[^13]of the blessed God, with which I was entrusted.[^14]

## The grace of God

**12** I thank Christ Jesus our Lord who enables me, because He considered me faithful, putting me into a ministry;

**13** I, who was formerly a blasphemer and a persecutor and arrogant; but I was shown mercy, because being ignorant[^15]I did it in unbelief;

**14** the grace of our Lord overflowed, along with the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus.[^16]

**15** Here is a trustworthy word, worthy of total acceptance: Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners—of whom I am chief;

**16** precisely for this reason I was shown mercy, that in me, the ‘chief’, Jesus Christ might display all his forbearance, as an example for those who are going to rely on Him into eternal life.[^17]

**17** Now to the King eternal, immortal, invisible, to God who alone is wise,[^18]be honor and glory forever and ever. Amen.

## Wage the good warfare

**18** Son Timothy, I am giving you this charge in accordance with the prophecies about you that lead the way,[^19]that in them you may wage the good warfare,

**19** holding on to faith and a good conscience[^20](which[^21]some having rejected have suffered shipwreck concerning the Faith;

**20** of whom are Hymenaeus and Alexander—whom I have handed over to Satan that they may be taught[^22]not to blaspheme).
