# Titus 2

**1** Now you, speak the things that suit sound doctrine:

**2** older men are to be sober, dignified, prudent, sound in the faith, in love and in endurance;

**3** similarly older women are to be reverent in behavior, not slanderers nor enslaved to much wine, teachers of what is good

**4** —so that they may instruct the young women[^16]to love their husbands, to love their children,

**5** to be prudent, pure, good homemakers, subordinating themselves to their own husbands, so that the Word of God not be maligned.

**6** Similarly, exhort the young men to be self-controlled,[^17]

**7** in all things presenting yourself as an example of good works:[^18]showing integrity, dignity and incorruptibility[^19]in your teaching

**8** —healthy speech, beyond reproach—so that the opponent may be put to shame, having nothing slighting to say about us.[^20]

**9** Exhort slaves to subordinate themselves to their own owners, to be well pleasing in all things, not talking back,

**10** not pilfering[^21]but showing total trustworthiness, so as to adorn the doctrine of our Savior God in everything.[^22]

## Saving grace

**11** For the saving grace of God has appeared for all men,

**12** teaching us that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we should live prudently, righteously and godly in this present age,[^23]

**13** looking for the blessed hope,[^24]even the glorious appearing of our great God and Savior, Jesus Christ,

**14** who gave Himself for us, so that He might redeem us from all lawlessness[^25]and purify for Himself a special people, zealous for good works.[^26]

**15** Speak these things, whether you exhort or reprove, with all authority. Let no one disdain you.[^27]
