# Psalms 126

*A Song of degrees.*

**1** > When the LORD turned again the captivity of Zion, we were like them that dream.[^686]

**2** > Then was our mouth filled with laughter, and our tongue with singing: then said they among the heathen, The LORD hath done great things for them.[^687]

**3** > The LORD hath done great things for us; whereof we are glad.


**4** > Turn again our captivity, O LORD, as the streams in the south.

**5** > They that sow in tears shall reap in joy.[^688]

**6** > He that goeth forth and weepeth, bearing precious seed, shall doubtless come again with rejoicing, bringing his sheaves with him.[^689]
