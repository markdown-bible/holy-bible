# Psalms 20

*To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David.*

**1** > The LORD hear thee in the day of trouble; the name of the God of Jacob defend thee;[^115]

**2** > Send thee help from the sanctuary, and strengthen thee out of Zion;[^116][^117]

**3** > Remember all thy offerings, and accept thy burnt sacrifice; Selah.[^118]

**4** > Grant thee according to thine own heart, and fulfil all thy counsel.

**5** > We will rejoice in thy salvation, and in the name of our God we will set up our banners: the LORD fulfil all thy petitions.


**6** > Now know I that the LORD saveth his anointed; he will hear him from his holy heaven with the saving strength of his right hand.[^119][^120]

**7** > Some trust in chariots, and some in horses: but we will remember the name of the LORD our God.

**8** > They are brought down and fallen: but we are risen, and stand upright.

**9** > Save, LORD: let the king hear us when we call.
