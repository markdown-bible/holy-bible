# Psalms 150

**1** > Praise ye the LORD. Praise God in his sanctuary: praise him in the firmament of his power.[^769]

**2** > Praise him for his mighty acts: praise him according to his excellent greatness.

**3** > Praise him with the sound of the trumpet: praise him with the psaltery and harp.[^770]

**4** > Praise him with the timbrel and dance: praise him with stringed instruments and organs.[^771]

**5** > Praise him upon the loud cymbals: praise him upon the high sounding cymbals.

**6** > Let every thing that hath breath praise the LORD. Praise ye the LORD.
