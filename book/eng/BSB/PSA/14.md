# Psalms 14

## The Fool Says There Is No God

*For the choirmaster. Of David.*


**1** > The fool[^24]> says in his heart,> > “There is no God.”
> They are corrupt; their acts are vile.> > There is no one who does good.


**2** > The LORD looks down from heaven> > upon the sons of men> to see if any understand,> > if any seek God.

**3** > All have turned away,> > they have together become corrupt;[^25]> there is no one who does good,> > not even one.[^26]


**4** > Will the workers of iniquity never learn?
> They devour my people like bread;> > they refuse to call upon the LORD.

**5** > There they are, overwhelmed with dread,> > for God is in the company of the righteous.

**6** > You sinners frustrate the plans of the oppressed,> > yet the LORD is their shelter.


**7** > Oh, that the salvation of Israel would come from Zion!
> When the LORD restores His captive people,> > let Jacob rejoice, let Israel be glad!
