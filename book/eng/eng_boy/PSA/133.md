# Psalms 133

**1** A Song of Degrees.> Behold now, bless ye the Lord,
> All the servants of the Lord,
> Who stand in the house of the Lord,
> In the courts of the house of our God.

**2** > Lift up your hands by night in the sanctuaries,
> And bless the Lord.

**3** > May the Lord, who made heaven and earth,
> Bless thee out of Zion.
