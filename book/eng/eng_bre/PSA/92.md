# Psalms 92

**1** For[^179]the day before the Sabbath, when the land was first inhabited, the praise of a Song by David.The Lord reigns; he has clothed himself with honour: the Lord has clothed and girded himself with strength; for he has established the world, which shall not be moved.

**2** Thy throne is prepared of old: thou art from everlasting.

**3** The rivers have lifted up, O Lord, the rivers have lifted up their voices,[^180]

**4** at[^181]the voices of many waters: the billows of the sea are wonderful: the Lord is wonderful in high places.

**5** Thy testimonies are made very sure: holiness becomes thine house, O Lord,[^182]for ever.
