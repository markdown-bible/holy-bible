# Psalms 146

**1** Alleluia, a Psalm of Aggæus and Zacharias.Praise ye the Lord: for psalmody is a good thing; let praise be sweetly sung to our God.

**2** The Lord builds up Jerusalem; and he will gather together the dispersed of Israel.

**3** He heals the broken in heart, and binds up their wounds.

**4** He numbers the multitudes of stars; and calls them all by names.

**5** Great is our Lord, and great is his strength; and his understanding is infinite.

**6** The Lord lifts up the meek; but brings sinners down to the ground.

**7** Begin the song with thanksgiving to the Lord; sing praises on the harp to our God:

**8** who covers the heaven with clouds, who prepares rain for the earth, who causes grass to spring up on the mountains, [[^262]and green herb for the service of men;]

**9** and gives to cattle their food, and to the young ravens that call upon him.

**10** He will not take pleasure in the strength of a horse; neither is he well-pleased with the legs of a man.

**11** The Lord takes pleasure in them that fear him, and in all that hope in his mercy.
