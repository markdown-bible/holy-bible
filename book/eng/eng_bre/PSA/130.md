# Psalms 130

**1** [^234]A Song of Degrees.O Lord, my heart is not exalted, neither have mine eyes been haughtily raised: neither have I exercised myself in great matters, nor in things too wonderful for me.

**2** I shall have sinned if I have not been humble, but have exalted my soul: according to the relation of a weaned child to his mother, so wilt thou recompense my soul.

**3** Let Israel hope in the Lord, from henceforth and for ever.
