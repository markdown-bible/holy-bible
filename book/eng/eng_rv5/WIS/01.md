# The Wisdom Of Solomon 1

**1** > Love righteousness, ye that be judges of the earth,
> Think ye of the Lord> with a good mind,
> And in singleness of heart seek ye him;

**2** > Because he is found of them that tempt him not,
> And is manifested to them that do not distrust him.

**3** > For crooked thoughts separate from God;
> And the supreme Power, when it is brought to the proof,[^1]> putteth to confusion the foolish:

**4** > Because wisdom will not enter into a soul that deviseth evil,
> Nor dwell in a body that is held in pledge by sin.

**5** > For a holy spirit of discipline will flee deceit.
> And will start away from thoughts that are without understanding,
> And will be[^2]> put to confusion when unrighteousness hath come in.

**6** > For[^3]> wisdom is a spirit that loveth man,
> And she will not hold a[^4]> blasphemer guiltless for his lips;
> Because God beareth witness of his reins,
> And is a true overseer of his heart,
> And a hearer of his tongue:

**7** > Because the spirit of the Lord hath filled[^5]> the world,
> And that which holdeth all things together hath knowledge of every voice.

**8** > Therefore no man that uttereth unrighteous things shall be unseen;
[^6]> Neither shall Justice, when it convicteth, pass him by.

**9** > For in the midst of his counsels the ungodly shall be searched out;
> And the sound of his words shall come unto the Lord
> To bring to conviction his lawless deeds:

**10** > Because there is an ear of jealousy that listeneth to all things,
> And the noise of murmurings is not hid.

**11** > Beware then of unprofitable murmuring,
> And refrain your tongue from backbiting;
> Because no secret utterance shall go on its way void,
> And a mouth that belieth destroyeth a soul.

**12** > Court not death in the error of your life;
> Neither draw upon yourselves destruction by the works of your hands:

**13** > Because God made not death;
> Neither delighteth he when the living perish:

**14** > For he created all things that they might have being:
> And[^7]> the generative powers of the world are healthsome,
> And there is no poison of destruction in them:
> Nor hath Hades[^8]> royal dominion upon earth,

**15** > For righteousness is immortal:

**16** > But ungodly men by their hands and their words called[^9]> death unto them:
> Deeming him a friend they[^10]> consumed away,
> And they made a covenant with him,
> Because they are worthy to be of his portion.
