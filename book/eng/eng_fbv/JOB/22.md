# Job 22

**1** Then Eliphaz the Temanite responded and said,

**2** “How can anyone be of help to God? Even wise people are only helpful to themselves.

**3** Is it any benefit to the Almighty if you're a good person?[^138]What does he gain if you do what's right?

**4** Does he correct you and bring charges against you because of your reverence?[^139]

**5** No: it's because you're so wicked! Your sins are never-ending!

**6** For no reason at all you took your brother's clothing as a security for a debt, and left them stripped naked.

**7** You refused water to the thirsty; you denied food to the hungry.

**8** Is it because the land belongs to the powerful, and only the privileged have a right to live there?[^140]

**9** You have sent widows away empty-handed; you have crushed the outstretched arms of orphans, begging for help.[^141]

**10** That's why you're surrounded by traps to catch you, and why you suddenly panic in terror.

**11** That's why it's so dark you cannot see, and why you feel like you're drowning.[^142]

**12** Doesn't God live high in heaven and looks down on even the highest stars?

**13** But you ask, ‘What does God know? How can he see and judge what happens down here in darkness?

**14** Thick clouds cover him so he can't see anything as he walks around in heaven.’[^143]

**15** Why do you insist on following the traditional thinking of the wicked?[^144]

**16** They were taken before their time; all they had built was washed away.

**17** They had told God, ‘Get lost! What can the Almighty do to us?’

**18** And yet he was the one who had filled their homes with good things—but I don't accept their way of thinking.[^145]

**19** Those who do right rejoice when they see the destruction of the wicked,[^146]and the innocent mock them,

**20** saying, ‘Our enemies are destroyed, and fire has burned up all that's left of them.’

**21** Come back to God and be reconciled to him, and you'll be prosperous again.

**22** Listen to what he tells you and keep his words in mind.

**23** If you return to God you will be restored. If you renounce your sinful life[^147]

**24** and give up your love of money and desire for possessions,[^148]

**25** then the Almighty will be your gold and your precious silver.

**26** Then you will find delight in the Almighty, and be able to face him without feeling ashamed.

**27** You will pray to him, and he will hear you, and you will keep your promises to him.

**28** Whatever you decide to do will be successful, and wherever you go, light will shine on you.

**29** When others are humbled, and you say, ‘please help them,’ God will save them.

**30** God saves those who are innocent, and you will be saved if you do what is right.”[^149]
