# PSALMS 2

**1** > Why are the nations plotting rebellion?[^1]> The peoples devise schemes, but they're pointless.

**2** > The kings of the world prepare to attack,[^2]> and the rulers conspire together against the Lord and his anointed one,[^3]> saying,

**3** > “Let's break the chains and throw away the cords that bind us.”

**4** > But the one who sits enthroned in heaven laughs. The Lord mocks them.

**5** > He will thunder at them, and terrify them in his fury, saying,

**6** > “It is I who placed my king on Zion, my holy mountain.”

**7** > “I will proclaim the Lord's decree,” says the king. “He told me, ‘You are my son. Today I have become your father.[^4]

**8** > Ask me, and I will give you the nations as your possessions—the whole earth will belong to you.

**9** > You will break them with a rod of iron, smashing them like pottery.’”

**10** > So then you kings, be wise![^5]> Be warned, you rulers of the world!

**11** > Serve the Lord in reverence, celebrate with trembling!

**12** > Submit to his son[^6]> so that he will not become angry and you die suddenly. His anger[^7]> flares up quickly, but how happy are all those who go to him for protection.
