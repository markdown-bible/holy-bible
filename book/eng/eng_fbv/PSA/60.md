# PSALMS 60

*For the music director. According to “Lily of the Testimony.” A psalm (miktam) of David, useful for teaching, about the time he fought against Aram-naharaim and Aram-zobah, and then Joab returned and killed 12,000 Edomites in the Valley of Salt.*

**1** > You, God, have rejected us! You have broken us; you have been angry with us; but now you have to welcome us back![^183]

**2** > You have sent earthquakes on our land and split it apart. Now heal the cracks, for the land is still having tremors.

**3** > You have been very hard on your people; you gave us wine that made us stagger around.

**4** > But you have given those who respect you the banner of truth to unfurl and rally around.[^184]> Selah.

**5** > Rescue those you love! Answer us, and save us by your power!

**6** > God has spoken from his Temple: “Triumphantly I divide up Shechem, and portion out the Valley of Succoth.[^185]

**7** > Both Gilead and Manasseh belong to me. Ephraim is my helmet, and Judah is my scepter.[^186]

**8** > I will treat Moab as my washbasin; I will place my sandal on Edom; I will shout in triumph over Philistia.”[^187]

**9** > Who will bring me into the fortified city? Who will lead me into Edom?[^188]

**10** > Have you rejected us, God? Won't you lead our armies?

**11** > Please give us help against our enemies, for human help is worthless.[^189]

**12** > Our strength is in God, and he will crush our enemies.
