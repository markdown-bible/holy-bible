# PSALMS 134

*A song for pilgrims going up to Jerusalem.*

**1** > Praise the Lord, all you servants of the Lord who worship at night in the house of the Lord.

**2** > Lift up your hands towards the holy place and praise the Lord.

**3** > May the Lord bless you from Zion, he who made the heavens and the earth.
