# PSALMS 150

**1** > Praise the Lord! Praise God in his holy place![^481]> Praise him in the great expanse of heaven!

**2** > Praise him for the amazing things he does! Praise him for how wonderfully great he is!

**3** > Praise him with the sound of the trumpet, praise him with harp and lyre!

**4** > Praise him with tambourine and dancing, praise him with strings and flutes!

**5** > Praise him with the clash of cymbals, praise him with really loud cymbals!

**6** > Let everything that breathes praise the Lord! Praise the Lord!
