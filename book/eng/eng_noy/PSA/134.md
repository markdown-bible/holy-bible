# Psalms 134

**1** “A psalm of the steps, or the goings up.” Praise the LORD, all ye servants of the LORD, Who stand in the house of the LORD by night!

**2** Lift up your hands to the sanctuary, And praise the LORD!

**3** May the LORD, who made heaven and earth, Bless thee out of Zion!
