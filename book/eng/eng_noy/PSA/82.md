# Psalms 82

**1** “A psalm of Asaph.” God standeth in God's assembly, He judgeth in the midst of the gods.

**2** “How long will ye judge unjustly, And favor the cause of the wicked? [[Pause.]]

**3** Defend the poor and the fatherless; Do justice to the wretched and the needy!

**4** Deliver the poor and the destitute; Save them from the hand of the wicked!

**5** They are without knowledge and without understanding; They walk in darkness: Therefore all the foundations of the land are shaken.

**6** I have said, Ye are gods, And all of you children of the Most High;

**7** But ye shall die like men, And fall like the rest of the princes.”

**8** Arise, O God! judge the earth! For all the nations are thy possession.
