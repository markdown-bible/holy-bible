# Numbers 28

## The Daily Offerings

**1** Then the LORD said to Moses,

**2** “Command the Israelites and say to them: See that you present to Me at its appointed time the food for My food offerings, as a soothing aroma to Me.


**3** And tell them that this is the food offering you are to present to the LORD as a regular ascension offering each day: two unblemished year-old male lambs.

**4** Offer one lamb in the morning and the other at twilight,[^82]

**5** along with a tenth of an ephah of fine flour[^83]as a grain offering, mixed with a quarter hin of oil from pressed olives.[^84]


**6** This is a regular ascension offering established at Mount Sinai as a soothing aroma, a food offering to the LORD.

**7** The drink offering accompanying each lamb shall be a quarter hin. Pour out the offering of fermented drink to the LORD in the sanctuary area.

**8** And offer the second lamb at twilight, with the same grain offering and drink offering as in the morning. It is a food offering, a soothing aroma to the LORD.

## The Sabbath Offerings


**9** On the Sabbath day, present two unblemished year-old male lambs, accompanied by a grain offering of two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour[^85]mixed with oil, as well as a drink offering.


**10** This is the ascension offering for every Sabbath, in addition to the regular ascension offering and its drink offering.

## The Monthly Offerings


**11** At the beginning of every month, you are to present to the LORD an ascension offering of two young bulls, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old, all unblemished,

**12** along with three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour[^86]mixed with oil as a grain offering with each bull, two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering with the ram,

**13** and a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering with each lamb. This is an ascension offering, a soothing aroma, a food offering to the LORD.


**14** Their drink offerings shall be half a hin of wine[^87]with each bull, a third of a hin[^88]with the ram, and a quarter hin with each lamb. This is the monthly ascension offering to be made at each new moon throughout the year.


**15** In addition to the regular ascension offering with its drink offering, one male goat is to be presented to the LORD as a purification offering.

## Passover and the Feast of Unleavened Bread


**16** The fourteenth day of the first month is the LORD’s Passover.

**17** On the fifteenth day of this month, there shall be a feast; for seven days unleavened bread is to be eaten.


**18** On the first day there is to be a sacred assembly; you must not do any regular work.

**19** Present to the LORD a food offering, an ascension offering of two young bulls, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old, all unblemished.

**20** The grain offering shall consist of fine flour mixed with oil; offer three-tenths of an ephah with each bull, two-tenths of an ephah with the ram,

**21** and a tenth of an ephah with each of the seven lambs.

**22** Include one male goat as a purification offering to effect purgation for you.


**23** You are to present these in addition to the regular morning ascension offering.

**24** Offer the same food each day for seven days as a food offering, a soothing aroma to the LORD. It is to be offered with its drink offering and the regular ascension offering.


**25** On the seventh day you shall hold a sacred assembly; you must not do any regular work.

## The Feast of Weeks


**26** On the day of firstfruits, when you present an offering of new grain to the LORD during the Feast of Weeks,[^89]you are to hold a sacred assembly; you must not do any regular work.


**27** Present an ascension offering of two young bulls, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old as a soothing aroma to the LORD,

**28** together with their grain offerings of fine flour mixed with oil—three-tenths of an ephah with each bull, two-tenths of an ephah with the ram,

**29** and a tenth of an ephah with each of the seven lambs.


**30** Include one male goat to effect purgation for you.

**31** Offer them with their drink offerings in addition to the regular ascension offering and its grain offering. The animals must be unblemished.
