# Psalms 2

## The Triumphant Messiah

**1** > Why do the nations rage> > and the peoples plot in vain?

**2** > The kings of the earth take their stand> > and the rulers gather together,> against the LORD> > and against His Anointed One:[^1]

**3** > “Let us break Their chains> > and cast away Their cords.”


**4** > The One enthroned in heaven laughs;> > the Lord taunts them.

**5** > Then He rebukes them in His anger,> > and terrifies them in His fury:

**6** > “I have installed My King on Zion,> > upon My holy mountain.”


**7** > I will proclaim the decree> > spoken to Me by the LORD:> “You are My Son;> > today I have become Your Father.[^2]

**8** > Ask Me, and I will make the nations Your inheritance,> > the ends of the earth Your possession.

**9** > You will break them[^3]> with an iron scepter;> > You will shatter them like pottery.[^4]> > ”


**10** > Therefore be wise, O kings;> > be admonished, O judges of the earth.

**11** > Serve the LORD with fear,> > and rejoice with trembling.

**12** > Kiss the Son, lest He be angry> > and you perish in your rebellion,
> > when His wrath ignites in an instant.
> Blessed are all who take refuge in Him.

## Footnotes

[^0]: Or noisily assemble; see Revelation 11:18.
[^1]: Cited in Acts 4:25–26
[^2]: Literally today I have begotten You; cited in Acts 13:33, Hebrews 1:5, and Hebrews 5:5
[^3]: LXX You will rule them or You will shepherd them
[^4]: Cited in Revelation 2:27; see also Revelation 12:5 and Revelation 19:15.
