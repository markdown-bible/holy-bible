# Romans 3

## God Ana Bosunusunub

**1** Jew orot inamamatar boro Ufun Sabuw inanatabirih? Naatu a’ar kanabin hina’a’afuw ana gewasin i boro men tomar inab?

**2** Ef tata’ane etei i gewasih, wantoro’ot God ana tur i Jew sabuw hitutumih hitih.

**3** Baise sabuw afa men hibitumitum isan, kwanotanot God ana omatanen isan boro men nabosunusunub?[^6]

**4** En anababatun! God ana tur i mar etei turobe, baise sabuw i baifuwenayah. Buk Atamaninamaim iti na’atube hikirum,> “Tur awamaim etitit i mar etei asinafumaim initurobe,
> naatu baibatiyen ana veya
> hai tur boro inisnowah.”

**5** Baise it ata sinaf kakafihimaim God ana sinaf gewasin bebeyan natit sabuw hina’i’itin ana veya, it boro mi’itube tanao? God baifuwenamaim yan so’ar baimakiy ebitit? it i anababatun taikoko’aw tao kwanekwan.

**6** Nati tur i men turobe tao’omih, anayabin sinaf nati na’atube nama’am, God boro mi’itube tafaram ana sabuw nibatiyih?

**7** Orot babin ta boro nagam nao, “Ayu i men bosunusunubayan, baise au bowabowamaim turobe God nowan tit irerereb sabuw hi’itin God hifai tebobora’ara’ah, aisim God ayu kakafu rouw eo ekukusairu?”

**8** Naatu iban boro kwanao maiye, kakafin tanasinaf, saise sinaf gewasin boro ine natit. Sabuw afa ayu iti na’atube o hirouw higam tiu’uwu, God nati sabuw boro baimakiy nitih.

## Men Yait Ta Gewasin

**9** Iti sawar ana an i abisa? It Jew ata gewasin i ra’at Eteni Sabuw tanatabirih? En anababatun! Jew sabuw naatu Eteni Sabuw etei i bowabow kakafin ana fair babanamaim tama’am.

**10** Buk Atamaninamaim iti na’atube hikirum,> “Orot babin men yait ta ana ef mutufurin, men kafa’imo orot ta’imon;

**11** > Men yait ta ana not rerekabin,
> men yait ta God enunuwih.

**12** > Etei hitatabir kouh God hitin,
> etei hisinaf kakaf hikowarar,
> men kafa’imo yait ta gewasin esisinaf,
> men kafa’imo orot ta’imon.[^7]

**13** > Sikah dub i tahahab sabuw tetotonan,
> menah kautabitabir ana efan matar,
> ufurihimaim kok tuwamorob awakirurin awan karatan.”

**14** > “Awah i orarafen awan karatan naatu tenakuyakuy.”

**15** > “Iti sabuw umah i rara awan karatan.

**16** > Menamaim tenan ma kakaf naatu gurugurusen mar etei imaim emamatar,

**17** > Naatu tufuwamaim ma ana ef i men hiso’ob.

**18** > Naatu God matanamaim men kafa’imo tibibiruw.”

**19** Imih it ata so’obamaim ofafar hikikirum i sabuw iyab ofafar babanamaim tema’am isah, saise awah etei nabofafaren, naatu tafaram wanawanan sabuw etei nabow nan God ana baibatiyenamaim hinatit.

**20** Isan imih God boro men sabuw ofafar tebobosiyasiyar imaim hai ef mutufurin narouw nabowamih, ofafar i orot babin ana bowabow kakafin botait ebirerereb.

**21** Baise boun God sabuw yamutufurih isan ana ef i boubun botawiy. Moses naatu dinab oro’orot nati isan i ofafaramaim hio’orereb tanowar, men ofafar tanabi’ufununimaim nabuwitamih.

**22** It yamutufuren boubun Godane enan i sabuw iyab Jesu Keriso tibitumitum imaim enan, naatu God matanamaim it etei ata’itinin i men tata’amih.

**23** Anayabin sabuw etei bowabow kakafin hisinaf naatu God ana gewasin bain isan umah kabom.

**24** Baise manaw kabeber God ana siwar na’atube Jesu Keriso wanawananamaim na it ata kakafihine tubunit[^8]botaitit tatit.

**25** Bowabow kakafin notawiyen isan God Keriso biyan siboromih yai, ana rara suwa re saise baitumatumamaim tanan biyan tanatit. Sawar iti na’atube sinaf saise bebeyan ti’obaiyit i ana ef i mutufurin. Anayabin I ana yatenub kakafiyin imaim bowabow kakafih marasika tasisinaf isubun rabon.

**26** Naatu iti boun it ata veya’amaim i turobe ebi’obaiyit i ana ef mutufurin. Imih i karam sabuw iyab Jesu tibitumitum boro nafufunih gewas nayamutufurih.

**27** Isan imih it boro abisa isan tanaora’ara’at? Ofafar tabobosiyasiyar isan tarara’at? En anababatun! Baise baitumatum isan i tarara’at.

**28** Anayabin sabuw iyab tibitumatum i hai ef mutufurin rouw ebowabow men ofafaramih.

**29** Imih kwanotanot God i Jew sabuw akisih hai God? Ai kwanotanot Ufun Sabuw auman hai God? Baise anao kwananowar, iti God i Ufun Sabuw auman hai God.

**30** Kwanaso’ob God i ta’imon men rou’ab, imih sabuw iyab hai ar kanabih hi’afuw o men hi’afuw etei i baitumatum ta’imonamaim hiyawas.

**31** Kwanotanot iti baitumatumamaim aki ofafar abobosa’ir? En anababatun, aki i ofafar aiwa’an tit ana efan bai ebirerereb.
