# 1 Corinthians 13

## Yabow

**1** Ayu i karam boro menau nabotabir sabuw hai turamaim anao, o tounamatar hai tur anao, baise auru yabow en na’at, au tur ao i naniyan en ber terab etefotef na’atube.

**2** Naatu ayu i God biyanane tur abai ao’orereb, ayu i so’ob wairafu, tur wa’iwa’irih hai kirikirifot etei aso’ob, ayu i baitumatum auru karam oyaw anao na’uy ra’ah nan. Baise auru yabow en na’at, ayu i au yabin en.

**3** Ayu karam au sawar etei yababan sabuw ana faramih naatu biyau ana kwahir siboromih hina’afun, baise auru yabow en na’at, ana gewasin boro men ta anab.

**4** Yabow i yatenuban, yabow i koununub gewasin, yabow i men baibobowenayan,[^16]men bai’o’orotoyan, men ora’ara’atayan, men baitengogorayan,

**5** yabow i men okwanekwaneyan, yabow men i taiyuwin ana gewasin enunuwetamih, men baiyaso’arayan, sabuw hai kakafih ana bukamaim men ekikirum,

**6** yabow kakafin isan men ebiyasisir, baise turobe isan i ebiyasisir.

**7** Yabow i mar etei etatafafar, mar etei ebitumatum, mar etei nuhifotamaim ema’am, mar etei ebitafofor.

**8** Yabow i ana yomanin en,[^17]dinabatur tao’orereb boro nasawar, menat ebotabir tur afa tao etei boro hinanutanub, abisa tasoso’ob etei boro nasisinasair.

**9** Anayabin it i turinawat taso’ob, naatu dinabatur tur i turinawat tao’orereb. boro men tutufin tanaorereb nan yomanin tanisawarimih.

**10** Baise koubaitotor ana veya nanan, sawar men koubatotoramaim ti’inu’in boro nasawar.

**11** Kek ana veya, kek hai tur ao, kek hai not anot, kek hai naniyanamaim ama, boun ana abi’orot ana veya, kek ana bowabow etei aihamiy.

**12** Abisa boun ta’i’itin i men ta’i’inan gewas, anayabin kiyam[^18]matan sukwayan na’atube, imih God men tai’itin gewas, baise veya nanan, imaibo yumatan boro tana’itin naatu itabo yumatat na’itin. Naatu sawar tutufin etei boro tanaso’ob nan na’asa’ub, boun i turinawat taso’ob.

**13** Naatu boun i sawar tounu tema’am, baitumatum, nuhifot, yabow, baise wanawanahimaim yabow i gagamin.

## Footnotes
