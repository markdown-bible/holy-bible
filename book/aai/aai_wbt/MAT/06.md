# Matthew 6

## Yababan Wairafih Baibaisih Ana Bai’obaiyen

**1** “Yate nanub, men iti baifa’i isan a bowabow gewasih sabuw nahimaim kwanabowamih, nati na’atube kwanasisinaf na’at, Tamat maramaim boro men a baiyan nitimih.

**2** “Imih veya ta akir wairafin sawar inabitin men inakurereb sabuw etei hinanowaramih, men sabuw wanawanah rerekabih Kou’ay Bar wanawanan, naatu ef gagamih yahimaim sabuw itih baifa’ih isan tisisinafube kwanasinafumih, anababatun a tur ao’owen, nati sabuw i boro men hai siwar ta God nitihimih.

**3** Isan imih akir wairafin inabibais, wa’iwa’iramaim inibais, saise a’of iti sisib ema’am men naso’ob o abisa kusisinaf.

**4** Iti sawar i wa’iwa’iramaim inasinaf, saise Tamat maramaim o abisa wa’iwa’iramaim isisinaf na’iti boro a baiyan nit.

## Jesu Yoyoban Ibi’obaiyih

**5** “Naatu yoyobanamih men wanawanah rerekabih tisisinafube, kwanasinafumih, anayabin i tekokok Kou’ay Bar wanawanan hinabat hinayoyoban, o ef na’ih awah kaukusebamaim hinabat hinayoyoban, saise sabuw hina’itih, baise anababatun a tur ao’owen, nati sabuw hai baiyan i hibaika.

**6** Baise o yoyobanamih a bar inarun, etawan inahir, Tamat wa’iwa’iramaim ema’am isan inayoyoban, naatu Tamat abisa wa’iwa’iramaim isinaf i’itin boro a baiyan nit.

**7** Naatu yoyobanamih men a tur nidun namomonamih, Eteni Sabuw iyab God men hisusu’ub i na’atube tisisinaf. Tenotanot yoyoban manin hinayoyoban God boro hai yoyoban nanowar.

**8** Men i hai sinaf kwani’u’ur, anayabin abisa inotanot Tamat i so’obabo o kubifefeyan.

**9** Imih iti na’atube kwanayoyoban,> ‘Tamai maramaim,
> O wab i kakafiyin.

**10** > A aiwob tan,
> tafaramamaim a kok atasinaf,
> maramaim tisisinaf na’atube,

**11** > Biyai ana fair isan mar etei ai bay initi.

**12** > Ai kakafih kunotawiyen,
> taituwai hai kakafin anonotawiyen na’atube.

**13** > Men routobonamaim inanawiyi’imih,
> baise kakafihine inatar fafari, a aiwob, a fair, a marakaw nama wanatowan, Amen.’

**14** “Taituwa o isa bowabow kakafin hisisinaf inanotawiyen, Tamat maramaim o a kakafih auman boro nanotawiyen.

**15** Baise hai kakafih men inanotanotwiyen na’at, o a kakafin auman Tamat boro men nanotawiyen.

## Foufur Ana Bai’obaiyen

**16** “Kwanayoyohar[^7]ana veya men sabuw wanawanah rerekabih kwani’u’urih, anayabin nati na’atube teyoyohar, saise taituwah hina’itih yoharayah hinarouw hinifa’ih isan. Anababatun a tur ao’owen nati sabuw hai baiyan i hibaika.

**17** Baise kwanayoyohar ana veya yumat kwanasouwen arib raiy kwanay kwanisuwar,

**18** saise sabuw afa boro men hina so’ob kwa i kwayoyohar, baise Tamat wa’iwa’iramaim ema’ama, o abisa akisimo isinaf i’itin boro a baiyan nit.

## Maramaim I Sawar Moumurih

**19** “A sawar gewasih iti tafaramamaim inaya inatototo, ganidor boro na’aan fufur naatu simur boro na’arah, naatu bainowah auman boro a bar hinakwib hinarun a sawar hinabain.

**20** Imih a sawar etei maramaim inaya inatoto, saise ganidor boro men hina’aan, naatu simur boro men na’arah, naatu bainowah boro men a bar hinakwib hinarun hina bainuwihimih.

**21** A toto abuyoy menamaim kuya’aya, o dogor boro imaim mar etei nama.

## Matat I Biyat Ana Marakaw

**22** “Matat i biyat ana hinow, matat gewasin, biyat etei boro marakawin.

**23** Baise matat kakafin, biyat tutufin etei boro gugumin kakafin, naatu nati gugumin i wanu’umin kakafin.

## Orot Rou’ab Isah Men Kwanabow

**24** “Orot ta’imon men karam boro orot gagamih rou’ab isah nabow, ana gewasin orot ta i boro nakwahir, naatu orot ta isan niyabow, orot ta ana tur i boro nanowar naatu orot ta boro nifutuw.

## Men Kwaniyababan

**25** “Isan imih a tur ao’owen, ayawas isan men kwaniyababan, a tom isan, biya isan, naatu ar faifuw isan. Kwanotanot yawas i sawar gagamin ar faifuw naatu a tom hairi natabirih?

**26** Kwanuw mamu kwa’itah, men masaw tebob naatu men masaw tefafour, na’atube aurih bay ana korom en[^8]baise Tamat maramaim kaifih bay ebitih. Imih kwa i men mamu ekakaifih na’atube ekakaifimih.

**27** Imih yababan men karam boro a yawas nakumanin kwanama’amih[^9]

**28** “Naatu aisim ar faifuw isah kwabiyababan? Sigar beran kwanuw kwa’itih, men kafai hibow raro’oh ebababan, o biyah isan ar faifuw tesasakiramih.

**29** Baise a tur ao’owen, aiwob orot Solomon toto buyoy wairafin, ana faifuw eo’osen i men ta iti beran ana gewasinabe’emih.

**30** God kaiyar fotan ebi’osen wainih kwana’itin, fotan iti boun natasasar, maras boro nakimow nare wairaf wan hinayara’aten hina’arah. Baise kwa i Tamat God uman yanamaim ekakaifi, aisim men kwabitumitum?

**31** Imih men kwaniyababan kwanao, au ar au faifuw boro menamaim anabow ana’osen, naatu bay boro menamaim anabow ana, harew menamaim anahun anatom?

**32** Eteni Sabuw i sawar iti isah tibiyababan, naatu Tamat maramaim kwa a yababan etei i so’ob.

**33** Baise wan i mar ana aiwob naatu ma gewasin[^10]kwananuwih kwanab, naatu sawar iti etei boro tafan naya’abar nit kwanabow.

**34** Isan imih maras isan men kwaniyababan, anayabin maras ibo akisin ana yababan. Na’atube veya ta’ita’imon hai yababan men inabo’ay inanot a yababan nara’atamih.
