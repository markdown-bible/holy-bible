# Luke 22

## Jesu Ana Morob Isan Baban Hio.

**1** Faraw Wanawanan Yeast en ana hiyuw wabin Tar Nowaten aa isan ana veya i na kabom.

**2** Naatu nati ana veya’amaim firis ukwarih naatu Ofafar Bai’obaiyenayah wa’iwa’iramaim ef hinunuwet mi’itube Jesu hitab hitarab tamorob isan, anayabin bebeyanamaim sinafumih sabuw hibiruwih.

## Judas Ibasit Jesu Morob Isan Baban Eo

**3** Nati ana veya’amaim, Jesu ana bai’ufununayah 12 wanawanahimaim orot Judas wabin ta Iscariot Satan dogoron wanawanan run.

**4** Basit na firis ukwarih naatu Tafaror Bar ma’utenayah hai orot ukwarih biyah tit, Jesu mi’itube nab nabitih isan bairi hiyakitifuw.

**5** Naatu orot ukwarih hiyasisir Judas kabay baitinin isan hio’matan.

**6** Judas ibasit naatu ef ana gewasin wa’iwa’iramaim nuwet, mi’itube Jesu tab tabitih, sabuw men hitaso’ob.

## Jesu Ana Bay Aafufun

**7** Faraw Wanawanan Yeast en hiyuw ana veya natit, nati ana veya’amaim i Tar Nowaten isan sheep natunatuh lamb terouw sibor teya’aya.

**8** Imih Peter, John hairi Jesu eobaimanih iyafarih eo, “Kwanan Tar Nowaten ana hiyuw kwanabogaigiwas tanaa.”

**9** Hibatiy hio, “Menamaim kukokok boro ana bogaigiwas?”

**10** Jesu iyafutih eo, “Kwanan Jerusalem kwanatitit auman orot ta boro noukwatamaim harew nahun na’abar nanan bairi kwanitar, naatu kwani’ufunun bairi kwanan bar erurumaim kwanarun.

**11** Naatu bar matuwan orot kwanibatiy, ‘Bai’obaiyenayan,’ o isa iti na’atube eo. ‘Ayu au bai’ufununayah bairi nanawan bar awan menatan boro imaim Tar Nowaten ana bay anaa?’

**12** Naatu i boro bar awan gagamin yate tafan ni’obaiyi kwana’itin, nati’imaim sawar etei hirereb hiyabuna inu’in, imaim kwanabogaigiwas.”

**13** Orot rou’ab hitit hinan sawar etei Jesu eo na’atube hi’itah, basit Tar Nowaten hiyuw hibogaigiwas.

**14** Bay aa isan ana veya tit, naatu Jesu ana tur abarayah bairi bay aa ana efanamaim himare.

**15** Baise iuwih eo, “Ayu akokok kwanekwan iti Tar Nowaten ana bay kwa bairit tanaa, imaibo ani’akir biyau nababan.

**16** Anayabin a tur ao’owen, ayu i boro men kafa’imo iti bay anaa maiye, ana veya’amaim iti sawar yabin anababatun niturobe God ana aiwob wanawananamaim.”

**17** Imaibo Jesu kerowas bai, God ana merar yi, naatu ana bai’ufununayah itih eo, “Kerowas iti kwabai etei’imak kwafaram kwatom.

**18** A tur ao’owen, veya iti boun ebubusuruf ayu iti wine boro men anatom, ayu boro iti na’atube ana ma nan God ana aiwob nan natit.”

**19** Naatu rafiy bai God ana merar yi sawar, imasib ana bai’ufununayah itih eo, “Iti i ayu biyou, kwa abit, iti na’atube kwanasinaf saise imaim ayu kwananuhu.”

**20** Naatu kerowas isan i na’atube sinaf, bay hi’aa ufunamaim bai itih eo. “Iti kerowas i God ana Obaibasit Boubun ayu au rara’amaim ibour kwa etei isa ebisuwai.

**21** Baise kwanaso’ob orot yait ayu morobomih babau eo, i iti gemamaim airi ama’am.

**22** Orot Natun i boro namorob God isan yayakitifuw na’atube, baise orot yait baban eo emomorob i boro baimakiy gagamin na’in nab.”

**23** Basit bai’ufununayah taiyuwi hibabatiyih hio, “Iti kou’ay wanawanamaim o yait iti na’atube sinafumih iyakitifuw ima kunotanot?”

## Bai’ukwarin Isan Iigam

**24** Bai’ufununayah taiyuwih wanawanahimaim gamin matar hai kokok hitaso’ob yait i orot gagamin.

**25** Jesu iuwih eo, “Tafaram wanawanan Eteni Sabuw hai aiwob ana sabuw kaifih isan i hai fair ema’am imih sabuw tekakaifih, naatu sabuw iyab onowaten hibai sabuw tebobonawiyih, i tekokok wabih nara’at Bonawiyenayah hinarouw.

**26** Baise kwa wanawanamaim i men na’atube nama, o yait orot gagamin mataramih taiyuw inayare inikek, naatu o yait bonawiyenayan mataramih taituwa isah ini’akir inabow.

**27** Yait i orot gagamin, orot bay aayan o orot bay semorayan? Orot mare ma bay eaau i turobe orot gagamin. Baise ayu kwa wanawanamaim i akir wairafin na’atube ama kwa isa abowabow.

**28** Au routobon fokarih wanawananamaim men kwaihamiyu, baise sisibu’umaim mar etei bairi tama’am.

**29** Tamai aiwob ana fair itu abi’aiwob[^12]na’atube nati fair ta’imon kwa abit kwani’aiwob[^13]

**30** Kwa boro au aiwob wanawananamaim kwanaa, kwanatom yasisir kwanab, aiwob hai ura ma’ama’amaim kwanamare Israel sabuw hai big etei 12 kwanakaifih.”

## Peter, Jesu Yaub

**31** Jesu eo, “Simon, Simon! Satan i baibasit God biyanane bai, kwa routobon nit nakubaituturimih. Orot masaw bowayan ana rice erab erububunai ani’aanin nabin eyai, naatu kanabin nabin eya’iyai na’atube.

**32** Baise Simon, ayu o isa ayoyoyoban, saise a baitumatum men nara’iy, naatu ayu isou inamamatabir ana veya, taituwa koufair initih hinabatkikin.”

**33** Baise Peter iya’afut eo, “Regah ayu airit na dibur run naatu na morob isan abobuna ama’am.”

**34** Jesu eo, “Peter, boun gugumin o boro mar tounu inayoub inao, ‘Ayu orot nati men aso’ob,’ i ufunamaim kokorere boro nao inanowar.”

## Kaukut, Hafoy, Naatu Kaiy

**35** Imaibo Jesu ana bai’ufununayah ibatiyih eo, “Kwanotanot ayu ai yuni aur kaukut, hafoy naatu a baibiyon en kwatit kwanan ana veya’amaim, sawar afa isah kwaiyababan.” Hiya’afut hio, “Men abistan ta isan ai yababanamih.”

**36** Jesu iuwih eo, “Baise a tur ao’owen, o yait aur kaukut, o hafoy ema’am inab, naatu o yait aur kaiy en, a biya baibiyon inab inan hinatubun naatu kabay inab kaiy inatubun uma inisinfafar.

**37** Anayabin a tur ao’owen, Buk Atamaninamaim iti na’atube hio hikirum, ‘Hi’i’itin i bainowan mowanabe,’ naatu nati tur i ayu isou hikirum, naatu boro anasinaf nan yabin namatar’’”

**38** Bai’ufununayah hio, “Regah kaiy rou’ab iti abobotanen kui’itan.” Jesu iyafutih eo, “Nati ana fofonin.”

## Jesu Olive Oyawemaim Yoyoban

**39** Jesu ana bai’ufununayah bairi matan fufur tisisinaf na’atube Jerusalem hihamiy hiyen hin Olive Oyawemaim hitit.

**40** Jesu na nati efanamaim titit ana veya, basit ana bai’ufununayan iuwih eo, “Kwanayoyoban saise routobon nanan boro kwanarukouw.”

**41** Imaibo in fotafot wan ana fofonin na’atube, sun yowen yoyoban

**42** eo, “Tamai inakokok na’at, basit iti biyababan ana kerowas biyau’umaim kubosair, men ayu au kok, baise abisa o kukokok na’atube namatar.”

**43** Jesu dogoron wanawan yababan yan awan karatan, dogoron tutufin etei yoyoban ana foyab rara na’atube hituhorohoror me yan hire, naatu God ana tounamatar marane hire hina fair hitin.[^14]

**45** Yoyoban sawar misir inan, ana bai’ufununayah matah fot hi’inu’in itih, hai yababan ra’at kwanekwan easbunih hi’inu’in.

**46** Iuwih eo, “Aisim kwa’inu’in? Kwamisir kwayoyoban saise men routobon kwanab.”

## Jesu Hibai Hifatum

**47** Jesu iti na’atube bat eo auman, bai’ufununayah 12 wanawanahimaim orot ta wabin Judas sabuw rou’ay gagamin na’in nawiyih hina hitit. Naatu Judas mutufor in Jesu biyan tit bai mamay.

**48** Baise Jesu iu, “Judas o iti mamayenamaim Orot Natun ibai rakit sabuw kubitih?”

**49** Bai’ufununayah bairi hibatabat Jesu isan abisa mamatar hi’itin, basit hibatiy, “Regah karam boro kaiyomaim bairi aniyow?”

**50** Naatu ana bai’ufununayah orot ta ana kaiy bora’ah, Firis Gagamin ana akir wairafin ta tainin asukwafune buru’um.

**51** Baise Jesu eo, “Men inasinaf maiye kunutanub!” Naatu Jesu orot tainin butubun efanin matar maiye.

**52** Imaibo Jesu firis ukwarih, Tafaror Bar ana firis ukwarin, furisiman, naatu regaregah ai’in Jesu bain fatuminamih hinan iuwih eo, “Kwa iti kaiy, kefat yuwu rabu’umih kwabow kwanan ana itinin i ayu orot kakafu bainowan na’atube?

**53** Ayu mar etei Tafaror Bar gagamin wanawanan bairit tama’am, men kwakok boro imaim ayu kwatafatumu? Baise iti boun i kwa a veya, imih abisa kwakokok na’atube kwasinaf. Anayabin gugumin ana orot ebobonawiyi.”

## Peter, Jesu Yaub

**54** Jesu hibai hifatum hibai hin Firis Gagamin ana bar hitit, naatu Peter nabineika bat i’uf nunih bairi hin hitit.

**55** Wairaf merar yan foun hi’asir himare hima rararih, Peter na wanawanah mare bairi hima rararih,

**56** basit akir wairafin babitai ta nuw Peter ma wairaf rarar itin, naatu matan kubar itinbunai sawar imaibo eo, “Iti orot auman i Jesu hairi hima hireremor.”

**57** Baise Peter yaub eo, “Babitai, ayu nati orot i men kafa’i aso’ob.”

**58** Hima kafa’imo, orot ta itin maiye eo, “O i ni’i orot ana bai’ufununayan ta.” Baise Peter iya’afut eo, “Aro, ayu men nati orot ana bai’ufununayan ta’amih.”

**59** Hima one hour na’atube sawar, basit orot tabo iban eo maiye “Tur anababatun iti orot i Galilee matuwan Jesu hairi hima hireremor men kasikasiy ta’amih.”

**60** Baise Peter orot iya’afut eo, “Aro ayu men kafa’i aso’ob o abisa kuo.” Iti na’at eo auman kokorere eo.

**61** Regah tatabir mutufor nuw Peter itin, naatu Regah abisa eo i not, “Boun gugumin o boro mar tounu ayu inayayoubu ufunamaim kokorere boro nao.”

**62** Basit Peter misir tit in ufun re rerey i gagagamat.

## Jesu Baiyowayah Hi’i’iyab

**63** Orot afa Jesu hima’uh hima’am himisir faifuw ta hibai matan hisum,

**64** imaibo hi’i’iyab naatu hiborabirab hio, “Kuo anowar, yait o rabi?”

**65** Naatu tur kakafih moumurih maiyow hiu kwanikwaniy hi’i’iyab.

## Jesu Kanisel Nahimaim Bat Hibatiy

**66** Mar sibisib auman sabuw hai regaregah ai’in, firis ukwarih naatu Ofafar Bai’obaiyenayah etei hiru’ay hita’imon, imaibo Jesu hinawiy hina Kaniser nahimaim bat

**67** naatu hibatiy, “Kuo anowar o i Keriso?” Jesu iyafutih eo, “A tur ana’owen boro men kwanitutumu’umih,

**68** naatu ana bibatiyi auman boro men kwaniyafutu’umih.

**69** Baise mar enan Orot Natun boro God fairin uman asukwafune namare nama’am kwana’itin.”

**70** Naatu etei’imak hibatiy hio, “Bo o God Natun?” Jesu iyafutih eo, “Ayu God Natun kwarouw kwao i tur anababatun kwao.”

**71** Imaibo sabuw hio, “Men takokok koubuna’ayah sabuw afa tanabow, anayabin tur awanamaim titit i tainitamaim tanowar!”
