# Mark 10

## Kwahikwahiren

**1** Jesu nati efan ihamiy harew Jordan rabon rewan rounane Judea wanawananamaim tit. Ibanak maiye sabuw rou’ay gagamin maiyow hiru’ay naatu Jesu ana yawas mar etei esisinaf na’atube ma i’obaibiyih.

**2** Pharisee afa hina Jesu biyan hitit naatu hisinaftobon hitikubibiruw isan hibatiy hi’o, “Ku’o anowar ai ofafar eo i karam orot aawan boro nakwahir?”

**3** Jesu ibatiyih eo, “Moses ana ofafaramaim mi’itube eobaiyuni?”

**4** Hiya’afut hi’o, “Aki Moses ibasit orot aawan kwahirin isan boro kwahikwahiren ana fef nakirum babin nitin nab auman nan.”

**5** Baise Jesu iya’afutih eo, “Moses iti ofafar kirum, anayabin kwa obai’obaiyen tur nowar isan dogor i fokar kwanekwan.

**6** Baise aneika mar tafaram mamatar ana veya God ‘Orot babin hairi sinafen himatar.’[^4]

**7** ‘Ana an nati isan orot boro hinah tamah nihamiyih naatu i boro aawan hairi hinita’imon.[^5]

**8** Naatu rou’ab boro nan biyah ta’imon namatar.’ Imih i boro men biyah rou’ab baise biyah ta’imon.

**9** Isan imih orot babin hairi God bita’imonih men yait ta natarbounih.”

**10** Imaibo himatabir hin bar hititit ana veya Jesu ana bai’ufununayah hibatiy, “Iti tur anayabin i abisa?”

**11** Hai tur eowen eo, “Orot yait ta a wan nakwahir nare babin ta nabaib ana moser i takweb.[^6]

**12** Na’atube babin yait aawan nakwahir, nare orot ta nabaib ana moser i takweb.”[^7]

## Jesu Kek Gidigidih Igegewasinih

**13** Sabuw afa kek gidigidih hibow Jesu butubunih isan hinan, ana bai’ufununayah sabuw hikwararih hiotanih.

**14** Baise Jesu iti i’itin ana veya men iyasisir, naatu ana bai’ufununayah isah eo, “Kek gidigidih kwaihamiyih tena isou men kwanarufutih, anayabin God ana aiwob i kek gidigidih iti na’atube i nowah.

**15** Anababatun a tur ao’owen, o yait God ana aiwobomaim runamih, inayare ana itinin kek gidigidih inamamatar boro inarun.”

**16** Imaibo Jesu kek gidigidih buwih uman tafah yara’aten naatu igegewasinih.

## Orot Guguw Wairafin

**17** Jesu misir busuruf inan auman, orot ta nunuw na Jesu nanamaim tit sun yowen, ibatiy, “Bai’obaiyenayan gewas, abisa ana sinaf boro yawas wanatowan nowau’umih namatar?”

**18** Jesu iya’afut eo, “Aisimamih ayu gewasu irouw ku’o? God akisinamo i gewasin men yait ta.

**19** Ofafar o iso’ob. ‘Men asabunuwen isa nama, men turanah a’aawah ufuh inan, men inabain, men inakutabitabir, men inifufuwen, hinat tamat inabosiyasiyarih.’”

**20** Orot eo, “Bai’obaiyenayan, ayu kek kikimu ana veya iti ofafar etei’imak abosiyasiyar.”

**21** Jesu mutufor nuw orot itin naatu isan yabow auman iu, “Sawar ta’imonamo men isinaf. Inan a sawar etei sabuw hinatobon, kabay inab gagaganiy sabuw initih naatu o inan ayu i ni’ufnunu, saise maramaim o boro guguw wairafi inama.”

**22** Orot iti tur nonowar anamaramaim gubamin hurir naatu ereyababan auman in anayabin orot i guguw wairafin.

**23** Imaibo Jesu tatabir ana bai’ufununayah isah eo, “Guguw wairafih God ana aiwob efanamaim run isan i fokar kwanekwan.”

**24** Bai’ufununayah iti tur hinonowar hifofofor men kafaita, baise Jesu ibanak eo maiye, “Ayu natunatu, God ana aiwob run isan ana ef i fokar anababatun.

**25** Sinak ana sou’umaim camel sorabon isan i hamehamen maiyow boro nasorabon, baise orot guguw wairafih God ana aiwobomaim run isan i fokar kwanekwan.”

**26** Iti tur hinonowar bai’ufununayah hikasiy men kafaita naatu taiyuwih hima hibabatiyih, “Yait boro yawas nab?”

**27** Jesu mutuforomo nuw itih naatu iya’afutih eo, “Sabuw isah iti i fokar, baise God isan i hamehamen maiyow, anayabin sawar etei God boro nasinaf hinamatar.”

**28** Peter Jesu isan eo, “Kwi’itin aki sawar etei aihamiyen naatu o abi’ufnuni.”

**29** Jesu eo, “Anababatun a tur ao’owen, yait ta ana bar, taintuwan, ruburubun, hinah, tamah, natunatun, naatu ana tafaram ihamiyen, na’atube ayu isou naatu tur gewasin isan iti sawar bihamiyen.

**30** Iti boun ana veya i boro ana bar, taintuwan, ruburubun, hinahinah, natunatun naatu ana me etei i boro hundred ta’ita’imon tafan anayababar anitin, bai’akir kakafin auman, yomaninamaim boro yawas wanatowan nab.

**31** Baise sabuw moumurih maiyow boun wan tibi’iyon boro hini’uf naatu sabuw iyab tibi’uf boro hini’iyon.”

## Jesu Mar Baitaunin Ana Morob Isan Eo

**32** Jesu ana bai’ufununayah bairi hitit Jerusalem ana ef hibai naatu Jesu ana bai’ufununayah aunah i’iyon, ana bai’ufununayah ufun hinan hinuw hi’i’itin ana veya hai kasiy ra’at, naatu sabuw iyab hibi’ufunun hai bir ra’at. Jesu ana bai’ufununayah 12 buwih nabinamaim hin naatu busuruf hai tur eowen sawar boro mi’itube hinamamatar isan.

**33** Hai tur eowen eo, “Kwananowar, it i au Jerusalem tayey nati’imaim Orot Natun boro hinabonawiy nan firis hai ukwarih naatu Ofafar bai’obaiyenayah biyah natit. Imaim boro morobomih hinao naatu hinabonawiy nan Eteni Sabuw biyah.

**34** Imaim hini’i’iyab, hinakwaitututur, hinarab, naatu hina’asabun namorob, baise veya tounu ufunamaim i boro nayawas na misir maiye.”

## James Naatu John Hairi Hai Kok

**35** Zebedee natunatun rou’ab, James naatu John hairi hina Jesu biyan hitit naatu hifefeyan, “Bai’obaiyenayan aki akokok abisa isan anabifefeyani inasinaf.”

**36** Jesu ibatiyih, “Bo abistan kwakokok boro isa anasinaf?”

**37** Hiya’afut hi’o, “O a aiwob ana bonamanamarinamaim inabi’ukwarin ana veya, aki akokok ina’uwi ta uma a’asukwafune namare ta uma a beyawane namare.”

**38** Jesu iyafutih eo, “Kwa men kwaso’ob abistan isan kwabifefeyan? Karam biyababan ana kerowas ana tomatom boro kwanatom? Naatu morob wanawanan anarun ana momorob boro kwanarun?”

**39** Hiya’afut hi’o, “Aki karam.” Jesu uwih eo, “Biyababan ana harew i karam boro kwanatom naatu morob wanawanan boro kwanarun.

**40** Baise o ta au asukwafune mare naatu ta au beyawane mare i men karam boro ayu kwa anit. Nati i sabuw iyabowat God isah yayabuna boro nitih.”

**41** Bai’ufununayah nah ten iti tur hinonowar yah so’ar, James, John hairi isah.

**42** Basit Jesu etei’imak eafayuwih hina biyan hitit naatu uwih eo, “Eteni Sabuw wanawanahimaim orot hirurubin akisinamo ana fair ema’am boro sabuw nakaifih, naatu i akisinamo ana fairamaim boro nabonawiyih.

**43** Baise kwa wanawananamaim iti na’atube i men ema’ama, o yait kukokok iti kou’ay wanawanan orot gagam mataramih, o i boro turanah isah ini’akir inabow.

**44** naatu o yait kukokok iti kou’ay wanawanan wan bai’iyonamih, o i boro bai’akirayan inamatar.

**45** Anayabin Orot Natun i men sabuw isan bowamih na; baise sabuw isah nabow ana yawas isah ni’inuw, saise sabuw moumurih natubunih.”

## Oro Bartimias Matan Fim Jesu Iyawas

**46** Hina tafaram Jericho hitit, naatu Jesu ana bai’ufununayah bairi nati efan baihamiyinamih sabuw rou’ay gagamin maiyow hiru’ay, naatu orot matan fim fefeyanayan wabin Bartimais, Timias natun ef sisibinamaim ma’am.

**47** Jesu Nasaret mowan na inan hi’o nowar. Basit eaf, “Jesu David uwan kwiwanbabanu!”

**48** Sabuw moumurin maiyow orot matan fim hi’u awan fotamih. Baise men karam fanan aumetawat iwow eaf, “David uwan kwiwanbabanu!”

**49** Jesu an kutan naatu eo, “Kwa’af kwa’u ena.” Basit orot matan fim isan hi’af, “Yate inbainub! Kumisir, isa eafa’af.”

**50** Orot duku iwa’an misir ana faifuw tafan bosair yare remor na Jesu biyan tit.

**51** Jesu ibatiy, “Abistan kukokok o isa ana sinaf?” Matan fim iya’afut eo, “Bai’obaibiyenayan ayu akokok matu nigewasin ananuw maiye.”

**52** Jesu iu eo, “Kwen o abaitumatumamaim iyawasi.” Mar ta’imon matan igewasin nuw naatu busuruf Jesu efamaim inan i’ufunun hairi hin.
