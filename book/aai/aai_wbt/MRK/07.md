# Mark 7

## Kousouwen Naatu Gubagub

**1** Pharisee naatu Ofafar bai’obaiyenayah afa Jerusalemane hinan hina Jesu hibebera’uh.

**2** Naatu ana bai’ufununayah umah souwena’e eregubagub auman hi’aa hitomatom hi’itih.

**3** Pharisee naatu Jew sabuw etei’imak i hai binanakwar ti’ufunun hai a’agir hi’o na’atube umah tesouwenabo te’aau.

**4** Ahar efanane tenan boro men abistan hina’aan, baise wantoro’ot i boro hinakif nakusouwih, naatu hai binanakwar afa maumurih maiyow tebi’ufunun, boun kerowas, naukwat, ya’aya baibitab ana noukwat, i na’atube tesasouwen.

**5** Imih Pharisee naatu Ofafar bai’obaiyenayah Jesu hibatiy, “Aisimamih o abai’ufununayah bai’obaiyen it ata a’agir hibai nan men tibi’ufununimih, naatu baise i umah gubagub auman te’aau?”

**6** Iyafutih eo, “Isaiah kwa arerekabih isan eo i turobe, Bukamaim eo kikirum na’atube,> ‘Iti sabuw awahimaim ayu tekakafiyu,
> baise dogoroh ayu isau i ef yokaika inu’in.

**7** > I hai kwafiren ayu isau i yabin en,
> anayabin i hai bai’obaiyen i orot ofafar hiya’aya imaim tebi’obaibiyih!’

**8** Kwa God ana obaiyunen kwabosair sa’ab kwayai naatu orot hai bai’obaiyen kwabai kwabukikin kwanan.”

**9** Naatu Jesu iuwih eo, “Kwa God ana obaiyunen bosairen sa’ab ya’in naatu taiyuw abinanakwar bai’ufnunin isan i kwaso’ob kwanekwan!

**10** Moses na’atube eo, ‘hinat tamat inakakafiyih, yait ta hinah tamah erarafih i boro hina’asabun namorob.’

**11** Baise o ta hinat tamat isah iti na’atube inao, abistan ayu’une kwa abibaisi i God ana sibor yayasairen.

**12** Tur nati na’atube nao, naatu boro men kafa’imo hinah tamah isah abisa ta nasinafumih.

**13** Kwa a’a’agir hai binanakawar kwabukikin naatu God ana tur i kamomow ebi’en naatu sawar afa moumurih maiyow auman nati na’atube kwasisinaf.”

**14** Iban maiye Jesu sabuw rou’ay gagamin eafih hina iuwih eo, “Kwa etei iti tur anao i kwananowar naniyan kwanab.

**15** Men sawar iti ufunane en orot wanawanan irun iwa’an gub kakafin etatounimih, baise abisa orot wanawananane etitit imaim iwa’an orot gub kakafih etatoun.

**16** Tain hinama’am na’at tur kwanonowar kwananot.”

**17** Sabuw rou’ay gagamin ihamiyih in bar rur ufunamaim ana bai’ufununayah iti oroubon isan hibatiy.

**18** Iuwih eo, “Kwa auman boro’ika kwakakasiy? men abistan ta ufunane en erun iwa’an gub kakafin orot etatounimih.

**19** Anayabin men orot dogoronamaim erurumih, baise en yan kabutin wanawanan erur naatu eafuwatait ere’er.” Iti na’atube eo ana maramaim Jesu kurerereb eo, “Bay etei’imak i gewasih kwanaa.”

**20** Naatu ibanak eo maiye, “Abistan orot wanawananane etitit imaim iwa’an i’isan gub kakafin etatoun.

**21** Anayabin orot dogoron wanawanantoro’ot etitit, i not kakafin, sesebar, bar kweb, asabunubunuw, turahinah a’aawah ufuh na,

**22** kabat, tur karur, tenagogor, baiwa’an ana yeyewra’aten, bahiy, koutabitabir, bai’o’orot, naatu baifufuwen.

**23** Iti not kakafih etei’imak i orot wanawanane enan imaim iwa’an i’isan gub kakafin etatoun.”

## Babin Ana Baitumatum

**24** Jesu efan nati ihamiy in tafaram Taiya wanawananamaim tit, naatu bar wanawanan run, men kok sabuw hitaso’ob i nati’imaim ma’am, baise men karam boro yumatan tibun.

**25** Nati’imaim babin natun babitai kikimin afiy kakafin tarasum ma bi’a’afiy Jesu ana tur nowar na biyan tit anamaim ra’iy.

**26** Babin i Greek matuwan, baise ana tufuw an i Fonisia imaim tufuw tafaram Syria wanawanan, i Jesu ifefeyan natun biyanamaim afiy kakafin tanun tatit isan.

**27** Baise Jesu babin isan eo, “Wantoro’ot i boro kek abisa tekokok tanituwih, men ef ema’am boro kek hai bay tanab tanarub haru nahimaim nare hinab hina’aan.”

**28** Baise babin eo, “Turobe Regah baise kek hai bay te’aa momosarih gem baban tere’er haru te’aau.”

**29** Imaibo Jesu babin iu, “O abaiya’afotenamaim imih kwen, afiy o natu ihamiyika.”

**30** Babin matabir in ana bar tit ana kek gem yan inu’in itin afiy ihamiy titaka.

## Jesu Orot Tainin Gugurin Naatu Awan Gugin Iyawas

**31** Imaibo Jesu tafaram Taiya ihamiy Sidon wanawananamaim remor in Galilee harew kukuf yan re, naatu rabon Bar Merar Etei Umat Ronron hai me yan tit.

**32** Nati’imaim sabuw afa orot tainin gugurin, naatu menan sarusarubet i hibai hina Jesu biyan hitit naatu uman biyan tabutubun isan hifefeyan.

**33** Imih Jesu orot bai tit sabuw rou’ay gagamin ihamiyih akisinamo nabinamaim, naatu uman orot tainin wanawanan iuturiy, imaibo kwaitutur naatu orot menan butubun.

**34** Matan au mar nuw ra’at dogoron tutufin erawous naatu orot isan eo, “Efata,” anayabin “Kubotawiy!”

**35** Mar ta’imonamo orot tainin botawiy tur nowar naatu menan yamutufur tur eo gaigiwas.

**36** Naatu Jesu sabuw eotanih eo, “Men yait ta ana tur kwana’owen.” Baise i mar etei sisinaf imih mar etei isan hio.

**37** Sabuw anababatun hai kasiy ra’at hifofofor men kafaita, naatu hio “Sawar etei’imak sinafen gewas! Naatu i karam boro niwa’an tainih gugurih tur hinanowar naatu awah gugih tur hinao!”
