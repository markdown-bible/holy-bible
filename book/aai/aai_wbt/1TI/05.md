# 1 Timothy 5

## Sinaf Gewasin Baitumatumayah Isah

**1** Orot gagamin[^2]men kwararin yamutufurin isan iniwa’an, baise tamat na’atube koufair initih. Naatu orot baubuh tait na’atube isah inasinaf.

**2** Baibina’ah inakakafiyih o hinat kukakafiy na’atube, naatu baibin baubuh not gewasinamaim ruburub na’atube inakaifih hinama.

**3** Kwafur baibin iyab baibais tekokok ina’itih inibaisih.

**4** Baise kwafur babin yait natunatun o wawawan auman, gewasin nati kek i boro hai Kirisiyan ana bowabow hinasinaf hai nibur isah naatu hinah tamah, uwahinah hikakaifih isan wan hinay hinibaisih, anayabin nati i God ana kokok gagamin.

**5** Kwafur babin yait ema’ama sibasiba’u, naatu orot babin men yait ebibais, i ana not ana baitumatum etei i God nitin fai mar God baibaisin isan nayoyoban.

**6** Baise kwafur babin yait biyan ana yasisir ebaib, biyan ufunane i yawasin baise wanawanan i murubin.

**7** Iti roube’aten tur auman ini’obaiyih hinaso’ob, saise hinasinaf gewas naatu men yait ta ubar nitih.

**8** Orot babin yait ta tain tuwan men ebi’u’uwanih, i taiyuwin ana nibur na’atube, nati orot i ana baitumatum eyayaub naatu sabuw baitumatum atih natabirih kowarar anababatun.

**9** Kwafur babin yait ana tabin ta’imon, naatu ana kwamur 60 sasawar i wabin kwafukwafur hai bukamaim kwanakirum.

**10** Wabin gewasin, kek baituwayan, bai merarayowayan, God ana sabuw ah souwenayan, sabuw iyab yababan tebaib ebibaisih, naatu sawar gewasih sinaf isan mar etei ana not imaim yai esisinaf.

**11** Baise baibin baubuh iyab kwafur tema’ama wabih men inakirum, anayabin hinama’am hai naniyan tabinamih hinabiwa’an, kouh boro Keriso hinitin hinatabin,

**12** naatu taiyuwih hai kakafinamaim boro bit hinab, anayabin hai omatanen wantoro’ot hio’omatan i hi’astu’ub.

**13** Men nati akisin, baise boro hinanokow ah yan nadeder bar, bar hinarun hinatit hinaremor kwanekwan, naatu men nokonokow akisin, baise boro yanuwayah hinamatar, naatu sabuw afa hai bowabow baimateteyan isah okwanekwaneyah hinamatar, naatu sawar afa isah men hitao i boro hinao.

**14** Imih ayu akokok baibin iyab baubuh kwafur tema’am i hinatabin kek hinabow hinituw, naatu hai nibur hinakaifih, saise ata rakit sabuw uwit isan ana ef tinunuwet boro tanahir.

**15** Anayabin kwafukwafur afa i hitatabiraka Satan tibi’ufunun.

**16** Baise baitumatumayan babin yait ana nibur wanawananamaim kwafur baibin hinama’am na’at nibaisih, men ekalesia hinab, saise ekalesia i kwafur baibin iyab anababatun tibiyababan i hinibaisih.

**17** Ai’in[^3]iyab ekaleisia ana bowabow tebobonawiy gewas i kwanakakafiyih naatu hai ma gewas isan tafan kwanaya’abar auman kwanibaisih, sabuw iyab binanuyah naatu bai’obaiyenayah i kwana’itih baibais gewasin kwanitih.

**18** Anayabin bukamaim iti na’atube eo, “Ox rice nawawaskweyakweyar awan men kwana’utan,” naatu “Bowayah hai baiyan i kwanitih.”

**19** Ain orot ana kakafin hinabow hinan hinao kwananonowar men saise sinaf isan kwananotamih, baise orot rou’ab o tounu hinan sif hinaruboun kwananowarabo kwanasinaf.

**20** sabuw iyab bowabow kakafin hinasisinaf bebeyanamaim inakwararih inayamutufurih, saise sabuw afa baimatnuwen hinab.

**21** God nanamaim, naatu Keriso Jesu na’atube tounamatar kakafiyih etei matahimaim ayu tur fokarin maiyow au’uwi, iti raube’aten tur inabukikin naatu sabuw etei isah i ana fofoninamaim ina’uwih, men ta aukoun inabat ta inarukauwimih.

**22** Mata men nakabiy Regah ana bowabow isan sabuw inabow fair initih, naatu sabuw afa hai kakafin bairi men kwanafaram, biya inakubaitutur gewas.

**23** Men harew akisin inatom, wine kikimin inisuwai auman inatom, anayabin ya ana babaninamaim o mar etei kusasawow.

**24** Sabuw afa hai kakafih i boro marta’imon bebeyan ina’inan, naatu baibatiyen hinab, baise sabuw afa i wa’iwa’iramaim kakafih tisisinaf ufibo tibirerereb.

**25** Ef nati ta’imon sawar gewasih i bebeyan tai’itah, naatu men abisa ta isisinaf boro wa’iwa’irin na’inumih etei boro hinan rerereb yan hinatit.
