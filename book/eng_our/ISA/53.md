# Isaiah 53

**1** [^210]> *Who has trusted our message?*[^211]> > *To whom has the arm of Adonai been revealed?*[^212]

**2** [^213]> *For he grew up before him as a tender plant,*> > *and as a root out of dry ground.*[^214]> *He has no good looks or majesty.*> > *When we see him, there is no beauty that we should desire him.*

**3** [^215]> *He was despised,*[^216]> > *and rejected by men;*> *a man of suffering,*[^217][^218]> > *and acquainted with disease.*> *He was despised*[^219]> *as one from whom men hide their face;*> > *and we didn’t respect him.*


**4** [^220]> *Surely he has borne our sickness,*[^221]> > *and carried our suffering;*[^222]> *yet we considered him plagued,*[^223]> > *struck by God, and afflicted.*

**5** [^224]> *But he was pierced for our rebellious breaches of relationships.*[^225]> > *He was crushed for our depravities (moral evils).*[^226]> *The punishment that brought our peace was on him;*[^227]> > *and by his stripes and wounds we are healed and made whole.*[^228]

**6** [^229]> *All we like sheep have gone astray.*> > *Everyone has turned to*[^230]> > *his own way;*
[^231]> > *and Adonai has laid on him את the depravity (moral evil) of us all.*

**7** [^232]> *He was oppressed,*> > *yet when he was afflicted he didn’t open his mouth.*[^233]> *As a lamb that is led to the slaughter,*[^234]> > *and as a sheep that before its shearers is silent,*
> > *so he didn’t open his mouth.*[^235]

**8** [^236]> *After forcible arrest and*[^237]> *sentencing, he was taken away;*> > *את and none of his generation protested*
[^238]> > *his being cut off out of the land of the living*[^239]
> > *for the rebellious breach of relationship of my people, who deserved the punishment themselves.*

**9** [^240]> *They made his grave with את the wicked,*[^241]> > *את and with a rich man in his death;*[^242][^243]> *although he had done no violence,*> > *nor was any deceit in his mouth.*[^244]


**10** [^245]> *Yet it pleased Adonai to bruise him.*> > *He has caused him to suffer.*[^246]> *To see if he would*[^247]> *present himself as a fault incurring guilt offering.*> > *If he does, he will see his offspring,*[^248]> *and he will prolong his days,*> > *and Adonai ’s pleasure will prosper in his hand.*

**11** [^249]> *After this ordeal, he will see satisfaction*> > *“By his knowing [pain and sacrifice].*[^250]> *My righteous*[^251]> *servant will justify many;*> > *for it is their depravities (moral evils) that he suffers.*

**12** [^252]> *Therefore will I give him a portion with the great,*[^253]> > *את and with the strong he will divide the spoils;*> *because*[^254]> *he exposed himself to death,*[^255]> > *and was counted with את the rebellious who break away from authority thus breaking relationship;*[^256][^257]> *while actually bearing the crime deserving peanlty, sin ·missing the goal· of many,*[^258][^259]> > *and interceding for the rebellious who break away from authority thus breaking relationship.*
