# Isaiah 6

**1** In the year that king Uzziah died, I saw 'Adonay [Lord] sitting on a throne, high and lifted up; and his train filled the temple.

**2** Above him stood the seraphim. Each one had six wings.[^12]With two he covered his face. With two he covered his feet. With two he flew.

**3** One called to another, and said,> “More holy than the holiest holiness is Adonai Tzva'ot [Yahweh Commander of heaven’s armies]![^13]> > The whole earth is full of his kavod ·weighty glory·!”

**4** The foundations of the thresholds shook at the voice of him who called, and the house was filled with smoke.[^14]

**5** Then I said, “Woe is me! For I am undone, because I am a man of unclean lips, and I dwell among a people of unclean lips: for my eyes have seen the King, Adonai Tzva'ot [Yahweh Commander of heaven’s armies]!”

**6** Then one of the seraphim flew to me, having a live coal in his hand, which he had taken with the tongs from off the altar.

**7** He touched my mouth with it, and said, “Behold, this has touched your lips; and your depravity (moral evil) is taken away, and your habitual sin ·missing the mark· forgiven.”

**8** I sh'ma ·heard obeyed· the voice of 'Adonay [Lord], saying, “Whom shall I send, and who will go for us?” Then I said, “Here I am. Send me!”

**9** [^15][^16]*He said, ‘Go, and tell this people,*> *“You sh'ma ·hear obey· sh'ma ·hear obey·*> > *yet still don’t understand;*> *and you indeed see,*> > *but don’t perceive.”*[^17]

**10** > *Make the heart of this people fat.*> > *Make their ears heavy, and shut their eyes;*> *lest they see with their eyes,*> > *and sh'ma ·hear obey· with their ears,*
> > *and understand with their heart,*
> > *and turn again, and be healed.’*[^18][^19]

**11** Then I said, “Lord, how long?” He answered,> “Until cities are waste without inhabitant,> > and houses without man,
> > and the land becomes utterly waste,

**12** > > And Adonai has removed men far away,
> > and the forsaken places are many within the land.

**13** > If there is a tenth left in it,> > that also will in turn be consumed:> as a terebinth, and as an oak, whose stock remains when they are felled;> > so the holy seed is its stock.”
