# Psalms 62


**1** > My soul rests in God alone.> > My yishu'ah ·salvation· comes from him.

**2** > He alone is my rock and my yishu'ah ·salvation·, my fortress—> > I will never be greatly shaken.

**3** > How long will you assault a man,> > would all of you throw him down,
> > Like a leaning wall, like a tottering fence?

**4** > They fully intend to throw him down from his lofty place.> > They delight in lies.
> > They bless with their mouth, but they curse inwardly. Selah ·contemplation with musical interlude·.

**5** > My soul, wait in silence for God alone,> > for my expectation is from him.

**6** > He alone is my rock and my yishu'ah ·salvation·, my fortress.> > I will not be shaken.

**7** > With God is my yesha' ·salvation· and my kavod ·weighty glory·.> > The rock of my strength, and my refuge, is in God.

**8** > Trust in him at all times, you people.> > Pour out your heart before him.
> > God is a refuge for us. Selah ·contemplation with musical interlude·.

**9** > Surely men of low degree are just a breath,> > and men of high degree are a lie.> In the balances they will go up.> > They are together lighter than a breath.

**10** > Don’t trust in oppression.> > Don’t become vain in robbery.> If riches increase,> > don’t set your heart on them.

**11** > God has spoken once;> > twice I have sh'ma ·heard obeyed· this,
> > that power belongs to God.

**12** > Also to you, Lord, belongs chesed ·loving-kindness·,> > for you reward every man according to his work.[^124]
