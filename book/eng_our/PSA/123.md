# Psalms 123


**1** > To you I do lift up my eyes,> > you who sit in the heavens.

**2** > Behold, as the eyes of servants look to the hand of their master,> > as the eyes of a maid to the hand of her mistress;
> > so our eyes look to Adonai, our God,
> > until he has mercy on us.

**3** > Have mercy on us, Adonai, have mercy on us,> > for we have endured much contempt.

**4** > Our soul is exceedingly filled with the scoffing of those who are at ease,> > with the contempt of the proud.
