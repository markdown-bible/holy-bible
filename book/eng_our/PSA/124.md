# Psalms 124


**1** > If it had not been Adonai who was on our side,> > let Israel [God prevails] now say,

**2** > if it had not been Adonai who was on our side,> > when men rose up against us;

**3** > then they would have swallowed us up alive,> > when their wrath was kindled against us;

**4** > then the waters would have overwhelmed us,> > the stream would have gone over our soul;

**5** > then the proud waters would have gone over our soul.

**6** > Blessed be Adonai,> > who has not given us as a prey to their teeth.

**7** > Our soul has escaped like a bird out of the fowler’s snare.> > The snare is broken, and we have escaped.

**8** > Our help is in Adonai’s name,> > who made heaven and earth.
