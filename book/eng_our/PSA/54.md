# Psalms 54


**1** > Save me, God, by your name.> > Vindicate me in your might.

**2** > Sh'ma ·Hear obey· my prayer, God.> > Listen to the words of my mouth.

**3** > For strangers have risen up against me.> > Violent men have sought after my soul.
> > They haven’t set God before them. Selah ·contemplation with musical interlude·.

**4** > Behold, God is my helper.> > 'Adonay [Lord] is the one who sustains my soul.

**5** > He will repay the evil to my enemies.> > Destroy them in your truth.

**6** > With a free will offering, I will sacrifice to you.> > I will yadah ·extend hands in thankful praise· to your name, Adonai, for it is good.

**7** > For he has delivered me out of all trouble.> > My eye has seen triumph over my enemies.
