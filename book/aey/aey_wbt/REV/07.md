# JAHUNEC JE CAMASAC MUDEN 7

## Anut uqa Isrel dana 144,000 sisi maden

**1** Ceteh eu hedocob ija ensel 4-pela age maha medeh 4-pelana tatawegin fem. Age mahana fufu 4-pela gagadicca eu mahafo, macasfo, na osofo fuluadeiaunnu hewein.

**2** Odocob ija ensel oso uqa cam tobi bena isec cali hohon fem, uqa Anut cebac bilina uqana sisi mec hewen. Uqa co suli u ensel 4-pela Anut maha macasca me qee mudoqagannu gagadic odoc aden eu utai

**3** maaden, “Age mahafo, macasfo, nafo eu mahuc cain me qee madowain. Ege casac egena Anutna sisi mec eu uqana cewel oc dana ilomoga domonnana madeqan,” aden.

**4** Odocob ija dana cajaca sisi madec oin eu sanijec 144,000 ec dom, eu dana cajaca 144,000 age Isrel agena sihul cunugnadec.

**5** > 12,000 age Juda sihulnadec age sisi madec oin,
> 12,000 age Ruben sihulnadec age sisi madec oin,
> 12,000 age Gad sihulnadec age sisi madec oin,

**6** > 12,000 age Asa sihulnadec age sisi madec oin,
> 12,000 age Naptalai sihulnadec age sisi madec oin,
> 12,000 age Manasa sihulnadec age sisi madec oin,

**7** > 12,000 age Simion sihulnadec age sisi madec oin,
> 12,000 age Liwai sihulnadec age sisi madec oin,
> 12,000 age Isaka sihulnadec age sisi madec oin,

**8** > 12,000 age Sebjulan sihulnadec age sisi madec oin,
> 12,000 age Josef sihulnadec age sisi madec oin,
> 12,000 age Benjamin sihulnadec age sisi madec oin. Dana i cunug age Anutna sisi mec oin.

## Dana cajaca cegulec am ben bahic age Anutnu cuha fein

**9** Ceteh eu hedocob ija feciimig dana cajaca cegulec am ben bahic fem, oso sanijadecnu ihoc qee bilel. Dana cajaca i age dewenega filfilnadecca, sihul filfilnadecca, hatu filfilnadecca, je madec filfilnadecca age king bilec cabalca Sipsip Nagca amalana tawein. Age lotoc cecelac senenec tacecca hinahin ebenegana hewecca tawein.

**10** Age conoga suli u madein:> “Egena Anut uqa king bilec cabalna bilinaca Sipsip Nagca ale cahahagesin eunu binanala suluquna!” ein.

**11** Odocob ensel cunug age king bilec cabalca, dana matu ageca, ceteh cebac 4-pelaca taliladimeig king bilec cabal amegna toni mahuli cosolimeig Anutnu cuha fein.

**12** Age odi madein:> “Mele bahic!
> Ege egena Anut uqa kobolkobolca, doc silecca, binanca, gagadic odocca, hihibanca eunu ege gaid eeldocomun catanena,> > ege hegaga ec gaid madocomun catanena!> Mele bahic!” ein.

## Dana i age dain ben adec eu qila qee mena

**13** Odocob dana matu eundec oso sisilti maten, “Dana cajaca lotoc cecelac senenecca tacec eundec age an? Dana cajaca eu age anadec hoin?” ten.

**14** Odocob ija jejeg hewi madon, “O Dana Ben, ija qee dugina qa hina dogona,” don. Odocob uqa maten, “Dogona, dana cajaca i age cilehadec ben eunadec cagasiaden. Age agena lotoc cecelac wasacudein, agena lotoc eu Sipsip Nag golacna soidocobil senen men.

**15** Eunu> ‘age Anutna king bilec cabal amegna tatawi uqana tempelna cahineg witicca gaid cewel utogina.
> King bilec cabalna bilina uqa agena cofadec gaid biligian.

**16** > Sipsip Nag uqa king bilec cabal gemona taweia uqa sipsip cofadec danawe cofadigian.
> Eunu age haun wen qee adeiaun, wa gab qee adeiaun.
> Cam haun dewenega qee cijadeiaun, dain oso qee welacadeiaun.
> Odimei uqa iwaladi cocobi cebac bilec wa fifiji becna euna ehi li madigian.
> Odocob Anut uqa amaga amulunuga cunug cacu heliadigian,’ en,” ten.
