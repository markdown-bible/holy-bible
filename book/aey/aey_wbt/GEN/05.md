# JENESIS 5

## Adamna sihul

**1** Je jaqoc ina Adamna sihul ijanaga nijia. Anut uqa dana ifanen saen euna uqa dana Anut cinigwe muden.

**2** Uqa manahalca aidca ifanalen. Ifanaalen uqa eelalimei ijanala “Dana Cajaca” aqalen.

**3** Saen Adam uqana cabi gel 130 eu odi bibili bilei uqa aideg mel oso basen. Mel eu uqa cinigwe uqa hahun umei cali hon. Odocob uqa ijan Set qon.

**4** Aria Set cali hocob Adam uqa bileceb li uqana cabi gel 800 eu odi calen. Oodon Adam aideg uqa melahul ategulca haun basaden.

**5** Eunu Adam uqana cabi gel cunug gabandoc eu 930. Odocob uqa cal men.

**6** Aria Set uqa bibilen li uqana cabi gel eu 105 eu odi caleceb uqa aideg mel oso basen. Odocob ijan Enos qon.

**7** Odimei Set uqa bibilen li cabi gel 807 on. Oodon uqa aideg melahul ategulca haun basaden.

**8** Eunu Set uqana cabi gel cunug gabandoc eu 912. Odocob uqa cal men.

**9** Aria Enos uqa bibilen li uqana cabi gel 90 ocob uqa aideg mel oso basen. Odocob ijan Kenan qon.

**10** Odimei Enos bibilen li cabi gel 815 on. Oodon uqa aideg melahul ategulca haun basaden.

**11** Eunu Enos uqana cabi gel cunug gabandoc eu 905. Odocob uqa cal men.

**12** Aria Kenan uqa bibilen li uqana cabi gel 70 ocob uqa aideg mel oso basen. Odocob ijan Mahalalel qon.

**13** Odimei Kenan bibilen li cabi gel 840 on. Oodon uqa aideg melahul ategulca haun basaden.

**14** Eunu Kenan uqana cabi gel cunug gabandoc eu 910. Odocob uqa cal men.

**15** Aria Mahalalel uqa bibilen li uqana cabi gel 65 ocob uqa aideg mel oso basen. Odocob ijan Jared qon.

**16** Odimei Mahalalel bibilen li cabi gel 830 on. Oodon uqa aideg melahul ategulca haun basaden.

**17** Eunu Mahalalel uqana cabi gel cunug gabandoc eu 895. Odocob uqa cal men.

**18** Aria Jared uqa bibilen li cabi gel 162 ocob uqa aideg mel oso basen. Odocob ijan Inok qon.

**19** Odimei Jared bibilen li cabi gel 800 on. Oodon uqa aideg melahul ategulca haun basaden.

**20** Eunu Jared uqana cabi gel cunug gabandoc eu 962. Odocob uqa cal men.

**21** Aria Inok uqa bibilen li cabi gel 65 ocob uqa aideg mel oso basen. Odocob ijan Metusela qon.

**22** Odimei Inok uqa cabi gel 300 eu odi Anutca cobosin. Oodon uqa aideg melahul ategulca haun basaden.

**23** Eunu Inok uqana cabi gel cunug gabandoc eu 365.

**24** Aria Inok mahana bibili uqa Anutca cobosin. Oodon Anut uqa ehuden. Eunu uqa jajanu qelen.

**25** Aria Metusela uqa bibilen li cabi gel 187 ocob uqa aideg mel oso basen. Odocob ijan Lamek qon.

**26** Odimei Metusela bibilen li cabi gel 782 on. Oodon uqa aideg melahul ategulca haun basaden.

**27** Eunu Metusela uqana cabi gel cunug gabandoc eu 969. Odocob uqa cal men.

**28** Aria Lamek uqa bibilen li cabi gel 182 ocob uqa aideg mel oso basen.

**29** Odocob uqa maden, “Maha i Anut wele geuldon eunadec mel i ege cehi jecnu cabi culumenca oqonanu mel i uqa cesulgigian,” en. Eunu mel eu ijan Noa[^6]qon.

**30** Odimei Lamek bibilen li cabi gel 595 on. Oodon uqa aideg melahul ategulca haun basaden.

**31** Eunu Lamek uqana cabi gel cunug gabandoc eu 777. Odocob uqa cal men.

**32** Aria Noa uqana cabi gel 500 hedocob uqa melahul ijedca bilen. Age ijanaga eu Semca Hamca Jafetca.

## Footnotes

[^6]: 5.29 “Noa” uqana gug eu “cesuldoc.”
