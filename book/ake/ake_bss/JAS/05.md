# ISENS 5

## Imaminke' Ton Panamato'

**1** Apʉne etatɨ', ɨmɨrɨ'nokon imaminke' ton, akarantɨ' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tʉukɨ'pɨ'nʉnpɨ'se akarantɨ', apʉne pʉra usewankaman nɨto' uye'nɨ pɨ' man ɨpona'nokon.

**2** Ɨimamin kon a'motasa' man, ene amʉ' uya ɨpon kon ɨ'sa' man.

**3** Ɨkorʉʉi kon mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ɨsirʉpaai kon ukaranpɨ'sa' man. Serɨ ikaranpɨ'sa' uya apa'nʉkʉ kon mɨrɨ, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ɨpun kon anɨ iya apo' kasa. Eke pe kuru ɨimamin kon amʉranʉ'sa' auya'nokon serɨ iweyu ton u'matʉ yau.

**4** Entɨ'! Amainarʉ kon yau iye'tʉrawasomasa' kon epe'pʉ, to' e'ma'pʉ auya'nokon pʉra na'ne' ike ukaran nɨ pɨ' man apa'nʉkʉ kon pe. Ɨi'kiyari kon akʉ'pʉsa' anʉnnin nʉ'san ukaramʉ uya Meruntɨ pe kuru te'sen ye' Itepuru pana eposa' man.

**5** Ɨuko'mansa' kon man serɨ non po eke pe rɨ ɨimamin kon tepose, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ipan pe i'se ɨwe'to' kon kupʉ pɨ'. Ɨyeka'pɨ'sa' kon ɨwɨnɨ kon weyu a'taino pe.[^9]

**6** Ɨri ku'nin nʉ'san pen nɨ eno'ma'pʉ mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tʉ'ka'pʉ auya'nokon, ɨyewa'nomanin kon pokena' nan pen nɨ.

## Tʉuseta'kai' Pʉra E'nɨto'

**7** Mɨrɨ pɨ' si, tʉuseta'kai' pʉra e'tɨ', utonpa ton, Itepuru uyepʉ pʉ'kʉ pona rɨ. Apʉne temaina'tɨsen entɨ': tenuri'sen non uya epe'kena' kiyari epetanʉkʉ enupɨkɨrɨ, tʉuseta'kai' pʉra te'sen tuna e'sara'tɨ mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tuna iu'matʉ yawon enupɨkɨrɨ.

**8** Ɨmɨrɨ'nokon nɨrɨ tʉuseta'kai' pʉra e'tɨ', pana' pe e'soto'katɨ', apʉne pʉra a'ko pe Itepuru uye'to' man.

**9** Ɨtonpa kon pɨ' te'naipɨ'se pʉra, utonpa ton, ɨyeseru kon kuru nin aimenka mɨrɨ. Eseru aimenkanin man mʉra'ta nau rɨ iye'soto'kasa'!

**10** Utonpa ton, tʉuseta'kai' pʉra e'nɨto' ento' ipɨ' ɨsɨ moronka a'taino pe, pu'kena' amʉ' entɨ', isauro'sa' kon Itepuru ese' yau.

**11** Mi'tuyatʉi'ne' kasa rɨ, ipʉremasa' pe to' esi ta, kamoro imoronkanin nʉ'san pɨ'. Isopu uya imoronkasa' rʉ'pʉ etasa' auya'nokon, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ iu'matʉ pe ene'pʉ Itepuru nʉkupʉ'pʉ rʉ'pʉ. Itepuru esi eke pe tʉusentu'masen mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ aiko tawon nɨ.

**12** Mɨrɨ entai kuru, utonpa ton, ɨnku'ton kon pɨ' ausaurokʉ kon a'tai Epʉn kʉsesa'tʉu, mɨrɨ pe pʉra non nɨ, mɨrɨ pe pʉra ɨ'rɨ rɨ. Tɨwɨ, “Ewai',” tato' auya'nokon nesii, ewai' pe rɨ; “Kane,” tato' auya'nokon nesii, kane pe rɨ, ɨyeno'ma kon kuru nin mɨrɨ.

## Apurɨto' Yau Ɨpʉreman Nɨto'

**13** Ɨtonpa kon nɨ na'kɨ nai ekamʉn yau? Tɨwɨ nɨpʉremai. Ɨnʉ' rɨ pe' nai pori' pe? Tɨwɨ apurɨpɨ'to' eremu yai nʉserenkai.

**14** Ɨtonpa kon nɨ na'kɨ nai takuru'ke? Tɨwɨ so'si epuru ton kɨ'mai tʉpona to' ɨpʉremato' pe, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tʉkarapaimato' pe Itepuru ese' yau.

**15** Apurɨto' yau iyɨpʉremasa' uya takuru'kenan epi'tɨ mɨrɨ; Itepuru uya rɨ i'mʉ'sa'ka. Imakoitasa' pe iyesi yau, ipɨ' rɨ usentu'man.

**16** Mɨrɨ pe iyesi pɨ', ɨtonpa kon pɨ' rɨ amakooi kon ekamatɨ', mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ɨtonpa kon pona rɨ ɨpʉrematɨ', ɨusepi'tɨ kon pa. Ipokena' e' pe te'sen ka'pon ɨpʉrema esi mɨrɨ ipan pe meruntɨ pe mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tʉwotori'pɨ ke.

**17** Iraisa esi'pʉ ka'pon uwarai'nokon nɨ. Iyɨpʉrema'pʉ tʉuta'kwarʉpɨ'se, tuna uyepʉ pʉra iye'to' pe, tuna uyepʉ pʉra si iyesi'pʉ non amankanin pe osorʉwawon pona a'sa'mʉyai wʉipiya airɨ rɨ.

**18** Iyɨpʉremakaa'pʉ kanan, mɨrɨ a'tai ka' awonsi'kɨ si tuna uyee'pʉ, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ si non uya kiyari epetanʉkʉ'pʉ.

**19** Utonpa ton, tikin nɨ rɨ ɨtonpa kon utano'ma pe iyesi yau i'napairon apai, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tʉron nɨ uya ina'po,

**20** se nesii ɨyenu kon yau: Ɨnʉ' uya rɨ tʉmakooikenan na'po ipokenan pen apai uya ipika'tɨ mɨrɨ ima'tato' apai, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tu'kan nɨ makooi nɨpontɨ iya mɨrɨ.
