# ISENS 2

## Tenu Yawon Ke E'nɨ Namai'no

**1** Utonpa ton, a'kwa yawon Uyepuru kon Sises Kʉrai apurɨnin pe ɨwesi kon koro'tau, tenu yawon ke ke'tʉu.

**2** Ka'pon uyepʉ pe iyesi yau ayamʉrasa' kon koro'ta', korʉ pun konekasa' irimuui pe, wakʉ ya' iyepontɨpɨ'sa', mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ entu'manin, ɨri rɨ nin ipon nɨrɨ uyepʉ.

**3** Kʉrɨ kuru wakʉ pe iyepontɨpɨ'sa' kupʉ auya a'tai wakʉ pe, “Se kuru wakʉ ayapon,” tai'ma ipɨ', entu'manin pɨ', “Mɨ airɨ e'soto'ka',” mɨrɨ pe pʉra, “Ereuta' non po u'ta piyau,” tai'ma. Mɨrɨ ku'sa' auya'nokon a'tai,

**4** iye'menkasa' kon pe pʉra pe' me'ku'atʉu mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ eseru aimenkanin pe ɨyenasa' kon, ipoken pʉra ɨri pe ɨusenuminka kon winɨ rɨ?

**5** Etatɨ', utonpa ton nʉ'kwɨ: Papa uya pe' kamoro pata enu yau entu'manin nan pe te'san anʉnsa' pʉra nai apurɨto' yau eke pe to' e'to' pe mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ isauro'sa' rʉ'pʉ, esa' wannɨ pe te'to' anʉnnin pe ti'nʉnkanin nan e'to' pe?

**6** E'tane entu'manin nan sapemasa' auya'nokon. Imaminke' ton pen pe' kamoro ɨkota'manin kon? Kamoro pen pe' kamoro ko'tʉ ta' ɨpi'si'nin kon?

**7** Kamoro pen pe' kamoro eke ipɨ' ɨye'tʉsa' kon na'ne' ese' sapema pe tʉusauro'san pen?

**8** Eke, Itepuru maimu iye'menukasa' na'ne' itekare yau yawɨrɨ kuru ɨwesi kon yau, “Ɨtonpa i'nʉnkakɨ, mɨsi'nʉnkayai'ne' kasa,”[^1]tawon, ipoken ɨwesi kon mɨrɨ.

**9** E'tane tenu yawon ke ɨwesi kon yau, aumakoita kon; Main, Mosi' winon uya nin si ama'tanʉkʉ kon mɨrɨ, mɨrɨ Main i'kwɨ'nin pe ɨwesi kon pɨ'.

**10** Apʉne pʉra ɨnʉ' rɨ tanporo Main yawɨrɨ na'ne' e'tane rɨ ita'mo'ka tikin nan yau rɨ esi mɨrɨ ɨri pe, tanporo rɨ i'kwɨ'sa' iya pe.

**11** Apʉne pʉra kʉrɨ rɨ, “Iyemari'masa' kʉsekumankai,”[^2]tawon uya rɨ marɨ, “Ɨnʉ' rɨ kʉ'wɨnɨi,”[^3]tasa' esi. Iyemari'masa' ekumankanin pe pʉra rɨ ɨwesi yau, e'tane iwɨsa' auya yau, main i'kwɨ'nin pe rɨ ɨyenasa' mɨrɨ.

**12** Asauro'tɨ' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ iku'tɨ', kamo Main, mo'kanin wannɨ yai, iteseru aimenkato' naimenkaton kon pe,

**13** apʉne pʉra Papa uya eseru aimenka usenpoikato' oton usentu'man pe pʉra kʉrɨ isentu'masa' rʉ'pʉ pen pona. Usentu'man uya eseru aimenka wapiya'ka mɨrɨ!

## Tapurɨsen Mɨrɨ Awonsi'kɨ Tʉku'sen

**14** Wakʉ pe pe' iyesi, utonpa ton, ka'pon e'kupʉ yau apurɨto' esa' pe e'tane ɨ'rɨ rɨ kupʉi'ma pʉra? Mɨrɨ warai pe' apurɨto' uya ipika'tɨ?

**15** Utonpa kon, warawo' mɨrɨ pe pʉra uri'san esi yau ipon pʉra, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ wʉi kaisa rɨ inanʉmʉnʉ iti'kiyari pʉra,

**16** ɨtonpa kon uya ta pe iyesi yau ipɨ', “Enta, wakʉ pe e'kɨ ukayai' ɨpɨ'; ikomi' pe pʉra ɨko'mankɨ, wakʉ pe mata're'tɨi',” e'tane ɨ'rɨ kupʉi'ma pʉra rɨ i'se iye'to' pɨ', wakʉ pe pe' iyesi?

**17** Mɨrɨ kasa rɨ, apurɨto' esi, ikupʉ uya isakonʉ'tɨ pʉra iyesi yau, iyeri'sa' pe rɨ iyesi.

**18** E'tane tʉron nɨ uya ta poken iyesi, “Apurɨto' esa' pe ɨwesi; iku'to' esa' pe esi,” ta. E'tane ta uya nin, “Anapurɨnʉ ikupʉi'ma pʉra enpoikakɨ uyena', mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ unapurɨnʉ ikupʉi'ma enpoikato' pe uya ɨyena',” tasa'.

**19** Apurɨ auya moron tikin nan nɨ Papa. Oso! Mɨrɨ rɨ marɨ apurɨnin nan kamoro ɨri a'kwarʉ ton, ɨsi'nʉkʉi'ma ipɨ'.

**20** Iya'kwarʉ pʉnon ɨmɨrɨ'nokon! Ɨni'tupai pe' me'atʉu apurɨto' ikupʉi'ma pʉra e'nɨ esi notʉn pe?[^4]

**21** Utamokori kon pe' Epʉra'an pɨ' ipokena' e' ta'pʉ pen pe' inkupʉ'pʉ wenai Aisi' tʉmu tʉsa' iya a'tai, Papa ena' use'man nɨto' apon po?

**22** Ene auya'nokon inapurɨnʉ mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ inkupʉnʉ e'tʉrawasoma'pʉ ɨsi'kaisa rɨ, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ inkupʉnʉ uya nin si inapurɨnʉ enuka'nʉ'sa' mɨrɨ.

**23** Mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ itekare iye'menukasa' nin si uta'ku'tɨsa', “Epʉra'an uya Papa apurɨ'pʉ, mɨrɨ pɨ' nin si ipokena' pe te'sen ta,”[^5]mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ Papa tonpa tukai' esa'sa' na'ne'.

**24** Ene auya'nokon ka'pon koneka Papa uya wakʉ pe inkupʉnʉ winɨ, inapurɨnʉ winɨ rɨken pen.

**25** Mɨrɨ kasa rɨ, Reyapu, ekumanpɨ' te'sen pɨ' ipokena' e' ta'pʉ inkupʉ'pʉ wenai, ama'ai non ense'na itɨsa' kon apontɨsa' iya a'tai, mɨrɨpan kon ennoko'pʉ iya tʉron nɨ awɨrɨ pɨ' pen pe'?

**26** Pun, iya'kwarʉ pʉn esi iyeri'sa' pe kasa, apurɨto' tʉku'sen a'kɨrɨ pʉra esi iyeri'sa' pe rɨ.
