# ISENS 4

## Papa Ena' E'nonkatɨ'

**1** Ɨ'rɨ uya epanta'man ɨsipɨ' e'nɨ emapu'tɨ ɨ'koro'tau'nokon? Ipan pe i'se ɨwe'to' kon ayau'nokon na'ne' usewa'noman pɨ' yapai pe' iyepʉ pen mɨ?

**2** Ɨ'rɨ rɨ i'se ɨwesi kon e'tane eporo auya'nokon pʉra iyesi. Iwenai ɨye'wɨnɨ kon mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ɨ'rɨ rɨ enkuui pe ɨwesi kon, e'tane i'se ɨwe'to' kon eporo auya'nokon poken pʉra iyesi. Ɨsipɨ' ɨwesi kon mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ɨyepanta'ma kon. Eporo auya'nokon pʉra iyesi, apʉne pʉra Papa pɨ' ekama'po auya'nokon pʉra iyesi pɨ'.

**3** Ekama'po auya'nokon a'tai, eporo auya'nokon pʉra iyesi, apʉne pʉra ɨri pe iteseru esii'ma ekama'po auya'nokon, ɨneporo'pʉ kon ma'tanʉkʉ ke ipan pe i'se ɨwe'to' kon nɨ kupʉ pɨ'.

**4** Ɨmɨrɨ'nokon temari'masa' kon e'tane tekumasan waraino kon ka'pon amʉ', i'tu auya'nokon pʉra pe' iyesi, orʉ kupʉ auya'nokon ɨtonpa kon pe a'tai, Papa ewaruma'tɨ auya'nokon pe iyesi? Ɨnʉ' rɨ orʉ tonpa pe e'paino ena mɨrɨ Papa eyaton pe.

**5** Mɨrɨ pe pʉra ɨusenuminka kon nɨ katɨ iye'menukasa' Papa Maimu uya ta iwenai pʉra rɨ, “Iya'kwarʉ uyau'nokon iko'manto' pe inkupʉ'pʉ esi tiwano' pe rɨken ui'se'nokon,”[^7]tukai'?

**6** E'tane eke pe miyarɨ parɨ wakʉ nonkasa' iya upona'nokon. Mɨrɨ pɨ', iye'menukasa' Papa Maimu uya ta,> “Papa uya mɨ pe te'ku'sen ewaruma'tɨ,> > e'tane mɨ pe te'ku'sen pen pona wakʉ nonkasa' tʉrʉ iya,”[^8]ta iya.

**7** Mɨrɨ pɨ' si, Papa ena' e'nonkatɨ'. Makoi iwa'kʉ'tɨ', mɨrɨpan ainʉnto' pe ɨpiyapai'nokon.

**8** A'ko pe Papa piya' ɨsi'tɨ', a'ko pe ɨpiya'nokon iye'to' pe. Ɨyemiyatʉ kon ikumi'tɨ', tʉmakooikena' nan ka'pon amʉ', mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ɨyewan kon ikorʉkatɨ', ikasimanin ye' ton kuru.

**9** Aya'kwarʉ kon tainʉnpai' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tʉuta'kwarʉpɨ'se, tʉukɨ'pɨ'nʉnpɨ'se akarantɨ'. Auta'kɨrɨto' kon ensimatɨ' tʉuta'kwarʉpɨ'se ukaran nɨ pe, pori' pe ɨwe'to' kon ipan pe ɨusewankono'ma kon pe.

**10** Mɨ pe te'ku'sen pe pʉra enatɨ' Itepuru rau, ayanʉnto' kon pe iya.

**11** Utonpa ton, ɨtonpa kon pɨ' ɨri pe isapema pe tʉusauro'se pʉra e'tɨ'. Ɨnʉ' rɨ ɨri pe isapema pe tʉtonpa pɨ' tʉusauro'sen, mɨrɨ pe pʉra iteseru aimenkanin usaurokʉ main, Mosi' winon pɨ' ɨri pe isapema pe mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ iteseru aimenka iya. Main eseru aimenka auya a'tai, iyawɨrɨ ɨwesi pen mɨrɨ, e'tane iteseru aimenkato' auya po ɨyereuta.

**12** Moro tikin nan nɨken Main tʉnin mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ iteseru aimenkanin esi, kʉrɨ rɨ kʉrɨ rɨ tʉmeruntɨrʉ kenan ipika'tɨto' ike mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ima'tanʉ'to' ike. E'tane si ɨmɨrɨ—ɨnʉ' pe ɨwesi ɨtonpa eseru aimenka pa?

## Awanapairon Pɨ' Utapurɨn

**13** Apʉne etatɨ', ɨmɨrɨ'nokon, “Serɨ pe, mɨrɨ pe pʉra awanapai rɨ se pata, mɨrɨ pe pʉra mɨ pata ya' utɨn mɨrɨ, tikin nɨ wʉipiya enno'se'na mɨrɨ yau, pʉrata epoto' kupʉ pɨ' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ pʉrata eporo pɨ',” tawon nan kon.

**14** I'tu auya'nokon pʉra rɨ iyesi ɨ'rɨ e'kupʉ awanapai rɨ tukai'. Ɨ'rɨ mɨ ɨuko'manto' kon? Ɨmɨrɨ'nokon esi kapuru' pe, aka'ne' pe rɨ tʉusenpoikasen mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tapasen kasa.

**15** E'tane ru'ku, “Itepuru eseru pe iyesi yau, se, mɨrɨ pe pʉra mɨ kupʉ pɨ', uko'man nɨ'nokon mɨrɨ,” tato' auya'nokon pe iyesi.

**16** Na'ne' nin esi, mɨ pe ɨye'kupʉ kon mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ autapurɨ kon. Tanporo serɨ waraino utapurɨn esi ɨri pe.

**17** Ɨnʉ' rɨ, wakʉ tʉnkupʉnʉ i'tunin, ikupʉ iya pʉra na'ne' umakoita mɨrɨ.
