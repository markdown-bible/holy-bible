# PIRI'PAI 4

**1** Mɨrɨ pe iyesi pɨ', utonpa ton, ɨmɨrɨ'nokon uni'nʉnkanʉ ton mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ itenupɨkɨrɨ e'to' ton, pori' pe uku'nin nan mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ uyarʉko kʉron ton, mɨrɨ kasa kuru meruntɨ pe Itepuru yau te'mʉ'sa'kai' e'tɨ', utonpa ton nʉ'kwɨ!

## Panaman Nɨto'

**2** Iyotiya mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ Sinti'ki, ɨyekama'po uya 'nokon ipan pe ɨseseru yau ɨwesi kon pa Itepuru yau.

**3** Ɨmɨrɨ, uisakon pe te'tʉrawasomasen[^3]ekama'po uya pʉsamoro uri'sami'si amʉ' ipika'tɨkɨ uya'kɨrɨ wakʉ itekare pɨ' ita'sisa' kon, Kʉremen pokon pe mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tʉron kon e'tʉrawasoma tonpa ton, to' ese' e'menukapɨ'sa' na'ne' uko'man nɨto' karetaai pɨ'.

**4** Pori' pe Itepuru yau e'tɨ' tɨwɨrɨ rɨ. Takapʉ uya kanan: Pori' pe e'tɨ'!

**5** Tɨwɨ tanporon kon pɨ' ɨsɨ pe pʉra ɨwe'to' nʉsenei. A'ko pe Itepuru man.

**6** Ɨ'rɨ pɨ' rɨ kɨsewankamatʉu, e'tane tanporon yau rɨ, ɨwɨpʉrema kon mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ekama'po auya'nokon yau, ɨyepori'ma kon pe, ɨnesakʉnʉ kon itʉtɨ' Papa ena'.

**7** Mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tʉusewankamai' pʉra e'nɨto' Papa iwano', tanporon nɨ ito'ka'nʉ'to' entaino rɨ esi ɨyewan kon mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ɨusenuminkato' kon erasu pe Kʉrai Sises yau.

**8** Iu'matʉ pe rɨ, utonpa ton, ɨ'rɨ rɨ i'nai rɨ na'ne', ɨ'rɨ rɨ tese ke na'ne', ɨ'rɨ rɨ ipokena', ɨ'rɨ rɨ esukenan, ɨ'rɨ rɨ wakʉ pe oso tato' ipɨ' pe na'ne', ɨ'rɨ rɨ en yau na'ne'—ɨ'rɨ rɨ wakʉ pe kuru rɨ na'ne' mɨrɨ pe pʉra tapurɨpɨ'sen ye' pe, mɨrɨ waraino ton pɨ' kuru ɨsenuminkatɨ'.

**9** Ɨ'rɨ rɨ uwinɨpai ɨusenupa'pʉ kon pɨ' mɨrɨ pe pʉra ananʉmʉ'pʉ kon mɨrɨ pe pʉra ɨneta'pʉ kon mɨrɨ pe pʉra ɨnene'pʉ kon uyau—iku'tɨ' si. Mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ nin si Papa usewankaman nɨto' pʉn nɨ e'to' pe ɨpiyau'nokon.

## Tenki Ta Itʉsa' Ton Pɨ'

**10** Eke pe kuru epori'mayai' Itepuru yau, emennʉ'pʉ rɨ upɨ' ɨusewankamato' kon enpoikasa' auya'nokon pɨ'. I'nairɨ rɨ sa'ne, pena upɨ' ɨusewankama'pʉ kon, e'tane enpoikato' auya'nokon a'tai pʉra iyesi'pʉ.

**11** Ekota'ma pɨ' ta uya pen serɨ, apʉne pʉra isenupasa' urɨ ɨ'rɨ pe rɨ e'nɨ yau pori' pe te'sen pe.

**12** I'tu uya rɨ ekota'man nɨto', mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ i'tu uya rɨ eke pe itesa' pe e'nɨto'. Usenupasa' man mɨrɨ yau e'nɨto' eseru pɨ' pori' pe te'sen pe ti'tui' pʉra rɨ mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tanporon yau rɨ e'nɨto' pɨ', eke pe rɨ tenta'nai' mɨrɨ pe pʉra iwan pe rɨ e'nɨto' yau; eke pe rɨ itesa' pe e'nɨto' yau mɨrɨ pe pʉra ipʉra e'nɨto' yau.

**13** I'tu uya rɨ, tanporon nɨ kupʉ, imeruntɨrʉ ke.

**14** E'tane wakʉ pe rɨ upika'tɨ pɨ' ɨwe'sa' kon man ekamʉn yau esi koro'tau.

**15** Mɨrɨ tonpa'tɨ pe, ɨmɨrɨ'nokon Piri'pai pon kon ni'tuyai'ne' kasa rɨ, pena wakʉ itekare to'ka'nʉkʉ pɨ' ɨyena kon yau Ma'sitoniya poi utɨ a'tai, tikin nan nɨ so'si uya upika'tɨsa' pʉra iyesi urɨ tʉrepai' i'se e'to' ke, ɨmɨrɨ'nokon nɨken;

**16** Tesaroni'ka po rɨ kanan esi a'tai i'se e'to' enno'pɨtʉ pɨ' ɨwe'pɨtʉ'pʉ kon.

**17** Urepato' auya'nokon, enupɨkɨrɨ esi pen, e'tane ɨnkupʉ'pʉ kon epe'pʉ eporo auya'nokon enupɨkɨrɨ nin esi.

**18** Tanporo kuru rɨ, iye'to' entai rɨ ɨwinɨpai'nokon eposa' uya man, serɨ kanan si epoka'sa' uya E'pa'pʉrotai'tas winɨ ɨnennoko'pʉ kon urepato' auya'nokon ike. A'po'nan ikɨtun tʉtʉsen pe iyesi, tanʉnsen ye' use'man nɨto' pe iyesi Papa pori'manin pe iyesi.

**19** Mɨrɨ pɨ' nin si Papa itese' pɨ' e'to' uya tanporon nɨ i'se ɨwe'to' kon ke ɨrepa kon mɨrɨ eke pe rɨ itesa' pe te'to' ke Kʉrai Sises yawɨrɨ.

**20** Papa itese' pɨ' e'nɨto' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ukʉipʉnʉ kon iwano' pe eke pe iku'to' nesii inke rɨ, inke rɨ te'sen pe. Amen.

## Iu'matʉ Pe Miyarɨ Ta

**21** “Miyarɨ,” ka'tɨ' Kʉrai Sises yau wakʉ ton pɨ'. Utonpa kon amʉ' upiyawon kon, “Miyarɨ,” tato' ennoko pɨ' man.

**22** Tanporo wakʉ ton, “Miyarɨ,” tato' ennoko pɨ' man ɨyena'nokon, kamoro kuru Sisa iwʉ' tawon kon.

**23** Wakʉ nonkasa' Itepuru Sises Kʉrai iwano' nesii aya'kwarʉ kon piyau. Amen.[^4]
