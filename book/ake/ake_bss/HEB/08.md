# IPURU 8

## Sises, Emenna' Ekonekan Nɨto' Epuru

**1** Se kuru sa'ne ta ina uya: Pʉse rɨ warai use'man nɨto' tʉrawasomanin nan epuru esa' pe e'nɨ, iyereutasa' rʉ'pʉ enwo'ne' miya' winɨ eke pe kuru te'sen ye' apon po Epʉn po,

**2** te'tʉrawasomasen ɨpʉreman nɨto' iwʉ' tau, i'nairon kuru ta'porin, Itepuru rɨ ni'mʉ'sa'ka'pʉ, ka'pon ni'mʉ'sa'ka'pʉ pen.

**3** Tanporon kon use'man nɨto' tʉrawasomanin nan epuru pe anʉnsa' uya asa'rɨ rɨ tʉtʉsen mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ use'man nɨto' ike tʉrʉ, mɨrɨ kasa rɨ si pʉse rɨ nʉtʉton nɨ marɨ esi'pʉ.

**4** Non po iyesi yau, use'man nɨto' tʉrawasomanin pe pʉra rɨ iyesi mɨrɨ, apʉne pʉra moro ka'pon amʉ' esi pɨ' tʉtʉsen tʉnin nan pe main uya ekamasa' awɨrɨ.

**5** To' e'tʉrawasoma Epʉn pon ɨpʉreman nɨto' iwʉ' a'kwarʉ'pʉ tau rɨ, ikasa ipatakasa' pe te'sen. Mɨrɨ pe iyesi pɨ', Mosi' panama'pʉ mɨrɨ ɨpʉreman nɨto' iwʉ' amɨ pɨ' iyekoneka koro'tau: “Menei', tanporon nɨ koneka auya, ɨyena' wʉ' po enpoika'pʉ na'ne' kasa ento' auya ipɨ' pe,”[^27]tukai'.

**6** E'tane Sises tʉrawasooi ineporo'pʉ esi eke pe Mosi' tʉrawasooi entai, wakʉ kuru ekonekan nɨsa' Papa winon eposa' iya tʉntʉton eke pe penaro' entai, mɨrɨpan e'tʉsa' wakʉ ton kuru nin e'ku'to' pɨ' isauro'sa' po.

**7** Apʉne pʉra wapiyaro' ekonekan nɨto' esi yau wakʉ pe, tʉron nɨ ne'warinpai'no pen nɨ.

**8** E'tane Papa uya ɨri eporo'pʉ tʉmunkɨ amʉ' pɨ', mɨrɨpan uya ta'pʉ,> “Iweyu uye'nɨ pɨ' man, ta pɨ' Itepuru man,> > emenna' ekonekan nɨto' konekato' uya weyu> Esuwerʉ amʉ' pokon pe,> > mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ Isuta amʉ' pokon pe.

**9** > To' tamokori ton pokon pe unkoneka'pʉ> > ekonekan nɨto' waraino pen,> to' emiya pɨ' to' a'si'pʉ uya a'taino> > to' ato' pe uya Isi' apai,> apʉne pʉra tʉmɨrɨ'nokon pokon pe ekoneka a'tai tʉusaurokʉ'pʉ kon awɨrɨ pʉra to' esi'pʉ,> > mɨrɨpan kon piyapai era'tɨ'pʉ,ta Itepuru uya.

**10** > Se si serɨ ekonekan nɨto' konekato' uya Esuwerʉ amʉ' pokon pe,> > mɨrɨ pata'pʉ pe, ta Itepuru uya,> Umaimu nonka uya mɨrɨ to' usenuminkato' ya'> > mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ imenuka uya to' ewan pɨ'.> To' e'to' itese' pɨ' pe e'to' oton,> > mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ umunkɨ amʉ' pe to' e'to' oton.

**11** > Serɨ enta' ka'pon uya tʉtonpa enupato' oton pen,> > mɨrɨ pe pʉra ka'pon uya takon enupato' oton pen, ‘Itepuru i'tukɨ,’ tukai',> apʉne pʉra tanporo to' uya ui'tuto' oton> > sepan kon apai eke ton pe kuru te'san pona rɨ.

**12** > Ɨri to' eseru pɨ' usentu'mato' oton,> > mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ uyenu yau to' makooi ku'to' uya oton pen nɨ mɨrɨ enta',”[^28]tasa'.

**13** Serɨ si ekonekan nɨto' esakʉ iya winɨ “emenna',” tukai', wapiyaro' ku'sa' iya tapurɨsen pe pʉra; mɨrɨpan si tapurɨsen pe pʉra te'sen mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ipooi'pɨ pe te'sen pʉra iyena serɨ pe rɨ.
