# IPURU 13

## Iu'matʉ Pe Panaman Nɨto'

**1** Tʉusi'nʉnkai' e'tɨ' ɨsitonpa pe.

**2** Kɨpu'tɨka'tʉu ka'kʉran amʉ' ereutanʉ'to' pɨ', mɨrɨ ku'nin nan tʉron kon ka'pon amʉ' uya inserʉ amʉ' ereutanʉkʉ'pʉ mɨrɨ i'tui'ma pʉra.

**3** Pariki'si ta' a'sisa' kon tʉku'se ɨyenu kon yau, to' pokon pe rɨ a'sisa' pe ɨwesi kon pe te'ku'se, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ kamoro ɨsɨ pe tʉkota'masan, ɨmɨrɨ'nokon nɨ ekota'ma pe.

**4** Tanporon kon emari'man nɨto' namai, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ iyemari'masa' kon nesii tʉmaimu rʉ'pʉ kon awɨrɨ, apʉne pʉra temari'masa' e'tane iyekumasa' rʉ'pʉ mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tanporo tekumasan eseru aimenkato' oton Papa uya pɨ'.

**5** Pʉrata wenai tʉuta'kwarʉkai' pʉra ɨko'mantɨ', ɨyenau'nokon na'ne' pɨ' rɨ tepori'mai' e'tɨ', apʉne pʉra Papa uya,> “Ɨnɨmɨ uya pʉra kuru iyesi;> > ɨnonka uya pʉra kuru iyesi,”[^48]tasa'.

**6** Uyenu kon yau si esii'ma ta serɨ,> “Itepuru esi upika'tɨnin pe; enari'nʉmʉ pʉra rɨ iyesi.> > Ɨ'rɨ pe ken ka'pon uya ukupʉ?”[^49]ta'nokon.

**7** Ɨyepuru kon amʉ' iku'tɨ' ɨyenu kon yau, Papa Maimu ekamanin nʉ'san ɨpɨ'nokon. To' uko'mamʉ'pʉ otori'pɨ pɨ' ɨsenuminkatɨ', to' uya apurɨto' ipatakapɨ'tɨ'.

**8** Sises Kʉrai kʉrɨ rɨ, kʉrɨ rɨ rɨ ko'mamiyau iye'sa' rʉ'pʉ, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ serɨ pe, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ inkero' rɨ.

**9** Ti'tui' pʉra rɨ ti'tupɨ'sen pen nɨ enupan nɨto' ayano'matɨ'nin. Wakʉ pe rɨ iyesi uyewan kon umeruntɨtato' pe wakʉ nonkasa' ke, kiyari rɨ pɨ' teseru ke e'nɨto' winɨ pen, ipɨ' tenta'nasan pika'tɨnin pen nɨ.

**10** Moro use'man nɨto' apon esi ipoi kamoro ta'porin tau te'tʉrawasomasan enta'nato' itau pen nɨ.

**11** Use'man nɨto' tʉrawasomanin nan epuru uya o' mʉnʉ rʉ'pʉ arɨ Ipan Wakʉ Kuru Pata ya' makoi ku'sa' epe'pʉ pe tʉtʉsen, e'tane nin ipun nʉ'pʉ po'tɨ ɨnnɨ rɨ pʉroroi apai.

**12** Mɨrɨ kasa rɨ marɨ, Sises ekota'ma'pʉ e'ne' pe eke kuru pata mʉra'taai pʉroroi entau, wakʉ pe tʉmunkɨ amʉ' ekonekato' pe tʉmʉnʉ winɨ.

**13** Utɨnpai'nokon ipiya' ɨnnɨ pata apai, ipiyɨ'nʉ'nin pe te'sen moronkai'.

**14** Apʉne pʉra serɨ yau inke tʉuko'mansen eke upata kon pʉra iyesi, e'tane tʉuye'ton eke pata enupɨkɨrɨ e'nɨ'nokon serɨ.

**15** Mɨrɨ pe iyesi pɨ', Sises poro miyarɨ rɨ Papa ena' use'man pe apurɨpɨ'pai'nokon—mʉta tʉrawasooi winɨ itese' pɨ' tʉsauro'sen.

**16** Wakʉ ku'to' auya'nokon mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ɨtonpa kon pokon pe te'repai' ɨwe'to' kon kʉ'pu'tɨka'nʉ'tʉu, serɨ warai use'man nɨto' esi Papa pori'manin pe.

**17** Ɨyepuru kon amʉ' maimu awɨrɨ e'tɨ', to' pana'pano o'no te'tʉse e'tɨ'. Apa'sima kon pɨ' to' esi, tʉusekama ton kon pe Papa pana ya'. To' maimu awɨrɨ e'tɨ' to' tʉrawasooi e'to' pe pori' pe e'nɨto' pe, e'tape'ne' pe pʉra, mɨrɨ uya ɨpika'tɨ kon pʉra iyesi.

**18** Ina pona ɨpʉrematɨ'. Ina uya i'tu ina ewan esi wakʉ pe, ekamʉn ku'sa' ina uya pʉra mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tanporon yau rɨ wakʉ pe tʉnamasan pe ɨko'manpaino kon pe.

**19** Ɨyekama'po uya 'nokon kuru ɨwɨpʉrema kon pa, ɨpiya'nokon enna'poto' pe inke pʉra.

**20** Tɨwɨ Papa tʉusewankamai' pʉra e'nɨto' esa', siyaro' pe ekonekan nɨto' mʉnʉ poro iyeri'sa' uyepuru kon Sises i'mʉ'sa'kanin nʉ'pʉ, kʉrɨ rɨ eke kuru karimeru amʉ' Pa'simanin,

**21** ɨrepai'nokon tanporon wakʉ ton ke i'se iye'to' kupʉ kon pa, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tɨwɨ uyau'nokon nʉtʉrawasomai tʉpori'manin pe te'sen, Sises Kʉrai poro, itiwano' pe eke pe iku'to' nesii, inke rɨ, inke rɨ. Amen.

**22** Utonpa ton, ipan pe ɨyekama'po uya 'nokon apanamato' kon uya eta kon pa, apʉne pʉra to'ken nʉ'kwɨ rɨ kareta menukasa' uya ɨyena'nokon.

**23** I'tu auya'nokon i'se e'ai' utonpa ton Temu'ti si nonkasa' man. Serɨ pe iwa iye'sa' yau, iya'kɨrɨ utɨ mɨrɨ ɨyense'nokon.

**24** Miyarɨ ka'tɨ' ɨyepuru kon amʉ' pɨ' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tanporo Papa munkɨ amʉ' pɨ'. Kamoro I'tari winon kon miyarɨ nɨkayan ɨpɨ'nokon.

**25** Wakʉ nonkasa' nesii ɨpiyau'nokon.
