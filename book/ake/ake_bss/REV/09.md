# ISENPOIKASA' 9

## 5 Pe Inserʉ Amʉ' Ku'nin

**1** Mɨrɨ a'tai, 5 pe to' ku'nin inserʉ uya tʉronpi' turunpa'pʉ. Sirikɨ ka' poi ita'mo'kasa' rʉ'pʉ non pona ene'pʉ uya; irepa'pʉ iya'mu pʉn iteuta[^17]a'kokato' ike ki ke.

**2** Iteuta a'koka iya a'tai, ikɨrɨsin epa'ka'pʉ iyapai, eke apo' epo'tɨto' en yapai ikɨrɨsin epa'ka kasa, wʉi enu mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ka' ena'pʉ ewarupɨ pe iteuta kɨrɨsin ke.

**3** Kairau amʉ' u'tɨ'pʉ ikɨrɨsin koro'tau non pona, mɨrɨpan kon upana'panta'pʉ mɨnɨ' amʉ' pana'pano kasa.

**4** To' auro'ka'pʉ to' uya non pon wana' mɨrɨ pe pʉra ti'tui' pʉra rɨ yʉi muku'pɨ, mɨrɨ pe pʉra yʉi ma'tanʉkʉ to' uya namai', e'tane ka'pon amʉ' rɨken, Papa ma'kʉʉi a'si'sa' pʉra na'ne' nan to' pee'pɨ pɨ'.

**5** Kairau amʉ' uya ka'pon amʉ' eri'nʉ'kapɨtʉ namai' to' e'kupʉ'pʉ e'tane to' kota'mato' pe rɨken 5 kaisa rɨ kapʉi pʉ'kʉ pona rɨ. Mɨrɨ si e'ne' e'to' pe mɨnɨ' uya ka'pon si'posa' a'taino kasa.

**6** Mɨrɨ 5 kaisaron kapʉi koro'tau uma'tan nɨto' warinpato' oton ka'pon amʉ' uya, e'tane to' uya epoto' oton pen; eri'pai to' e'tane, uma'tan nɨto' utɨpɨ'to' oton to' piyapai.

**7** Kairau amʉ' usene'pʉ kaware amʉ' e'kamasa' te'wɨto' kon pe a'taino kasa, to' i'pai po'ne korʉ pun konekasa' waraino aro', kʉron tato' amʉ' esi'pʉ, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ to' emʉ' usene'pʉ ka'pon amʉ' emʉ' kasa.

**8** To' i'pai i'po' esi'pʉ uri'san amʉ' inse' kasa, to' yɨ esi'pʉ rayan yɨ kasa.

**9** To' pʉropo nɨpon usene'pʉ wa'ka pun kasa, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ to' apiri useta'pʉ kaware amʉ' uya tararan pi'sikʉ a'merumʉ ɨsewa'nomase'na to' utɨ a'taino kasa.

**10** Taukʉ ke'ne to' esi'pʉ, si'pon nɨto' ike ipɨ'ne mɨnɨ' kasa. To' aukʉ yau to' pana'pano esi'pʉ ka'pon amʉ' kota'mato' to' uya ike 5 kaisa rɨ kapʉi yau.

**11** Tepuru kon pe kin pe, iya'mu pʉn iteuta inserʉʉi ku'sa' to' uya esi'pʉ. Itese' Ipuru amʉ' maimu yau “Apatan,” mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ Kiri' amʉ' maimu yau “A'poriyon”.[^18]

**12** Tikin nɨ ɨsɨ, nari' kuru utɨka'sa' mɨrɨ; mɨrɨ e'ma'pʉ pe asa'rɨ ɨsɨ, nari' ton kuru tʉuye'ton e'ku'sa'.

**13** Mɨrɨ tʉpo, 6 pe to' ku'nin inserʉ uya tʉronpi' turunpa'pʉ. Main eta'pʉ uya korʉ pun konekasa' use'man nɨto' apon ekun retʉ poi Papa piyau te'sen winɨpai.

**14** Main uya ta'pʉ tʉronpi' turunpanin nʉ'pʉ inserʉ pɨ', “Kamoro asakɨrɨ'nan kon inserʉ amʉ' arankapɨ'kɨ eke tuna tʉu'tɨsen Yu'pʉretes itese' piyau auronpɨ'sa' kon.”

**15** Asakɨrɨ'nan kon inserʉ amʉ' eukapɨtʉ'pʉ iya, to' e'kamasa' esi'pʉ serɨ a'taino pe, serɨ wʉi a'tai, serɨ kapʉi pɨ', mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ serɨ wʉipiya pɨ', itonpa pe rɨ, ⅓ e'to' airɨ ka'pon amʉ' tʉ'kato' pe to' uya.

**16** Kaware pon kon soisa amʉ' tu'kanoro eta'pʉ uya, 200 meriyan kaisa rɨ to' esi'pʉ.

**17** Kaware amʉ' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ to' pon kon ene'pʉ uya, to' pʉropo nɨpon esi'pʉ isuweyu pe apo' kasa, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tʉtarire, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ kara'kwa' pe, sorʉ'pa tato' kasa. Kaware amʉ' i'pai esi'pʉ rayan amʉ' i'pai kasa, to' mʉta yai apo', ikɨrɨsin mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ sorʉ'pa epa'ka'pʉ.

**18** Itonpa pe rɨ, ⅓ e'to' airɨ ka'pon amʉ' wɨnɨ'pʉ mɨrɨ osorʉwawon paran; apo', ikɨrɨsin mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ sorʉ'pa kaware amʉ' mʉta yai tepa'kasen uya.

**19** Kaware amʉ' pana'pano esi'pʉ to' mʉta yau mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ to' aukʉ yau. To' aukʉ esi'pʉ ɨkʉi kasa ti'pai ke, mɨrɨpan kon uya ka'pon amʉ' i'kapɨtʉ'pʉ.

**20** Ka'pon amʉ' tonpa'san mɨrɨ paran nʉwɨnɨ'san pen era'tɨ pʉra iyesi'pʉ tʉnkoneka'pʉ kon piyapai. Makoi amʉ' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ korʉ ka'sa', sirʉpa ka'sa', karukuri ka'sa', tɨ' ka'sa' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ yʉi konekasa' apurɨto' tʉuya'nokon ereutanʉkʉ to' uya pʉra iyesi'pʉ—ennin pen, etanin pen, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tʉusasen pen.

**21** Mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ te'wɨto' kon, enwoke' pe te'to' kon, tekumato' kon, mɨrɨ pe pʉra ama' pe te'to' kon apai to' era'tɨ pʉra iyesi'pʉ.
