# ISENPOIKASA' 5

## Kareta Amon Mɨrɨ Awonsi'kɨ Karimeru Mʉre

**1** Ene'pʉ uya iyereutasa' apono' po emiya enwo'netʉ yau kareta amon iye'menukasa' ɨsi'ratoi pɨ', 7 kaisaron a'si'to' ike ke a'si'sa'.

**2** Mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ pana'pan inserʉ ene'pʉ uya, eke pe ekama'po'pʉ iya, “Ɨnʉ' nai wakʉ serɨ kareta amon a'si'sa' rʉ'pʉ i'kwɨ'nin pe mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ a'kokanin pe?” ta'pʉ iya.

**3** E'tane ɨnʉ' pʉra rɨ iyesi'pʉ Epʉn po, serɨ non po, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ serɨ non o'no,[^11]mɨrɨ kareta amon a'koka ton mɨrɨpan yawon enton pe.

**4** Mɨrɨ a'tai, ukaramʉ'pʉ ipan pe, apʉne pʉra ɨnʉ' rɨ wakʉ ka'pon e'seporo pʉra iyesi'pʉ a'koka ton mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ yawon enton.

**5** Mɨrɨ a'tai, tikin nan itepuru ton tonpa uya ta'pʉ upɨ', “Kakaramʉi. Enkɨ! Moro rayan Isuta ko', Tepi' atʉ rʉ'pʉ uya iwapiya'kasa' man; kʉrɨ rɨ uya si 7 kaisaron a'si'to' ike i'kwɨtʉ mɨrɨ, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ kareta amon a'koka iya,” ta'pʉ iya.

**6** Mɨrɨ a'tai, karimeru mʉre ene'pʉ uya. Iye'mʉ'sa'kasa' esi'pʉ apono' aporo' po, asakɨrɨ'nan kon ene amʉ' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ itepuru ton esi'pʉ iwoi. Karimeru mʉre usene'pʉ iwɨsa' rʉ'pʉ pe, 7 kaisa rɨ iretʉ mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ 7 kaisa rɨ itenu ton esi'pʉ. Mɨrɨ ton esi'pʉ 7 kaisaron kon Papa A'kwarʉ amʉ'[^12]serɨ non emʉ' kaisa rɨ enno'sa' kon.

**7** Itɨ'pʉ kareta amon mo'kai', apono' po iyereutasa' na'ne' emiya enwo'netʉ apai.

**8** Mɨrɨ kareta amon mo'ka iya a'tai, asakɨrɨ'nan kon ene amʉ' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ 24 kaisaron kon itepuru ton e'sekunka'pʉ irakʉi'. Tanporo wai, a'pu tato' esa' pe to' esi'pʉ mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ to' pʉsawʉ ton korʉ pun konekasa' yau a'po'na' nan esi'pʉ. Mɨrɨ esi Papa munkɨ amʉ' ɨpʉremasa' rʉ'pʉ.

**9** To' userenka'pʉ emenna' eren pɨ':> “Wakʉ kuru ɨmɨrɨ, kareta amon mo'kanin> > mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ a'si'pɨ'sa' a'kokanin,> apʉne pʉra iwɨsa' rʉ'pʉ pe ɨwesi,> > mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ɨmʉnʉ ke ka'pon amʉ' enna'pʉ auya Papa iwano' pe,
> > tanporon kon rʉko' rɨ, tʉmaimuron kon, tʉponaron kon mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tʉpataron kon ka'pon amʉ' koro'tapai.

**10** > To' konekasa' auya esa' wannɨ pe to' e'to' pe, use'man nɨto' tʉrawasomanin nan pe, itese' pɨ' ina e'to' apiyo' pe,> > mɨrɨpan kon e'to' pe serɨ non esanon pe,” tukai'.

**11** Uyenu tʉrʉ'pʉ uya, mɨrɨ a'tai tu'kan kon nɨ inserʉ amʉ' maimu eta'pʉ uya, tu'kan kon nɨ, tu'kan kon nɨ, 1,000 kaisaron kon pona rɨ 1,000 e'to' airɨ, 10,000 ite'kwa rɨ 10,000 kaisaron kon. To' e'soto'pɨ'sa' esi'pʉ apono' woi, ene amʉ' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ itepuru ton woi.

**12** Mɨrɨpan kon userenka'pʉ eke pe:> “Wakʉ kuru karimeru mʉre iwɨsa' rʉ'pʉ uya
> tʉpana'pano, timamin, tʉpu'ketʉ, tʉmeruntɨrʉ,
> tʉnamato', eke pe ti'tuto' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tapurɨpɨ'to' eporo!”tukai'.

**13** Mɨrɨ a'tai, tanporon kon nɨ Papa nʉkapʉ'san maimu eta'pʉ uya Epʉn po, non po, non o'no, parau kau, tanporon kon nɨ ka'tawon kon nɨ. To' userenka'pʉ:> “Apono' po iyereutasa' na'ne' rɨ karimeru mʉre iwano' pe,
> apurɨpɨ'to', inamato', eke pe i'tuto' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ meruntɨ nesii,> > inke rɨ, inke rɨ!”tukai'.

**14** Asakɨrɨ'nan kon ene amʉ' uya eikʉ'pʉ, “Amen!” Mɨrɨ a'tai si, itepuru ton e'sekunka'pʉ apurɨpɨ'se'na.
