# ISENPOIKASA' 16

## Papa Uya Kota'man Nɨto' En Ton

**1** Mɨrɨ yau eke main eta'pʉ uya Papa ena' use'man nɨto' iwʉ' tapai 7 pan kon inserʉ amʉ' auro'ka iya, “Entantɨ', Papa uya kota'man nɨto' en amʉ' 7 kaisaron i'kwanpɨ'tantɨ' non pona,” ta'pʉ iya.

**2** Wapiyaro' inserʉ utɨ'pʉ, mɨrɨpan uya ti'sawʉ yawon i'kwamʉ'pʉ non pona. Ɨri mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ e'ne' ton ere' amʉ' epa'ka'pʉ ene ma'kʉʉi eponin nʉ'san pɨ' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ipe ika'sa' apurɨpɨ'nin nʉ'san pɨ'.

**3** Itisakon nʉ'pʉ uya ti'sawʉ yawon i'kwamʉ'pʉ parau ka'. Tuna era'tɨ'pʉ iyeri'sa' ka'pon mʉnʉ kasa, mɨrɨpan tanporo rɨ ikau te'san erikʉ'pʉ.

**4** To' osorʉwano rʉ'pʉ uya ti'sawʉ yawon i'kwamʉ'pʉ tʉu'tɨsen tuna ka' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ non yai tepa'kasen tuna pona'ne rɨ, mɨrɨpan ton era'tɨ'pʉ mʉn pe.

**5** Inserʉ tuna ton esa' pe te'sen uya ta eta'pʉ uya,> “Wakʉ pe kuru me'ai',> > ka'pon amʉ' eseru aimenkato' auya yau,
> > Ɨmɨrɨ Wakʉ, moro na'ne' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ moro iye'sa' rʉ'pʉ.

**6** > To' uya wakʉ ton mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ pu'kena' amʉ' mʉnʉ ennoko'pʉ.> > Mɨrɨ pe iyesi pɨ', mʉn ensito' pe to' uya iku'sa' auya, tepe'pʉ kon eporo to' uya,”ta'pʉ iya.

**7** Mɨrɨ a'tai, use'man nɨto' apon uya eikʉ eta'pʉ uya, se tukai':> “Ewai', Itepuru Papa Meruntɨ pe kuru te'sen ye',> > i'napairon pe kuru rɨ mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ wakʉ pe kuru ka'pon amʉ' eseru aimenkato' auya man,”tawon.

**8** To' asakɨrɨ'pano rʉ'pʉ uya ti'sawʉ yawon i'kwamʉ'pʉ wʉi enu pona, mɨrɨpan a'ne' pe iyesi uya ka'pon amʉ' puto' pe iku'sa' esi'pʉ.

**9** A'ne' pe iyesi uya ka'pon amʉ' puru'pʉ, mɨrɨpan kon uya Papa ese' pa'nʉkʉ'pʉ, tanporo serɨ paran amʉ' esa' pe te'sen. E'tane tʉmakooi kon apai era'tɨpai pʉra to' esi'pʉ apurɨpɨ'se'na.

**10** 5 pe to' ku'nin inserʉ uya ti'sawʉ yawon i'kwamʉ'pʉ ene apon pona. Ewarupɨ pe itesa' pe iye'to' ena'pʉ. Ka'pon amʉ' uya tani kon i'kapɨtʉ'pʉ, apʉne pʉra e'ne' pe tesi kon pɨ'.

**11** Mɨrɨpan kon uya Papa, Epʉn pon pa'nʉkʉ'pʉ e'ne' pe tesi kon pɨ' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ erekʉ' pe tesi kon pɨ'. E'tane ɨri teseru kon piyapai era'tɨpai pʉra to' esi'pʉ.

**12** 6 pe to' ku'nin inserʉ uya ti'sawʉ yawon i'kwamʉ'pʉ eke tuna tʉu'tɨsen Yu'pʉretes itese' ka', mɨrɨpan mɨrɨ tuna a'muna'pʉ kin amʉ' wʉi enu uyepʉ winɨpaino kon uye'to' pe iyawɨrɨ.

**13** Mɨrɨ tʉpo, osorʉwawon kon ɨri a'kwarʉ ton ene'pʉ uya, pɨrɨtuku waraino kon. To' epa'kapɨtʉ'pʉ urupere mʉta yapai, ene mʉta yapai, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ennakanin pu'kena' mʉta yapai.

**14** Kamoro si esi'pʉ makoi a'kwarʉ ton eke tʉtʉrawasooi kon kupʉ pɨ'. Pʉsamoro osorʉwawon kon a'kwarʉ amʉ' utɨto' oton non emʉ' kaisaron kon kin amʉ' amʉranʉ'se'na to' usewa'nomato' pe Papa Meruntɨ pe kuru te'sen ye', eke iweyu a'tai.

**15** “Etakɨ! Ama' kasa uye'ai'! Pori' pe kuru kʉrɨ rɨ esi mɨrɨ te'nʉnse pʉra nenkurunyai'ne' tʉpon mainanpa pɨ' ipon pʉra tʉusarɨ namai' ka'pon amʉ' koro'tau tɨpiyɨtʉ namai',” ta'pʉ iya.

**16** A'kwarʉ amʉ' uya kin amʉ' amʉranʉkʉ'pʉ tanporo pata Amaketan tukai' Ipuru amʉ' nesakʉnʉ ya'.

**17** 7 pe to' ku'nin inserʉ uya ti'sawʉ yawon i'kwamʉ'pʉ kakʉi'. Eke main uyee'pʉ apono' winɨpai Papa ena' use'man nɨto' iwʉ' tapai, “Iu'ma'sa' man!” ta'pʉ iya.

**18** Kamiri' e'weuweuma esi'pʉ, ta'merunsen esi'pʉ, ɨsɨ pe korʉme e'toi'pɨtʉ esi'pʉ, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ɨsɨ rɨ tʉrʉ'pʉnʉ esi'pʉ. Mɨrɨ waraino tʉrʉ'pʉnʉ esa' pʉra iyesi, ka'pon ekoneka'pʉ si'kɨrɨ, serɨ tʉrʉ'pʉnʉ esi'pʉ ɨsɨ pe tanporon entai.

**19** Eke pata e'mopɨtʉ'pʉ osorʉwau iye'to' airɨ, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tʉpataron kon pata'se' ton uta'morʉka'pʉ. Papa uya eke Paperan kupʉ'pʉ tenu yau, mɨrɨpan uya tʉyuku ensito' pe tʉkokʉ yai ikupʉ'pʉ, kota'man nɨto' tʉuya euku.

**20** Tanporon nɨ opa'wʉ amʉ' pʉra iyena'pʉ mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ wʉ' amʉ' pʉra iyena'pʉ.

**21** Eke ton tuna tɨ'mooi 100 pon kaisaro' nan uta'pokʉ'pʉ ka' winɨpai ka'pon amʉ' pona. Mɨrɨ pɨ', ka'pon amʉ' uya Papa pa'nʉkʉ'pʉ, apʉne pʉra ɨsɨ pe kuru mɨrɨ paran esi pɨ'.
