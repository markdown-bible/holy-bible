# ISENPOIKASA' 8

## 7 Pe Iku'nin A'si'sa'

**1** Karimeru mʉre uya 7 pe iku'nin a'si'sa' a'kokasa' a'tai, e'pana Epʉn e'paka'sa' esi'pʉ a'sa'mʉyai kʉra' emiya era'tɨto' airɨ rɨ.

**2** Mɨrɨ tʉpo 7 kaisarɨ inserʉ amʉ' e'mʉ'sa'kasa' ene'pʉ uya Papa rau, mɨrɨpan kon repa'pʉ 7 kaisarɨ tʉronpi' ke.

**3** Tʉron nɨ inserʉ, korʉ pun konekasa' a'po'nan yen esa' uyee'pʉ, mɨrɨpan e'mʉ'sa'ka'pʉ use'man nɨto' apon piyau. Eke pe a'po'nan ke irepa'pʉ tanporon Papa munkɨ amʉ' ɨpʉrema piri'nin pe iye'to' pe, mɨrɨpan po'tɨto' pe korʉ pun konekasa' use'man nɨto' apon po apono' piyau te'sen.

**4** A'po'nan epo'tɨ kɨrɨsin enuku'pʉ, Papa munkɨ amʉ' ɨpʉrema pokon pe, Papa rakʉi', inserʉ emiya apai.

**5** Mɨrɨ tʉpo inserʉ uya a'po'nan yen anʉmʉ'pʉ mɨrɨpan uya apo' use'man nɨto' apon pon emaika'pʉ iya', mɨrɨpan eno'ma'pʉ iya non pona. Korʉme e'toi'pɨtʉ esi'pʉ, ta'merunsen esi'pʉ, kamiri' e'weuweuma'pʉ, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tʉrʉ'pʉnʉ esi'pʉ.

## Tʉronpi' Amʉ'

**6** Kamoro si 7 pan kon inserʉ amʉ' 7 kaisaron tʉronpi' esanon e'kama'pʉ iturunpato' pe tʉuya'nokon.

**7** Wapiyaro' inserʉ uya tʉwaai turunpa'pʉ. Mɨrɨ a'tai tuna tɨ'mooi ton mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ apo' e'sumasa' mʉn pokon pe uta'pokʉ'pʉ non pona. Mɨrɨ yau ipʉ'kʉ pe rɨ, ⅓ e'to' airɨ non epo'tɨ'pʉ mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ipʉ'kʉ pe rɨ, ⅓ e'to' airɨ rɨ yu' epo'tɨ'pʉ tanporo rɨ isenan nɨ wana' epo'tɨ'pʉ.

**8** Itisakon nʉ'pʉ uya tʉwaai turunpa'pʉ. Mɨrɨ a'tai, eke wʉ' waraino epo'tɨ apo' ke eno'ma'pʉ to' uya parau ka'. Ipʉ'kʉ pe rɨ, ⅓ e'to' airɨ parau ena'pʉ mʉn pe.

**9** Mɨrɨ a'tai, itonpa pe rɨ, ⅓ e'to' airɨ rɨ parau kau te'san eri'pɨtʉ'pʉ, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ itonpa pe rɨ, ⅓ e'to' airɨ rɨ kanau epotorʉ'pʉ ton uma'ta'pʉ.

**10** Itosorʉwano rʉ'pʉ uya tʉwaai turunpa'pʉ. Mɨrɨ a'tai, eke sirikɨ epo'tɨsa' ta'sʉrai' kasa uta'mo'ka'pʉ ka' ponsi'kɨ tuna tʉu'tɨsen ton tonpa ka', ⅓ e'to' airɨ mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ non yai tepa'kasen tuna ton ka'ne.

**11** Sirikɨ ese' esi I'ne'.[^16]Tuna tonpa, ⅓ e'to' airɨ ena'pʉ i'ne' pe. Mɨrɨ tensise tu'kan kon ka'pon amʉ' eri'pɨtʉ'pʉ, apʉne pʉra i'ne' pe tuna enasa' ke.

**12** Mɨrɨ tʉpo to' asakɨrɨ'pano uya tʉwaai turunpa'pʉ. Mɨrɨ a'tai, ⅓ e'to' airɨ wʉi enu pʉ'kʉ, ⅓ e'to' airɨ kapʉi pʉ'kʉ mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ⅓ e'to' airɨ sirikɨ amʉ' tonpa ton e'wɨnɨ'pʉ, ⅓ e'to' airɨ rɨ. A'kwa pe iye'to' pʉra to' esi'pʉ, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ⅓ e'to' airɨ wʉi naino pʉ'kʉ esi'pʉ iweyu pʉra, ⅓ e'to' airɨ kanan ewarupɨ pʉ'kʉ.

**13** Uyenu tʉrʉ'pʉ uya, mɨrɨ yau imisimɨ arinta ka'tau maimu eta'pʉ uya, eke pe, “Ɨsɨ' kusa! Ɨsɨ' kusa! Ɨsɨ pe iyesi kamoro moro non po na'ne' nan pona, tʉronpi' uturumʉ useta a'tai osorʉwawon kon inserʉ amʉ' ne'kamayai'ne' iturunpa pɨ'!” tawon.
