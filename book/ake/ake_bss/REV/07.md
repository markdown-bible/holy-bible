# ISENPOIKASA' 7

## 144,000 Kaisaron Kon Esuwerʉ Ko' Amʉ'

**1** Serɨ tʉpo, asakɨrɨ'nan kon inserʉ amʉ' ene'pʉ uya. To' e'mʉ'sa'kapɨ'sa' asakɨrɨ'nan non ekun airɨ'ne, asakɨrɨ'nan a'setun amʉ' a'si'pɨ'sa' to' uya esi'pʉ, non, parau mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ yʉi posi'ma iya namai'.

**2** Mɨrɨ a'tai, tʉron nɨ inserʉ uyepʉ ene'pʉ uya wʉi enu uye'to' winɨpai, Papa nurɨnan uya a'si'pɨ'nɨto' ike esa' pe, mɨrɨpan ukɨ'pɨ'nʉnpɨtʉ'pʉ eke pe asakɨrɨ'nan kon inserʉ amʉ' pana ya' Papa nʉrepa'san to' pana'pano ke non mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ parau kota'mato' pe to' uya.

**3** Inserʉ uya ta'pʉ, “Non, parau, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ yʉi kʉ'ma'tanʉ'tʉu Papa, itese' pɨ' ina e'to' poitorʉ ton ma'kʉi'tɨpɨtʉ to' pee'pɨ pɨ'ne a'si'pɨ'to' ike ke ina uya pʉ'kʉ pona rɨ.”

**4** Kamoro Papa uya a'si'pɨ'nɨto' ike ke ima'kʉʉi'tɨpɨ'sa' kon to' pee'pɨ pɨ' tu'kanoro ekama'pʉ to' uya upɨ'. To' esi'pʉ 144,000 kaisa rɨ Esuwerʉ pon kon.

**5** 12,000 kaisa rɨ a'si'sa' kon esi'pʉ Isuta ko' amʉ', 12,000 kaisa rɨ Rupen ko' amʉ', 12,000 kaisa rɨ Kiyatʉ ko' amʉ',

**6** 12,000 kaisa rɨ Aisarʉ ko' amʉ', 12,000 kaisa rɨ Na'tari ko' amʉ', 12,000 kaisa rɨ Mana'sa ko' amʉ',

**7** 12,000 kaisa rɨ Simiyan ko' amʉ', 12,000 kaisa rɨ Ripai ko' amʉ', 12,000 kaisa rɨ E'sa'karʉ ko' amʉ',

**8** 12,000 kaisa rɨ Sepuran ko' amʉ', 12,000 kaisa rɨ Isose' ko' amʉ', mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ 12,000 kaisa rɨ Pensiman ko' amʉ'.

## Anpisin Ton Ka'pon Amʉ' Aimu'nan To' Pon Ton

**9** Serɨ e'ma'pʉ pe, anpisin, anpisin pe kuru rɨ ka'pon amʉ' amʉrasa' ene'pʉ uya urau, ɨnʉ' uya tanporo to' i'tu poken pʉra rɨ. To' esi'pʉ tʉponaron kon ka'pon amʉ' pe'ne rɨ tʉron kon ko' pe rɨ, tʉpataron kon winɨpaino pe rɨ, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tʉmaimuron kon pe'ne rɨ to' esi'pʉ. To' e'mʉ'sa'kasa' esi'pʉ apono' piyau mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ karimeru mʉre piyau. To' epontɨpɨ'sa' esi'pʉ aimu'nan ya', temiyatʉ kon yau'ne patawa ye' apɨmu ton a'sipɨ'sa' to' uya esi'pʉ.

**10** Eke pe to' ukɨ'pɨ'nʉnpɨtʉ'pʉ:> “Epika'tɨn nɨto' esi Papa, ina e'to' itese' pɨ' winɨpaino,
> iyereutasa' na'ne' apono' po,
> mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ karimeru mʉre winɨpaino,”tukai'.

**11** Tanporo inserʉ amʉ' e'mʉ'sa'ka'pʉ apono' woi, itepuru ton mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ asakɨrɨ'nan kon ene amʉ' woi. To' uta'mo'kapɨtʉ'pʉ temʉ' kon po apono' piyau, mɨrɨpan kon uya Papa apurɨpɨtʉ'pʉ:

**12** > “Amen!
> Apurɨpɨ'to' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ eke pe iku'to',
> ipu'ketʉ, tenki tato', inamato',
> imeruntɨrʉ, ipana'pano
> nesii Papa iwano' pe tɨwɨrɨ rɨ.
> Amen!”ta'pʉ to' uya.

**13** Mɨrɨ yau, tikin nan itepuru ton tonpa uya uyekama'po'pʉ, “Ɨnʉ' yan pʉsamoro aimu'nan ya' to' epontɨpɨ'sa' na'ne' nan? Nai yawon kon uye'sa'?” ta'pʉ iya.

**14** “Mooi, mi'tuyai',” ta'pʉ uya ipɨ'. “Pʉsamoro kamoro ekota'man nɨto' yai iyepa'kasa' kon, tʉpon kon korʉkasa' to' uya aimu'ne'ne karimeru mʉre mʉnʉ ke.

**15** Mɨrɨ pe iyesi pɨ',> “Papa apon piyau to' esi mɨrɨ,> > to' esi i'se iye'to' kupʉ pɨ' wʉi nai mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ewarupɨ nau, ipɨ' ɨpʉreman nɨto', use'man nɨto' iwʉ' tau;> kʉrɨ rɨ apono' po iyereutasa' na'ne' uko'manto' oton to' pokon pe.

**16** > Serɨ tʉpo, iwan pe mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tuna'kiri' ke to' enato' oton pen,
> wʉi enu mɨrɨ pe pʉra a'ne' pe te'sen uya to' kota'mato' oton pen,

**17** > apʉne pʉra karimeru mʉre apono' aporo' po na'ne' e'to' oton to' esa' pe,> > to' ato' iya oton to'panʉ'nin non yai tepa'kasen tuna ka'.> Mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ Papa uya to' enu paru a'munakato' oton,”ta'pʉ iya.
