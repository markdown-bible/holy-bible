# ISENPOIKASA' 6

## A'si'sa' Ton

**1** Mɨrɨ a'tai, karimeru mʉre uya 7 pan tonpa wapiyaro' a'si'sa' a'koka ene'pʉ uya. Mɨrɨ a'tai, tikin nɨ asakɨrɨ'nan kon ene amʉ' tonpa usaurokʉ eta'pʉ uya korʉme kasa, “Ɨsi'kɨ!” tawon.

**2** Uyenu tʉrʉ'pʉ uya, aimu'nan kaware ene'pʉ uya urau. Ipo te'sen uya urapa a'sisa' esi'pʉ, aro', kʉron tato' ke to' uya irepa'pʉ, mɨrɨpan utɨ'pʉ iwapiya'kanin ye' pe, iwapiya'kai'.

**3** Karimeru mʉre uya isakon pe te'sen a'si'sa' a'kokasa' a'tai, ene isakon uya, “Ɨsi'kɨ!” ta eta'pʉ uya.

**4** Tʉron nɨ kaware epa'ka'pʉ, isuweyu. Ipo te'sen kupʉ'pʉ to' uya pana' pe iye'to' pe, e'wɨn nɨto' ku'to' pe iya serɨ non po tʉtonpa kon wɨto' pe ka'pon amʉ' uya mɨrɨpan repa'pʉ to' uya eke supara ke.

**5** Karimeru mʉre uya itosorʉwano pe te'sen a'si'sa' a'kokasa' a'tai, ene amʉ' osorʉwano uya, “Ɨsi'kɨ!” ta eta'pʉ uya. Mɨrɨ yau, i'kʉrʉn kaware esi'pʉ. Ipo te'sen uya e'wemapɨ'nɨto' ipoi a'sisa' esi'pʉ tenau.

**6** Mɨrɨ a'tai, main waraino eta'pʉ uya kamoro asakɨrɨ'nan kon ene amʉ' koro'tau, “Aiko iten yai pʉrowa,[^13]tikin nan wʉi yau e'tʉrawasoman nɨsa' epe'pʉ pe,[^14]mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ osorʉwau iten yai a'nai', pari tato' e'pasa' tikin nan wʉi yau e'tʉrawasoman nɨsa' epe'pʉ pe. E'tane karapa, are' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ waain kʉ'ma'tanʉkʉi!” tawon.

**7** Karimeru mʉre uya isakɨrɨ'pano a'si'sa' a'kokasa' a'tai, ene amʉ' sakɨrɨ'pano uya, “Ɨsi'kɨ!” ta eta'pʉ uya.

**8** Mɨrɨ yau, enkɨ'sɨimu'nan kaware ene'pʉ uya urau. Ipo te'sen ese' esi'pʉ Eri'nɨto', mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ekota'man nɨto', Etis[^15]itese' esi'pʉ ite'ma'pʉ winɨ, a'ko pe. ¼ pe non esa' pe to' e'to' pe, to' kupʉ'pʉ to' uya tʉwɨsen pe to' e'to' pe supara ke, iwankan ke, paran ke, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ annin nan ene amʉ' ke.

**9** Karimeru mʉre uya 5 pe iku'nin a'si'sa' a'kokasa' a'tai, use'man nɨto' apon o'no ka'pon amʉ' a'kwarʉ ene'pʉ uya, iwɨpɨ'sa' kon Papa Maimu ekama to' uya pɨkɨrɨ mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ Papa nʉkupʉ'pʉ tʉpɨ'nokon ekama pɨ' to' e'sa' pɨ'.

**10** To' ukɨ'pɨ'nʉnpɨtʉ'pʉ eke pe, “Meruntɨ Itepuru, wakʉ ɨmɨrɨ, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ i'napairon! Ɨ'rɨ a'tai non pon kon ka'pon amʉ' eseru aimenka auya mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ to' kota'mato' auya ina mʉnʉ wenai?”

**11** To' kaisa rɨ aimu'nan pon ke to' repapɨtʉ'pʉ, mɨrɨpan kon auro'ka'pʉ mara rɨ to' enkurunto' pe tʉtonpa kon ipoitorʉ ton mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tʉtonpa kon to' esi'pʉ kasa iwɨton kon tu'kanoro e'seporo pona rɨ.

**12** Mɨrɨ a'tai, karimeru mʉre uya 6 pe iku'nin a'si'sa' a'koka ene'pʉ uya. Mɨrɨ yau ɨsɨ pe tʉrʉ'pʉnʉ esi'pʉ. Wʉi enu ena'pʉ i'kʉrʉn pe, isa'ka'si atʉ kapirita i'po' ke ikonekasa' kasa, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tanporo kapʉi ena'pʉ isuweyu pe mʉn kasa,

**13** sirikɨ amʉ' uta'pokʉ'pʉ ka' poi non pona, iyakʉ'pʉsa' pen kiyari, pi' eperu a'pokʉ a'setun uya kasa.

**14** Ka' pʉra iyena'pʉ kareta amon e'mepɨtʉ a'taino kasa, tanporo wʉ' amʉ' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ opa'wʉ ton utamapɨtʉ'pʉ tʉpata'se' yawonsi'kɨ'ne.

**15** Mɨrɨ a'tai si kin amʉ' non pon kon, itepuru amʉ', soisa epuru amʉ', ipʉrataaikena' ton, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ eke ton, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tanporon kon nɨ poitorʉ ton mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ɨnʉ' enawon kon pen nɨ e'so'nanpɨtʉ'pʉ tɨ' euta na'ne' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ wʉ' po te'san tɨ' o'koi'ne.

**16** To' ukɨ'pɨ'nʉnpɨtʉ'pʉ wʉ' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tɨ' amʉ' pɨ', “Ina pona ata'mo'ka', ina onankɨ kʉrɨ rɨ apono' po iyereutasa' na'ne' enu piyapai mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ karimeru mʉre uya kota'man nɨto' piyapai!

**17** Apʉne pʉra eke to' uya kota'man nɨto' weyu si serɨ yau man. Ɨnʉ' uya ken si a'kʉre'tɨ?” ta'pʉ to' uya.
