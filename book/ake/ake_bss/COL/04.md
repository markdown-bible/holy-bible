# KORO'SE 4

**1** Epuru ton, ayapiyo' kon amʉ' irepatɨ' ipokena' ke mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ wakʉ ke, apʉne pʉra i'tu auya'nokon ɨyepuru kon kanan esi Epʉn po.

## Miyarɨ Rɨ Enupan Nɨto'

**2** Tɨwɨrɨ rɨ tʉwɨpʉremasan pe e'tɨ', tenu ke esii'ma mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tenki tawon pe.

**3** Ina pona rɨ marɨ ɨpʉrematɨ', Papa uya ina maimu e'ma a'kokato' pe, ama'aino Kʉrai eseru enpoikato' pe ina uya, ipɨkɨrɨ i'sen yau e'ai'ne'.

**4** Ɨpʉrematɨ' wakʉ pe tʉto'ka'nʉ'sen pe, iye'to' awɨrɨ ekamasa' uya e'to' pe.

**5** Ti'tui' kuru e'tɨ' ɨ'rɨ rɨ kupʉ auya'nokon a'tai so'si tawon kon pen pɨ'; wakʉ pe ɨweyu kon tapurɨi' e'tɨ'.

**6** Tɨwɨ wakʉ nonkasa' pe kuru amaimu kon nesii, ine'ne'masa' pe, tanporo ka'pon amʉ' maimu eikʉ eseru i'tu kon pa.

## Iu'matʉ Pe Rɨ Miyarɨ Tato'

**7** Tai'ki'kas uya tanporo uyekareei ekama mɨrɨ ɨpɨ'nokon. Tonpa rʉ'kwɨ kʉrɨ rɨ, i'nairon pika'tɨnin nɨ mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ipoitorʉ pe e'nɨ tonpa Itepuru yau.

**8** Ennoko uya serɨ ɨpiya'nokon mɨrɨto' pe tukai', ɨ'rɨ kasa ina emakʉ tukai' i'tu kon pa,[^10]mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ɨyewan kon pori'mapɨ'se'na.

**9** Utɨn pɨ' man, Onisimos pokon pe, i'nairon mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ utonpa kon nʉ'kwɨ, ɨtonpa kon nɨ apata kon apaino. To' uya tanporo serɨ yau te'ku'sen ekama mɨrɨ ɨpɨ'nokon.

**10** Utonpa, a'sisa' pe te'sen Aresta'kos miyarɨ ta pɨ' man ɨpɨ'nokon, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ Ma', Panapas e'se uya nɨrɨ. (Ipɨ' ayauro'kato' kon epokaa'pʉ auya'nokon; ɨpiya'nokon iye'sa' yau, mereutanʉ'tʉi'.)

**11** Sises, Isustas tukai' tesa'sen kanan miyarɨ ta pɨ' man. Pʉsamoro rɨken Esuwerʉ amʉ' e'tʉrawasoma tonpa ton Papa e'to' esa' wannɨ pe iwano' pe, to' uya enpoikasa' upori'mato' tʉuya'nokon.

**12** E'pa'pʉras, ɨtonpa kon apata kon apaino, Kʉrai Sises poitorʉ miyarɨ ta pɨ' man. Inke kuru ɨpʉreman pɨ' e'suran man pɨ' man ɨiwano' kon pe, pana' pe ɨye'mʉ'sa'ka kon pa Papa e'to' i'se yau, iyakʉ'pʉsa' pe mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ipana'tɨsa' pe.

**13** Ɨnekamapai e'ai' ɨpɨ'nokon, sa'man pe kuru iye'tʉrawasoma ɨiwano' kon pe, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ Ʉrawote'siya mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ Era'poris pon kon iwano' pe.

**14** Utonpa kon nʉ'kwɨ, Ʉru', ta'ta pe te'sen mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ Temas miyarɨ ta pɨ' man.

**15** Miyarɨ ta to' uya utonpa ton Ʉrawote'siya pon kon pɨ', ekamatɨ' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ Ninpa[^11]pɨ' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ itiwʉ' tau so'si pɨ' te'san pɨ'.

**16** Serɨ kareta usekama ɨpɨ'nokon tʉpo, Ʉrawote'siya po so'si pɨ' kanan isekama mentʉi', mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ɨmɨrɨ'nokon kanan Ʉrawote'siya pon kon karetaai unennoko'pʉ rɨ marɨ mekamatʉi'.

**17** A'ki'pas pɨ' ka'tɨ': “Ɨtʉrawasooi enuka'nʉ'ka'kɨ ɨneporo'pʉ Itepuru yau,” ka'tɨ'.

**18** Urɨ, Paarʉ, uya serɨ miyarɨ tato' menuka serɨ uyemiyatʉ ke. A'sisa' pe esi mi'tutʉi'. Wakʉ nonkasa' nesii ɨpiyau'nokon.
