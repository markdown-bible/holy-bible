# KORO'SE 1

**1** Paarʉ, Kʉrai Sises nennoko'pʉ itekare ekamai' Papa eseru awɨrɨ, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ utonpa kon Temu'ti winɨpaino,

**2** Wakʉ ton mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tʉusaurokʉ'pʉ awɨron kontonpa ton Kʉrai yau Koro'se pon kon enakan: Tɨwɨ wakʉ nonkasa' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tʉusewankamai' pʉra e'nɨto' nesii ɨyenakan kon pe, Papa, ukʉipʉnʉ kon winon.[^1]

## Tenki Tato' Mɨrɨ Awonsi'kɨ Ɨpʉreman Nɨto'

**3** Inke rɨ tenki ta pɨ' ina man, Papa, uyepuru kon Sises Kʉrai kʉipʉnʉ pɨ', ina ɨpʉrema ɨpona'nokon a'tai,

**4** apʉne pʉra Kʉrai Sises apurɨsa' auya'nokon pɨ' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tanporo wakʉ ton i'nʉnkasa' auya'nokon etasa' ina uya pɨ'—

**5** apurɨto' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ usi'nʉnkan nɨto' Epʉn po na'ne' ɨiwano' kon pe ɨye'ku'to' kon pɨkɨron, ɨnetakaa'pʉ kon nʉ'pʉ i'nairon main yau, wakʉ itekare

**6** ɨpiya'nokon iye'sa' na'ne'. Tanporo non poro serɨ wakʉ itekare esi epetan pɨ' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ epan nɨ pɨ', ɨ'koro'tau'nokon ne'ku'taine' kasa kuru eta'pʉ auya'nokon si'kɨrɨ mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ Papa uya wakʉ nonkasa' to'ka'nʉkʉ'pʉ auya'nokon tanporo i'nai rɨ iye'to' yau.

**7** Serɨ pɨ' ɨusenupasa' kon ina tonpa rʉ'kwɨ E'pa'pʉras winɨ, Kʉrai apiyo' pe tʉusaurokʉ'pʉ awɨrɨ te'tʉrawasomasen ina pe rɨ,

**8** mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ekama'pʉ iya ina pɨ' ɨusi'nʉnkato' kon esi Iya'kwarʉ uya ɨkupʉ kon ke.

**9** Mɨrɨ wenai, ɨyeta'pʉ kon tʉuya'nokon si'kɨrɨ ɨpona'nokon tʉwɨpʉremato' kon mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ Papa ekama'poto' tʉuya'nokon ayarʉ'tɨto' kon pe iya iteseru i'tuto' auya'nokon ike ke tanporo iya'kwarʉ ipu'ketʉ mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ito'ka'nʉ'to' poro ereutanʉ'sa' ina uya pʉra rɨ iyesi.

**10** Ina ɨpʉrema serɨ pɨ' ɨuko'mamʉ kon pa Itepuru enu yau wakʉ pe te'sen uko'man nɨto' yau, mɨrɨpan pori'manin pe ɨwesi kon pa tanporon yau rɨ, tanporon yau rɨ wakʉ tʉrawaso yau epetan pɨ' Papa i'tuto' yau usemo'kan pɨ',

**11** imeruntɨtasa' pe tanporon meruntɨ ke a'kwa yau iye'to' meruntɨrʉ ke, imoronkato' esa' pe ɨwesi kon pa eke pe mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tʉuseta'kai' pʉra ɨwe'to' kon, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ pori' pe esii'ma

**12** tenki ta pɨ' Ikʉipʉnʉ pɨ', ɨkonekanin nʉ'pʉ kon wakʉ ton pokon pe ɨwesi kon pa a'kwa pe iweyu pe te'sen e'to' esa' wannɨ pe anʉmʉ pɨ'.

**13** Apʉne pʉra ewarupɨ meruntɨrʉ apai umo'kasa' iya 'nokon, mɨrɨpan uya une'sa' kon tʉmu, tʉni'nʉnkanʉ e'to' esa' wannɨ pe ya',

**14** kʉrɨ rɨ winɨ umakooi kon use'masa',[^2]upɨ'nokon isentu'ma ke.

## Eke Pe Kʉrai E'to'

**15** Kʉrai esi tʉusensen pen Papa pe rɨ, Eke Kuru tanporon iye'ka'sa' ton na'ne' entaino.

**16** Kʉrɨ rɨ winɨ Papa uya tanporon nɨ koneka'pʉ: ka'yawon ton mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ non pon ton nɨ, tʉusensen mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ tʉusensen pen, apono' amʉ', pata esanon, meruntɨ mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ eke ton nɨ kanan; tanporon ekoneka'pʉ iwinɨ mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ itiwano' pe.

**17** Tanporon nɨ wapiyaro' pe iyesi, mɨrɨpan yau tanporon nɨ uta'si a'samʉtʉ pe.

**18** Tʉpun, so'si i'pai pe iyesi; mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ipʉrakon pe iyesi, iyepiya'tɨ pe iyepa'kasa' rʉ'pʉ pe iyesi eri'nɨto' apai, mɨrɨ pɨ' tanporon epoi eke pe kuru te'to' pe.

**19** Apʉne pʉra Papa esi'pʉ pori' pe tanporo rɨ tʉmɨrɨ rɨ esi pɨ' iyau,

**20** mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ Sises poro tanporon nɨ ekonekato' pe Papa pokon pe non mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ ka' po na'ne' rɨ, kʉrɨ rɨ imʉnʉ iye'kwansa' rʉ'pʉ kurusu' po uya tʉusewankamai' pʉra e'nɨto' ku'sa'.

**21** Ɨnnɨ Papa piyapai ɨwesi'pʉ kon, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ iteyaton non pe ɨwesi'pʉ kon ɨusenuminkato' kon yau apʉne pʉra ɨri kupʉ auya'nokon pɨ'.

**22** E'tane serɨ pe ɨkonekasa' iya 'nokon tʉpokon pe, Kʉrai pun winɨ iyeri'to' winɨ, ɨtʉto' kon pe tʉuya wakʉ pe tenu yau, imaku'pɨ pe pʉra, ɨpɨ'nokon e'nɨto' iwenai pʉra—

**23** miyarɨ rɨ apurɨto' auya'nokon yau ɨwesi kon yau iye'mʉ'sa'kasa' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ pana'pan pe, tʉutamai' pʉra, ɨye'ku'to' kon apai wakʉ itekare nekama'pʉ. Serɨ serɨ wakʉ itekare ɨneta'pʉ kon mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ isekamasa' rʉ'pʉ tanporon kon ika'sa' kon pana ya' ka' o'non kon iwano' pe, mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ urɨ, Paarʉ, enasa' ipoitorʉ pe wenai.

## Paarʉ E'tʉrawasomato' So'si Iwano' Pe

**24** Serɨ pe si pori' pe e'ai', ɨiwano' kon pe ekota'man nʉ'pʉ pɨ', mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ upun yau nin si arʉ'tɨ uya ipʉra na'ne' Kʉrai ekota'ma'pʉ airɨ, ipun pɨkɨrɨ, so'si pe na'ne'.

**25** Ipoitorʉ pe enasa' man, Papa nʉtʉrʉ'pʉ unkupʉnʉ pe uyena' winɨ, ɨyena'nokon itʉto' pe uya imaimu tanporo iye'to' awɨrɨ—

**26** ama'aino tonan tau iko'mansa' rʉ'pʉ inke kuru rɨ penaro' kon piyapai rɨ nɨrɨ, e'tane serɨ pe isenpoikasa' wakʉ ton ena'.

**27** To' ena' enpoikato' pe tʉuya Papa e'ku'sa' Esuwerʉ amʉ' pen koro'tau ama'aino eke pe rɨ te'sen, Kʉrai pe na'ne' ayau'nokon, eke pe e'nɨto' pɨ' e'ku'nɨto' pe te'sen.

**28** Ina man ekama pɨ', ka'pon amʉ' auro'ka pɨ' mɨrɨ awonsi'kɨ to' enupa pɨ' tanporon ipu'ketʉ pokon pe, ina uya tanporon kon tʉto' pe wakʉ pe Kʉrai yau.

**29** Serɨ pʉ'kʉ pona e'tʉrawasoma serɨ, e'suranman pɨ' tanporo ipana'pano ke iye'tʉrawasoma meruntɨ pe kuru uyau.
