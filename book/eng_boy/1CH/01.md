# Chronicles I 1

**1** Adam, Seth, Enosh,

**2** and Kenan, Mahalalel, Jared,

**3** Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech,

**4** Noah: the sons of Noah, Shem, Ham, Japheth.

**5** The sons of Japheth, Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Elisa, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras.

**6** And the sons of Gomer, Ashkenaz, and Riphath, and Togarmah.

**7** And the sons of Javan, Elishah, and Tarshish, the Kittim, and Rodanim.

**8** And the sons of Ham, Cush, and Mizraim, Put and Canaan.

**9** And the sons of Cush, Seba, and Havilah, and Sabta, and Raamah, and Sabteca: and the sons of Raamah, Sheba, and Dedan.

**10** And Cush begot Nimrod: he began to be a mighty hunter on the earth.

**17** The sons of Shem, Elam, and Asshur,

**24** and Arpachshad, Shelah,

**25** Eber, Peleg, Reu,

**26** Serug, Nahor, Terah,

**27** Abraham.

**28** And the sons of Abraham, Isaac, and Ishmael.

**29** And these are their generations: the firstborn of Ishmael, Nebaioth, and Kedar, Abdeel, Mibsam,

**30** Mishma, Dumah, Massa, Hadad, Tema,

**31** Jetur, Naphish, Kedemah: these are the sons of Ishmael.

**32** And the sons of Keturah Abraham’s concubine—and she bore him Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, Shuah: and the sons of Jokshan; Dedan, and Sheba;

**33** and the sons of Midian; Ephah, and Epher, and Hanoch, and Abida, and Eldaah; all these were the sons of Keturah.

**34** And Abraham begot Isaac: and the sons of Isaac were Jacob, and Esau.

**35** The sons of Esau, Eliphaz, and Reuel, and Jeush, and Jalam, and Korah.

**36** The sons of Eliphaz: Teman, and Omar, Zepho, and Gatam, and Kenaz, and Timna, and Amalek.

**37** And the sons of Reuel, Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah.

**38** The sons of Seir, Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan.

**39** And the sons of Lotan, Hori, and Hemam; and the sister of Lotan was Timna.

**40** The sons of Shobal; Alvan, Manahath, Ebal, Shepho, and Onam: and the sons of Zibeon; Aiah, and Anah.

**41** The sons of Anah, Dishon: and the sons of Dishon; Hemdan, and Eshban, and Ithran, and Cheran.

**42** And the sons of Ezer, Bilhan, and Zaavan, and Akan: the sons of Dishan, Uz, and Aran.

**43** And these are their kings, Bela the son of Beor; and the name of his city was Dinhabah.

**44** And Bela died, and Jobab the son of Zerah of Bozrah reigned in his stead.

**45** And Jobab died, and Husham of the land of the Temanites reigned in his stead.

**46** And Husham died, and Hadad the son of Bedad reigned in his stead, who smote Midian in the plain of Moab: and the name of his city was Getthaim.

**47** And Hadad died, and Samlah of Masrekah reigned in his stead.

**48** And Samlah died, and Shaul of Rehoboth by the river reigned in his stead.

**49** And Shaul died, and Baal-hanan son of Achbor reigned in his stead.

**50** And Baal-hanan died, and Hadad son of Barad reigned in his stead; and the name of his city was Pai.

**51** The princes of Edom: prince Timna, prince Alvah, prince Jetheth,

**52** prince Oholibamah, prince Elah, prince Pinon,

**53** prince Kenaz, prince Teman, prince Mibzar, prince Magdiel,

**54** prince Zaphoin. These are the princes of Edom.
