# Psalms 128

**1** A Song of Degrees.> Many a time have they warred against me from my youth,
> Let Israel now say:

**2** > Many a time have they warred against me from my youth:
> And yet they prevailed not against me.

**3** > The sinners wrought upon my back:
> They prolonged their iniquity.

**4** > The righteous Lord has cut asunder the necks of sinners.


**5** > Let all that hate Zion
> Be put to shame and turned back.

**6** > Let them be as the grass of the housetops,
> Which withers before it is plucked up.

**7** > Wherewith the reaper fills not his hand,
> Nor he that makes up the sheaves, his bosom.

**8** > Neither do they that go by say, The blessing of the Lord be upon you:
> We have blessed you in the name of the Lord.
