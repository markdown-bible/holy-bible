# LAMENTATIONS 5

**1** Lord, have thou mind what befell to us; see thou, and behold our shame.

**2** Our heritage is turned to aliens, our houses be turned to strangers.

**3** We be made fatherless children without a father; our mothers be as widows.

**4** We drank our water for money, we bought our wood for silver.

**5** We were driven by our heads, and rest was not given to faint men.

**6** We gave hand to Egypt, and to Assyrians, that we should be [ful] filled with bread.

**7** Our fathers sinned, and be not, and we bare the wickednesses of them

**8** Servants were lords of us, and none was, that again-bought from the hand of them.

**9** In our lives we brought bread to us, from the face of [the] sword in desert.

**10** Our skin is burnt as a furnace, of the face of tempests of hunger.

**11** They made low women in Zion, and virgins in the cities of Judah.

**12** Princes were hanged [up] by the hand; they were not ashamed of the faces of eld [or old] men.

**13** They misused young waxing men unchastely, and children fell down in tree.

**14** Eld [or Old] men failed from [the] gates; young men failed from the quire or choir of singers.

**15** The joy of our heart failed; our song is turned into mourning.

**16** The crown of our head fell down; woe to us! for we sinned.

**17** Therefore our heart is made sorrowful, therefore our eyes be made dark.

**18** For the hill of Zion, for it perished; foxes went in it.

**19** But thou, Lord, shalt dwell without end; thy seat shall dwell in generation and into generation.

**20** Why shalt thou forget us without end, shalt thou forsake us into [the] length of days?

**21** Lord, convert thou us to thee, and we shall be converted; make thou new our days, as at the beginning.

**22** But thou casting away hast cast away us; thou art wroth against us greatly.
