# ZECHARIAH 5

**1** And I was converted, and raised mine eyes, and saw, and lo! a book flying.

**2** And he said to me, What seest thou? And I said, Lo! I see a book flying; the length thereof was of twenty cubits, and the breadth thereof of ten cubits.

**3** And he said to me, This is the curse, that goeth on the face of all earth; for each thief shall be deemed, as it is written there; and each man swearing falsely shall be deemed of this also.

**4** I shall lead out it, saith the Lord of hosts, and it shall come to the house of a thief, and to the house of him that sweareth falsely in my name; and it shall dwell in the middle of his house, and shall waste him, and his trees, and his stones.

**5** And the angel went out, that spake in me, and said to me, Raise up thine eyes, and see, what this thing is that goeth out.

**6** And I said, What is it? And he said, This is an amphora, either a pot, going out. And he said, This is the eye of them in all earth.

**7** And lo! a talent of lead was borne up from it; and lo! a woman sitting in middle of the pot.

**8** And he said, This is unpiety, either unfaithfulness. And he casted down her in the middle of the pot, and sent a gobbet of lead into the mouth thereof.

**9** And I raised mine eyes, and saw, and lo! two women going out, and a spirit in the wings of them; and they had wings as the wings of a kite, and raised the pot betwixt heaven and earth.

**10** And I said to the angel that spake in me, Whither bear these the pot?

**11** And he said to me, That an house be builded thereto in the land of Shinar, and be stablished, and set there on his foundament.
