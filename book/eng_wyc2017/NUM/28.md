# NUMBERS 28

**1** Also the Lord said to Moses,

**2** Command thou to the sons of Israel, and thou shalt say to them, Offer ye by their times mine offering, and loaves, and incense of sweetest odour.

**3** These be the sacrifices which ye owe to offer; two lambs of one year, without wem, each day, into ever-lasting burnt sacrifice.

**4** Ye shall offer one lamb early, and the tother at eventide.

**5** The tenth part of ephah of tried flour, that shall be sprinkled with purest oil, and have it the fourth part of hin.

**6** It is continual burnt sacrifice, which ye offered in the hill [or mount] of Sinai, into odour of sweetest incense to the Lord.

**7** And ye shall offer the fourth part of hin of wine, by each lamb, in the saintuary of the Lord.

**8** And ye shall offer in like manner the tother lamb at eventide, by all the custom of the morrow sacrifice, and of the moist [or liquor] sacrifices thereof, an offering of sweetest odour to the Lord.

**9** Soothly in the sabbath day ye shall offer two lambs of one year, without wem, and two tenth parts of tried flour sprinkled altogether with oil, in sacrifice,

**10** and the moist [or liquor] sacrifices that be poured by custom, by all sabbaths, into everlasting burnt sacrifice.

**11** Soothly in the calends, that is, in the beginnings of months, ye shall offer burnt sacrifice to the Lord, twain [or two] calves of the drove, one ram, seven lambs of one year, without wem,

**12** and three tenth parts of [tried] flour sprinkled altogether with oil, in the sacrifice, by each calf, and two tenth parts of [tried] flour sprinkled altogether with oil, by each ram;

**13** and the tenth part of the tenth of [tried] flour sprinkled altogether with oil, in the sacrifice, by each lamb; it is burnt sacrifice of sweetest odour, and of incense to the Lord.

**14** Soothly the moist [or liquor] sacrifices of wine, that shall be poured by all the slain sacrifices, shall be these; the half part of a hin by each calf, the third part by a ram, the fourth part by a lamb; this shall be the burnt sacrifices by each month, that come one after another while the year turneth about.

**15** Also a buck of goats shall be offered to the Lord for sins, into everlasting burnt sacrifice, with his moist [or liquor] offerings.

**16** Soothly in the first month, in the fourteenth day of the month, shall be pask, either passing over, of the Lord;

**17** and in the fifteenth day shall be the solemnity of therf loaves. By seven days ye shall eat therf loaves;

**18** of which the first day shall be worshipful and holy; ye shall not do any servile work therein.

**19** And ye shall offer burnt sacrifice to the Lord, two calves, one ram, seven lambs of one year, without wem;

**20** and the sacrifices of each by itself of [tried] flour, which be sprinkled altogether with oil, three tenth parts by each calf, and two tenth parts by a ram,

**21** and the tenth part of the tenth by each lamb, that is, by seven lambs.

**22** And ye shall offer one buck of goats for sin, that cleansing be made for you,

**23** besides the burnt sacrifice of the morrowtide, which ye shall offer ever-[more].

**24** So ye shall do by each day of [the] seven days, into the nourishing of fire, and into the sweetest odour to the Lord, that shall rise of the burnt sacrifice, and of the moist [or liquor] sacrifices of each offering.

**25** Also the seventh day shall be most solemn and holy to you; ye shall not do any servile work therein.

**26** Also the day of the first fruits, when ye shall offer new fruits to the Lord, when the weeks shall be fulfilled, shall be worshipful and holy; ye shall not do any servile work therein.

**27** And ye shall offer burnt sacrifice to the Lord, into sweetest odour; two calves of the drove, one ram, and seven lambs of one year, without wem;

**28** and in the sacrifices of those [or them]ye shall offer three tenth parts of tried flour sprinkled altogether with oil, by each calf, two tenth parts by the rams,

**29** the tenth part of the tenth by the lambs, which be altogether seven lambs.

**30** And ye shall offer a buck of goats, which is offered for cleansing,

**31** besides [the] burnt sacrifice ever-lasting, and the moist [or liquor] sacrifices thereof; ye shall offer all things without wem, with their moist [or liquor] sacrifices.
