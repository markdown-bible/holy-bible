# JONAH 2

**1** And Jonah prayed to the Lord his God from the fish’s womb,

**2** and said, I cried to God of my tribulation, and he heard me; from the womb of hell I cried, and thou heardest my voice.

**3** Thou castedest me down into deepness, in the heart of the sea, and the floods compassed me [about]; all thy swells and thy waves passed on me.

**4** And I said, I am cast away from the sight of thine eyes; nevertheless again I shall see thine holy temple.

**5** Waters encompassed me till to my soul, deepness environed me, the seaweed covered mine head.

**6** I went down to the uttermost places of hills, the bars of earth enclosed me altogether, into without end; and thou, my Lord God, shalt raise up my life from corruption.

**7** When my soul was anguished in me, I bethought on the Lord, that my prayer come to thee, to thine holy temple.

**8** They that keep vanities, forsake their mercy idly.

**9** But I in voice of praising shall offer to thee; whatever things I vowed, I shall yield to the Lord, for mine health.

**10** And the Lord said to the fish, and it casted out Jonah on/to the dry land.
