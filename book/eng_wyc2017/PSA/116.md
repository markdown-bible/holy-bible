# PSALMS 116

**1** Alleluia. I loved the Lord; for the Lord shall hear the voice of my prayer.

**2** For he bowed down his ear to me; and I shall inwardly call him in my days.

**3** The sorrows of death encompass-ed me; and the perils of hell found me. I found tribulation and sorrow;

**4** and I called inwardly the name of the Lord. Thou, Lord, deliver my soul;

**5** the Lord is merciful, and just [or rightwise]; and our God doeth mercy.

**6** And the Lord keepeth little children; I was meeked, and he delivered me.

**7** My soul, turn thou into thy rest; for the Lord hath done well to thee.

**8** For he hath delivered my soul from death; mine eyes from weepings, and my feet from falling down.

**9** I shall please the Lord; in the country of them that live.

**10** I believed, for which thing I spake; forsooth I was made low full much.

**11** I said in my passing; Each man is a liar.

**12** What shall I yield to the Lord; for all things which he hath yielded to me?

**13** I shall take the cup of health; and I shall inwardly call the name of the Lord.

**14** I shall yield my vows to the Lord before all his people;

**15** the death of saints of the Lord is precious in his sight.

**16** O! Lord, for I am thy servant; I am thy servant, and the son of thine handmaid. Thou hast broken my bonds,

**17** to thee I shall offer a sacrifice of praising; and I shall inwardly call the name of the Lord.

**18** I shall yield my vows to the Lord, in the sight of all his people;

**19** in the foreyards of the house of the Lord, in the midst of thee, Jerusalem.
