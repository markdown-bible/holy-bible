# JOB 25

**1** Then Bildad the Shuhite answered, and said,

**2** Power and dread is with him, that is, God, which maketh according in his high things.

**3** Whether there is a number of his knights? and upon whom shineth not his light?

**4** Whether a man comparisoned to God may be justified, either a man born of a woman may appear clean?

**5** Lo! also the moon shineth not, and [the] stars be not clean in his sight;

**6** how much more man, that is rot, and the son of a man, that is a worm, is unclean in comparison to God.
