# PSALMS 87

*A song. A psalm of the descendants of Korah.*

**1** > The Lord founded the city on his holy mountain.

**2** > Jerusalem is the city that the Lord loves more than any other city in Israel.[^300]

**3** > Many wonderful things are said of you, city of God! Selah.

**4** > I mention Egypt[^301]> and Babylon as those who know me, and in addition Philistia, Tyre, and Ethiopia—“this man was born there.”[^302]

**5** > It will be said concerning Jerusalem, “Everyone was born there,”[^303]> and the Most High will make it secure.

**6** > When the Lord registers the nations, he will write, “They were born there.”[^304]> Selah.

**7** > Singers and dancers alike say, “Living here I am at home.”[^305]
