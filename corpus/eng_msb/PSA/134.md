# Psalm 134

**1** A song of ascents. Come, bless the LORD, all you servants of the LORD who serve by night in the house of the LORD!

**2** Lift up your hands to the sanctuary and bless the LORD!

**3** May the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth, bless you from Zion.
