# PSALMS 101

**1** The psalm of David. Lord, I shall sing to thee; mercy and doom. I shall sing,

**2** and I shall understand in a way without wem; when thou shalt come to me. I went perfectly in the inno-cence of mine heart; in the middle of mine house.

**3** I setted not forth before mine eyes an unjust thing; I hated them that made trespassings.

**4** A shrewd heart cleaved not to me; I knew not a wicked man bowing away from me.

**5** I pursued him that backbited privily his neighbour. With the proud eye and an heart unable to be filled; I ate not with this.

**6** Mine eyes were to the faithful men of earth, that they sit with me; he that went in a way without wem, ministered [or served] to me.

**7** He that doeth pride, shall not dwell in the middle of mine house; he that speaketh wicked things, served not in the sight of mine eyes.

**8** In the morrowtide I killed all the sinners of earth; that I should lose from the city of the Lord all men working wickedness.
