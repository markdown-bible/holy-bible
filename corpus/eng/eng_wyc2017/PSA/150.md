# PSALMS 150

**1** Alleluia. Praise ye the Lord in his saints; praise ye him in the firmament of his virtue.

**2** Praise ye him in his virtues; praise ye him by the multitude of his greatness.

**3** Praise ye him in the sound of a trump; praise ye him in a psaltery and harp.

**4** Praise ye him in a tympan and quire; praise ye him in strings and organ.

**5** Praise ye him in cymbals sounding well, praise ye him in cymbals of jubilation;

**6** each spirit, praise the Lord.
