# PSALMS 106

**1** Alleluia. Acknowledge ye to the Lord, for he is good; for his mercy is without end.

**2** Who shall speak of the powers of the Lord; shall make known all his praisings?

**3** Blessed be they that keep doom; and do rightfulness [or rightwiseness] in all time.

**4** Lord, have thou mind on us, in the good pleasance of thy people; visit thou us in thine health.

**5** To see in the goodness of thy chosen men, to be glad in the gladness of thy folk; that thou be praised with thine heritage.

**6** We have sinned with our fathers; we have done unjustly, we have done wickedness.

**7** Our fathers in Egypt understood not thy marvels; they were not mindful of the multitude of thy mercy. And they going into the sea, into the Red Sea, stirred him to wrath;

**8** and he saved them for his name, that he should make known his power.

**9** And he parted the Red Sea, and it was dried; and he led forth them in the depths of waters, as in desert.

**10** And he saved them from the hand of haters; and he again-bought them from the hand of the enemy.

**11** And the waters covered men troubling them; one of them abode not.

**12** And they believed to his words; and they praised the praising of him.

**13** They had soon done, they forgat his works; and they abided not his counsel.

**14** And they coveted covetousness in desert; and tempted God in a place without water.

**15** And he gave to them the asking of them; and he sent fullness into the souls of them.

**16** And they wrathed Moses in the castles [or tents]; Aaron, the holy man of the Lord.

**17** The earth was opened, and swal-lowed [down] Dathan; and covered on the congregation of Abiram.

**18** And fire burnt on high in the synagogue of them; flame burnt [the] sinners.

**19** And they made a calf in Horeb; and worshipped a molten image.

**20** And they exchanged their glory; into the likeness of a calf eating hay.

**21** They forgat God, that saved them, that did great works in Egypt,

**22** marvels in the land of Ham; fearedful things in the Red Sea.

**23** And God said, that he would lose them; and he would have, if Moses, his chosen man, had not stood in the breaking of his sight. That he should turn away his ire; lest he lost them.

**24** And they had the desirable land for nought, for they believed not to his word,

**25** and they grutched in their taber-nacles; they heard not the voice of the Lord.

**26** And he raised his hand on them; to cast down them in desert.

**27** And to cast away their seed in nations; and to lose them in countries.

**28** And they made sacrifice to Baal-peor; and they ate the sacrifices of dead beasts.

**29** And they wrathed God in their findings; and falling, either death, was multiplied in them.

**30** And Phinehas stood, and pleased God; and the vengeance ceased.

**31** And it was areckoned to him to rightfulness [or rightwiseness]; in gen-eration and into generation, till into without end.

**32** And they wrathed God at the waters of against-saying; and Moses was travailed for them, that is, troubled in soul,

**33** for they made bitter his spirit, and he parted [or ordained] in his lips.

**34** They lost not [the] heathen men; which the Lord said to them to do.

**35** And they were meddled [or mingled] among heathen men, and learned the works of them;

**36** and served the graven images of them; and it was made to them into cause of stumbling.

**37** And they offered their sons, and their daughters, to fiends.

**38** And they shedded out innocent blood, the blood of their sons and of their daughters; which they sacrificed to the graven images of Canaan. And the earth was slain in bloods,

**39** and was defouled in the works of them; and they did fornication in their findings.

**40** And the Lord was wroth by strong vengeance against his people; and he had abomination of his heritage.

**41** And he betook them into the hands of heathen men; and they that hated them, were lords of them.

**42** And their enemies did tribulation to them, and they were meeked under the hands of enemies;

**43** oft he delivered them. But they wrathed him in their counsel; and they were made low in their wicked-nesses.

**44** And he saw, when they were set in tribulation; and he heard the prayer of them.

**45** And he was mindful of his testa-ment; and it repented him by the multitude of his mercy.

**46** And he gave them into mercies; in the sight of all men, that had taken them.

**47** Our Lord God, make thou us safe; and gather together us from nations. That we acknowledge to thine holy name; and have glory in thy praising.

**48** Blessed be the Lord God of Israel from the world and till into the world; and all the people shall say, Be it done, be it done.
