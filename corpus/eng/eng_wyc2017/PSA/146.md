# PSALMS 146

**1** Alleluia. My soul, praise thou the Lord;

**2** I shall praise the Lord in my life; I shall sing to my God as long as I shall be.

**3** Do not ye trust in princes; neither in the sons of men, in whom is no health.

**4** The spirit of him shall go out, and he shall turn again into his earth; in that day all the thoughts of them shall perish.

**5** He is blessed, of whom the God of Jacob is his helper; his hope is in his Lord God,

**6** that made heaven, and earth; the sea, and all things that be in those [or them]. Which keepeth truth into the world,

**7** he maketh doom to them that suffer wrong; he giveth meat to them that be hungry. The Lord unbindeth fettered men;

**8** the Lord lighteneth blind men. The Lord raiseth men hurled or hurtled down; the Lord loveth just men.

**9** The Lord keepeth comelings; he shall take up a motherless child, and a widow; and he shall destroy the ways of sinners.

**10** The Lord shall reign into the worlds; Zion, thy God shall reign in generation and into generation.
