# PSALMS 62

**1** To victory, over Jeduthun, the psalm of David. Whether my soul shall not be subject to God; for mine health is of him.

**2** For why he is both my God, and mine health; mine up-taker, I shall no more be moved.

**3** How long fall ye on a man? all ye slain; as to a wall bowed, and as a wall of stone without mortar cast down.

**4** Nevertheless they thought to put away my price, I ran in thirst; with their mouth they blessed, and in their heart they cursed.

**5** Nevertheless, my soul, be thou subject to God; for my patience is of him.

**6** For he is my God, and my saviour; mine helper, I shall not pass out.

**7** Mine health, and my glory is in God; God is the giver of mine help, and mine hope is in God.

**8** All the gathering together of the people, hope ye in God, pour ye out your hearts before him; God is our helper without end.

**9** Nevertheless the sons of men be vain; the sons of men be liars in balances, that they deceive of vanity into the same thing.

**10** Do not ye have hope in wicked-ness, and do not ye covet ravens; if riches be plenteous, do not ye set the heart thereto.

**11** God spake once, I heard these two things; that power is of God,

**12** and, thou Lord, mercy is to thee; for thou shalt yield to each man by his works.
