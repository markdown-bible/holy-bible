# JOB 31

**1** I made [a] covenant with mine eyes, that I should not think on a virgin.

**2** For what part should God above have in me, and what heritage should Almighty God of high things have in me?

**3** Whether perdition is not to a wicked man, and alienation of God is to men working wickedness?

**4** Whether he beholdeth not my ways, and numbereth all my goings?

**5** If I have gone in vanity, and my foot hath hasted to go in guile [or treachery],

**6** God weigh me in a just balance, and know he my simpleness.

**7** If my steps have bowed from the way; and if mine eyes have pursued [or followed] mine heart, consenting to lust, and if a spot have cleaved to mine hands;

**8** sow I, and another eat, and my generation be drawn out by the root.

**9** And if mine heart was deceived on a woman, and if I have set ambush at the door of my friend;

**10** my wife be then the whore of another man, and other men be bowed down upon her.

**11** For this is unleaveful, and the most wickedness.

**12** Fire is devouring till to wasting, and drawing up by the root all generations.

**13** If I despised to take doom with my servant and with mine handmaid, when they strived against me.

**14** What soothly shall I do, when God shall rise up to deem? and when he shall ask, what shall I answer to him?

**15** Whether he, that wrought also him, made not me in the womb, and one God formed me in the womb?

**16** If I denied to poor men that, that they would, and if I made the eyes of a widow to abide;

**17** if I alone ate my morsel, and a fatherless child ate not thereof;

**18** for merciful doing increased with me, from my young childhood, and it went out of my mother’s womb with me;

**19** if I despised a man passing forth by me, for he had not a cloth, and a poor man without covering;

**20** if his sides blessed not me, and were not made hot of the fleece of my sheep;

**21** if I raised up mine hand upon a fatherless child, yea, when I saw me the higher in the gate;

**22** my shoulder fall from his joint, and mine arm with his bones be all-broken.

**23** For ever[more] I dreaded God, as waves waxing great upon me; and I might not bear his burden.

**24** If I guessed gold my strength, and I said to pured gold, Thou art my trust;

**25** if I was glad on my many riches, and for mine hand found full many things;

**26** if I saw the sun, when it shined, and the moon going clearly;

**27** and if mine heart was glad in private, and if I kissed mine hand with my mouth;

**28** the which is the most wickedness, and denying against the highest God;

**29** if I had joy at the falling of him, that hated me, and if I joyed fully [or full out joyed], that evil had found him;

**30** for I gave not my throat to do sin, that I should assail and curse his soul;

**31** if the men of my tabernacle said not, Who giveth, that we be [ful] filled of his fleshes?

**32** a pilgrim dwelled not withoutforth; my door was open to a way-goer or a way-faring man;

**33** if I as [a] man hid my sin, and covered my wickedness in my bosom;

**34** if I dreaded at [the] full great multitude, and if despising of neigh-bours made me afeared; and not more, I was still, and went not out of the door;

**35** who giveth then an helper to me, that Almighty God hear my desire? that he that deemeth, write a book,

**36** that I bear it on my shoulder, and encompass it as a crown to me?

**37** By all my degrees I shall pronounce it, and I shall as offering offer it to the prince.

**38** If my land crieth against me, and his furrows weep with it;

**39** if I ate fruits thereof without money, and I tormented the soul of earth-tillers of it;

**40** a briar grow to me for wheat, and a thorn for barley.
