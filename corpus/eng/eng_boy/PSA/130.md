# Psalms 130

**1** A Song of Degrees. O Lord, my heart is not exalted, 
Neither have mine eyes been haughtily raised: 
Neither have I exercised myself in great matters, 
Nor in things too wonderful for me.

**2** I shall have sinned if I have not been humble, 
But have exalted my soul: 
According to the relation of a weaned child to his mother, 
So wilt thou recompense my soul.

**3** Let Israel hope in the Lord, 
From henceforth and forever.
