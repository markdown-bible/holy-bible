# Psalms 133

**1** See how good and how pleasant it is for brothers to live together in unity!

**2** It is like the precious oil on the head, that ran down on the beard, 
even Aaron’s beard, 
that came down on the edge of his robes,

**3** like the dew of Hermon, that comes down on the hills of Zion; 
for there the LORD gives the blessing, 
even life forever more.
