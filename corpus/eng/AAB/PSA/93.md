# Psalms 93

**1** The LORD reigns! He is robed in majesty; the LORD has clothed and armed Himself with strength. The world indeed is firmly established; it cannot be moved.

**2** Your throne was established long ago; You are from all eternity.

**3** The floodwaters have risen, O LORD; the rivers have raised their voice; 
the seas lift up their pounding waves.

**4** Above the roar of many waters— the mighty breakers of the sea— 
the LORD on high is majestic.

**5** Your testimonies are fully confirmed; holiness adorns Your house, O LORD, 
for all the days to come.
