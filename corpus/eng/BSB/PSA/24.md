# Psalms 24

**1** The earth is the LORD’s, and the fullness thereof, the world and all who dwell therein.

**2** For He has founded it upon the seas and established it upon the waters.

**3** Who may ascend the hill of the LORD? Who may stand in His holy place?

**4** He who has clean hands and a pure heart, who does not lift up his soul to an idol 
or swear deceitfully.

**5** He will receive blessing from the LORD and vindication from the God of his salvation.

**6** Such is the generation of those who seek Him, who seek Your face, O God of Jacob. Selah

**7** Lift up your heads, O gates! Be lifted up, O ancient doors, 
that the King of Glory may enter!

**8** Who is this King of Glory? The LORD strong and mighty, 
the LORD mighty in battle.

**9** Lift up your heads, O gates! Be lifted up, O ancient doors, 
that the King of Glory may enter!

**10** Who is He, this King of Glory? The LORD of Hosts— 
He is the King of Glory. Selah
