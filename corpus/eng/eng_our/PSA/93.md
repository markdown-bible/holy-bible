# Psalms 93

**1** Adonai reigns! He is clothed with majesty! 
Adonai is armed with strength. The world also is established. It can’t be moved.

**2** Your throne is established from long ago. You are from everlasting.

**3** The floods have lifted up, Adonai, the floods have lifted up their voice. 
The floods lift up their waves.

**4** Above the voices of many waters, the mighty breakers of the sea, 
Adonai on high is mighty.

**5** Your testimonies stand firm. Holiness adorns your house, 
Adonai, forever more.
