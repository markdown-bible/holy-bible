# TEHILLIM 123

**1** (Shir HaMa'alot [Song of Ascents]) 1 Unto Thee lift I up mine eyes, 
O Thou that dwellest in Shomayim.

**2** Hinei, as the eyes of avadim look unto the yad adoneihem, 
and as the eyes of a shifchah (maiden) unto the yad of her gevirah; 
so our eyes wait upon Hashem Eloheinu, 
until He have channun (favor, grace) upon us.

**3** Channenu Hashem, channenu; 
for we are more than sated with buz (contempt).

**4** Nafsheinu (our nefesh) is more than sated with the scorn of those that are at ease, 
and with the buz (contempt) of the g'eiyonim (proud ones, arrogant ones).
