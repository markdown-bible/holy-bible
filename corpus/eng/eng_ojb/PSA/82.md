# TEHILLIM 82

**1** (Mizmor of Asaph) 1 Elohim standeth in the Adat El; 
He judgeth among the elohim.

**2** Ad mosai (How long) will ye judge unjustly, 
and show partiality to the resha'im? 
Selah

**3** Defend the poor and yatom (fatherless); 
do justice to the oni (afflicted) and needy.

**4** Deliver the poor and needy; 
rid them out of the yad resha'im.

**5** They know not, neither will they understand; 
they walk on in choshech; 
all the foundations of ha'aretz are shaken.

**6** I have said, elohim ye are; 
and all of you are Bnei HaElyon.

**7** But ye shall die like adam, 
and fall like one of the sarim (princes).

**8** Arise, Elohim, judge ha'aretz; 
for Thou shalt inherit kol HaGoyim.
