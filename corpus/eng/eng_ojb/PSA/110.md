# TEHILLIM 110

**1** (Of Dovid. Mizmor) 1Hashem said unto Adoni, 
Sit thou at My right hand, 
until I make thine enemies 
a footstool for thy feet.

**2** Hashem shall stretch forth 
the rod of thy might out of Tziyon; 
rule thou in the midst of thine enemies.

**3** Thy people, willing in the Day of thy Army, 
will be arrayed in the splendor of kodesh (holiness); 
from the womb of the dawn, 
cometh to thee thy young men as the tal (dew).

**4** Hashem hath sworn, and will not relent, 
Thou art a kohen l'olam al divrati Malki-Tzedek 
(kohen forever in respect to the order of Malki-Tzedek).

**5** Adonoi at thy right hand shall dash melachim (kings) 
in pieces in the Yom Afo (Day of His Wrath).

**6** He shall judge among the Goyim, 
He shall heap up geviyyot (corpses); 
He shall dash in pieces Rosh (Head); al Eretz Rabbah (over Great, Whole Earth).

**7** Of the brook in the Derech shall he drink; 
therefore shall he lift up [as victor] the Rosh.
