# Psalms of Solomon 8

**1** Distress and the sound of war hath my ear heard; 
The sound of a trumpet announcing slaughter and calamity,

**2** The sound of much people as of an exceeding high wind, 
As a tempest with mighty fire sweeping through the Negeb.

**3** And I said in my heart, 
Where then will God judge him?

**4** A sound I hear moving toward Jerusalem, the holy city.

**5** My loins were broken at what I heard, 
My knees tottered, my heart was afraid: 
My bones were dismayed like flax.

**6** I said, They establish their ways in righteousness.

**7** I thought upon the judgments of God since the creation of heaven and earth; 
I held God righteous in his judgments which have been from of old.

**8** God laid bare their sins in the full light of day; 
All the earth came to know the righteous judgments of God.

**9** In secret places underground their iniquities were committed to provoke him to anger; 
They wrought confusion, son with mother and father with daughter;

**10** They committed adultery, every man with his neighbor’s wife. 
They concluded covenants with one another with an oath touching these things.

**11** They plundered the sanctuary of God, 
As though there was no avenger.

**12** They trod the altar of the Lord, coming straight from all manner of uncleanness; 
And with menstrual blood they defiled the sacrifices, as though these were common flesh.

**13** They left no sin undone, wherein they surpassed not the heathen.

**14** Therefore God mingled for them a spirit of wandering; 
And gave them to drink a cup of undiluted wine, that they might become drunken.

**15** He brought him that is from the end of the earth, that smiteth mightily; 
He decreed war against Jerusalem, and against her land.

**16** The princes of the land went to meet him with joy: 
They said unto him, Blessed be thy way! Come ye, enter ye in with peace.

**17** They made the rough ways even, before his entering in; 
They opened the gates to Jerusalem, they crowned its walls.

**18** As a father entereth the house of his sons, so he entered Jerusalem in peace; 
He established his feet there in great safety.

**19** He captured her fortresses and the wall of Jerusalem; 
For God himself led him in safety, while they wandered.

**20** He destroyed their princes and everyone wise in counsel; 
He poured out the blood of the inhabitants of Jerusalem, like the water of uncleanness.

**21** He led away their sons and daughters, whom they had begotten in defilement.

**22** They did according to their uncleanness, even as their fathers had done: 
They defiled Jerusalem and the things that had been hallowed to the name of God.

**23** But God hath shown himself righteous in his judgments upon the nations of the earth; 
And the pious servants of God are like innocent lambs in their midst.

**24** Worthy to be praised is the Lord that judgeth the whole earth in his righteousness.

**25** Behold, now, O God, thou hast shown us thy judgment in thy righteousness; 
Our eyes have seen thy judgments, O God.

**26** We have justified thy name that is honored forever; 
For thou art the God of righteousness, judging Israel with chastening.

**27** Turn, O God, thy mercy upon us, 
And have pity upon us;

**28** Gather together the dispersed of Israel, with mercy and goodness; 
For thy faithfulness is with us.

**29** And though we have stiffened our neck, 
Yet thou art our chastener;

**30** Overlook us not, O our God, 
Lest the nations swallow us up, as though there were none to deliver.

**31** But thou art our God from the beginning, 
And upon thee is our hope set, O Lord;

**32** And we will not depart from thee, 
For good are thy judgments upon us.

**33** Ours and our children’s be thy good pleasure forever; 
O Lord our Savior, we shall never more be moved.

**34** The Lord is worthy to be praised for his judgments with the mouth of his pious ones; 
And blessed be Israel of the Lord forever.
