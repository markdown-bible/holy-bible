# Additions to Esther 5

**1** Esther, what is the matter? I am thy brother, be of good cheer: thou shalt not die, for our commandment is for our subjects: come near.

**2** And as she was speaking, she fell down for faintness. Then the king was troubled, and all his servants comforted her.
