# JOB 36

**1** Also Elihu added, and spake these things,

**2** Suffer thou me a little, and I shall show to thee; for yet I have that, that I shall speak for God.

**3** I shall rehearse my knowing from the beginning; and I shall prove my worker just [or rightwise].

**4** For verily my words be without leasing, and by them perfect knowing shall be proved to thee.

**5** God casteth not away mighty men, since he is mighty;

**6** but he saveth not wicked men, and he giveth doom to poor men.

**7** He taketh not away his eyes from a just [or rightwise] man; and he setteth kings in their seat without end, and they be raised up there.

**8** And if they be in chains, and be bound with the ropes of poverty,

**9** he shall show to them their works, and their great trespasses; for they were violent, either raveners.

**10** Also he shall open their ears, that he chastise them; and he shall speak to them, that they turn again from their wickedness.

**11** If they hear him, and keep his behests, they shall full-fill their days in good, and their years in glory.

**12** Soothly if they hear not, they shall pass away by sword, and they shall be wasted in folly.

**13** Feigners and false men stir the wrath of God; and they shall not cry to God, and acknowledge their guilt, when they be bound.

**14** The soul of them shall die in tempest; and the life of them among womanish men.

**15** He shall deliver a poor man from his anguish; and he shall open his ear in tribulation.

**16** Therefore he shall save thee from the strait mouth of the broadest tribulation, and not having a founda-ment under it; and the rest of thy table shall be full of fatness.

**17** Thy cause is deemed as the cause of a wicked man; and thou shalt receive thy cause and thy doom.

**18** Therefore wrath overcome thee not, that thou oppress any man; and the multitude of gifts bow thee not.

**19** Put down thy greatness without tribulation, and put down all strong men by strength.

**20** Delay thou not the night, that peoples go up for them.

**21** Be thou ware, that thou bow not to wickedness; for thou hast begun to follow this wickedness after wretched-ness.

**22** Lo! God is high in his strength, and none is like him among the givers of law.

**23** Who may seek out the ways of God? either who dare say to him, Thou hast wrought wickedness?

**24** Have thou mind, that thou knowest not his work, of whom men have sung.

**25** All men see God; each man be-holdeth afar.

**26** Lo! God is great, over-coming our knowing; the number of his years is without number.

**27** He taketh the drops of rain; and he poureth out rains at the likeness of floodgates,

**28** which come down of the clouds, that cover all things above.

**29** If he will stretch forth clouds as his tent,

**30** and lightning with his light from above, he shall cover, yea, the hinges of the sea.

**31** For by these things he deemeth peoples, and giveth meat to many deadly men.

**32** In hands he hideth light; and commandeth it, that it come again.

**33** He telleth of it to his friend, that it is his possession; and that he may ascend [or go up] to it.
