# JOB 35

**1** Therefore Elihu spake again, these things,

**2** Whether thy thought seemeth even, either rightful, to thee, that thou should-est say, I am right-fuller [or more right-wise] than God?

**3** For thou saidest, That, that is good, pleaseth not thee; either what profit-eth it to thee, if I do sin?

**4** Therefore I shall answer to thy words, and to thy friends with thee.

**5** See thou, and behold heaven, and behold thou the air, and know that God is higher than thou. [Behold heaven, and look, and mindfully see the clouds, that is higher than thou.]

**6** If thou sinnest, what shalt thou annoy him? and if thy wickednesses be multiplied, what shalt thou do against him?

**7** Certainly if thou doest justly [or rightwisely], what shalt thou give to him; either what shall he take of thine hand?

**8** Thy wickedness shall annoy a man, which is like thee; and thy right-wiseness shall help the son of a man.

**9** Men shall cry for the multitude of false challengers, and they shall wail for the violence of the power of tyrants.

**10** And Job said not, Where is God, that made me, and that gave songs in the night?

**11** And the which teacheth us above the beasts of [the] earth, and he shall teach us above the birds of heaven.

**12** There they shall cry, and God shall not hear them, for the pride of evil men.

**13** For God shall not hear without cause, and Almighty God shall behold the causes of each man.

**14** Yea, when thou sayest, He behold-eth not; be thou deemed before him, and abide thou him.

**15** For now the Lord bringeth not in his strong vengeance, neither he avengeth felonies greatly here.

**16** Therefore Job openeth his mouth in vain, and multiplieth words without knowing.
