# Psalms 134

**1** Behold, bless ye the Lord, all ye servants of the Lord, 
Which by night stand in the house of the Lord.

**2** Lift up your hands in the sanctuary, 
And bless the Lord.

**3** The Lord that made heaven and earth 
Bless thee out of Zion.
