# Psalms 150

**1** Alleluia. Praise God in his holy places: 
Praise him in the firmament of his power.

**2** Praise him on account of his mighty acts: 
Praise him according to his abundant greatness.

**3** Praise him with the sound of a trumpet: 
Praise him with psaltery and harp.

**4** Praise him with timbrel and dance: 
Praise him with stringed instruments and the organ.

**5** Praise him with melodious cymbals: 
Praise him with loud cymbals.

**6** Let everything that has breath praise the Lord.
