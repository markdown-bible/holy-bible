# Psalms 57

**1** For the end. Destroy not: by David, for a memorial.

**2** If ye do indeed speak righteousness, 
Then do ye judge rightly, ye sons of men.

**3** For ye work iniquities in your hearts in the earth: 
Your hands plot unrighteousness.

**4** Sinners have gone astray from the womb: 
They go astray from the belly: they speak lies.

**5** Their venom is like that of a serpent; 
As that of a deaf asp, and that stops her ears;

**6** Which will not hear the voice of charmers, 
Nor heed the charm prepared skillfully by the wise.

**7** God has crushed their teeth in their mouth: 
God has broken the cheek teeth of the lions.

**8** They shall utterly pass away like water running through: 
He shall bend his bow till they shall fail.

**9** They shall be destroyed as melted wax: 
The fire has fallen and they have not seen the sun.

**10** Before your thorns feel the white thorn, 
He shall swallow you up as living, as in his wrath.

**11** The righteous shall rejoice when he sees the vengeance of the ungodly: 
He shall wash his hands in the blood of the sinner.

**12** And a man shall say, Verily then there is a reward for the righteous: 
Verily there is a God that judges them in the earth.
