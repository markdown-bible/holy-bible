# Psalms 137

**1** By the rivers of Babylon, there we sat down. Yes, we wept, when we remembered Zion.

**2** On the willows in that land, we hung up our harps.

**3** For there, those who led us captive asked us for songs. Those who tormented us demanded songs of joy: 
“Sing us one of the songs of Zion!”

**4** How can we sing the LORD’s song in a foreign land?

**5** If I forget you, Jerusalem, let my right hand forget its skill.

**6** Let my tongue stick to the roof of my mouth if I don’t remember you, if I don’t prefer Jerusalem above my chief joy.

**7** Remember, LORD, against the children of Edom in the day of Jerusalem, who said, “Raze it! 
Raze it even to its foundation!”

**8** Daughter of Babylon, doomed to destruction, he will be happy who repays you, 
as you have done to us.

**9** Happy shall he be, who takes and dashes your little ones against the rock.
