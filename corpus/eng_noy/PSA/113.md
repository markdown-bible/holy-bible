# Psalms 113

**1** Praise ye the LORD! Praise, O ye servants of the LORD! Praise the name of the LORD!

**2** Blessed be the name of the LORD From this time forth, even for ever!

**3** From the rising of the sun to its going down, May the LORD'S name be praised!

**4** The LORD is high above all nations; His glory is above the heavens.

**5** Who is like the LORD, our God, That dwelleth on high,

**6** That looketh down low Upon the heavens and the earth?

**7** He raiseth the poor from the dust, And exalteth the needy from the dunghill,

**8** To set him among princes, Even among the princes of his people.

**9** He causeth the barren woman to dwell in a house, A joyful mother of children. Praise ye the LORD!
