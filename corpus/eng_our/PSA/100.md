# Psalms 100

**1** Shout for joy to Adonai, all you lands!

**2** Abad ·Serve· Adonai with gladness. 
Come before his presence with singing.

**3** Know that Adonai, he is God. It is he who has made us, and we are his. 
We are his people, and the sheep of his pasture.

**4** Enter into his gates with thanksgiving, into his courts with tehilahot ·praise songs·. 
Yadah ·Extend hands in thankful praise· to him, and bless his name.

**5** For Adonai is good. His chesed ·loving-kindness· endures forever, 
his faithfulness to all generations.
