# Psalms 15

**1** Adonai, who shall dwell in your sanctuary? Who shall live on your holy hill?

**2** He who walks blamelessly does what is right, and speaks truth in his heart;

**3** He who does not slander with his tongue, nor does evil to his friend, 
nor casts slurs against his fellow man;

**4** In whose eyes a vile man is despised, but who honors those who fear Adonai; 
he who keeps an oath even when it hurts, and does not change;

**5** he who does not lend out his money for usury, nor take a bribe against the innocent. 
He who does these things shall never be shaken.
