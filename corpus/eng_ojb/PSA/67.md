# TEHILLIM 67

**1** (For the one directing. On Neginot. With stringed instruments. Mizmor. Shir)

**2** Elohim be channun (gracious) unto us, and put a brocha upon us; 
and may He make His face to shine upon us. 
Selah

**3** That Thy Derech may be known upon HaAretz, 
Thy Yeshu'ah (salvation) among kol Goyim.

**4** Let the Amim praise Thee, O Elohim; 
let kol Amim praise Thee.

**5** O let the peoples be glad and sing for joy, 
for Thou shalt judge the people righteously, 
and govern the peoples upon HaAretz. 
Selah

**6** Let the people praise Thee, O Elohim; 
let kol Amim praise Thee.

**7** Then shall eretz yield her yevul (increase, produce, harvest); 
and Elohim, even Eloheinu, shall put a brocha upon us.

**8** Elohim shall bless us; 
and kol afsei aretz (all the ends of the earth) shall fear Him.
