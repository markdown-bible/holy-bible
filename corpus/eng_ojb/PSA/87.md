# TEHILLIM 87

**1** (Of the Bnei Korach. A mizmor. A shir) 1 On the Harei Kodesh (Holy Mountain) stands His foundation.

**2** Hashem loveth the sha'arei Tziyon 
more than all the mishkenot Ya'akov.

**3** Glorious things are spoken of thee, 
O Ir HaElohim. 
Selah

**4** To them that know me, I will make mention of Rachav and Babylon; 
hinei, Philistia, and Tzor, with Ethiopia: 
this man was born there.

**5** But of Tziyon it shall be said, 
This one and that one were born in her; 
and Elyon Himself shall establish it.

**6** Hashem shall register, when He writeth up the people, 
that this one was born there. 
Selah

**7** The sharim (ones singing) as well as the cholelim (players on instruments) shall be there; 
all my fountains are in thee.
