# TEHILLIM 13

**1** (For the one directing. Mizmor Dovid)

**2** How long wilt Thou forget me, Hashem? Netzach (forever)? 
How long wilt Thou hide Thy face from me?

**3** How long shall I take counsel in my nefesh, 
having sorrow in my lev daily? 
How long shall mine oyev be exalted over me?

**4** Consider and hear me, Hashem Elohai; 
enlighten mine eyes, lest I sleep the sleep of mavet;

**5** Lest mine oyev say, 
I have prevailed against him; 
and those that trouble me rejoice 
when I am defeated.

**6** But I have trusted in Thy chesed; 
my lev shall rejoice in Thy Yeshu'ah (salvation). 
6 I will sing unto Hashem, 
because He hath dealt bountifully with me.
