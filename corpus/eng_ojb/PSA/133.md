# TEHILLIM 133

**1** (Shir HaMa'alot, of Dovid) 1Hinei, mah tov umah na'im shevet achim gahm yachad! 
(how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity)!

**2** It is like the precious shemen upon the rosh, 
that ran down upon the beard, 
even Aharonʼs beard; 
that went down to the collar of his robes;

**3** As the tal of Chermon, 
and as the dew that descended upon the mountains of Tziyon; 
for there Hashem commanded the brocha, 
even Chayyim Ad Olam.
