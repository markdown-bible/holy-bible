# TEHILLIM 12

**1** (For the one directing. According to the Sheminit. Mizmor Dovid)

**2** Help, Hashem; for the chasid is no more; 
for the emunim (faithful ones) vanish from among Bnei Adam.

**3** They speak vanity every one with his re'a (neighbor); 
with flattering lips and with a double heart do they speak.

**4** Hashem shall cut off all flattering lips, 
and the leshon that speaketh gedolot;

**5** Who have said, With leshoneinu (our tongue) will we prevail; 
our lips are our own: who is Adon over us?

**6** For the oppression of the aniyim, 
for the groaning of the needy, 
now will I arise, saith Hashem; 
I will set him in safety from him that sneers at him.

**7** The words of Hashem are amarot tehorot (pure words); 
as kesef refined in the furnace on the earth, 
purified seven times.

**8** Thou shalt be shomer over them, Hashem, 
Thou shalt preserve him from this generation forever.

**9** The resha'im strut on every side, 
when the vilest among the Bnei Adam are exalted.
