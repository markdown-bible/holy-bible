# TEHILLIM 40

**1** (For the one directing, Of Dovid. Mizmor)

**2** I waited patiently for Hashem; 
and He inclined unto me, 
and heard my cry.

**3** Hashem brought me up also out of a horrible pit, 
out of the miry clay, 
and set my feet upon a rock, 
and established my goings.

**4** And He hath put a shir chadash in my mouth, 
even tehillah (hymn of praise) unto Eloheinu; 
many shall see it, and fear, 
and shall trust in Hashem.

**5** Ashrei is that man that maketh Hashem his trust, 
and looketh not to the proud ones, 
nor such as turn aside to lies (false g-ds).

**6** Many, O Hashem Elohai, 
are Thy wonderful works which Thou hast done, 
and Thy thoughts which are to us-ward; 
they cannot be recounted unto Thee; 
if I would declare and speak of them, 
they are more than can be numbered.

**7** Zevach and minchah Thou didst not desire; 
mine ears hast Thou pierced; 
olah (burnt offering) and chata'ah (sin offering) hast Thou not required.

**8** Then said I, Hinei, I come; 
in the megillat sefer it is written of me,

**9** I delight to do Thy will, O Elohai; 
yea, Thy torah is within my heart.

**10** I have preached tzedek in the kahal rav (great congregation); 
hinei, I have not restrained my lips, 
O Hashem, Thou knowest.

**11** I have not hid thy tzedek within my lev; 
I have declared Thy emunah (faithfulness) and Thy teshuah (salvation); 
I have not concealed Thy chesed 
and Thy emes from the kahal rav.

**12** Withhold not Thou thy rachamim from me, O Hashem; 
let Thy chesed and Thy emes continually preserve me.

**13** For innumerable evils have compassed me about; 
mine avonot (iniquities) have overtaken me, 
so that I am not able to look up; 
they are more than the hairs of mine head; 
therefore my lev faileth me.

**14** Be pleased, O Hashem, to deliver me; 
O Hashem, make haste to help me.

**15** Let them be ashamed and mutually confounded 
that seek after my nefesh to destroy it; 
let them be driven backward and put to shame 
that wish me evil.

**16** Let them be appalled because of their boshet (shame) 
that say unto me, Aha, aha.

**17** Let all those that seek Thee 
rejoice and be glad in Thee; 
let such as love Thy teshuah say continually: 
Hashem be magnified!

**18** But I am poor and needy; 
yet Adonoi thinketh upon me; 
Thou art my ezer and my deliverer; 
make no tarrying, O Elohai.
