# TEHILLIM 125

**1** (Shir HaMa'alot [Song of Ascents]) 1They that trust in Hashem 
shall be as Mount Tziyon, which is immoveable, 
and abideth l'olam.

**2** As harim (the mountains) surround Yerushalayim, 
so Hashem surrounds His people 
from henceforth v'ad olam (and to forever).

**3** For the shevet (scepter) of the resha shall not rest 
upon the goral (allotment, property) of the tzaddikim; 
lest the tzaddikim put forth 
their hands unto iniquity.

**4** Do good, Hashem, unto the tovim, 
and to them that are upright in their hearts.

**5** As for such as turn aside unto their crooked ways, 
Hashem shall turn them away with the workers of iniquity; 
but shalom shall be upon Yisroel.
